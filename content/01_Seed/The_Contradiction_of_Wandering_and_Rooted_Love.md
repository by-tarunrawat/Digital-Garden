One thing I feel strongly about is freedom and equality. These are big concepts, what do I really feel strongly about. I feel We should be free to make our own choices. I believe we should be able to say or do anything. We should be free to express ourselve. We are censored in this world today. Now I think I'm just parroting about things. What do I feel strongly about. I feel strongly about love. I feel strongly about relationships. I don't know man. I feel strongly about, life. What am i talking about. Its not life. I feel like we should be able to explore things. We should travel the world. We should meet people. Live with each other. But I also like my life of going to friends having someone to love. Aren't these contradictory. Where I want to go out in the world and live in a shared environment as well. How do I find balance between the two. I feel I shouldn't be bound by a job. I feel like I should be able to do things that I want. Nobody has ever stopped me ehn why haven't I done anything. When you say you want to write stories then what does that mean if you're not exactly writing. You read books pon writing but are not doing anything. Not applying any concepts. Not sticking to anything. What are you going to achieve with that. Are you sure you even want to become a writer. IT will be always this hard. And what is the payoff? You don't know. IT might not even be good. Are you sure you want to do this. I think I do. I feel quite strongly about the fact that I want to do something on my own. Be a writer, a painter a filmmaker. I want to do all these things. So to do this I will have to be disciplined. I will have to take action steps. I can't sit still. I have to change things. But I need money as well. What to do? I have to find a way to make money tosustain myself. I ahve to in my remaining hours take action towards my goals. I want to be a writer, then be a writer. Write a fucking story. Show it to people. Write another. WAnt to be a filmmake. Go out. DO it. Write and make a story. CApture stories around you. DO whatever you want to do. GO be.
