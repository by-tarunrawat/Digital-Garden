Date: 21-05-2019
Topic: Write about a time you changed your mind

I would call myself an open person. By that I mean I’m open to other ideas. My own opinions aren’t fixed. I might change my mind if you present a convincing argument.
Last year, a few women came forward with their stories about sexual harassment and sexual assault. It exposed a few powerful men in Hollywood. Some of them had to face charges in court. That started an entire movement based on sexual harassment in the film industry and in the workplace. More powerful people were brought to face charges. Women have been speaking about their experiences for a long time. There have never been enough ears to listen. This time their were plenty.
It worked in a really efficient way. A woman comes forward with a name and story. IN some cases even the stories weren’t needed. Just a name was enough. If the person was famous and powerful, their name would appear in an article, or a column exposing them. That article would then be picked up on facebook or twitter where hundreds of people see it. Hundreds of people now become thousands of people with hundreds sharing it with their network. Their is criticism. People attack the harasser online. The express their intent to never watch their content again. TO never use their product. The harasser is then fired. His regular life taken from him. It was tainted with his crimes anyway.
In the beginning, I believed all the women that came forward. I agreed with their claims. And criticized these disgusting men in power. I wished bad things on them. The metoo movement was exactly what was needed. It was brining bad people to justice.
Then I thought about it. Everyday there was a claim from someone accusing a celebrity, businessman or politician. And immediately there were consequences. It seemed like justice. But, who is filtering out the wrong claims from the right ones? Is anyone verifying the stories. The accused have been given a public hearing and punished swiftly. No one really needs to be right. They just need to have a claim. 
I realized this was not the right way of doing this. It has its merits. It brings to attention stories that have been buried for years. We see the real faces of some of the most beloved and respected people around the world. But do we believe it as told to us? Feminists say yes. We should always believe the victim. But should we believe all victims? Some of them lie. They are like people. Some people are good and some are bad. It’s as simple as that. WE do want the harassers and the rapists, and violent people exposed and properly punished. And so there is a satisfaction in seeing justice met so swiftly. But do we want innocent people to be punished as well. Destroy their lives. And their lies are probably linked to other people as well. Heir family, thier friends. People they work with. 
We all want criminals to be punished. We want bad people to be exposed. But we can’t be responsible for destroying an innocent person. That would be criminal in my eyes. How can we carry on giving out justice to people if we are committing crimes along the way.
