The dilemma at the heart of my story is the how to go from wishing to becoming. How can I or the protagonist  to actually take himself seriously. And take the first step. THe first dilemma I think is should I look for a job. Then maybe the writer dies. Because then it is not a serious consideration. Maybe it is I don't know. But the dilemma is to try look for a job, that you can't find anyway, or to take the fist step. Start writing. Just write and rewrite and publish. make a blog. Write humour. Write songs, poetry. Try making songs out of it. So the dillema is whether take a leap of faith and become a full time writer/traveler/filmaker, or whatever you want to be or glook for a job find something asolve your money problems and then write on the side. In the first case you are leaving out money altogether. THere is not money in it for you. You have to accept that. THen what. DO you not make money. DO you keep yourself dependent on your family. Maybe. In the second scnario, you'll probably do a job that you don't like. You will perhaps have no time to write. You have a girlfriend, and freinds that you have social obligations with. SO what do you do. THere is so much going on in your life. how do you make time for something.
