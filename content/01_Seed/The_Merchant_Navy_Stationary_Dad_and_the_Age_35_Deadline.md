Whenever he came back home we would just sit at home. THe most we would do is go to Pauri, where my grandmother lived. It's not that I didnt lke my grandmmother. I loved her. But I never saw the world. Never left dehradun. THe first time I travelled on a train was after my teenage years. Think about that.
My dad travelled so much for work, that when he came home he was sick of it. So we just didn't go anywhere. I think a little of his qualities have seeped into me. I want to go travel, but I hesitate. It might be because of money and job. If I had a lot of money. I can still make that dream come true.
I just have to make a lot of money very fast. I don't have time. I'm 31 already and I can't take 10-20 years to make that kind of money. I need to make money before I'm 35. I want my mother to be in great shape to travel.
