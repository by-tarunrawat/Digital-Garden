my greatest regret has been not pursuing a career in arts or sports. I feel like that is something I would have been great at. I was great at sports. Cricket, I felt like a king. I felt like a rckstar each time I had a ball and a bat in hand. I felt like I could do anything. Most importantly I had the will to do the hardwork for it. I would've toiled on the ground for hours and hours for days on end If it meant I was playing cricket. I don't mind the sun. THe rain i can manage, just give me a bat in hand. I'll even stand on the outfield waiting for the ball to come to me.
Writing also made me feel that way. I don't remember anyone writing anything in school I would write poems, and I used to draw and my friends would give me compliments. They were as surprised as I was that I could write what I wrote. But I felt proud. WHy did I stop. I could've continued. I could've kept on working on my language and skills. Maybe today, when I have nothing else to do I would've been a published author that no one has read. That would be better than an author that hasn't writen anything. I would've certainly improved. College wouldv'e been my prime. I wouldv'e killed it if I wouldn't have stopped writing. 
I wrote in school man. I wrote in college. I wrote in fucking Infosys. And people read me. THey liked what I wrote. It's weird but only I think I'm bad.
