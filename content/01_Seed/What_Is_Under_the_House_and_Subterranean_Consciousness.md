Date: 11-06-2019
Topic: Write about what’s under your house.

There are people. I take care of noise when I workout. I don’t want them to get disturbed by the pitter patter I make with my feet. Sometimes, when I’m watching TV, I’m lying down in the corner. I can smell the smoke from a cigarette. I can hear them talking on the phone. 
In Dehradun, my house was the first one to be built in the area. The whole are was empty. There was a huge piece of the plot of land. It was covered with bushes and trees. A section had been cleaned by nearby children to play cricket. Our house was built on a part of that land. 
I don’t know what is under my house. There probably are remnants of trees and plants. Those might have been dug up. If you dig deeper, maybe you could find oil. Maybe, fossils.
Another world maybe? Are their people living there? Is there some kind of life? What kind of life would even exist. One that travels through mud and concrete and metal. It has to be small. To move around the smallest gaps between atoms. Some kind of abstract shape. Something like smoke. It’s transparent. But conscious. It can move through small gaps. The ones that are between a stack of jenga tiles. We can’t really see the gaps, but air can still pass through. 
There are gases trapped inside the earth. What if the gases had a consciousness. Oxygen has consciousness. Maybe it’s a hive species. It knows where each and every part of it is in the universe. Same with others. 
It’s probably stupid to consider what I’m thinking seriously. But we understand so little about consciousness. We talk about it from our own perspective. So what if inanimate object like rocks and pebbles have consciousness. What if trees and plants are conscious — a statement that I fully believe in. We don’t have a clue how it works.
