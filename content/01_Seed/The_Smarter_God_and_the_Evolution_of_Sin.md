Date: 14-03-2019
Topic: Write about a justifiable sin.

Lying, cheating, stealing, murder, violence. We can bundle all the sins in these categories. A justifiable sin would be one of these things done for a good cause. Who decides a good cause? Lying for someone’s good. Lying to save someone from their feelings getting hurt. Killing for country. Religion. Stealing to give it away to the poor. 
I don’t consider lying to be a huge sin. You’re deceiving someone when you lie. But so does a chameleon, an octopus, when they change colors to catch their food. There are plants that deceive insects and rats. They lure them in with smell and a promise of food. And kill them as soon as they get close enough. 
Murder, deceit, violence. Nature permits everything. In fact it demands it from us. We decided all of this is sin. We can’t go around killing people. For anything. We can control the nature in us. 
Why? Why can’t we go around killing people? I mean. Why can’t we have every man, woman and children for themselves. Because that would mean that even I am left alone on my own. I have to defend myself against anyone trying to kill me. I cant offend anyone. So, comedy is out of the question. I have to fight for resources. For mates. The biggest and the strongest rule. Groups succeed. One or two strong people can’t stay alive without cooperation.
Maybe that’s how we figured out civilization and laws and morals and ethics. WE were probably just killing each other. Fighting for mates. Fighting for food. Killing each other for petty squabbles. But we lived in groups. So, we were cooperating with each other anyway. We figured, if we need some things that we didn’t have, we need to cooperate with other groups as well. We started making alliances. 
But there were problems still. We were breaking our alliances when the opportunity came. We cheated our friends. Giving them less than what was agreed. Threaten them with force. Probably killed people in those groups. And took over their things. 
Now we needed something else. We tried making an alliance with another group. They knew of our reputation. They decided not to trust us. And rightly so. We would kill them when we got what we came for. We still wanted what they had. So, we attacked them. They knew this was going to happen. They  were prepared. They killed most of our people. Some of us survived. We knew we wouldn’t survive very long. So we contacted another group. And asked them for an alliance. They did not trust us either. 
Who would trust a group like that. We all are the same species. They are as smart as us. They know what we’re capable of because they are capable of the same things. They can have the same thoughts. Because it’s our nature. It’s what it demands of us.
This was happening all over the world. Some alliances survived and prospered. Most didn’t work out. We had to figure out how to cooperate with each other without killing each other. Or deceiving each other. We started making rules. Rules of trade. We would exchange goods and resources. It would happen under some rules. But it would happen again. And again. We didn’t trust others because they were us.
This was a problem for our species. We weren’t thinking on that level. On an individual level, we were just thinking about our survival. We would live day to day. We would see the sun rise every morning. And that gave life to everything. Things were brighter. More colorful. Flowers would bloom. Fruits ripened. Other animals would come out. But then it would go away, and everything was dull. Cold. Animals out hunting us. We decided that sun gave us life along with everything else in the world. It created everything. 
It was one of our early gods. There are other contenders as well. Lightening, oceans, storms. Things we had no understanding of. Someone smart asked, if this is our god, and he created us and everything. Then what does he want from us? What does he want us to do? This was probably a strange question at the time. We’ve been asking that question for thousands of years, and it still fells strange. 
Someone even smarter, said god wants us to be nice to each other.
