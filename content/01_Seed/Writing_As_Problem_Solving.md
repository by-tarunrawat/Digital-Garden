There had been a tsunami recently, news was filled with instances of bomb blasts and terrorist attacks; the number of people dying were hundreds and thousands. It was a big deal for me because these guys were writers themselves. I loved their work. I would go as far as to say I was their fan. I stopped writing after I left Infosys. I started again at MICA, towards the end of first year. I cowrote a play with a friend. I had taken the premise from something I had read online and adapted it for the play. I had written a scene and my friend had written another scene, but we still had to connect them somehow and make in a part of one play. I figured that shit out. That was amazing. I think that is how you figure out a story. You write things down and treat it like a problem that you have to solve. Anyway, we performed that play, and got a standing ovation. One of the proudest moments of my life. It was a competition, and we were doing it non competitively. The judges approached us after the play was over and asked us if we want to make it a part of the competition. We considered it for a bit, but the fact that they considered it worthy enough was a huge compliment.
