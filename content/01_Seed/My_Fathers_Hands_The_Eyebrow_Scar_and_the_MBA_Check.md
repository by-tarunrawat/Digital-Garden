Date: 16-04-2019
Topic: Write about your father’s hands

My father had hard hands. Clean and hard. There was a tinge of yellow in his nails. He was a smoker. I had always hated his smoking. He had never hit me. Except once. I don’t remember what I did to piss him off — which wasn’t too hard to do. He did have a short temper. But he wasn’t a violent man. He hit me on the back of my head. Which made my forehead hit the sharp corner of a trunk. He immediately realized his mistake and patched me up. It left a mark on my eyebrow which is still there. He never hit me again.
He had big hands. They were clean. Only the standard lines that are on everybody’s hands. I don’t know what else to write about them. I’ve never thought about his hands. I do have an image in m mind. It’s pretty clear. I don’t know why I still remember them. It’s not something that I focused on as a kid.
Right now my hands are all rough. The skin is peeling off. I like that. Peeling off he skin from my hands. Pooja is crazy about it. But all of my fingers have their skin peeled off. I made a kid cry caressing her cheek. I don’t remember that happening to my father’s hands. His hands were smooth. 
He had a hand on my head all my life. He paid for my college. Engineering and MBA. I was prepared to take a loan for my MBA. I had gotten information from the bank. I had prepared the papers. At the last moment he proposed to pay it for me. It was a huge sum. Almost two lacs a trimester. He said he’ll pay a couple of semester. He paid for everything. I didn’t have to worry about paying the loan off. My friends had that burden for a long time. I didn’t. It made my life a little easier. I will always be grateful to him for that. 
He took care of me all his life. And although he wasn’t there for us physically, I want to think he was always thought about me. About my brother and sister. He loved us. If he were alive I would still be a burden on him. Like I am for my mother. His hand still on my head.
