Date: 28-05-2019
Topic: Write about slumming

For the past 4 years or so, I’ve been in and out of jobs. In fact I did just one job in between. I’ve been homeless a couple of times in my life. 
The first time I had left my house in Ahmedabad and moved in with a friend in Pune. I used their spare room and lived with them for a while. I would go to Bangalore to live with another friend. In his spare room. 
The room in bangalore was a music room cum store room with a single bed barely enough to cover my body. I would often get the mattresses on the floor to sleep. 
I didn’t have enough money to pay for things. I had to think about where I was spending money. Where I can save it. Going out with friend, I got irritated when I had to pay. Which made me feel embarrassed about being cheap. 
After going back and forth for a while, I found a job in Bangalore. I fully expected to work there for a long time. My boss gave me no reason to think otherwise. I was making good money. I was living in a comfortable house. And I had a girlfriend. Things were going well for me. I didn’t need much else in life.
Then I lost my job. I didn’t have anywhere to go. I was left to my own devices. I was looking for a job, but I didn’t know how to find one. I felt a kind of shame asking friends for help. Emailing an old acquaintance to see if there was a job in their company. Maybe I didn’t want to show my desperation. I didn’t want them to think I was a loser. Their opinions don’t matter. What others think about me doesn’t have any effect on me. It shouldn’t. I should only care about what I think about myself. And what people I love think. Pooja, mom, mannu, vartika.
These people think so highly of me. Why is there a doubt in me? I doubt there is anything to lose. THen what is stopping me. Maybe a purpose. I thought I was going to give writing a chance. Yes. Right. But what am I going to write about? What do I care about that I would like to do everyday? THings get stuck here. Maybe I want to write about my life. THe things I’ve discovered int the past few months of writing. Maybe that’s my why for now.
Anyway, getting back to the story. I lost my job. I was applying to openings. But I was scared to ask for help. I had some money saved. SO I could afford to live in the same house with a similar lifestyle. But that had to finish sometime. I had to leave my house after a year. I dint have a lot of money left, and didn’t know where to go. I moved in with Pooja for a month. Went to Pune again for another month. Next month it’ll be 3 years since I had a job. SInce I made any money. Since I had any reason to get up in the morning. 
TOday, I have nowhere to go. I live my girlfriend for a few weeks. Then go back to dehradun. I’ve been living like this for almost a year now. I don’t have money to pay rent. I feel completely useless. Am I?
