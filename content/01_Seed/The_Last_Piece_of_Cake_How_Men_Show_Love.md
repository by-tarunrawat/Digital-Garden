Topic: These are the things women don’t know about love.

Women are pretty good at romance. I mean, they know what they want. What kind of love they imagine should be in their lives. They want surprises, and flowers, and unforgettable moments in their daily lives. The onus of making that happen falls on men. Women as well, in a same sex relationship. Both women expecting an unforgettable moment from another. The whole fucking relationship. One big wait for surprises. Or maybe, they have the best relationships. THey both know what the other wants and expects out of the relationship. Maybe they’re as normal as a hetrosexual relationship. Both having no idea, of what the other wants.
I like conversations to be direct. I cringe at the empty sentences meant to decorate the conversation. Trying to skirt around the hidden agenda. In relationship it becomes even more important that my girlfriend be direct with me. And I try to be honest as well.
Women don’t know how men show love. I can talk only about my own experiences. But I show affection physically. I hug, and kiss. I’ll also ask you to eat whatever I’m eating every time. I’ll wonder at random — sometimes offensive — ideas with you, because you make me feel safe to say whatever I want. I show love by saving that last piece of cake for you. I show my love by following you around the house. Just to touch you one time. Just to kiss you.
Women have expectations from their relationships. Which is a good thing. But Except for love and respect I don’t expect much. I have such a hard time thinking about groups. Because no matter what I say keeping a group in mind, I have this thought that it can’t apply to everyone. It might be true for a majority of that group. It might be minority. But I feel that individuals can’t be put in boxes of classification.
