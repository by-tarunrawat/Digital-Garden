Date: 03-04-2019
Topic: Write about your mothers cooking.

For years I took it for granted. She would cook us different things everyday. Different things for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. I realized after moving out, what an amazing cook she is. I don’t get anything close to the food she makes in restaurants. 
It’s strange how everyone thinks their mother is the best cook they’ve seen. Everyone loves their mothers cooking. I’ve eaten food made by some of these mothers and it’s not the best. Then why do people think that their mothers cook the best food? 
I think it’s because the food we’ve eaten at home shapes our ideas of how food should be. If you mother cooked and fed you daal a certain way for years, you will think that daal is supposed to be like this. Then even the most well made dish might not plate up to your standards of how it should be.
I remember trying to get away from eating veggies. All the time. I would return from school. Start crying at the gate. Fearing being force fed veggies. As a kid I wasn’t very fond of her cooking. I liked eating outside more. I would go eat chowmein, momos, rolls and burgers. I loved going out to restaurants to eat. 
Now, I prefer eating at home. I still love eating those momos, and burgers. But, I’ve come to appreciate the tastiness of home cooked food. My mom loves feeding people as well. Sometimes forcefully. My grandmother used to do this. I remember someone telling me this story about her feeding some relatives for lunch. One of them was full and didn’t want anymore food. So, he kept a hand over his plate. My grandmother seeing this started outting rice over his hand, which he hand to remove right after that. My mom is not as crazy. But even she sometimes forces people to eat. 
I think, sometime even I have this tendency. I do it only to people I love and care about. Mom does that to everyone. Either she loves everyone, or she just has a big heart. Both are equally likely.
I have a list of my favorite things that mom makes. Her chicken curry — even her mutton curry is amazing. Spicy, full of flavor and oil. I love her rajma. Her rajma is the benchmark against which I test rajmas everywhere. Kadhi, chole, kheer the list can go on and on. But my favorite thing she makes is gajar ka halwa. It is the best version of the dish I’ve eaten anywhere. I have had gajar ka halwa in the most popular sweet shops. I’ve had it at weddings with expensive caterers. I’ve had it at other people’s houses, and at fancy restaurants. I haven’t eaten anything close to hers.
