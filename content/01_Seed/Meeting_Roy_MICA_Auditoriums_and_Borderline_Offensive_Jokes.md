Date: 27-06-2019
Topic: Write about meeting someone for the first time

The first time I met Roy, I was working at Infosys. I desperately wanted to get out of the job. I was looking at avenues. I had an opportunity to move to America for my project. I had turned it down. I thought it would have made me an IT professional for the rest of my life. I wasn’t happy there. I had taken MBA exams. Gotten a couple of interviews. I had also taken the GMAT. If I didn’t get into an Indian college, I would have taken any abroad. 
During this time, I had come to Ahmedabad for MICA admission process. This was my last hope to  do MBA in India. I  took up a hotel near the meeting point with MICA people next day. I was sharing a room with someone I met on my way. Some other people coming for the interviews had come to stay at the hotel as well. I could see them roaming around. Talking with each other. I did not engage with them.
In the evening I went out to see the sights with my new friends. They were nice people. But dull. They talked about their work, the interview next day, MBA preparations. All the things that I had been doing for over a year now. 
On our way back we met a guy. He was entering the hotel with us. He had a polythene bag with stuff in it. From what I could see, it had a couple of packets of chips, biscuits, coke and maybe cake. He was wearing plain clothes and had a weird egg shaped head. We met a few other people at the reception. They had their interviews that day. I remember Prateek Vaid, who had gotten through. We asked them questions on the process, the interviewers, and the questions being asked. 
I noticed that the egg head guy didn’t have a lot of questions but he was chummy with people he had met just a few minutes ago. He was cracking jokes at every other opportunity. Some of them good. Some of them bad. And some of them plain stupid. But he would make the boldest jokes without thinking about them twice. The sheer relentlessness of his jokes and the weirdness of them made me interested in him. 
We talked a little after that. His name was Abhimanyu Roy. We met in the bus next day. I don’t remember what we chatted about on the bus. I was nervous. At MICA all of us were seated in an auditorium, and given a speech. Roy didn’t take much time to make other friends. But we hung out. He had brought his laptop and we watched a couple of movies together. I liked his sense of humor. It was bordering on offensive. But it was funny.
When I came back to Pune, I got a call from Roy. SO we hung out a few more times. We’ve continued our friendship since then. He has become one of my best friend over the years.
