Date: 28-03-2019
Topic: The last time I saw…

The last time I saw my dad was when I had come home for a break. No. I think I hadn’t seen him since I joined MICA. The last time I saw him was before MICA. When I was preparing to go to Ahmedabad. Wow. The last time I saw him was so long ago. I didn’t talk much on the phone with him. Neither him nor I are keen talkers. But I think I talked to him a couple of times after joining MICA as well. 
What kind of a relationship is this. He was my father. And he was in my life like an old acquaintance. WE’ll meet if we’re in the same area. Neither one of us ever tried to make time for each other. I’ve always known this. But never this way. 
I don’t even remember what we talked about. He probably asked me if I was okay. And whether my studies were going well or not. I always had the same standard answer. Yeah. Everything good. No nuance, no details. Just saying ‘everything is okay’ was enough. Even now when I talk to mom, this is the extent of our conversation.
I don’t want my relationship with my mom to end like this. Should I talk to her more about my life. What should I tell her. Mom, I have a girlfriend that I love. I’m trying to find a job, but I’m more keen on becoming a writer. The problem is, I don’t know how to do that. Or if that will make me any money. I just don’t know what to do with my life ma. What do I do.
I can’t tell her this. She will get worried. She will probably console me at first, but I know she will think about this all the time. This will bother her. But it’s better to talk about things in relationship. No? I don’t want that one day my mom dies and I haven’t seen her in months and haven’t talked about anything. I blame my parents for what our relationship is. I’m to blame as well.
