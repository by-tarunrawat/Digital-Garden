Repeat the process one more time, putting yourself at center stage. What qualities in your creative self do you most admire? List your own accomplishments.Be direct and honest — no humility here please. If necessary, describe your creative life through someone else’s eyes. Perhaps this person can be more objective.

What qualities do I admire in myself” Let’s see. I think I can pick up anything and become decent at it. By anything I mean ANYTHING. I can play guitar. I’ve tried my hand at writing. I can act. I have directed a couple of plays. I can even paint. I never thought I could, but I can. And pretty well I might add.
But I’ve never tried to master anything. I’ve never given my all to one discipline. Music or guitar, or writing even painting. I would do it for a while. Get to a certain level and leave it. But I’m digressing. I’m supposed to talk about qualities I admire.
So I learn and adopt things really quickly. Yeah, learning is the key here. I learn things pretty fast. Before I started painting. I’d never done it. I didn’t even think about doing it before. But then one day I just bought a box of oil pastels. On a whim. And started drawing. My first wasn’t that great. It was okay, I was just checking how to work the colors on paper. But the next one was good, and so was the one after that, and the one after that. I learned really fast. I could have continued. I don’t know why I didn’t. I was constantly thinking about doing something to make money. Apply for jobs. Do something… worthwhile. This doesn’t seem like it. It gave me a lot of joy. Maybe that should have been enough for me. Thinking about money screws me up. And thats why I think I need money.
I’ve come up with pretty cool ideas when writing as well. I’ve written and performed two plays. I’m proud of both of them. But especially the one I did in MICA. We wrote it in a couple of days. The main problem with the play was that it was a couple of stories that somehow needed to connect. And I solved that problem. It felt great. Solving that problem. I’ve written poems that I’ve been quite proud of. I also wrote a song when I was learning guitar. I didn’t know much about music. But I did write a song with whatever limited knowledge I had. I think over-education could also have been a problem. The more I see and read, the more I realize how little I know. 
How little I know doesn’t matter. If I waited to know everything before I did something, then I would do anything. Ever. SO I have to use whatever little I already know. ANd do what I want to do. If I want to make music — which I want to do — I should give it a try. WOrk on it for a while. Don’t give up in a sitting. DO I think I will come up with a song in half an hour? Of course not. I have to work on it. Give it a few days. Work on it everyday. Not for half an hour — more. Same with writing. And painting.
Okay, so what else do I admire about myself. My curiosity? I ask a lot of questions. I read and try to find whatever I can about things. And I think that I incorporate in my work. When I started painting I had no idea how to do it. But I watched videos. I read about it. And eventually got pretty decent. So I can figure stuff out on my own. I need to get to a problem to solve it. For that I need to start till I come to a problem.
