Date: 13-03-2019
Topic: Write about a longing.

I wished for so many things in life. Thinking about this opic so man images and thoughts came to my mind. My first bike. Movies. Writing. Cricket. 
It was surprising to me that cricket was the last thing that came to me. It is something I’ve loved more than anything else I do. I would make any sacrifice to play cricket. I’m not even talking about getting paid for it. I don’t really expect to get paid for it. I just want to play it.
What stops me. Resources really. I don’t know how I would do it. Let’s say I had the resources. What would I do. I would have to start getting fit. Start running. Make a list of places I can play regularly in — clubs, sports complexes. Buy a kit. And the last thing most important: Go there regularly. Play seriously. Have fun.
1st thing. Make a list of all the clubs. Research how you could get into them. How can you play? Find out. Call if you have to call. E mail, if necessary. 
2nd. Get up early every morning. Run, skip, exercise for an hour, at least. 
3rd. Check the prices of a good kit. Buy it.
I’m just reminded of a crippling disease I have. This disease will make it really hard for me to play. This disease is unemployment. I can’t stay here that long to be a regular. How can I be? I don’t have money to stay here. I will have to find a job. But if I’m at a job, then it becomes really tough for me to play regularly. I need to find time for cricket. And I’m not going to stop writing. I need to find a job that has flexible hours.
Let’s see, I have to think about finding a job. It is also important because my relationship depends on it. I love Pooja and she loves me. But it is now clear to me that if I do not figure this out I will lose her. 
I want to learn something. Something that interests me. It should pay a decent amount. Something that I can work with in this expensive city. Do I want high pressure. I don’t mind it. I would rather do a low pressure job? That would make some time to write and play. Yeah. Maybe. What else? I want to be useful. Feel like contributing. Something I’m good at. Suits my skills.
So something interesting. Or something that interests me. Sports, books, movies, shows, entertainment. Are there more? I’ll write down everything that comes to me. Food, art, travel, photography, music, comedy. I can’t think of anything else actually. Gaming, adventure, TV, theater. That is pretty much it, I think.

What are my skills. Writing, marketing, playing guitar, playing cricket, email marketing, social media marketing. I can’t think of any more. Is that it?  

Next thing is to figure out where. Do I need to go one by one?

Sports: 
IPL or ISL franchise. Marketing. Writing. My own content. Admin. I don’t know much about administration. Maybe I’ll learn. Coaching staff. I’m not going to be a ball boy. 
Sports magazines. Sports websites. Writer. Content manager. Social media. Digital marketing.
