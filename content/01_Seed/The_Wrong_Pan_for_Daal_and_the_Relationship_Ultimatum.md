Topic: There were signs and signals

She would yell at him for small things. Things that did not matter. The pan I was heating daal in was wrong. I was touching her wrong. I was making all the wrong jokes. At first I felt she was under a lot of pressure. Work pressure, pressure from parents. She wanted me to get a job. Anywhere. Otherwise our relationship is all but done. I felt maybe that was the reason for her lashing out. 
Maybe it is. I’m not sure. There are things she liked till a while back. Now those things irritate her. I’ve been thinking about it for a few days now. She does love me — I think. But I have a feeling she doesn’t like me anymore. She shuts down the conversations I want to have with her. She looks at me in anger when I’m being silly. I feel like she is on a path to breaking up our relationship. 
This is all in my head by the way. I asked her and she said no. She doesn’t want to. But it feels like her subconscious is telling her something else. She probably doesn’t know it yet. I didn’t think we would ever break up. Everything has been great. I love her more than anything in the world. But it feels like she is slipping away. 
She has given me an ultimatum anyway. Figure your job scene or we’ll have to talk whether we should continue or not. It stung me when she said that. But I can’t blame her. She can’t stay with someone like me. Someone who is just floating around in life. With no purpose and no prospects. She could get anyone she wants. Someone who is doing something worthwhile and has similar ambitions. 
My ambitions? I can’t even say them out loud. I want to be a writer. Really? What have you written? Where is your blog? I want to make a movie? Movie eh? What’s stopping you? I don’t know. Money? Do you really need money? You have a video camera. It’s more than some of your heroes had growing up. It’s like a joke. I’m embarrassed by it.
I have to be prepared mentally for this eventuality. I don’t want it to happen. I will do everything in my power to stop it. I’m trying to find a job. I’m really trying. But I don’t know when it will happen. I don’t know if it will happen. I can’t expect her to wait forever. The signs are all there. I just need to accept it.
