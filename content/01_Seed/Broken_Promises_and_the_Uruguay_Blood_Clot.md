Date: 19-03-2019
Topic: Write about promises that were broken.

It’s eerie, I thought about broken promises a few hours ago. And the prompt I’m writing about is broken promises.
I don’t really care about promises. People break them. It’s no big deal. I understand. You have to look out for yourself. You give a verbal confirmation of something you will or will not do. The promise is supposed to act as an agreement between two people. 
I have no idea what is going to happen in a couple of days. I can tell myself I’ll do something at 1 PM two days from now, fully intending to stick to my plan. But something might happen that prevents me from doing that. 
Life is like that. You think you will live a little longer. Make plans with people. Promise them things. You know you’re good on your word. But you don’t know about the truck that hits you crossing the road next morning. Or a blood clot, that gives you heart attack. And you die. Still young. Your wife and son by your side. 
You think about how much you’ll miss them. The plans you made. How much you still had to do. You look at your wife. She has tears in her eyes. She is desperately trying to hold them back. You’ve failed her. She had three children all by herself. She raised them all by herself. She waited for you when you were gone for months. She waited years for you to be done. To have all your time. And now, you’re leaving her again. To go back to the world alone. This time for ever.
It’s unfair to expect people to keep promises. We can only actions. Barely. Mostly it’s our programming telling us to do things. We have no control over anything outside of us. Time, place, circumstances. We can’t control anything. And in a world like this, we are expected to sacrifice our self interest for a promise? I understand minor sacrifices. And I understand if people are willing to make big sacrifices to keep their word. But everyone would agree, that it gets difficult sometimes to keep promises. Yet we insist on them. It is because we believe we will be able to keep our promises. We are all decent people. And we want to do things for other people. Isn’t that the emotion behind a promise. To do something for someone else to the best of your ability.
So if someone breaks their promise. Forgive them. They were never in control.
