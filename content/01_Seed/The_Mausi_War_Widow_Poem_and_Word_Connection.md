My greatest accomplishment has been to get through a good MBA college. That has been my greatest accomplishment. Getting admitted to a school. I can say my greatest accomplishment was when I wrote a poen that made my Mausi cry. Her husband was in the army and killed in war. I wrote a poen and at the end of it, she had tears in eyes. I felt sad seeing her cry, but I felt a strage joy in connecting with someone with words. I think what I felt was my idea touching someone deeply. It made me proud. I still tell that story sometimes. 
Winning THe highest goalscorers in girsls football tournament was probably another moment I felt proud of. My accomplishments sound so small. 
There are people who play at the highest level. WHo have published works and I'm talking about nothing.

GEtting into infosys. 300 people got thorugh. How was that an accomplishment.
I think the laughter I saw in people when I wrote something funny. The surprise in their eyes was somethign that made me really happy.
