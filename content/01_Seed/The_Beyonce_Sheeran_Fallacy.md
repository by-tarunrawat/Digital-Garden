There is this image doing the rounds on social media. In it, Beyonce and Ed Sheeran are performing on stage. Ed Sheeran is wearing a casual T-shirt and a pair of jeans playing guitar. Beyonce, on the other hand, is wearing this incredibly strange, pink colored dress. It’s collars are weirdly shaped. And the dress is pretty long as well. It’s pretty elaborate.
Now the image doing the round has a copy written on top. It says something about the expectations we have from men and women on how they need to get dressed to work. It is being used by “feminists” to make the argument that there is a huge power difference between men and women. And this is one example showing that.
Now, first of all, I would like to say that I don not disagree withe the premise. I agree that it seems like there is a big power difference between men and women. Especially in workplace. But this image is not an example of that. 
Beyonce is a big Pop star. It is her brand to be lavishly dressed. Most of her clothing is elaborate if you see her live shows. Also, if you think that the power difference between Ed Sheeran and Beyonce is tipped towards Ed Sheeran. You need to get your head out of your pants. Beyonce is the biggest Pop star in the world. When the news of her husband’s, who is one of the biggest rap artist and music producer, infidelity came out in the news, it brought him down to his knees. That’s how big she is. And to suggest that she is doing this because of some power dynamic is insulting her. 
As I said before, she is the biggest pop star in the world. A powerful, independent woman. To suggest that she does what she does because of some patriarchy is ridiculous. It’s what she wants to do. It’s her brand.
When Avril Lavigne was on top, she dressed even more casual than Ed Sheeran. That was her brand. Pink dresses the way she wants. There is no patriarchy telling her what to do. Lady Gaga, Miley Cyrus, all of them do what they want to do. Beatles didn’t dress casually, they dressed according to their brand. There was no matriarchy dictating them. These are famous powerful people. And maybe the patriarchy dictates smaller upcoming artists who are trying to break into the industry, to dress a certain way. You can’t apply the same logic everywhere.
And talking about work. I agree there is a dominant patriarchal work culture. But, It manifests itself in how men and women are hired. How they are promoted and given a raise. Not how they dress. In fact, women can dress however they want. Traditional suits, sarees, western dresses, casuals. Men can’t even wear traditional clothes to work. There is an expectation from men to dress a certain way. Not the other way around.
Now I see the difference in how Beyonce and Ed Sheeran were dressed. And the contrast somehow heightens the dramatic effect. But that is both playing to their own brand. You can maybe blame Ed Sheeran for being a little sloppy, but it’s not sexism or patriarchy.
