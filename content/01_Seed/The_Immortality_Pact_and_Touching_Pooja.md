Topic: Write what you’ll miss when you die.


I haven’t thought about dying. I guess that comes with being young. Your body still in good condition. You’re still alert and fit. You do things for the future. You plan for it. As if you know when you will die. Nobody knows.
My father died unexpectedly. He was planning for the future. He saw the rest of his life living at home. Spending the rest of his life with my mother. Spend time with her. Travel. Something he wasn’t able to do all his life. All his plans destroyed with a single incident. All the things he wanted to do, all the things he loved, gone with him.
I don’t know when I’m going to die. I want to live forever. I want to see everything. I want to solve the mysteries of the universe. I want to travel. I want to spend time with my friends, family. With Pooja. I don’t think I’ll ever tire of her. 
I’ve asked people if they would want to live forever. Most of them said no. In fact I can’t remember anyone who wanted to be immortal. I do. But that might not happen. I’m saying might because I don’t know the future. There may be a way for me to live a long time. Scientists may cure death.
I don’t know why people don’t want to live forever. I know they don’t want to die. But they still say they don’t want to live forever. The reasons I’ve heard are silly. They don’t want to live with a fragile body. They say they don’t want to live without their loved ones. I don’t want to either. But I don’t want to die either. It is conflicting. But what in life isn’t.
But I am going to die. I can’t even imagine it. If I do have to die. I don’t want to live without Pooja. I will miss her the most. I want immortality, but I don’t want it only for myself. I would want it for everyone I love. My mom, my brother, sister, Pooja, Roy, Tushar, Amit. So many people. 
I will miss my friends. They make my life worth living. I remember when I lived in Ahmedabad. I was all alone. I didn’t have friends. I just wanted to leave that place. And as soon as my friends arrived. My life was so much better. 
I will miss sex. I want to have it with a lot of people. I don’t do it because Ive made a decision to have it with one person. I will still miss it with that one person. I miss it now, if I don’t have sex for a few days. It is definitely one of the things I will miss. 
There are so many wonders in the world. I watch wildlife documentaries. Space shows. There are so many things we don’t know about. I don’t know about. I’m curious. I want to know. As a species we will discover the answers. We will know if life exists outside our planet. We will travel outside our solar system. I hope it happens during my lifetime. If it doesn’t, I will miss it. I will miss knowing.
I will miss kissing. I love kissing Pooja. On her cheeks. On her neck. Her lips. Every part of her body. I will miss hugging her. How she fits into my body. Our curves and crevices are made perfectly for each other. I feel warm in my body. The touch of her skin is comforting. I keep touching her when I’m with her. WHen she has slept, and I’m doing something else on my phone or my laptop, or I’m reading something. I keep one hand on her. Or a leg. Or my thigh. Even a feet apart, I miss touching her. I can’t imagine not doing it.
I won’t miss anything else. Food, entertainment, travel. I don’t care if that shit never happens to me again. I’ll be dead. I do love all those things. But there are more important things for me.
