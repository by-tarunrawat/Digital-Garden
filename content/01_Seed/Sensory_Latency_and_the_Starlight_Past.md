Date: 02-01-2019
Topic: Write about the night sky

Fewer and fewer stars in the sky each day. The light obscures the view. There was a time when I could see Mussoorie from the terrace of my house. It blended with the stars. It was like it was a part of the sk. Now you can’t see a glimmer. You can still see a few stars in Dehradun; in Bangalore, nothing. 
When I was a kid, we used to go to the terrace to play. We would look at the stars in hope of spotting a shooting star. We believed that wishing on the star will make the wish come true. So we would lie down and try to look for a shooting star. I wanted a bat. Once, I wished that a girl I fancied would like me. It was just a young mind’s dreams. The point is there were so many stars in the sky, that we could just sit in the open for a while and we would see a shooting star. We like It was amazing. I don’t appreciate that enough.
There still are the same number of stars in the sky, if not more. We’ve hidden them behind the wall of light we’ve created. Maybe we don’t need to see them. It makes us realize how small we are. We are just floating around in space. We are nothing in front of them. Maybe looking at them freaks us out so much that we’ve had enough. But we still want to know more about them. It’s like a scary story. We don’t want to be scared, but we do want to know what our hero does.
There is this app from google called sky map. It’s an amazing software. As the name says, it gives us a map of the sky. You can point it anywhere and you can see the location of stars, galaxies. Planets in our solar system. You can see exactly where the sun is when it’s night, you can see the moon during the day. I like doing that to the night sky. Point my phone up and try identifying the lights in the sky. I look at the planets. It’s fun to see. I hope one day we can go to these planets and see them from close.
I have been fascinated by the night sky for a long time. Once, as a kid I found out something amazing about the stars. There is a definite speed of light. And what we see from stars is their light. So, when we see a star, we are seeing the actual star but a long time ago. The stars are so far away, that traveling at a huge speed, light that reaches us is still several years old. It has taken that much time to reach us. So, what we’re seeing right now is not even there. We’re seeing the past. Our own star, Sun, takes 8 minutes to send its light to us. This means that the sun that we see is the sun of 8 minutes ago. Even when we talk to other people, it takes a fraction of a second for the light from their face to reach us. The sound takes more time. SO what we see and hear has happened in the past. Every moment that we’re interacting with anything in the world through our senses is in the past. Even touch or taste. What our brain experiences by touching or tasting happens a few milliseconds later than the actual event.
So are we living in the past all the time. Maybe. The night sky seems to suggest that.
