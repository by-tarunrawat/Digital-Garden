Date: 22-03-2019
Topic: Write about taking the long way around

I want to be a writer. A professional writer. I want to make money off of it. Money is one thing I on’t want to think about. I want it out of my way when I’m making decisions. What to do, where to go, whether to help someone or not. I’ve read many books on writing and how I can become a writer, over the past year and half. I want to do it as fast as I can. So I have been trying to get all the wisdom I can from the masters. However, Most of these masters say it will not happen quickly. It takes time. It takes practice.
The reason I’m looking for a shortcut is because I really need the money. I don’t know if I can wait around much longer. I should’ve done all the practice and all the failing over the past three years. I just sat on my ass. But now I need to do something quickly. And all these masters are telling me that it takes time. I have to practice everyday. I have to give time to it everyday. WHat am I to do?
I tried flipping domains some while back. Didn’t make any money. Spent a few dollars. I don’t know what I have to do. It seemed easy enough. Pick a good domain name. Pay money for it. Repeat. People make hundreds of thousands of dollars doing it. If I can’t there must be a reason. I need to give it my time and energy to figure out what works. I just wanted a shortcut again. 
I’ve also been flirting with dropshipping for a while. I think I can make some money from it. Then maybe sell the business when it’s doing well. I just want a short cut. Which looking at the past record won’t work. Then what? What do I need to do? What is the long way round. I just open a store and start working on it. FIgure out what works and what doesn’t. And when it starts working. DO whatever the fuck you want. 
THere is no shortcut to anything. You have to work at it. You have to take the long way around. That is what I’m trying to do with my writing right now. ANd that’s what I’m going to do with the business.
