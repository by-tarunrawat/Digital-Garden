Date: 18-03-2019
Topic: On the eve of the funeral

I had just come back from Ahmedabad. I had heard the news on the phone. I was supposed to go a couple of days ago when my grandmother died. But my exams were in a few days and my family decided, it was best if I stayed put. I wasn’t going to see her funeral. My mom had gone to Uruguay. To be with my father. He had a heart attack and was not doing well. She didn’t know about it. The family decided not to tell her. A couple of days later, I recieved a phone call from my brother that my dad died.
I didn’t know how to react to it. I knew he wouldn’t lie to me. Especially about something like this. But I couldn’t believe that my father was gone. It still feels strange somehow. I went back to my room. Told my friends, who stayed with me. Consoling me. I told the college I wouldn’t be able to take the exam and booked my tickets back home.
On the eve of the funeral, I was at my grandmother’s house. It was filled with people. Crying, consoling each other. My aunt grabbed me and cried on my chest. She, and probably others, were expecting tears from me. There was nothing. 
Next day, my mom came home. My dad’s body was coming a few hours later. She was crying continuously. She hugged me and cried some more. I held her for a while. I didn’t know what to say. I don’t know if I could’ve said something that would have mattered. I didn’t cry. 
I felt like a psychopath. I did feel sadness in my body somewhere. But I thought, there should be more. Why am I not crying my eyes out? Why am I so calm? As if I had already made my peace with it. 
I felt like my mom had been cheated. I felt bad for her. She had lived alone all her life. Raising three children on her own. Deciding where we will live, how we will live, where our schools will be, getting admissions done. She was waiting for the time, when my dad would finally retire and live at home. They would travel together. Do things together. Marry their children. And all that was promised to her was taken away. I still feel a little angry when I think about it.
I can’t ask why it happened? I don’t know. I don’t think anybody can answer that question. Things happen. People die. I know that. Everybody knows that. There is no reason. How can there be. What can be the reason behind my father’s death. Behind all the deaths in the world. 
Are we looking for reasons or are we looking for god? What do we want from god? To tell us that there was a greater plan. A plan that only god knows. What if there is no god? There is no plan? Better still, what if there is a god, and he is as clueless as us. Going through life. Not giving two shits about the life that he has created.
