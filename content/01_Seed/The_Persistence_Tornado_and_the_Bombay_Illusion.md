The Hindi film industry can be very unforgiving, demanding, ruthless, unfair, inaccessible, almost dreamlike for the everyone else outside of the industry. In a normal scenario, if you want to work as an engineer, you just need the educational qualifications for it. You want to be a doctor, you study, if you get through the entrance, you study for a few years and become a doctor. This is the same for CAs, Lawyers, corporate management. Most thing you want to do you have a well laid out path that you have to walk upon to reach the destination. In the film industry, there is no such path. 
To make it in the film industry… actually, just to get a job in the film industry, everybody has to go to just one city. There are no job bulletin boards to get a job as an AD on a film, or a website to get a role as an actor in a movie. You go to Bombay and join the immense crowd of people who have the same aspiration as you, going from one production house to the other, one film set to the other, just to get one small part. 
There are hundreds, if not thousands of people who have been doing theater in colleges, in small and big stage productions. Trained actors who have been honing their craft for at a few years. Artists who have been performing on campus events, new year parties, bars, pubs and clubs. Even they struggle to find work. 
Eventually most of these people have to take up other jobs. Assistant content marketer at Falana pvt ltd, Client service manager at Dhimaka creative agency, if they are lucky. If they are unlucky and want to dedicate their lives to “make it”, well, then my sympathies for them. I’m not saying they will never get to realize their dreams. I’m saying that most of them will never realize their dreams. 
I’ve heard many self help gurus and life coaches say, it’s your drive that takes you to your dream life. Many successful people, in their biographies say, how persistent they were towards their goals and that’s why they were successful. You’ve got to be dedicated, work hard, single focus on your goals, challenge yourself, suffer for your cause, learn from your mistakes and on and on and on. We, simple minded people, take it as a roadmap to success. We tell ourselves, if I work hard enough I will make it. I’m sure I will make it because so many of my idols have. It’s all bullshit of course. 
Working hard is no guarantee to success and being persistent often pulls you in at 300 kms an hours towards the huge tornado of poverty, inside you find uprooted dreams and discarded talent. Just swirling around. Ready to be thrown somewhere far away. So far from the 
Poverty is a cruel thing. If you’ve never had to think where your next meal is coming from, it becomes harder to be persistent. How can you focus on the next audition if your stomach is screaming - my god you pathetic piece of existence, put something in me or so help me god…
