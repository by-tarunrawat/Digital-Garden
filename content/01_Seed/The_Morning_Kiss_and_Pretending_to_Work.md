Topic: Every Morning…

Every morning I wake up with a kiss from the woman I love. It’s the best way to wake up. Then I watch her undress and put on something amazing. She always does. I watch her leave. We kiss a few times. Then I just waste away my time. 
I drink tea and smoke. Watch a few youtube videos. That takes up a lot of time. I should think of a new schedule. One that takes up my time.
I wake up. Drink tea, exercise if I want to. Write your prompt first thing in the morning. Then do whatever chores you have to. Watch youtube videos. Then after lunch. Sit down for a couple of hours. Just sit without internet. Do a prompt, or revise something. Look at previous work. But sit and pretend to work if you want.
