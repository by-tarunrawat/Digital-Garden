Date: 19-03-2019
Topic: You’re in the backseat of a taxi

The window is open. I love the feeling of wind on my face. I loved it even more on my hair. But those days are gone now. Open fields sprawled across acres of land pass by in an instant. People standing outside their houses are a blur. I glance at some of the faces and they glance back at me. I would love to stop the car, get out and talk to these people. I want to know their stories. Can I just do it?
The driver is quiet. I haven’t been paying attention to the music in the car. I generally sleep on my commute. I haven’t yet. I ask the driver to stop somewhere for tea. 
There are strange mountains everywhere. There are green trees on some of them, but most of them are barren. Grey, orange rocks. I’ve grown up in a hill state, so these seem small compared to what I’m used to at home. In Dehradun, when I look at a mountain from the base, I can only see a part of it. I can see the entire mountain from a distance, but get close enough, and you see only a few meters. But here I can see the hill in its entirety. 
We get back in the car. THe driver asks me if I want anything else. I tell him no. There is so much beauty outside of the human settlement. We’ve crowded all the cities and towns and wrapped them up with concrete. Here is just nature — with some interference from humans of course. But this doesn’t seem bad. Maybe because I’m used to worse than this. Maybe I’m so used to looking at buildings and malls and roads and pavements that a man ploughing the field with cows seem like a natural thing to do. 
I’m surprised that I haven’t slept yet. I like sleeping. Especially when I’m traveling. I just fall asleep instantly. I can be in a car, bus or plane. I can’t help it. But today, every time I see something beautiful, I tell myself what an idiot I am. I stare at my phone all day, checking notifications and apps. Just so that I don’t miss anything. But travel, where I can see actual beauty, I miss all of it. 
I change the song on my phone. Electric indigo by the paper kites. A lake passes me in an instant. Then a field. What would it be like to sit next to the lake in the morning? Taking a din in the water. Going to the farm, working in the sun all day. Sometimes I wish I could do all that. I feel my life has been defined by things out of my control. There is nothing stopping me from doing all this. But there is something that is stopping me. I can’t just ignore all my education, my experience, and start farming. That’s ridiculous. 
I wonder if it really is ridiculous. I worked for someone who worked for someone who worked for someone. Is that what my education is for? I realize my dream by helping someone realize theirs who is helping someone else do the same. That is the way it should work ideally. But I doubt I realized any dreams. I doubt anyone realizes their dreams working like that. I realize my eyes have already closed. I’m not sure if I’m thinking this in my dreams. I want to open my eyes, but it’s too much effort. I go along with it. The song ends.
