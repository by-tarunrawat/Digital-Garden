Write about your need for a creative life or simply your need to write. Why do you do it? What needs are fulfilled through it? Call your essay “Why I write.” In your essay, be honest and be thorough. Try to achieve a better understanding of your impulse to write. Use this understanding to explain to The Judge, and to all the enemies, why you must write, despite blocks or guilt or a hundred other cool things to do.

I write because I don’t know what else to do with my life anymore. I’m educated. If the work you do in our life is a journey you take — which some might call a career. I’ve taken the safest trail that was in front of me. Engineering and MBA. It was the surest way to get and hold a job in life. Unfortunately my trail hasn’t led me anywhere. In fact it ends at the edge of a mountain where the only way I see is down hundreds of feet. Writing I feel is the simplest way to resume any kind of work. 
I don’t need much to become a writer. I need my brain — which I still have — and a laptop. I write because there are so may things that just rattle around in my head. I have so many things to say to people. I have too many opinions. I have a perspective — which I think is my own. I need to get it out. Otherwise I’m afraid I’ll lose it all. I’ve already lost a lot of it. 
I write because I can’t afford therapy. Recently I realized that when I’m writing, I veer into strange places. I write about stuff that I was too afraid to think about. It may give me some kind of insight into my own mind. It might give me closure with some issues I’m having in my life. It will be helpful even if all it does is makes me aware of them. I’m not yet aware of the nature of demons I’m keeping in me. I want to bring them out through my writing. So that I can deal with them properly. Because right now it feels like I’m ignoring all my problems and pretending they do not exist. 
I’m writing because writing is thinking. I want to be a better thinker. I don’t want to be an intellectual or anything. I just want to know my thoughts. I want to understand how I think. And I want to make my thoughts clear, concise and logical. A lot of times when I’m talking to people, I say things that I can’t follow up with proper arguments. I need to understand why I believe in certain things. Or why I say those things. There should be a logical reasoning behind things I say and think about. And I should be able to formulate my thoughts properly in a conversation. 
I want to write because I have countless stories inside me. I daydream all the time. I have these stories that I see with my eyes wide open. I want to get them out. Maybe they have a meaning behind them. Something that is meant for me. Something that will help me understand what my mind is trying to tell me. Maybe its just for other people. Maybe I just want to entertain other people.Make them laugh, cry, fear, gasp anything. I love people reacting to things I say or do. I really like when I can affect someone’s mood positively. I don’t want to hurt anyone. I just want to affect their minds. Touch their lives in some way. I don’t want to be a great man or anything also. I won’t be so presumptuous to have that ambition. I just want to be remembered. I want people to know me and say “He was an interesting guy. Remember he said something something.” OR “I never thought about it this way until He said ____”. 
I can’t think of any other reason. I don’t think writing is simple. It has taken me years to gather the courage to even write these many words. It is the only thing that makes sense to me right now. Maybe I should pursue it seriously. How do I become serious about it. By doing it everyday. I don’t know what I’ll produce. I don’t know if I’ll produce anything worthwhile let alone something that will make me money. But I’ll have to do it anyway. Because, what else am I going to do otherwise?
