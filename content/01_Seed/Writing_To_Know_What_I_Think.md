I want to write a book because I don’t know what else to do anymore. It’s the easiest way to make a career at this point. BUt I know it’s not easy. Hard as it is to write a book, It’s even more difficult to become a writer of note. Where people pay you to write. Or pay to read what you have to say. Give a crap about anything you have to say.
SO why do I think it’s easy. I think I don’t have to go to anyone to prove anything. Nobody is judging my work right now. DO I feel judged at work? Otherwise that thought wouldn’t have occurred to me. WHy? Why do I want to write. I think I have a lot of decent ideas. I think I reason well. I have a decent imagination. But those are some of the characteristics that I have that might make me a good writer. I have all the characteristics to do manual labor. Yet, I never went for that career. SHould I do Manual labor? Maybe I’ll be a good laborer. Because I’m fat and strong, I can lift more bricks and work longer. Because I have so much to lose already. I’m not too fat. I’m like a little off the point where I can call myself fit. But under the unfit obese point. So and human body can shrink a lot. A LOT. We’ve seen that happen with countless runway models, actresses and Christian Bale.
SO Why do I want to write. Is there something held up inside that wants to come out. What do I want to say even. DO I want to become famous? I would like to be famous. I don’t know how much though. I have problems with motivation. I can’t motivate myself to do anything. That’s why It’s so difficult to find out why? Maybe I want to write to understand Why I exist simply. Maybe I want to understand what I’m supposed to do. How does writing achieve it. I think it will help me to let out my thoughts. Maybe get some ideas. I don’t know why or what I’m writing yet. But Can I ask questions that help me figure it out. I think it will help me explore some of my own thoughts and feeling about my identity and my why.
So how do I explore my thoughts. I need to know what to explore. Next question to ask is what are the questions that I need to ask of myself? It has to be why. I need to understand my thoughts on different things in life. Why I do something. Anything.
Why am I writing? 
I’m writing to know, understand and articulate exactly what I think about things that matter in my life.

How will I do it? Blog? Essays? Video? Stories? This is probably the wrong thing to worry about right now. I think I need to figure out how I’m going to figure out what I want to explore. THere needs to be a system. So I mihgt have written a few things down. I can start with simple things and just start writing my thoughts. I can expand on whatever I want. Or finish when I can’t add anymore. I may need to read about it a little bit. Ill do all of that. THat’s research. It’s important. So I’ll pick something up and start expressing my thoughts about it. This is how I do it.
Write about what you think and feel about various things. Read more about it. Update your thoughts. Repeat.

What am I getting or giving anyone with this. I’m getting to know myself properly. I get to understand what my beliefs are. And hopefully find out my ‘Why’. If in my exploration, I find something entertaining or amusing I’ll share it in some form. Initially I think It’ll just be random thoughts. Maybe I get an idea while writing so I go off in another tangent and that gives me the best way to talk about  a topic. Or maybe just another perspective.
I want to articulate my thoughts to myself, and maybe find things that others find entertaining as well.


I’ll summarize main thoughts here. This is my the most accurate understanding of my belief at this moment. It might change later. And I will keep updating my intentions. 
I’m writing because I want to understand what I think about different things. I want to articulate my thoughts.
I’ll pick up a topic. And write about what I think and feel about it. Then read about it and update my thoughts. REpeat till satisfied.
I want to have a clear understanding of things in my life. If there is something worth sharing and entertaining. I will share it.

Now that I’ve written somewhat of a mission. What are the next steps? Before going and talking about routine. I think I have a few notes from some books I’ve read. I’ll try to understand them and If it makes sense to me I’ll include it in my guidelines.
