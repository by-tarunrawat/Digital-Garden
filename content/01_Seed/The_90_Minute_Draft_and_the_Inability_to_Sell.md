Something I know that no one knows is that there is absolutely nothing that can't be done. I've done so many things in my life. By done I mean tried my hand at. I've learnt that whatever you want to do you can. YOu just need to put some time into it. I didn't know how to draw or paint. I can do decent painting now. I've taught myself so many things. If i think I can do it, more often than not, I am able to do it. BUt this applies to individual things. Like, I could open a door, or learn something new or whatever. HOw can I use it is the question.
THe one thing I can't seem to figure out is how o sell. How to sell myself, my skills. WHat do I do? 
I know that If I wanted to I could write a novel. I could probably write a novel in a month. THe first draft at least. All I have to do is write 3000 words every day. How long is that. LEt's say I write 200 words per 5 minutes. That means I can write about 2000 words per hour. Is that right? 60 divided by 5 is 12. And 12 multiplied by 200 is 2400. SO yeah. I can write 2000 words in an hour. SO I will probably need 1.5 hours everyday. OR 18 5 minute chunks. 90 minutes.
