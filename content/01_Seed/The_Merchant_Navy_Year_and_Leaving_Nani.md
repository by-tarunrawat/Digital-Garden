Date: 06-03-2019
Topic: Write about someone who left.

I was quite young. 3 maybe 4 years old. My father worked in the merchant navy and my mother wanted to join him for a year. I was supposed to join school this year, and couldn’t go. My brother, who was 2 years younger than me, had no such problems. So they left. 
I was supposed to live my grandmother for that year. I was admitted in a school. Lower Kindergarten in a school nearby. I don’t remember much about that year. It’s been so many years since then. 
I remember going to a school. I faintly remember where it was. I remember getting to watch Charlie Chaplin and Laurel and Hardy movies every Saturday. Mu aunt tells me I had a thing with a girl back then. She says I used to give my lunch box to some girl in the school. I remember something like that. But I don’t remember the details. The whys and the hows.
It was fun staying with my nana, nani. Nani would give me all kinds of sweets and bread jam — I loved jam. My nana would take me down for a walk and buy me soft drinks that I was never able to finish. Nani would tell me stories before sleeping. There were kids my age around her house. We would go play cricket and Piththoo. 
After a while I didn’t feel their absence. I don’t remember missing them so much after a couple of months. I was excited when I found out they were coming back though. My mom was so happy seeing me. It would have been really hard for her to leave me like that. My brother, who was still a couple of years younger than me, was speaking English like he was born in US or UK. 
After that I had to leave my Nani’s house and go back to living with my mom. I don’t remember that transition happen. But it couldn’t have been easy. I loved my grandmother. I loved living with her. So this time, I left.
