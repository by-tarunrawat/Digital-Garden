# Crew Resource Management

Crew Resource Management (CRM) was introduced in commercial aviation to counteract steep cockpit authority gradients—where junior first officers hesitated to challenge senior captains making fatal errors.

### Core Failure Modes
- **Mitigated Speech:** Using polite hints or soft questions instead of direct operational commands during high-stress anomalies.
- **Authority Bias:** Deference to seniority overriding active telemetry and sensor warnings.

### Key Takeaway
Clear systems require explicit protocols that flatten hierarchy when critical thresholds are breached.
