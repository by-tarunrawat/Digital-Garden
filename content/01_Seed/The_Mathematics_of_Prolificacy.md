> **Actionable Next Steps:**

Write about a creative person you admire but don’t know personally, such as a famous painter or musician or writer, living or dead. As in the previous prompt, explain why you feel this person is or was so creative. If necessary, do a little research to find out more about this person’s creative gifts and habits. When you finish this piece, compare the people you wrote about in these two prompts. What qualities do they share? What qualities do you most admire in them.

A lot of names and images are coming to me when I think of creative people I admire. I love stand up comedians. Comedians like Norm Mcdonald, Dave Chapelle, George Carlin. They are hilarious, sharp and intelligent. They are so prolific as well. Then I also think about musicians. Ryan Adams, Bryan Adams, Paolo Nutini. There are just so many. Even AR Rehman, Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan. There are actors like Leonardo Dicaprio, Gary Oldman. So many people I admire. So many writers. Like Aaron Sorkin, What a brilliant dialog writer. He writes amazing scenes. Fast moving, intelligent. His characters too. Are so intelligent. But there is this image that is there in the background. The creative person I admire most — Stephen King. 
I haven’t read a lot of books by Stephen King. I think I have read two maybe three books written by him. I’ll read more; if not all. But I’ve seen movies made on his books. The Shining, The green mile, The fog — and my favorite — Shawshank Redemption. 
It’s not just about the kind of stories he writes, or how gripping they are or how original they seem. It’s about how prolific he is. It is astounding that he churns out a new novel every few months. Even at this age. Even after writing so many. 
I find two things amazing about him being so prolific at this stage of his career. First, He has written so many books and stories, it almost seems supernatural that he still has ideas and characters and scenarios for new books. Amazing. Second, even after publishing a huge amount of books and making unthinkable money for a writer as well as being widely popular, how does he find motivation to write more. He can easily sit on his laurels. I mean, he has already written enough books worth the career of at least 3 writers. How does he get up in the morning and go to his desk and churn out 6 pages every day — because that is exactly what he does. 

One of his books that I read was about writing. It was called ‘On Writing’. Here he describes how he works. He goes to his study, and does whatever he has to do to churn out 6 pages for that day. Without fail. 6 pages everyday. For the past I don’t know how many years. At his rate he can publish a thick novel every 100 days. When you put it like that, his prolificness doesn’t seem so strange. Maybe that is where his creativity lies. Actually there no maybe there. That’s waht he says. In his books, in interviews. He says that he treats it like a job. And his job is to write 6 pages of material everyday. 

I need to be like him. It’s not a question of desire. It’s about necessity. I need to be this way. Write at least a certain amount everyday for any creativity to come out. For stories to come out. For jokes to flow.
So, one of the questions in the prompt was the quality I admire in both people. It is their prolificness. The huge amount of material they come up with on a regular basis. And that’s what I want to be. I want to be someone who is just relentless. Relentless with my work. If I set my mind to something I do it. At least that was the case a few years ago. It needs to be that way again.
