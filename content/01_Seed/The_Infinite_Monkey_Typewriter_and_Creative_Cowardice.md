The most cowardly thing I've ever done is to lie to myself. We all lie to ourselves as well as each other. I think we are all cowards in that sense. But I did for a long time, and still do. 
I didn't tell my friends about what was going on with me. I didn't want to open myself up to them. WHat the fuck was rong with me. I still do it to a certain extent. BUt I think that is just subconscious. Counsciously I try to be open to them. I think I'm a coward because I don't have guts to do something for my situtation. FOr a long time I was afraid to write. What if I can't do it. CAn't do it? WHat does that mean. YOu can't beat your keyboard with your fingers. That's most of it. THere is this philosophical explanation of infinity. If you give a monkey, a typewriter, and he starts banging his hands on the keys. GIven Infinite time, that monkey will write shakespeare plays word for word. IT's true mathematically speaking. SO humans can do it too. Right? I mean we are intelligent. ANd we know what we want to type. Why treat ourselves special. Just bang the keys on your keyboard and type a masterpiece. You don't have infinite time. But who knows. You might get good at it someday!
