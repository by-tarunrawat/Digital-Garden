What breaks my heart is the fact that I see so many suffering everyday. I saw this old woman carry a bag of garbage through a lane al the way back to - wherever she was going. and in the middle of it, she dropped it. SHe was a frail woman and it was difficult for her to carry that load. SO she had to drag that bag on the road all the way back to- whereever she was going. ANd it just broke my heart. I went past her thinking, should I help her? WHat will I say? HOw far will I have to carry this. And I kept walking thinking all this. WHen I was quite far, I decided to help her. I turned around tooka couple of steps went towards her, Stopped came back, turned again, then turned back and went to where I was going. I don't now why I hesitated. WHat could be the reason. I wanted to help her. I could see that she was having trouble. Was it because I was afraid to do the work? Maybe. But I think I was thinking about telling my friends about it anyway. so THat was a big reward for my self esteem. I think I wouldn't mind the work in exchange of some praise. THen Why did I not help her? I can't think of a reason for hesitation. But there must be. Otherwise I would have done it. Was it because it was garbage? WAs it because I didn't feel as passionately about it as I think I did. Maybe. I hope I do help the next time I see someone in need.
