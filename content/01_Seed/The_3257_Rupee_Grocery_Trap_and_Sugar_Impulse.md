Date: 01-04-2019
Topic: You are in a grocery store

I go to the biscuits section. I pick up A bunch of biscuits. THis makes me realize I should’ve picked up a basket. I Gather all the packets in my arm and go towards the entrance. THe entrance has all the vegetables and fruits sectioned away nicely. Some of the vegetables look old. They’ll probably be thrown away. How may such things make it to the garbage I wonder. Perfectly fine to be eaten, yet not deemed fine enough for us. THey can be given away to the poor. I hope they are.
I take the basket and go towards the biscuits section again. I might have missed a couple of flavors. I scan the entire biscuit shelf. Pick up the brands I like, the ones I want to try and the ones that are always good to have. My shopping is over. Rest of the stuff is just simple food things. Rice, pulses, spices. I finish that up quickly. I picked up a few other items on way between shelves. 
I hate the long like at the check out counter. That is why I think people like shopping online. You don’t have to stand in line, if you shop online. I see Hershey’s choclate syrup on a shelf. I tell the person behind me to watch my place and go to pick it up. 
There are two more people in front of me. They have a lot of stuff to check out. I pick up a snickers from the counter next to the check out. I see bounty. I love bounty. I pick that up too. I should pick up 5 star too. They are not that expensive. I should take a bunch of them I pick 5. 
The checkout girl is nice. SHe tells me that if I buy another cinthol, I get another one free. I run back and bring two more. She tells me about similar offers for a couple of other items as well. And although buy two get one free doesn’t sound that appealing for Vim bar, I wasn’t going to pass it up on chocolates. 
THREE THOUSAND TWO HUNDRED FIFTY SEVEN RUPEES. What did I buy. I had come to buy rice, flour and a few other vegetables. IT seems like I’m buying groceries for a month. 15 packets of biscuits. Only two fifty gram arhar daal. 4 snickers, 2 bounty, 5 5stars, 2 packets of MnMs. HOw much sugar. DId I really need this. I can remove at least 30 things from the bill and I wouldn’t miss it. Why then do I buy all this shit?
