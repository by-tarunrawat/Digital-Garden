Topic: Write about a time you did something you didn't want to do

I do this a lot. I can’t say no to people, so I end up doing a lot of things that I don’t want to do. People ask me to do something. Ask me for favors, invite me for stuff. I can’t say no. It’s been a problem for a long time. In college, both engineering and MBA, people used to borrow my bike all the time. They new I wouldn’t say no. They would just ask for it and disappear. It was ridiculous. Even if I made excuses, it wasn’t enough. THey would still insist. And insistence is my greatest enemy. 
This is how I took my first job after MBA as well. I couldn’t say no. It was a small company. It was a brand manager role. I had shown no interest towards it. People from the placement committee had come to me to insist on applying to it. I did. And then I withdrew. But they came again. Some of them my friends, telling me how great an opportunity it was. I couldn’t say no. I applied again.
The interview went well. I didn’t know what to think of the guy who would be my boss. He didnt speak much. He would ask questions. I would answer them. Then he would just sit there looking at me. Not for a couple of seconds, but 15-20. At least. I thought maybe he was just listening intently. Trying to process what I was saying. I wasn’t saying anything complicated by the way. Standard things. About me, my background, my education and work experience, etcetera etcetera. Anyway, this didn’t seem like it was for me though. It didn’t seem interesting. And to be honest, I didn’t like the place. 
Down in the parking, all of us interviewees gathered to go back with Anisha, the placement committee person. She asked us, would you take the job if you were offered it. We were 10 people I think. 8 said yes. I said no, so did one other girl. Anisha smiled and finished her tea. A few minutes later, she took both of us aside and told us, we got the job. I didn’t want it, but I could do nothing. The offer was binding. Anisha did see the irony in us getting the job, rather than the other 8 who really wanted it.
I worked at NR Doshi and Partners for close to two years. I hated working there. THe work was stupid. THere were too many restrictions. It wasn’t fun. The toilet was terrible. I fail to see how a decent company could have such foul smelling, dirty toilet for it’s employees. I had no friends in the city. So I was lonely as well. 
You know, people say take every opportunity that comes in front of you, it will take you to one step closer to where you want to go. Be a yes man. I’m not a yes man. I have a decent gut instinct about things I won’t like and don’t want to do. I just go against it. Not by my own volition. No. If I could say no I would. Sometimes I have. But the yes is pulled out of me. Sometimes it is not even needed. I am a can’t say no man. Which is same as being a yes man, apart from the resentment.
