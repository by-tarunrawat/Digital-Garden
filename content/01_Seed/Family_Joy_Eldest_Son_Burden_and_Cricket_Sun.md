What brings me joy is my family's happiness. I want my brother and sister to be happy. I want my mother to be proud of me. I've seen her be envious of other kids. In school they would always get higher marks. I would be just above average. I know that made her sad. I think she was quite happy when I got my first job. SHe was sad to let me go, but she was happy that I was starting my life. Now she sees me in a dead end job. Just scraping by. WHile all my friends have either gotten married and settled down or are doing well in their career. I don't thikn I have given any happiness to my mother in a lot of years. My brother and sister look up to me. I think they still do. They see their brother struggling at his job. Being angry most of the time. THey themselves don't know what to do. THey want to do something meaningful and workthwhile. BUt they can't. Where will they get money. I want to make so much money that they don't have to think about it anymore. THey can just have fun and explore their lives the way they want. How can I do that? It will bring me great joy to see my family together, travelling and never having to toil again.
Another thing that brings me joy is playing cricket. I can do it anytime. IN the sun, IN winters. Whenever, wherever.
