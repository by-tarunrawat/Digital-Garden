My head is so round. It makes my face look like a smiley. With beard. If I shave I look exactly like a smiley face. Pooja loves my roundness. I love that she loves it. I don’t mind it either. Given a choice between growing back my natural hair and a bald head, I would probably choose a bald head. That’s not true actually. I choose hair. I can shave it off after. But it’s always better to have the option.
I used to have good hair on my head. I was never satisfied with them though. I liked the hairstyles that my friends had. I wanted their hair. There is one thing I could tell my younger self, it would be this: it doesn’t matter. I wasted a lot of time grooming. Brushing, applying chemicals, multaani mitti. I tried so many things to make my hair look better. When I started loosing hair, I applied even more chemicals. Took tablets that reduced testosterone. Ridiculous now that I think about it. I harmed my body. My self confidence. And achieved nothing.
My evening and morning hair routine was ridiculous as well. Applying lotion. Letting it dry, combing, then covering it up with a bandanna. Sleeping like that. Keeping hair in one place, so that I wouldn’t have to waste time in the morning to hide my bald spot. I wasted time in the morning anyway. I think I started taking a bath at night because the bald spot was at it’s most shining glory after the hair were wet. And to apply lotion and let my hair dry was a long process I didn’t have time for. 
Look at m head now. I carry it around proudly. People stare at it. It stares them back. Shines a light on them. It is so shiny I love it. I generally keep a beard to go with the bald head. Not to compensate for the lack of hair. TO compliment it. Also, I’m lazy and I don’t want to shave. So, I let the beard grow. Once every two weeks or so, I do the bare minimum by trimming it and keeping it in shape. 
Is it my favorite part of my body? I don’t know. I like my legs. Muscular and strong. Same with my shoulders. I have to exercise very little to get my shoulders to bulge. Yeah. I’d say my head is my favorite. It has my brain. My lips. I love kissing, so lips are really important to me. And eyes. I would rather go deaf than blind.
