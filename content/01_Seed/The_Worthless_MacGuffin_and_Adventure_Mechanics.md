Some of my favorite and most rewatchable movies are action adventure. I love the national treasure series, Indiana jones, any treasure hunt movie, jumanji and stuff. Just remembered, Mummy 1 and 2 were also pretty good. I can watch those movies any time it’s on. 

What do I love about them so much?
I like the problem solving part. It’s not like I’m solving any of it. It’s not like they find a riddle and I’m like, yes that’s the liberty bell. I like seeing the characters solve it in a cool way. 

So the part I like is character getting a clue, a riddle of some kind and them going through some kind of crisis or change to find the solution. In a cool way!


What about the treasure? 
Immense riches is a good treasure to have. Like in the National treasure series. Even in the Mummy they were looking for treasure. But the actual finding of the treasure came halfway through the movie. In jumanji they wanted their freedom from the game. In Indiana jones it was some kind of Magical artifact like the chalice or the ark. In along with the gods, the guy wanted moksha or forgiveness. The angels wanted freedom from their lives of a grim reaper. 
They all want different things. Something valuable. But it need not be valuable to you and me. But it is valuable and desired by the people in the universe. 

Something of value to the characters in the universe. 

What if what our hero has to find is worthless. To him/her or anyone else. Let’s say a plastic toy, a common drinking glass, a plate? How will that work out. 

*****

I also like the traveling. The new locations. Beautiful locations. Dangerous locations. It’s jungles, beaches, deep sea, mountains, desert. Cities as well. I like it. Is it a trope? Do we still need it? We might. I like it, might use it in a nice way. 
What do I hate about the travel part. There are villains everywhere, who seem to know the hero. Our hero has to a work hard to get information, but our villain knows exactly where he is. Won’t be that bad though. It’s fine I guess. THe villain needs to have some advantage.  

I also like the changes in location, when the characters have to travel places to find what they are looking for.
