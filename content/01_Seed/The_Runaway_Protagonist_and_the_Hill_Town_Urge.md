Topic: Write about leaving

The first time I left Dehradun was 10 years ago. God, it was exactly 10 years ago. Last month in November, in 2008. Before that I had never been out of Dehradun for more than a couple of days. I mean I had been to my grandmother’s place, and my mama’s place. But that doesn’t really count. I had never been out on my own.
Even when I was studying in a hostel, I was in Dehradun. My mom didn’t want us to be too far from her. Engineering — Dehradun. When most people were leaving their hometowns, I was stuck in mine. I got stuck here for twenty three years. Not because anyone forced me. I was comfortable here. I didn’t want to leave. 
But I did leave. The first time I left Dehradun for real was 10 years ago. This time, I wasn’t really coming back. I would visit for holidays and vacations. But it wasn’t my home now. I had to find my own place in the world. On my own. Although, I didn’t like the idea as a kid, when I actually did it, I was thrilled. I had so much independence that I didn’t know what to do with it. I imposed certain restrictions on myself. I wouldn’t stay out long. I wouldn’t drink. Not many I think. That was the whole point. To explore. 
I think I discovered quite a lot about myself in that time. I was working for the first time. I found out that I could be self sufficient. I could think about where to eat, when to eat. There was no one else making that decision for me. And that was great. 
It’s strange that you think you’ve learned a lot about yourself. Then time happens to you, and you realize there is a lot more to uncover. You can never be fully self aware. Well, maybe you can. Maybe I just have a long way to go. 
I sometimes feel the urge to leave again. It was great for me to leave Dehradun — to know myself. But I’m stuck in a rut again. I need to find out more. Maybe leaving would give me some answers. Who am I really. I thought I was someone 10 years ago. A simple guy. Who loves movies and TV shows. Who can watch friends all day long. Loves listening to music. Doesn’t drink, doesn’t smoke. I’m a different guy now. I really want to know who.
I’m in a tough spot for a couple of years now. Don’t have a job. Haven’t earned a single rupee. It’s almost as if I don’t know how to make money. And even after all this, it still feels sometimes that I’m not really struggling. I think that’s why I need to leave.
I’ve had this thought of leaving for a few years now. I thought, I would go to some hill town. Rent a house there. Live there for a while. Cut myself off from the world. From people I know. Maybe they are making me comfortable with failure. I don’t know. I don’t really know when was the first time I had this thought. But the first time I said it out loud was at MICA. I said it to Rekha and Tushar. Rekha got upset at it. I thought it was wrong of me to think like this. 
Even now, I think if I said this to Pooja or Mummy, they would freak out. Especially mom. I’m not sure how Pooja will take it. Maybe she will give me solutions. She’ll probably ask me why I want to do it. Then she’ll try to solve that. SHe is a good person. She doesn’t want to see me like this. She’ll probably think, I’m stressed — which is true — and that’s why I want to leave — this is not.
Maybe this is why I want my protagonist to run. The girl in my story. I want her to go away. To some place. It’s because I want to do it. Wow! I never thought about it this way. Maybe I should write this story. Maybe I will have to run away for it. 
How do you do it though? How do you just go away? Leaving all your obligations behind. People you love. People who depend on you. People who care about you. I feel if I did that they will be worried sick. How am I doing? How will they ever live without being in touch with me. Leaving would mean leaving all those relationships behind. How can I do that? Isn’t that drastic. When people say do what you want, it’s not entirely true. You can’t do everything you want without breaking a few hearts. Without hurting people. Do I want to be the person who does that? Or is it another excuse to not go for things I want to do?
