Date: 04-04-2019
Topic: It was a rainy day.

It had been raining since I got up from bed. The sky has gone a dark shade of blue. I can barely see their shape. The house next door has a tin roof. It’s making a loud noise. I can barely hear the TV. I tried going out in the rain. Take some of it on my skin. First step I took outside, I wanted to go right back in. It was cold. That’s how rain in December feels I guess.
I feel lazy. As I feel all the time. My laptop is right there. I pick it up. Open scrivener. Open the writing prompts project. And think about writing another prompt. But first, I need to check my email and facebook. 
3 hours later, after reading 10s of articles, and watching numerous videos, I realize, it stopped raining. I should go out for a smoke. I make up some excuse to go out. And head to the paan waala. 
While smoking I cursed myself for not writing anything. I think about how I’m wasting my life. I make plans to do something. I will go back home, and apply to a few jobs. I will write. And think about a business plan. Or something that can make me money. 
Determined to follow through, I open my laptop. Quickly deleted spam mails. Open a jobs mail from linkedin and apply to a bunch of jobs. One after the other. Same resume for each one. Content manager, content writer. Digital marketing manager, social media, community manager. I apply to anything that I think I can do. These are companies I think will accept me. If there is a job that I want. But I think the company will never call me for an interview, I don’t apply to it.
Now that that’s done,I get back to writing. I pick up a prompt. Write down 500 words. I’m proud of myself for doing this much. I’m proud of myself for doing the bare minimum. Wow.
