Today i was thinking about the friends I've got. I've watched a lot of selfhelp type videos from people like tony robbins and gary vaynerchuck, and they say you need to think about the poeple you are with. And Although I live my friends, and I get a lot of value out of them, like, I mean We share jokes, hang around, but I doubt they are adding anything to me as a person. 
My goal in lifeis to be happy, and to do that I feel Like i need to do things that I want to do. I want to be happy with my job and I want it to feel like something that I would get up for, something that I'm excited about. I think what these self help guru means that your company helps you to be the person you want to be. My friends are all nice people. But they are sick of their jobs. I doubt If i know anyone who is truly happy with what they are oing. I mean, They detest going to work, they think of changing jobs quite often and they do it to make their ircumstances better. I don't know how changing jobs will achieve that because to be honest, the root of the problem is still their. They don't really like what they do, they might have great prospects in life, they'll make a lot of money, they'll be in good positions in their company, but their work load will increase, they'll have longer hours and they'll still bitch about it to me or to other friends. 
Being with them, talking to them I feel like I share their views about corporate life. Corporations, not all but most of them, will suck the life out of you. And I believe that but complaining and not diong anything about it seems like a backward step. Because if you're not doing something about it now, you're leaving the problem as it is to deal with later, and compunding your current problems. Complaining to me is fine, and I get why someone would do that, but my point is that it doesn't really help me and gets me in a negative frame of mind.
I have  alot of problems of my own.I Don't have a job, I don't have money, I'm borrowing money from mhy mom, which is killing me inside, I'm ashamed of the fact that my mom is paying my bills. On top of that my friends compain without looking for solutions gets me in that mindset myself an is not helping in any way.
