Date: 25-03-2019
Topic: Write about getting caught in the act.

I am a careful person in certain situations. Some people might disagree. I have, on many occasion, given the impression that I’m not. In fact some people would say the opposite. To those people I would say, you are right. I leave keys on the bike, I leave my wallet in restaurants and shops, I forget things, and there are many other things I do that would make me careless. However, I did say certain situations. 
If I am doing something that I don’t want anyone to know about. Something that I fear getting caught doing. In those situations, I’m as careful as a young teenage boy masturbating. IN fact, masturbating is one of the things I’m really careful about. But these situations are not necessarily private. For example, I write when nobody knows I’m doing it. I don’t want to do it in front of people. Especially people I know. I don’t know why that is though. 
There are other things as well. Sex is the obvious one. Stealing stuff. I don’t steal stuff. Maybe once or twice I’ve taken weed from my friends maybe. They would’ve given me anyway. I didn’t want to ask. 
I don’t like to reveal what I’m doing with my career. Which is nothing really. But, there are a few things I’ve tried and failed at. I don’t want to tell anyone about them. Flipping domains. I did not sell even one. I thought I had good names. I don’t know where I failed. The first step was picking a domain name. I picket a few expired ones. They expired for a reason. Nobody wanted them. I need to be careful about what I pick from the expired domains. Selling comes next. I can then list it in different places. Maybe reach out to a business or a blog or something. But that comes later. But I do need to think about selling. 
I also started a blog on the SATs. I bought a domain from the expired domains website. I tried selling it for a while. I thought I would get good money. It didn’t happen. So I decided to make a website. Like a guidane, tutorial thing. It didn’t work out. I wrote a few articles. But then I lost steam and stopped. I didn’t know where I would take it really. I think the main problem was I had no interest in it. I didn’t give two shits about it. 
If I’m starting a website, I need to be interested at least and passionate ideally. Electronic goods store could be interesting. I like surfing the apps. This is something I can actually be interested in. I’ll sell cool shit you can’t find anywhere. That is my why of my business. I like to be the first one to know about things. To give recommendations. Something they will be really be surprised by. So I want to create a dropshipping business selling electronic stuff that is unique.
I don’t tell people about interviews and job hunting as well. I’m embarrassed at not being able to find and hold a job. I know that they think I’m wasting my life away. They probably think that I don’t want to get a job. And that I’m not applying and interviewing. That is partly because I don’t talk about it with anyone. I’ll try to hide it as much as possible. I’ll tell them if they ask a pointed question. But I will not volunteer that information.
Maybe I think they’ll see me applying to these shit jobs and think less of me. They been climbing the corporate ladder for the past 5-6 years. I’ve done it intermittently for 3. Maybe less. And for the past 3 years, I’ve been falling down the ladder. Based on how much I’ve fallen, my friends are too far up. Looking down at me. Still falling. Becoming smaller, and smaller. I fear that in no time I’ll become a dot, and fall so far down, that they don’t even see me anymore. Just another chapter in their life. That guy I was friends for a while. He had potential.
I should tell them how I feel. Am I courageous enough? This is something really intimate. Do I really want to reveal myself like this? How will they see me then? Will our relationship change? Maybe I should do it. We’re all having this conversation behind each others back. Maybe we need to turn to each other.
