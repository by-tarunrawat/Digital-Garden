Date: 04-02-2019
Topic: Once, in the midst of all the recklessness

I started painting. I was never an artist really. I used to sketch when I was little. I would make portraits of celebrities and random people. Never really took it seriously. And I never painted. I wasn’t very confident of it. The reason I give people for not painting is a teacher. I remember that an art teacher would give me a bad grade for painting outside the lines. We were never taught proper techniques to paint. Anyway, that is my reason. Pretty flimsy. Same reason I give for stopping writing. Am I this sensitive? I thought I had thicker skin. Just because one person did something, I stopped? Ridiculous behavior man. I can’t let this happen to me anymore. I can’t stop people from letting me do what I like. Especially people who don’t matter.
So, Sometime around a year and a half ago. It had been a year since I had a job. That was also recklessness I think. I just went with the flow. Didn’t really fight it. I didn’t have much to fill my days. I would look for jobs on linkedin, naukri.com. I would read a little. Play chess. But there wasn’t much happening. I didn’t know what else to do. Everybody was at work. All my friends. 
I don’t know how it happened. I was thinking about art and painting and stuff. And all of a sudden I decided to go to a stationery shop, and got myself a chart paper, pencils, erasure and oil pastels. I wanted to learn to paint. The first portrait I made was of Pooja. It wasn’t great. I was just trying paint on paper. I loved it. It was not horrible. But it wasn’t something to be proud of either. Yet, I was proud of it. I wanted to make more. SO I did. 
I made a portrait of Roy. I turned one of Mannu’s picture into a painting. I made a couple of scenes. I was learning new things. It was amazing. My friends loved it. My mom loved it. My relatives loved it. Then I stopped. I don’t know why. I was making one for my sister. I was halfway to a sketch. But then I abandoned it. It stopped being fun for some reason. Maybe I was doing it to impress people. And when they were I didn’t have a purpose. I still sometimes think about picking it up again. But I don’t. I don’t know where the motivation went.
My life is in shatters. I don’t have a job. I don’t have a purpose. I don’t have money. A lot of bad decisions. A little bit of the wrong attitude. In the midst of it all, I found something — just for a little while — something that I fell in love with. And let it go with all the recklessness.
