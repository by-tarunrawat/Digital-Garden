Date: 30-05-2019
Topic: Write about falling from grace.

I used to come home, and I would pay for stuff. I would buy groceries. I would buy medicines. Pay bills at restaurants. I was making decent money. After a while I was making good money even. Now, my mom gives me money when she sends me out to buy groceries. I should be embarrassed. Instead I feel a guilt. I feel like my mom shouldn’t have to worry about money. I should be taking care of that. She worries about me. She shouldn’t. I should make sure she shouldn’t. I should take responsibility of that.
I remember when we went to some place for dinner. My mom insisted that she should pay for it. But I just took the bill and paid. I could do that. And my mother could let me do it. It is a fall from grace for me. I was taking care of things. I would’ve been able to pay for my sister’s wedding as well. I could’ve given them a great gift. A trip somewhere. That would’ve been easy to do. It would’ve made them so happy. That would’ve made me happy. I failed them. I’m the eldest. I can’t behave like a baby anymore. I should be myself. Assertive. Responsible. Don’t know, what am I going to do about the job. I need to find something. Desperately. Can I just do an executive role. I feel like I’m so unemployable right now. 
Engineering doesn’t do me any good. My MBA counts for shit. My experience has been so far in the past, employers might just expect me to know nothing at all. I understand their apprehensions. Someone who hasn’t worked in three years isn’t going to be the horse you bet on. But then how do I become that horse. I need to get a track record that people can trust. 
THis all seems so difficult right now. I don’t know what to do here. I’m wasting my time I’m aware. But I’m aware that time is wasting me as well.
