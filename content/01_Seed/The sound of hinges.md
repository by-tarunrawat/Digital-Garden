# The Sound of Hinges

## The Spark
A character who tests the emotional temperature of a household purely by the weight and sound of the front door closing behind someone.

## Sensory Anchor
The friction of an un-oiled brass hinge dragging across a tight jamb; the silence before keys drop into the tray.
