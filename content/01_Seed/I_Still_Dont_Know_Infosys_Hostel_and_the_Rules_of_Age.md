Topic: I still don’t know


I still don’t know what I want to do with my life. Sometimes I think I want to make movies. Sometimes it’s writing. Sometimes I want to write comedy and perform. Sometimes its songs and sometimes it’s art. Most of the time I’m afraid, and I want to find a job. Live a stable life. But I know I won’t be happy doing that. 
I don;t know. Maybe I should have tried figuring this out when I was younger. I tried. I thought I wanted to do engineering. But maybe not. I did try to get away from as many jobs as possible. I was tryig for an MBA in my final year. I cleared a lot of entrance tests of It companies, but didn’t go for any interviews. Maybe that was my clue. Something inside me was telling me to get away. I did finally take a job. It was supposed to be a backup. I ended up working there.
I was quite happy at infosys as well. At first at least. When I was training with them. Living in a hostel. With friends. I was free. There weren’t too may responsibilities. Even when I was on bench I was happy. As soon as I started working on a project, my problems started. I wanted to prepare for MBA. I wanted to get away from it all. I managed to do it finally. And that made me happy too. 
I can’t remember a job where I was truly happy. THe only time I remember being happy was when I saw peope read my stuff. My poems, my prose. When I played cricket, I was the happiest. But I can’t do that anymore. Can I? I’m too old. And too far away from it. People give their lives to be good at it. Even then they don’t get to make a living from it. How can I expect to do anything with it. 
Can I really break those rules of age? I don;t know if I can. I may do something related to cricket. Something close to it. Writing, coaching. Maybe. It won’t give me the same happiness. But can I try?
Writing gives me some happiness. I’m afraid, I don’t know why. I have nothing to lose. I do like people appreciating my work. Maybe all I want is appreciation. I loved it when I was making art. I don’t know why I stopped that. It’s been two years now. I could’ve done a lot with it as well. I stopped. What an idiot. I can’t stop writing. That will be the dumbest thing I could do. I might do something with it. Maybe people will like it. Maybe not. Maybe I will become famous, maybe not. But I will never know if I stop.
