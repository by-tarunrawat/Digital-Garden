Dad
s should be retrained.
They think they have a specific job that they need to do. Lekin sab bache ek jaise nahi hote. to sabko alag level of attention chahiye hota hai.
Dad's think their job is to go to office come home spend time with the son or daughter while watching TV. Talk about studies and lecture us when mom tells him to.
They're doing it for us. I agree. They are trying to build a good future for us. But when that future is built, we are old enough to start building a future for our children.
And now our Dads are old and retired. and they need attention from us. But now we're trying to figure out how to make the future that he build fror us, work for us in the present. And hopefully building a better future for my children.
So are Dads destined to have this relationship. Moms have it different. Moms are invested in you as if you are a part of her. In fact you are a part of her. It's like if she gave up an arm. And that arm had life of it's own, learnt languages, talked to you. Then of course she will be invested in it's well being. 
But dads job is like. You know when someone builds a company they put their own money into it. THey work day and night to run it. Try making it successful. Then you need to expand that business and this one guy comes in. Puts some money in. And that's how the business grows. Who do you think is likley to sell their shares when there is something more advantageous. It's the investor. 
Dads are the investors in a business called the family. 
Moms are the ones who create the business and run it. Dads are the employees. Ideally it sould be a patnership but you know that rarely happens. 

So dads think they are supposed to go work. Making money is their job. And when they come home, there might be some time allocated to the child.
My Dad worked in the merchant navy. WHich meant he was on the ship 9 months. Away from us for 9 months. My mom raised us single handedly really. She was 21 when she had me. 21. She had my brother at 23 and when my sister was born she was a mother of 3 at 25.
That sometimes boggles me. It is more responsibility than I have taken in my entire life combined. And I'm 35. 
She raised 3 kids at 25 almost like a single parent for 3 quaters of our life. I never thought of my mother as a single parent. But she was. It wasn't as bad as for the actual single mothers because my dad was there. Somewhere. We did not know most of the time. But he was there. He'd return in a few months.
Long distance in those days sucked. We didn't have phones. Neither did the ship. So my dad would call whenever he reached shore from a pay phone. But we didn't have phones right? So we would go to thi PCO on a day and time that my dad would have mentioned to us in the previous call. We would sit there for a while. Waiting for dad to call. Then after a while he would call. My mom would talk to him for some time. And then it was over. We would go home waiting for next time. 
That was how a long distance relationship was for my mom. I sometimes feel angry. She was cheated of a full life. With her husband. I feel angry. I think I might be angry at my dad. How could he do this. He had to figure something else out. He tried i think. That office he opened at Rajpur road was an attempt to get out of that life. Stay with mom. What happened? He didn't have the aptitude for it? Could he have done more? He was a normal man. Like me. I can't seem to hold a job. He went back to his old job when he couldn't do anything else. I didn't. So I'm still wandering. 
What do I do? Can I learn something from my father. What did he do wrong. He did more than I did. But he didnt have a business plan. His mind wasn't into it. He couldn't charge people money and that made is unfeasible for him to continue with it.
I have to keep at it. Just keep on working. Everyday. Incremental progress.
