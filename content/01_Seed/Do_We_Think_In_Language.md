Celebrate your creative self, the writer inside you. Write about how writing is an important part of your life. Write about your gratitude in possessing such a gift.

Writing is one of the most important things that humans could have invented. We can transfer ideas from one head to the other. From one place to the other. And from one time to the other. It is difficult to imagine a world where the only way to transfer stories was telling them to each other. And they would then get transferred by another retelling. Losing some of the detail. Losing most of he emotion. And maybe all of the meaning. We’re so lucky to live in a world where we not only write, but also have technology that makes it extremely easy to do so.
As humans our brains are full of ideas. Me too. I have so many ideas in my head. So many thoughts that just come and go. Some I blurt out to people. Some I keep to myself. But these are novel thoughts. Not novel maybe, but these are ideas that people don’t normally get. I am so thankful for the fact that I think this way. I’m extremely grateful for that. The stuff that I’ve already written. The stuff that I’m going to write. The books that I WILL PUBLISH. And the stuff that’ll just be shite. All that shite helps. I have to get these out in order to get the truly good ones on the page.
How do I direct my writing towards particular goals though. I loved some of the stuff that I’ve written in the past. I’m really proud of it. That funny prose I wrote about the new Avatar movie coming out. I like that it was quite funny. I need to read that one again. I think that poem about girls was also pretty funny. I’m not that bad a writer. In fact I’m pretty decent. I need to write more. I need to put all my ideas on paper. 
I’m grateful that I have a laptop that I can write on anytime. If that wasn’t enough. I have a great mobile phone that I can use to write anywhere at all. Anyplace where I can’t take my laptop. What an amazing time we’re living in.Imagine the writers of old. They had to write on pages with their hands. Which is so slow. But the portability of it was great. You could write anywhere. Just a pen and paper is needed. I liked writing using paper and pen. I’ve written most of my stuff that way. Maybe I should try that again. But this is just so much convenient and faster.Anyway, I’m digressing.
Typewriter was an improvement. Still not as good as a laptop and a mobile. I’m pretty lucky to live in these times. I’m sure there will be a time in the future, where people will only have to think it and it’ll be on paper; or on the screen at least. THat’ll be just great. THe way an idea looks in my head is never how it looks on paper. How does that happen? Is language lacking in some way? Is language different from the way we think. I don’t know. I thought we thought in language mostly. Do we? I need to think about this more. I need to read about this as well. 

Let’s write it down before I forget: How do we think? DO we think in language? If not, how does it differ. How does language aid in thinking?
