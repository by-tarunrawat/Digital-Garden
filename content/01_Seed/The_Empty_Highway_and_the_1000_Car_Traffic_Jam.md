Topic: This much is known…

This much is known about the world: It’s not fair, it’s not cruel. It’s nothing. It just is. There is no one trying to make it fair to everyone. And no one is trying to make it particularly cruel. Things just happen through a combination of things. A butterfly effect of will and action. You have certain control over what happens though. And is is significant. But it is foolish to think you are even in control of the entire situation. 
You may have an illusion of control. You might think you are sitting in the driver’s seat. The road is empty. You can do anything you want. Drive anywhere. Drive the way you like. But you have to stay on the road. That is not in your control. You may change your route. But you’re still on a road, that you had absolutely no hand in making. If the road leads you north, you’re going north. If it’s going in circles, so are you. 
Add another car in the mix. It’s right in front of you. You might have to slow down. Think about how the person in front is driving. If you have to overtake that car then you have to change see a clear path to the right of that car, increase your speed and go. You don’t want an accident. You might still be in control. But one car, only one car, changed the way you drive. You had to take other things in consideration. Now add 9 more cars. How in control are you now? Are you still in control? Do your decisions depend on you entirely? Or on how the other cars are going? Now add a thousand cars.
Life is like that. You might think you are an individual. You might think you have the power to make all your decisions. You are in control of your life. But more often than not, you’re wrong. Anything you do, has had influences that you can’t even think of. There are things that you consider even when you make decisions. Your actions have an impact over other things. This impact might be big, or it might be small. But it’s there. There is no running away from it. It ripples over other lives. And you may or may not see it.
What do we do if we’re not in control? If nothing is completely in our hands, do we just stop doing things? Making decisions? Taking action? 
I don’t know. I only know that the world won’t stop if you stop. It will still move. If you stop somewhere on the road, other cars will move ahead and go wherever they wanted to be. You will see other cars approaching you. Then going past you. Where are you? At a standstill. Not willing to move ahead.
