Breakfast is my favorite meal of the day. The things I like eating for breakfast are not fancy or the most complex. Bread jam, parantha jam, omelette, french toast. I love it. It’s strange though that it the meal that I’m most likely to miss in the day. It’s not that I get up late. Or don’t have time to cook breakfast for myself. It’s because I’m not independent anymore. I don’t have a job. I don’t make any money. And I live with my girlfriend. I try to use as little of the groceries as possible. I don’t want to be a burden on their household. I feel useless already. I don’t want to feel entitled to it.
As a kid I wanted breakfast to be my first meal of the day. Now I know that breakfast is the first meal of the day. I mean regardless of the time, I wanted breakfasty things as my first meal. And my mom obliged. I loved my tiffin in school. It had all my favorite things. 
When I was in boarding school, breakfast was the shortest meal of the day. Before assembly, we had 10 minutes to finish our food. It was important that I eat properly. Other meals were not food. Not in my eyes at least. Daal had a 3 inch layer of water on top. Rotis were old and had so much flour that our clothes would be white after lunch and dinner. But they couldn’t do anything with the breakfast. It is difficult to screw up bread and jam. I discovered peanut butter here. My love affair with it continues till today.
Because breakfast was a 10 minute thing, I had to eat for the whole day in that time. I learned to eat fast. Stuff my mouth and stomach as quickly as I can. This continues to this day. For every meal. I have never participated in one of those speed eating contests. People eating 10 hotdogs in a minute. A full 10 pound burger in 5 minutes. But I know I can finish food faster than most people. 
Coming home, a bit of entitlement comes back. I eat breakfast here. My mom insists on it. In Bangalore, Pune, I don’t. The number of meals in a day has come down dramatically for me. Earlier I tried 5-6 smalls meals in the day. Now, two.
