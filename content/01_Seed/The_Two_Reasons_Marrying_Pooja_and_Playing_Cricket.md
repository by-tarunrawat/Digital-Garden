Date: 25-03-2019
Topic: Write what you didn't do.

So many things. I didn’t go to Seattle. I was up for a visa at least. I would’ve quit Infosys either way. I got into MICA a few days later. That is what I was actually aiming for. Even if I had gone for a visa, I would’ve probably tanked the interview. Although, if I had the visa, I still could’ve done something. Might not have done anything different. Maybe I wouldn’t have been so vehement about rejecting the opportunity. But either way, I was not too keen on it.
I never told my crushes in school, how I felt about them. I could’ve tried to have a relationship with them. Friends even. Maybe it could’ve developed into something more. I never took the initiative. I don’t really regret it. I don’t think of it as a big deal.
I never told Rekha I loved her. I told her after we broke up. But I should’ve said it. That might have been one of the reasons behind why we didn’t work. I can imagine how she must’ve felt. She didn’t know how serious I was about her. I didn’t talk about it. She meant a lot to me, and she deserved to know. I don’t talk about my feelings. It is uncomfortable for me. I’m sure it is the same for many others. I don’t do that anymore. I tell my girlfriend how much she means to me. And I love her. I don’t know if I do it enough though. 
I didn’t follow through on my writing projects. I didn’t follow through on my website. There is a lot of things that I didn’t do. 
Does that mean I should take all opportunities that come my way. Maybe. I’ve always filtered these things. I fear I’ll end up doing something I don’t want to do. Although, I’m not doing what I want right now either. But honestly I would be okay with it if I had money. I want to write. But I probably wouldn’t be writing if I had money. Should I take whatever comes my way. Maybe I should. What job can a 34 year old who hasn’t worked in 3 years get?
I never played cricket seriously. If I have a regret, that is it. I think I could’ve become someone. Not because I was great. I was decent of course. But I was so in love with it that I would’ve done anything to improve. I would’ve given it my all. I would’ve put in my 10000 hours. And more. Let’s calculate. If I practised 5 hours a day. Then in a year I would have close to 1900 hours. In 5 years I would’ve reached the minimum skill level I envision for myself. Even if that is not the case. I still would’ve played cricket for 10000 hours. That is the dream. I don’t even want money for it. I pay money now to play whatever I can. To have played serious cricket, at any level, would’ve been awesome. 
I need to make a plan where I get to play cricket everyday. I did this in another prompt. So I’ll see where it was. But I need to start with getting stable somewhere. I need to stay in one place which I can call home. Then I can do whatever I want. For that I needed a job. I need a job because I want to play cricket. Two reasons actually. I want to get a job because I want to marry Pooja. And, I want to play cricket. Only two reasons. I need to get a job for these two things. That is the only thing I want. 
I will continue to write. I think I’ve decided a path for myself there. I am a writer now. I’ll call myself a writer. There is no doubt in my mind. I need to get a job to then try to piece together my life.
