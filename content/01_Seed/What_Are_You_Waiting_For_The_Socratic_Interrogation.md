Date: 05-03-2019
Topic: What are you waiting for?

What are you waiting for? 
The perfect opportunity? 
It doesn’t exist. What is a perfect opportunity anyway?
Is someone going to give a bag of money to you? To do what?

Write the best story that only I can think of.
Paint the best portrait I can design.
Make the best movie. A movie that I want to see.
Sing the best tune, make the best jokes, make movie stars say my lines.

Why do you need a bag of money for that?
If you really want to do them. Do them.
You can do most of that stuff without money.
Are these things not important enough? 

It’s not that easy. There are a lot of things to think about.
Lots of things to consider. 
I need time. I’m waiting for… 

What?
For…
What are you waiting for?
…the right time.

Oh! The right time. The perfect moment? 

What if you’re late? 
What if you’re early but didn’t notice that the right time just went by?
It didn’t wait for you?
It didn’t care you were there?

Time passes you by. Moments are fleeting.
One just went by. So did this one… And another.
Soon it will be tomorrow. Then next week. Before you know it, it’s next year.

Isn’t this the moment you should take action. 
This one. Right now. 
Instead of waiting for the right time, try the one that you exist in.


What are you waiting for?
Your calling? Passion? Something that you would die for?

I won’t die for anything. That’s for certain. 
Maybe that’s why I don’t have a passion. 
I just want to live. What is wrong with that?

Distractions. That’s  not what I asked.
What are you waiting for?
Waiting for your passion, your love to arrive?
Maybe. 
What will you do in the meanwhile?
Till this passion comes to you. 
When will it come to you by the way?
In your sleep maybe? While you are watching YouTube videos?
Or checking Instagram perhaps? 
It’ll probably come when you’re changing channels on TV.
Or maybe you’ll swipe your mobile screen enough times to finally get some fucking passion.

I don’t know. I don’t know what I’m waiting for. 
I don’t know what I’m doing. Does anyone.
I don’t want a magic wand. I just want a direction. 
I want some encouragement. I need some courage.
I need belief. In myself.
