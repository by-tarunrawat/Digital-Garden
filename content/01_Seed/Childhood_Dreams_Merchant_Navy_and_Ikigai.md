My childhood dream was to be in merchant navy. That was because my father was in the Army. And at the time I thought that children are supposed to do what there parents did. It's like joining their business. THere were times I wanted to be a policeman, and autorickshaw driver, coolie, singer; basically everything they show in movies. 
When I gre up a little a used to play a lot of sports. My favorite sport was cricket. I wanted to become a cricketer. In fact my grandfather wanted me to become a cricketer as well. I don't know what happened. I didn't pursue it seriously. I used to play. A lot. BUt with kids from school, or random kids from the neighborhood.
I used to write as well, and wanted to become a poet. I think I just wanted to tell stories where I was the hero. I could be anything. A spy, a doctor, whatever I wanted. NOw I'm in marketing. It's a decent job. But this is not what I want to do everyday.
THere is a japanese concept called "ikigai' (I'm not sure if I'm spelling that correctly). It means something that gives you reason to wake up in the morning; a reason for being; a sense of purpoess. I don't have a purpose. I don't know my reason for being. I struggle with finding it for myself. I see people around me not quite content with life. But they seem happier than me.
