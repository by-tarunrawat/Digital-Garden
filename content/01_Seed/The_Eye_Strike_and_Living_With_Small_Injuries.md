Date: 18-03-2019
Topic: Write about small injuries


A cricket ball hit me once. On my eye. Left eye I think. It hurt a lot. I used to wear glasses, so it was fortunate that I hadn’t blinded myself. I had a few scratches around the eye. And although it hurt a bit, it was nothing that I couldn’t manage. Since, this incident, I’ve never been afraid of a leather ball. Yes, it’s hard. Yes, it hurts when it struck you on the body. But I knew I could survive. I had come stronger out of this.
I liked going out and playing sports. Like most moms, mine worried about me when I went out. She was worried that I would get injure myself. She was right. I did injure myself. Many times. But I would hide it from her. I was scared I would get scolded. For not being careful enough. 
Now that I think about it, she just wanted me to be safe. She probably wouldn’t have scolded me. She would have taken care of me. I don’t think she would have stopped me from going to play. It was my own fears. 
But it did teach me to live with small injuries. I see my friends, screaming in pain from scratches, pats on the back. Same people endure their periods like an MMA fighter. They take these scratches seriously. You never know what an injury can lead to. Maybe they are smart and I’m the dumb one. I’m not strong just stupid.
