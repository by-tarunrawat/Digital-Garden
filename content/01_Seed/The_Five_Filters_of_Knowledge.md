1. What You’ve been taught.
2. What you assume is true because you’ve heard it repeated by others.
3. What you feel, no matter how subtle.
4. What you don’t know.
5. What you learn from your own experience.
- Verlyn Klinkenborg (Several short sentences about writing)

These is the way be know things about the world. 
I need to keep these in mind as I’m beginning to think about what to write.

1. What you’ve been taught.
I need to recollect everything that I have been taught about the subject. In school, magazines, online. This will help me understand the range of my knowledge. Bring my knowledge to my attention. For me to analyze.

2. What you assume is true because you’ve heard it repeated by others.
These could be anecdotal evidence. People like to talk as if they know stuff. Recall what you’ve heard from others. Test it. Research on it. Find what holds true and what doesn’t. And, how it impacts your current knowledge on the subject.

3. What you feel, no matter how subtle.
I’m not sure what the author wants to say with this point. I’ll take a stab. I should write down what I feel about the subject. With whatever information that I already have.

4. What you don’t know.
Make a list of questions you have. Or, you want to investigate. Write down all the things you don’t know about the topic. Write down whatever you think will be important and you would like to know about.

5. What you learn from your own experience.
Write down about your experiences. As they relate to the topic.
