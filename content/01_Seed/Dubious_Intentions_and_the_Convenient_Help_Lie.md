Date: 07-05-2019
Topic: Dubious intentions

People don’t speak the truth. They hide what they really think and want. It’s a fairly common thing to ask people how they are. And the common response to that is, I’m good, how are you. It’s not what’s in their heart at all though. Inside they may be dying. Or angry. Or frustrated, sad. But they’ll always say I’m good. 
Even in normal conversations people rarely tell the truth. They ask you questions as if they are really interested. Give them the answer and they will go off on another tangent. Telling you a story from their own life. They offer help as if it is really genuine. But take them up on their offer, and you’ll find out that they want to get you off of their back. 
I don’t blame them though. It’s how communication has evolved. People want to appear friendly. THey want to appear interested. They might want to help you. But it should not be inconvenient. Even if someone says they will help you as much as they can. It’s a lie. What they really want to say is, I will help you as long as it’s not taking up too much of my time. I want to help you on my own terms. I want to help you till I’m bored. People doo hide their true intentions.
Even in bars. Guys trying to pick girls up will say the strangest things. They will make up lines and jokes. They would rattle their brain for interesting things to say. All the while what they really want to say is: I find you attractive and I would like to take you out sometime. Maybe sleep with you, as soon as possible. But that would be rude. Even though the girl knows what is exactly on the guy’s mind. 
In relationships people say something while they mean something else. Pooja would get angry at me all the time. I knew the anger wasn’t because of the thing she was talking about. But she wouldn’t say it. I confronted her a few times about this. I asked her why she was angry at me. Did she not like me anymore? Did she want to break up with me? She said no. And she meant it. Finally she said it all. 
I am guilty of this as well. I smile politely at people when I really want to shout at them. I compliment them when I don’t really care. I try to be honest with my words, my actions and my disposition. But most of the time I am not. I don’t want to hurt anybody’s feelings. Maybe that is one of the reason why other people do it too. They are polite. They don’t want to hurt anyone’s feelings. It’s a social norm to say certain things. And most people don’t take honesty too well. 
I don’t know how that will change. I doubt it will ever change. Maybe the world is better off this way. Maybe it isn’t. I feel there has to be a middle ground to all this. But I can’t change the world. Especially if I don’t know how. What I can do is change myself. Maybe I need to set up some rules. I do know that I want to be honest most of the time. And I know that is the way to go. I need to figure out when I hide my true intentions. And when I need to lie. I can’t stop lying. It’s such a wonderful tool.
