Date: 20-03-2019
Topic: This is a map to where I live.

More than a million people live in Dehradun now. Trees making way for roads and buildings. There was a time when houses had some distance between them. Now all the houses stick to each other. Zero fucks about the other house’s personal space. My house there was one of the only two houses in a kilometer radius. Now it’s one of the thousands or so in that one kilometer. 
Taking left of right from my house would lead to the city. Both over the Risapana river. Right takes you through narrow roads, and small houses. Our maids, plumbers, electricians, lived there. On the left you go through the main Haridwar road. 
Walking towards the Haridwar road, you see a shopping complex as you meet the Haridwar road. It is on the other side of Haridwar road. On this side of Haridwar road, on my right, is JSR hotel. It’s where we’re having a cocktail for my sister.
There is a road next to the shopping mall which leads to the Defence colony. Vidhan Sabha comes on the way. A river breaks the road in two. It is joined by Rispana Bridge. Named after the river. 
My houses is surrounded on three sides by other houses. Right behind lives Indu Mausi. SHe has been my mother’s childhood friend. On the left Negi nani ji. That was the second house in the area. ON the right is a small complex rented out for business. 
There is a house across the street. It belongs to a local sweet shop owner. It’s build on the ground where we used to play cricket growing up. There were a couple of other grounds where we would go to play. All of them have been taken and houses build on them. I don’t know where kids go to play anymore. I see it some places. But these are a few areas. And far apart.
