Date: 07-03-2019
Topic: Write about a time you won, Big.

I love playing cricket. I’m sure there was a time when I didn’t know anything about cricket. But I don’t remember that time. Must’ve been when I was really young. 9 maybe 10 months old. I played with kids living around. I played in school sometimes. 
When I was in GRD academy, I used to live in the hostel. There, I had access to more such fanatics. There we actually got up at 6 in the morning to play. You would always find people to play cricket. I started enjoying playing more than before. My friends took cricket seriously. So did I. 
After I left GRD, cricket also somewhat left me. I would play with friends sometimes. But it wasn’t enough. School didn’t have a lot of cricket. We focused on studies. Even during engineering college, I didn’t get to play it regularly. There was a yearly tournament though. I took part in it as well. The first year wasn’t that great. I got to play only one match. Second year we did quite well. I did extremely well. I scored the highest runs in thr tournament and was the second highest wicket take. I got 4 man of the match awards in 5 games. But it wasn’t really enough.
At MICA, it was the same as it was in GRD. We had people my age that were crazy about cricket. They wanted to play all the time. We would play all year round. There were tournaments and serious matches. Matches between seniors and juniors. Matches between hostels. Matches between sections in the same batch Ever game was contested seriously. I was in heaven. 
THe annual cricket tournament was a big deal. There were auctions held for players every year. There would be owners, spectators. First year, we didn’t do well. There were a lot of factors. Captaincy, strategy, team, form. I think about that some other time. Second year we did quite well. In fact we won that tournament.
I didn’t want to be captain that year. I just wanted a player. But nobody wanted to come forward. A couple of people had asked me to become the captain. I finally acceded. Auctions came. I didn’t have a strategy beforehand. I wanted allrounders. I had a couple of names in mind. Tushar, Chetan. THere were other captains too. So, I couldn’t get them. But I got a big player in the auction. Pratik. That helped me make a new strategy. I wanted another good bowler and a few bit part bowlers. I wanted one or two more decent batsman. I had a competitive team.
We plaed really well throughout the tournament. Our batting wasn’t clicking as much as we wanted. But our bowlers were on fire. In one of the matches against the favorites of the tournament, we got out for 30 odd runs. But we managed to bowl the other team for less than that. There was great chemistry between the team. We were playing well together. 
THe final was amazing. We scored our highest total of the tournament. We had such good bowlers that we defended that score easily. It was unbelievable. I just wanted to play cricket. And we had won. I had never experienced this. I had never played in a setting where it actually mattered. My teammates still talk about that tournament as if that was one of the biggest achievement of our life. Maybe it was. Is it a sad thing to say. Maybe. But it was the most fun I have had.
