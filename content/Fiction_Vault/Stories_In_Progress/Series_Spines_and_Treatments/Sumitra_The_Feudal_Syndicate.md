Sumitra was a girl of 12, when her mom was raped and murdered by a powerful man. She and her dad went to the police. They took the report, but arrested the wrong person. When they went to the station to tell them who it really was, they saw the man, who was a big jameendar in their village laughing with the police officers. 
Angry, Sumitra confronts them, they beat her father up to the point of death. The Jamindar threatens to rape Sumitra as well, and forces himself on her. IN her struggle with the Jamindar, Sumitra picks up a gun lying around and shoots the Jamindar. Then she turns around to shoot the two officers. (This could also be her father. But I feel if we make Sumitra do it, it will be like a dark past that she hasn’t gotten over yet. A wound if you will) Her father is horrified at this. Afraid of what will happen to her daughter if she gets caught, he takes the blame. They argue their case in court, and tell the judge the circumstances (self defense. Beating and the rape attempt) under which the killings happened. But the other officers along with other Jamindars of the village, plot against them and the father, Bheeshma, goes to Jail for 14 years.
Angry at this injustice Sumitra distrusts cops and the law. She vows to become so powerful that no one is able to take advantage of her family and anyone like her ever again.
Her grandfather and grandmother come to the village to raise her. Her grandfather used to be in military and trains her to fight and use weapons. Her grandmother used to be in politics. She teaches her at home. 
With the help of her grandfather and grandmother. Through clever manipulation and political connections they acquire more and more land in the village and become one of the biggest land holders in the village. 
Sumitra sees the other landholders (Jamindars) exploiting farmers and labor. She recruits men to fight these Jamindars off, and saves farmers from exploitation. This happens several times till most of the Jamindars are afraid of her. All villagers become loyal to her and go to her in case of any issue. Even the Village Pradhan starts taking her permission to carry on his day to day activities in the village.
Her father is about to get released from the prison. In the time between his imprisonment and release, Sumitra gained power in the village and drove off the Jamindar’s family out and took over their lands. The eldest son of the Jamaindar, Ambuj, vowed to become a police officer after his father’s death. He has been transferred to this village. He decides to bring his whole family and reclaim the land. He knows of Sumitra and her family is like a guda family, but doesn’t know that her father Bheeshma was responsible for his father’s death. He only knows that she has terrorized the entire village Jamindari and holds a lot of power in the entire district. The MLAs and MPs are afraid of her and go to her to win elections. He wants to go back, get back his family’s land and stand up to Sumitra to end her reign of terror. 
The Village pradhan is an evil fellow. He dislikes Sumitra and her family, but bows down to her because of the power she yields. He instigates Ambuj,to take revenge on her. He wants the power Sumitra holds. His plan is to acquire the farming lands and build factories and shopping malls. He has talked about this with Sumitra. But Sumitra and refused him in strong words. She has seen the damage these factories do to the neighboring lands, lakes and rivers by dumping their waste in it. 
The pradhan has tried to get the smaller farmers to sell off their lands, which they have agreed to, but Sumitra has ordered all of them not to do it. And being loyal to Sumitra they have listened to her. They believe that Sumitra would never do anything that is not in their interest.
When Ambuj arrives in the village, Sumitra doesn’t know his identity. But because she distrusts Police she doesn’t like him either. She orders him to bow down to her several times, but he refuses. This makes her dislike him even more. He doesn’t know that Sumitra’s father was the one who killed his father. He finds out when her father is released from the prison. His determination to end Sunitra’s stronghold gets even stronger. The pradhan finds out about the connection between them and instigates Ambuj.
Sumitra sees how honest and good Ambuj is and starts developing a soft corner for him. She might be falling in love with him. But then eventually finds out about his family and his father. She now wants to destroy his family just like his father destroyed hers. 
This will be her journey to realizing that she is becoming the person she hated.
