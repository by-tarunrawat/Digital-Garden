Date: 03-01-2019
Topic: Write about a brief encounter

I was on my way to home from college. I had just started engineering studies in Dehradun. It is common in engineering colleges to get ragged in the first few days. I was prepared for it. I knew I wanted to have some fun with it. And although I didn’t know to what extent I’ll go to, I was prepared to stand up for myself. 
The first few incidents were a few seniors telling us to wear proper clothing, and asking our names and stuff. This was disappointing. There had been serious incidences all over the country, and the government had stepped in to ban ragging. Punishments had been meted out to a few boys. So, naturally seniors in our college were scared to do anything. I was roaming around the college with a huge target on my back. But nothing happened. 
One day, Amit and I decided to do something about it. To look for someone who would rag us. SO we decided to take the local bus to go home. We knew that only seniors were taking local busses in the first few months. As we got on the bus, we were spotted. It was a good opportunity for seniors to show who’s boss, and for us to have some fun and experience true college life. 
At first we were asked why we weren’t wearing the right clothes. The we were told to sing double meaning item songs in the bus. As loud as we could. This was good. This is exactly what we wanted. Punishments that were fun for them and us. We were told to sell jeans. And do a bunch of other stuff. We were satisfied with our ragging. 
Wehn we got off the bus, one of the seniors got off as well. Amit and I are tall boys, and we are decently built. AS soon as this guy got off and looked at us standing there, he came to us to apologize. He told us that this was a part of college life. And we should take it in our stride. This is how you form a long lasting bond with seniors who will help you out with notes and books later. 
This was rather disappointing, but also amusing. I realized how scared these poeple are as well. You never know what the guy you are ragging can do to you. After all you are all in the same age group. They were also scared of people going to the authorities and complaining. If somebody did complain, then there careers would have been on the line. 
It wasn’t like I’m in favor of ragging. I wanted to experience it, because I thought I will be able to handle it. But not everyone can. I don’t remember who that senior was. I don’t remember his name or his face. But I do remember this incident. The only ragging I had to endure. For which I was apologized to. Strange.
