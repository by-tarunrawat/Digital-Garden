Date: 21-11-2018
Topic: After midnight

It used to be, I wasn’t allowed to be awake till midnight. Now there hasn’t been a day I’ve slept before it. We were told as kids that 12 o’clock is the time for ghosts. Clock strikes 12 and all kinds of bad spirits, chudails and ghosts come out to play. Horror movies reinforced it as well. I mean, nobody ever gave an explanation for it. Why 12? It starts getting dark around 6-6.30 in winters. SUmmers it might be a little later. But anytime after that would be a good time. Maybe its because everybody is asleep. SO they can come out to kill, eat do whatever to the rest of us? That doesn’t make sense either. We’re talking about ghosts. If they came in front a stadium, everybody would still run. Nobody will be like, ki chal dekhte hain. Yahan itne saare log hai kuch kar hi denge iska. What will we do man. It is already dead. What will it be afraid of. Why does it need us to sleep? 
You know who benefits from these stories? Two types of people. Thieves. THis will be like a good propaganda or marketing thing for thieves. Ask everybody to sleep so that they can do their job. But thieves don’t tell you these stories, do they? Unless your parents are thieves.
Another kind of people it benefits from this are parents. You might have guessed why? If you haven’t I’ll tell you. It’s becaus ethey want you to wake up early and go to school on time. And they wanted to have sex.They made you sleep at 9. 10 maximum. SO that by the time you are fast asleep. THey can do, you know. What they want to. With each other.
What do I do after midnight generally. I think I started sleeping late when I went to engineering college. Yeah, I think that’s when I started. I used to watch TV shows and movies. This continued after college as well. In fact I enjoyed it more after college. I was free to do things my own way. At home mom would shout at me. Asking me to sleep. But here I was on my own and could do whatever I wanted. It has carried on like that ever since. I don’t even remember a time I slept before midnight. Maybe when I was sick or something. Is it a good thing? I don’t know. Since I’ve got to get some discipline in my life, I need to think about changing these habits as well. Yeah. Maybe. I’ll think about it. I do think about it. But Let’s see.
