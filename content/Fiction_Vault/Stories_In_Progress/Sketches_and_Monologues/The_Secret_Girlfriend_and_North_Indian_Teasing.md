Topic: Write about a secret revealed

I don’t know about any big secrets. Not knowing something. And then revealed to me. I’m in shock. I’l have to think about it. 
I remember one now. I was dating Shweta in college. Nobody knew about it. My friends didn’t at least.
I had liked Shweta since the first year of college. She and I became friends. I hadn’t told her how I felt about her for a long time. My friends, Amit and group, thought there was something going on. They started teasing me with it. They started teasing Shweta from a distance as well. I think it was this that gave me some confidence. 
Second year, Shweta asked me point blank about how I felt. I told her. She didnt want to get involved. But I could see she was interested in me. I got even more confident. I used to tell her regularly that I liked her. SHe would shoot me down saying she wasn’t interested. But, she was spending a lot of time with me. In college and outside of it. She would often ride with me on my bike. I would drip her home quite often. 
In the final year — it was the last semester I think, we finally started dating. How it started is a different story. Shweta didn’t like my friends. This had put a strain in my relationship with my friends. I wouldn’t meet them anymore. I didn’t tell them about the new developments with Shweta. 
Two or three months into the relationship, I realized it wasn’t working. Shweta must’ve realized that too. She broke up with me. I had been keeping this relationship a secret from my friends. And now there were two. I had to also tell them that I broke up. 
I dont exactly remember how that went. But they weren’t surprised by the relationship at least. They were however surprised by the break up. 
I decided from that day onwards to not have such secrets. I had done that too often. In school as well. I wouldn’t tell my friends if I had a crush on a girl. Sujata, Akshi, Diksha. I had never explicitly told my friends I liked them. Big mistake looking back. I don’t do that again. It’s always better to talk about things. Even this. I was afraid that they will make fun of me. THey would tell someone. THey would do the kind of shit north indian boys do, and tease the girl. Which would hamper any chances I had with her. Bull shit. Maybe they will do all of that. But I had friends I could trust. So it was pointless.
