Thousands of parents have come together for a rally. Most of them are moms of boys and girls around 21-25.

They are standing in front of the parliament house. Asking the lawmakers of our country to update our country’s marriage laws. 
Let’s go talk to one of the protesters. 

Aapka naam?
Ji Mrs Upadhyay. (fixes her placard. Its says “Azaadi”)
Kya maange hain aapki Mrs Upadhyay?
Ji wo hum, Marriage ki leagal age badhana chahte hain.
Legal age?
Ji
Aapko nahi lagta ki jo current legal age hai, 18 aur 21 ki wo kaafi hai.
Nahi ji. Mera beta abhi 24 ka ho gaya hai use abhi omellete banana bhi nahi aata. APne kapde fold kar ke nahi rakhta. Kamra hamesha ganda. Ab aap hi bataiye, kya aisa insaan shaadi karne layak hai?
Lekin, ye to aap par hai na ki aap uski shaadi kab karayen.
Wo meri sunta kahan hai. Bolta hai job milte hi shaadi kar lega. 
To fir wo job dhoondh raha hai?
Haan pata nahi mobile se dhoondh raha hai. Pata nahi Job dhoondh raha hai ya ladki.
Matlab?
Tinder aur Linked in dono hain uske paas. Pata nahi kya karta hai.
To aap kya chahti hain, Legal age kya honi chahiye?
Kam se kam 30. 
30!!??
Ji. Mujhe lagta hai perfect age hai.
Okay (Surprised) Dhanyawad Mrs Upadhyay. Wish you all the best.

Ab ham chalte hain Mr and Mrs Parmar ke paas, jinki 27 saal ki beti ne apne liye ladka pasand kar liya hai, aur isi saal shaadi karne ka plan bana rahi hai. Aiiye.

Namaskar Mr and Mrs Parmar. (Mr Parmar wipes a single tear from his eye with his sleeve)
-Both- Namaste
Kya apko nahi lagta ki aapki beti shaadi ki umar ki ho gayi hai?
-Mrs- She’s only 27 (disgusted by the question)
(Surprised. Looks around to see if he is hearing correctly.) Yes. (beat) Exactly. Twenty seven. 
-Mr- Sir, Hamari beti hai sattais saal ki hai. Uska boyfriend hai do saal se. Wo usse shaadi karne…

-Mrs- Abhi time hi kitna hua hai date karte hue. Ye bacche samajhte hi nahi.
Lekin
