On a road. Somewhat busy street.

Vlogger (Nerdy boy. Glasses.): Hey guys, swagat hai aapka mere channel Engineer Bhai on Love and Attraction (EBOLA) me aur mai hun aapka dost aapka adviser engineer bhai.
Doston mai aap logon ko request karunga ki please comments me ma behen ki gaaliyan na likhen. Meri ma behen  bhi mere videos dekhti hain. Par koi nahi inme se kayi comments mere doston ke hain. *Grinding his teeth* unko to mai dekh lunga. Sunny to ruk, tujhe tere hostel me dekhta hun…
*Composing himself* To doston, mere kayi followers ne apne success stories bheji hain. Wo mai aapke saath aj share karna chahta hun.
*Picks up a piece of paper* First is, Arjun Khatri from Wadgaon Academia of Science and Technical Education. 3rd year, Mechanical student. *Stares at the paper**Slowly* Wadgaon Academia of Science and Technical Education… *Pauses**Chuckles* Waste… hahaha waste college…Hahaha. 
*Composes himself again* Sorry. To Arjun Khatri from Waste college likhte hain ki inki branch me ek ladki hai. *Looking at the camera with a sympathetic face* Mechanical ki tragedy.
Arjun Bhai ko ye ladki first year se pasand hai. Mera video “Ladki patane ke liye unse baat karni padti hai” dekha. Mai video ka link yahan de raha hun.*Points up but the video link comes on his face*
Doston mera video Arjun Bhai ne second year me dekha aur finally 3rd year me himmat juta ke us ladki ko hi bola, aur sabse khushi ki baat ye ki ladki ne wapas hi bola bhai ko. *Pause* 
*Puts the paper under his armpit and claps* Bahut badhiya Arjun bhai, girlfriend banane ki disha me ye aapka pehla kadam tha. Bahut khushi hui *Wipes fake tears from his eyes* hamare ek chote bhai ne aj apni hone waali girlfriend se pehli baar baat ki. Congrats.
Hamari second success story hai *Hesitates* 
Cameraman (We can’t see him. We see the camera shaking): Abey bol na.
Vlogger: Hamari second success story hai… *Hesitates again*
Cameraman: Bol na bhenchod. Khada kar diya hai idhar subah se.
Vlogger: Bas yahi thi. Aur koi success story nahi hai. 
*A slipper comes flying and hits the vlogger. He straightens himself up*
Vlogger: Hamari second success story hai… *Grinding his teeth* *Mumbles* First year ke Rajat Arora ki…
Cameraman: Abey dhang se bol na…
Vlogger: Abey kya dhang se… Saale first year me ladki patane chale hain… Yahan logon chautha saal repeat kar rahe hain ki is saal ek pategi *Boldly looking into the camera* Inke senior kya hath me drafter leke baithe hue hain. 
*looks at the paper* Mumbai and Saurashtra technical institute *Stares* MASTI??!! Kya bole bhenchod. *Looks at the camera and remembers he is still shooting*
*To the cameraman* Ye waala part edit kar ke upload karna please. 
*A thumb comes up to the camera, giving thumbs up to the Vlogger*
*Cut: A black screen with words “Ooops”

Vlogger (To the cameraman) *Hassled but calms himself down*: continue karen?
*Camera goes up and down*
Vlogger: To guys, Maine techniques aur strategy pe aapko kaafi theory di hai. Aj time hai Practical lesson ka. 
Ham khade hain Brijmohan Indumati technological college and hostel ke saamne.
