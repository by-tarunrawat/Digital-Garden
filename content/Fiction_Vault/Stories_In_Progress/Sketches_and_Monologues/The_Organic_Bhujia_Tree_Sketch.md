Welcome to 
It says on the board outside that these are all organic snacks. Is it true?
Oh of course sir. We only sell fresh farm grown, all indian, 100% pure organic snacks and baked goods.
So these snacks grow on farmland?
Yes sir. Farms, gardens, meadows. For example look at this masala peanuts sir. We grow them on trees. Then a few months later peanut masala.
I don't believe you. There is flour on the peanuts, mixed in masala, and fried.
Yes sir, that is how it comes out. 
That is how it comes out? That is how it comes out? You mean the flour covered, masala mixed, and roasted peanut is the fruit of a tree ?
Not peanut sir. Peanuts. The whole packet is the fruit. With all the masala and the plastic.
Here have a look a this picture. Shows a picture with masala peanuts growing on trees. The popcorn here. This nachos plate with the cheese dip and sauce, Mathri, khakhra, everything here is grown sir.
Well okay! What's the difference between these and the normal... Factory made snacks.
For the environment sir.
For the environment?
Correct. We don't want to pollute our mother earth so we are doing our bit.
But how is this even possible?
This is pretty recent. Our parent company invented a machine that takes any object... Say a packet of aloo bhujia... in one compartment... In another compartment it takes a bunch of dead seeds. They code the core snack components into the seeds. Viola! If you plant those seeds now, You'll get haldiram bhujia packets growing on trees in 4-6 months.
Wow!! Mind blowing... You can use this for so many things. Imagine growing mobile phones on trees. 
We already do that... We have an organic mobile phone company.
Imagine, growing clothes and shoes, and... and...watches and jewellery
Do that, do that and of course we do that.
Oh! how about medicine?
We have hospitals that can reattach limbs from organic hands and feet grown on our farms. Terminal heart diseases can be treated now because WE... GROW... HEARTS... ON... TREES.
Wow. That's pretty good. I'm sorry to have doubted you earlier.
Sure. No problem. I guess I'll take a nacho plate and a pop corn... How much do I owe you?
All this is free sir. We grow money on trees as well.
Really? 
Yes sir.
Is that even legal?
Sure is. We give a part of it to the government.
You mean bribes?
Its not a bribe.
How is this not a bribe? You are giving money to the government to let you do whatever you want.
Are we bribing you sir?
Me? Why?
Because we're giving away something that we grow on trees for free... To you. So am I bribing you?
No. But...
Same thing. We grow stuff on trees and give away our produce away for free. It's snacks to you its a leg to someone else and it's money to the government.
So what do you get out of it?
Well, I'm just an employee here so I don't know but I think I can say We are changing the world.
