Topic: Someone gave you flowers

Which is strange because nobody has ever given me flowers before. Probably because I’m a man. You are not supposed to bring flowers for men. We can not appreciate it. Beautiful. Smell great. But no you can’t give it to a man. Girls like flowers. Why do girls like flowers? It’s romantic apparently. It has symbolic meaning. Roses, red flowers. They are delicate. Okay. SO maybe the attraction is to symbolism. Girls like that flowers can convey love, friendship, support and a lot of other feelings. Flowers are decorative. They are nature’s jewelery. 
For men they don’t hold much meaning. I’m talking generally of course. I’m sure there are men who love getting flowers and arranging them and looking at them. I call those men… nothing. I don’t know these men. Never met them. But in the spirit of inclusiveness, let’s say these men exist. For the rest of us. There is not much meaning to flowers. They are pointless. They die. Even if they smell nice, you have to be really fucking close to them to experience it. And you can’t do anything with them. You can’t even show them off like a antique piece.
Hey Roy, look at the red roses Shivani got me.
Yeah, did you see my new wireless headphones.
Yeah. But dude, the roses. Look at them. Don’t they smell nice.
I can’t smell anything dude. It has surround sound, noise cancellation.
Yeah nice. But look how pretty. 
And 20 hours battery life. And did I tell you they were wireless.
Yeah. You did.
Oh look your flowers died.

For me things should have some kind of utility. It has to solve a purpose. Any purpose. I understand the statues and the plates and the pretty wine bottles. They are decorative. They fill spaces. Isn’t it strange that we look to do better in life. We want to live in big apartments with lots of space. We want more space for ourselves. We don’t want to share it with too many people. Then we fill those spaces with things. Reducing the space that we had.
Anyway, I understand the point of decorative items. Not flowers. 

Men are okay with flowers during weddings and funerals. Flowers at weddings is something no one would argue. It’s one function. Flowers are there for one day. They’ll look pretty, smell nice and that’s it. Even then we don’t want to be involved with the details. Which flowers, what arrangement. Petunia is a breed of dog for all I care. Then you see, 50K,100K for flowers. How many flowers do we need. Are these golden flowers. DO they look pretty, smell nice AND sing songs. Are they preventing mosquitoes. WHat are they doing that is worth 1 lakh rupees. 
Funerals also I understand. Death. Flowers die. Kind of makes sense. Egyptians used to bury jewellery, ornaments, treasures of immense value. But at least they were still of value when these graves were dug. 


I was reading about flowers on wikipedia and it’s fascinating. Flowers are really interesting things. They are the reproductive part of the plant. As I’m sure you know already. Plants can have 100s of flowers depending on the species. Imagine that. What an incredibly amazing thing. Imagine having 100s of dicks on your body. Or 100 of Vaginas. How do you even satisfy yourself. You know. Which dick do you jerk off. You will need to have so many hands. Men think about sex all the time to satisfy one dick. Ruins our life. So many decisions made by our dick. Should I finish that presentation, or go to the party. I Imagine what a 100 would do.
And we give it to our girlfriends, put it up in weddings. WOuld you ever do that with a lion dick? Hhow about a dolphin vagina. Would you sew it together and put it around your neck if it was a peacock penis? Retractable horse penises in funerals. We would never do it. Coz it’s wrong. And it’s disgusting. Yet we do that with flowers. Strange.

Now Flowers reproduce by pollinating. Anther produces pollen which then has to reach the stamen. Now to do that a flower uses 3 tactics. Through insects and small birds. By putting it on them, so that when they touch the stamen, pollen gets deposited. They do it using the wind, where they just spray it all over the place.
