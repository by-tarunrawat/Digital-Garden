“Look at it” the parrot squawked.
“What is it this time.” said Mr Amaze Balls as he looked up from his scroll. “Oh no. What happened baby?” 
“You mean you know who this is?” the parrot said again. 
“Of course. This is my wife, Gum” Mr Amaze Balls pointed his open hand to the huge fish standing on its tail. Mrs Gum Balls was covered in a rose bush. It looked like she had dragged it for a long distance. “And this is my close adviser and friend, Mr The Beast.” He pointed to the parrot with his thumb. 
Mrs Gum balls tried to speak, but neither of them could understand. “What happened honey”


 “It’s okay honey. Mr Beast is here to help us with your curse.” He then turned his eyes to her tail and asked, “What happened honey. 
Mrs Gum Balls had tears in her eyes. Her tail was bloody. She tried to speak. She gurgled as if there was liquid stuck in her throat. Bubbles came out of her mouth in Mr Balls’ direction. One caught The Beast on his beak. “Don’t worry Mrs Balls, we found this ancient formula to make animals talk like humans.” The bubble was still sitting on top of The Beast’s beak. It made his head look bigger, split in the middle, with four eyes. Mrs Balls gave out a laugh.  More bubbles filled the room. A few more caught The Beast. He opened his wings and shook his tiny body. The bubble on his beak still remained.
Mrs Balls gurgled again looked down and wrapped herself in her gills. “Honey, drink this. This will help you talk.”
Mr Balls walked towards Mrs Balls with a glass filled with a black liquid. He helped her drink the medicine and took a step back. Both The Beast and Mr Amaze Balls looked at Mrs Gum Balls expectantly. She starts coughing. A little at first. Then violently. THe whole room fills with bubbles. Mr Amaze Balls tries to fight the bubbles with the scroll in his hand. The Beast tries to fly to the top of the room to escape them. The room is filled with the sound of coughs, wings fluttering, and an ocassional AARRRRGGHHH from Mr Amaze Balls.
Suddenly the coughing stopped. “I can speak” said a gurgly voice. It sounded like someone’s throat was filled with phlegm. The fluttering stopped.
“That’s great honey. Now tell us what happened.” Said Mr Amaze Balls, as he emerged victorious in his battle. 
“The sorcerer said that he will not take back his curse until I apologize to him.” There were no bubbles this time.
Mr Amaze Balls’ eyes were wide looking at Mrs Balls in anticipation. “Then what?” He said.
“Then… Nothing I came back here, some kids started teasing me on the way and pushed me into a garden. I fell on a rose bush and so…” She waves her gills at her tail. 
“Mrs Balls, I think you misunderstood Amaze. What he meant was, what happened after you apologized.” The beast was now sitting on top of a pile of books. His feathers dripping water all over the books.
“Apologized? Why would I apologize to that freak?”
Mr Amaze Balls and The Beast looked at each other in amazement.
“Because you called him a freak.” Said Mr Amaze Balls “To his face.”
“He called me fat.” Relied Mrs Balls.
“He suggested you swim to exercise.”
“That means fat.”
“Honey, we have scoured all the books that we could find. We’ve looked through all the manuscripts we could steal from library. We even checked on google. There is no other way to cure you. You have to apologize to the sorcerer.” Mr Amaze Balls stretched his hand to the door. He was ordering her now. Mrs Balls put her gills on her hips. Mr Balls was familiar with this look. He took a couple of steps back, “I mean, if you want.” 
“I didn’t marry you so you could make me submit in front of a freak,” gurgled Mrs Balls, “I will not apologize to that dirty little man.”
“Honey don’t you want your old body back.” Mr Balls was pleading with her. “You can’t be a fish all your life.”
“I can’t?” said Mrs Balls, “Who says?”
Mr Balls didn’t know what to say anymore. He looked at the parrot, “The Beast…” He was urging him to say something.
Mr Beast nodded at Mr Amaze Balls. He turned his head to Mrs Balls, “Forgive me Mrs Balls, but do you want to be a fish all your life?”
“Why not.  I have almost zero fat. My eyesight is great. I looove the colors,” Mrs Balls swirled on her tail. The rose bush was still there. “Owwwww”. She untagled some of the branches. “The talking thing was a little annoying but you guys figured it out. So, I love it.” Said Mrs Balls.
“But don’t you want to have a normal life?” said The Beast.
“What normal life?” asked Mrs Balls.
“You know normal things. Cooking, cleaning…” The Beast hesitated. He looked at Mr Amaze Balls who nodded him to go on. “…sex” he said.
“I don’t care really. I love my new fish body. So I’m going to keep it.
