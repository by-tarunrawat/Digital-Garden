Election khatam hue abhi. Maine vote nahi diya. I feel aap log mujhe judge kar rahe ho. Dekho mere saath around 36 percent logon ne bhi nahi kiya. Mai minority me hun. Mujhe samajh hi nahi aata kise vote karun. Bahut difficult choice hai. Har koi hamse jhooth bolta hai. Har koi. Tum kisi bhi neta ko utha ke uska purana record dekh lo. I guarantee usne junta ko jhoothe waade jhoothi tassaliyan di hongi. 

At this point hum vote karke ye decide kar rahe hain ki better jhooth kaun bolta hai.Ek bolega hum education best kar denge, dusra bolega hum development best kar denge. Hum soch rahe hain. Yaar education waala jhooth to bahut ho gaya. Ye development waala ekdum naya hai. Pata nahi jhooth hai bhi ki nahi? Real nikla to? Kahin kar hi na de development. CHalo decided. Is baar isko vote karte hain. 

5 saal ho jaate hain. Kuch hota nahi. Is beech aapko dono side jhooth bolti hain. Ek kehti hai kuch nahi kiya is sarkar ne. Sarkar bolti hai dekho ye road banayi to. Ye dekho ye statue banayi to. Aur tumhe lagta hai haan yaar. Meri road to nahi bani. Lekin chalo koi to bani hai. 
Fir election aa jaate hain. Sarkar fir junta ke saamne. Hum development karenge. Junta: Kahan sir. Ye waala jhooth to ho gaya. Koi aur sunao. Sarkar: *Thinking* okay okay… how about this. Musalmano se hame khatra hai. Junta: Arey sir, wo to ho gaya na. Kashimir gujarat maratha sab jagah. Sarkar: Umm.. Nahi nahi. Is baar poore desh ko khatra hai. Junta: Oo bete ki aisa kya. Sir ye waala mast hai. Chalo ote aapko diya.

TO matlab ye jhooth bol raha tha!! PAr last time to usko diya tha. Last time to wo jhooth bol raha tha. Oooohhh… Matlab sab hooth bolte hain. Oh my god. Ye to dimaag fat gaya mera.


But at this point I feel hum loog decide kar rahe hain, ki zyada acha jhooth kaun bolta hai. Dekho corrupt to sabhi hai. Kaam koi karta nahi. Bolte sab hai ki ham kuch to karenge. Lekin karta koi nahi. To is point pe hame ye to realize kar hi lena chahiye ki sab jhooth bol rahe hain. Itna time to ho gaya, ab to samajh jaana chahiye. Samajh me to aata hai na ki jhooth khya hota hai. Jab tum kehte kuch ho, lekin uska matlab kuch nahi reh jaata. Wo hame kuch bolte hain. AUr hum sun lete hain. Kisi pe vishwas karte hain. Kisi pe nahi. VIshwas kispe karte hain? Jiski baat samajh aaye. Jo lage ki ye karega. Ye sahi bol raha hai. Ye hi layega badlav. Fir kya. VOte dete hain. Fir bakchodi chaalu. Scam. Corruption. Unemlpoyment. Demonetization. 
Is point pe to realize ho hi jaana chahiye ki hamse jhooth bola ja raha hai. Fir bhi har baar uth ke ham vote dene chale jaate hain. Kisi na kisi ko vote deke aa jaate hain. Kyunki hame lagta hai ki iska jhooth best hai. Sabse convincing to yahi bola. Matlab bola to wo bhi ki Education pe paisa lagayega. Crime situation ko better karega. Lekin wo feel nahi aaya. Laga nahi ki wo sahi me karega.  Lekin ye banda jo bola. Wah. Health care pe paisa kharch karega. Wah. Employment layega. Muaah. Wah. YE sabse convincing tha. Mera vote isko.
