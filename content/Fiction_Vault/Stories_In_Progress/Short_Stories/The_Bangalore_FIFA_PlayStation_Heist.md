Date: 18-12-2018
Topic: When I awoke the next morning…

When I awoke the next morning, there was no one in the room. I looked around to see everything cleaned up. It was almost like before we had come into it. Almost. Except, I was there. 
I’m at Rob’s place? What the fuck am I still doing at Rob’s place. What happened? Where is Chris?
 
I tried to think about the previous night. We had wanted to come into this house for a couple of months now. Not to steal anything mind you. We’re not thieves. We just wanted to play Fifa on the dude’s playstation. Now I know you might think that it is a stupid reason to break into somebody’s house. But it wasn’t like we planned it. I should clarify first — by we I mean me and my pal Chris. 
Chris had tried to establish a friendship with the guy. We thought maybe we could be invited to play if we’re friends with him. Chris was not very good at these things though. But at least he was better than me. Unfortunately, we needed him to do better than he was doing. He kept bumping into the guy in the cigarette shop whenever we could spot him from our window. 
About a week ago, this guy, lets call him Rob, was walking back to his house. He had a lot of packets in his hands. Chris spotted him from the window and ran down. After initially refusing the help, Rob gave some of his stuff to Chris. Chris had already messaged me to be ready. He had been invited into the house for a smoke.
They talked about work, life and weather in general. Trying to make small talk — which Chris is horrible at. He kept bringing up the playstation — asking what kind of games he had. He hung around awkwardly for a few minutes after they had smoked. Rob told him he was going out of town for a couple of weeks.
Chris came home disappointed. He thought he would at least get to play one game. Anyway, we decided to forget about the situation now and pick it up when the dude returns.
We come back to last night now. We had gone for a party to a friend’s fiance’s place. To our surprise we found he had a playstation as well. We had to spend a few hours at this place and we were sure that we will get to play for a little bit. 
Excited about the prospect of playing Fifa, we drank and socialized. A couple of hours into the party, the fiancee switched on the console, and started playing Fifa with his roommate. I asked if I could play a game or something. The roommate was an asshole. He told us that he doesn’t like to share his controller with anybody. What will anyone do the controller? Kidnap it? Give it AIDS. Asshole. I turned to the fiancee. He was a little more sharing. A little not much. He said, “Sure man. If I get beaten You can take the controller.
I decided to talk to Sonam. She was one of the Fiance’s work colleague. I don’t know what we talked about, I just kept looking at the game going on. She was gorgeous. But I really wanted to play fifa. On any other day, I would’ve been way into this girl — not today.

We played board games, drinking games, pictionary. All the while my attention completely on Fifa. During pictionary, Sonam turned to me and said “You know if you’re really interested in doing something about that thing, you should give me your number. We’ll get in touch” 
“About what?” I looked at her blankly.
She smiled and said, “About the charity stupid. We were talking about it. You were really interested in doing something.”
I nodded my head. Still looking at Fifa. “Yeah. I mean. Sure.”
“Let’s do something. I’ll give you my number and you can call me.” 
“Sure. Sure.” I said.
“Or not. Even if you want to talk or… You know chill sometime.” 
I couldn’t believe my ears. I was so obsessed with Fifa that I didn’t notice that this girl was into me.
“Really?” I said like a person who couldn’t believe his luck. “That would be great.”
“My turn.” A random dude with funny glasses ran towards the console. My head turned towards him. There was this girl, that could be a model one day if she decided. Giving me her number. To talk and to chill. And all I could think was that it was my turn to play.
“Okay, here, take this.” She gave me a piece of paper. There was a number on it. I assume it was hers. SHe smiled at me.
“Thanks, I’ll definitely give you a call. Can you excuse me for a minute” I said as I stood up and went to the console. The new guy was a couple of goals down. I was excited as I would finally get to play.
As expected the new guy got beaten. I tool the controller from his hands, and started selecting my team.
“Dude it’s my turn” the fiance said, coming out of nowhere.
What the fuck is his problem. “But that guy just lost. And you lost before…”
“Dude I get the turn after whoever loses.” He replied.
“But…”
“You can play after me dude. For sure. I’ll give the controller to you. I promise.”

It was not my apartment. It was not my console. I had no choice but to give it back to him. I got up and stood behind the chairs.
The next hour I saw the Fiancee beat the roommate in every game they played. At one point I saw him take a team from the Korean league and still beat his Barcelona. Others at the party had also asked for a turn. But the stipulation was repeated to them. People had started getting frustrated at this point, and started to leave. I looked around to spot Sonam, but even she had left. Seeing no sign of either of these guys giving up, Chris and I decided to leave as well.  
I should tell you that we had been drinking for 3 hours now. Add about 3 joints to the equation, and we were not in our minds. Going home in the cab, both of us were commiserating each other on how fucked people are. Empathy, kindness and sharing were qualities of an extinct breed of men.
I can’t tell you what came over me, because I don’t do shit like I did a few minutes later. I told the driver to stop at Rob’s house. Chris thought we had reached back to our place. Rob’s apartment was on the ground floor. I thought it wouldn’t hurt anybody if I had a peak inside. It was just a harmless peak. Nothing more. 
I looked in the apartment, it was dark. I could see the outline of the television from the faint streetlight coming into the apartment. I cupped my hands and put them on my eyes to see better. The window was loose, it opened with just a little push. 
My heart was racing. I told myself, I shouldn’t do what I was about to do. But there was this other voice that kept telling me how unfair the world is. How people with playstations are assholes. They have their console with them all day everyday, yet they are unwilling to share it with anyone else. The argument did it’s job. We weren’t doing it to a good person. We were doing it to the Assholes of the world. I climbed in. 

Chris was standing right behind me waiting for me to carry him inside. On seeing me climbing up the window, he got confused. He looked around, his face becoming red. He asked me what the hell was I doing. Suddenly he was completely sober. We argued for less than a minute — after which he climbed in. It wasn’t as if my arguments convinced him, it was mainly the fear of leaving me alone breaking into somebody else’s apartment. He wasn’t going to leave his buddy. 
I told Chris that we will play a couple of games and then leave. And this is what I truly believed in the moment I said it. But it was before Chris beat me two games in a row. 
A couple of hours later, and after being satisfied with how the night had gone, we decided to leave. But I wanted water first. There was no cold water in the fridge, but it was filled with Hoegarden. I picked one, and started drinking. Chris looked at me. I picked up another one and brought it to Chris. We decided to play a couple more games.
It was 3AM. We were drunk out of our minds. We had gotten food delivered. Neat trick — we waited outside to receive the delivery. There were boxes all over the floor, food on the furniture, and it looked like Chris had puked in the corner. 
Chris’ eyes were closing. Even I felt incredibly sleepy. The last thing I remember I was trying to talk Chris into leaving before anyone sees us. And now this morning. Chris gone. Everything back in it’s place. Clean.
***************
The door was slightly ajar. I left through the window. I didn’t have my mobile on me. I ran back to my apartment. I wanted to ask Chris what happened. Chris sat at home chilling. He got up on seeing me.
“What happened dude?” He asked.
“What do you mean what happened? I just got up and left? You tell me what the fuck happened.”
“Dude there was a lady at the door. She had a key. I went out the window. There was no time to wake you up.”
“There was nobody inside when I got up. Did she call the police?”

After sharing all the information between us. The story we had was, both of us had passed out after drinking all the beer from the fridge. At some point in the night I had shifted to Rob’s bedroom and put Rob’s blanket on myself and drooled over Rob’s pillow.
In the morning. A bell woke Chris up. He panicked. He could see an older woman on the door. He came to the bedroom to wake me. He snatched the blanket from my body and gave me a couple of shakes. He would’ve have tried even harder but the woman had a key and she was about to open the door. He put the blanket over my body and left through the window. Something happened in an hour between then and when I woke up. And after that, you know the story already.
We had a lot of questions and a few answers of our own.
Who was that woman? Probably the maid. Could be his mother.
If it was either of those why didn’t she call the police? Maybe she saw the blanket over me and thought it was Rob.
Seems unlikely. But can happen. The blanket was probably on my face. I can’t really remember.

Chris wanted to let it go. A part of me did as well. 
“Where is my jacket?” I suddenly remembered. “Fuck. I think I left it at Rob’s place.”
“Forget it dude. It was a cheap jacket anyway.”
“But it had that girl’s number in it. What was her name…”
“Sonam” replied Chris.
“Yeah that’s it. I’ve got to get that number back.”
“Dude. It’s pure luck that we didn’t get arrested. We can’t go back now. What will we even say?”

I somehow convinced Chris to at least observe from a safe distance. We can see if there is any chance to get the jacket back. I changed my clothes and took Chris back to Rob’s apartment. 

We lurked outside. There was no police there. We went towards the window. There was a large parking area where we could observe from a distance. The older woman was sitting inside watching TV. 
“What’s your gameplan here?” asked Chris after 20 minutes. “You can’t stare at somebody’s window all day.”
He was right. I didn’t have a plan. I was hoping somebody would just come and tell us the whole story. 
Hey you know somebody broke into the house last night. Rob’s mother had come in the morning and thought it was Rob sleeping. Cleaned the house to find him gone. He called her and found out Rob is in another city.”
But nobody came. I was hoping for too much. 
“Chris. You know, our theory about this lady being Rob’s mom.” I finally said.
“Yeah.”
“Why wouldn’t his mom know he is out of town?”
“Because…” Chris seemed unsure “… maybe she thought he came home a week early. Maybe they don’t talk much. Could be anything man.”
“Does that sound like any moms you know?”
Chris struggled with it for a while, then said, “Yeah well. Still, we can’t stand here all day.”
Chris was right again. I had to either let it go or do something.
“Come with me.” I said. He obliged. We went to Rob’s door and asked him to knock. Chris didn’t think this was a good idea. But after explaining to him that there is no way she would recognize either of us he agreed. I told him what to say, when she opens the door.
“Hello.” The older lady answered the door. She looked like a mom. But not of a 25 year old guy. She looked close to 40, wearing jeans and T-shirt.
“Hello auntie. I’m Chris.” Chris said “Is Rob in?”
“He is not here right now.” She looked annoyed at something.
“When will he be back?”
“I don’t know.” We stared blankly at each other’s faces. It looked like there was nothing else Chris could ask her.
“Are you his mom?” I said, gathering some courage. 
She looked at us. It looked like she was thinking about the answer.
“No.” She finally said.
That wasn’t the answer I was looking for. If she said she was his mother, I would’ve left knowing that my theory was probably right. This woman didn’t look like she was his mom, but how can you really tell somebody’s age. 
She had had enough of us. She gave us a fake smile and was about to close the door.
“I’m sorry. I’m sure this is getting annoying for you. But who are you?”
She looked in no mood to answer me. She held the door half open and just stared at me annoyed.
“We’re Rob’s friends. We have never seen you around. So…” I was struggling to complete and she was in no hurry to help me out.
“Hasn’t Rob told you.” She said “I thought you were his friends.”
“Yeah. I mean… You know… We’re not that close. We’ve just hung around a couple of times” Chris said.
“Well call him ask.” 
We nodded our heads like idiots. Not knowing what to say or do. “You do have his number, right?” She said.
“No, we’ve just met around the place.” Chris finally said.
“Listen, as far as I’m concerned, you guys are strangers. And I’m not comfortable talking to you and revealing details about myself.” She tried closing the door again.
“Wait, wait” I remembered my jacket, “I left my Jacket here the other day. Can I at least take it back”
“Black, with a zipper?”
“Yes”
“Hooded”
“Yes”
“Yeah, I threw it out.”
“What? You threw out somebody else’s jacket. How can you do that? It’s not yours to throw.”
“Listen, whatever your name is, this is my apartment, and I will do as I please with the stuff inside.”
“Your apartment? I thought this was Rob’s place.”
“Not really. He was living with me…” she pause, took a deep breath and said “Living here for a couple of months.”
“He was? Isn’t he living with you… I mean living here now?”
“Not after the shit he and his friends pulled last night.”
Oh man, she thinks Rob did it. Should I tell her. Fuck no. She threw my jacket. What kind of person does that. I looked at Chris. He had a smirk on his face. I felt a little guilty.
She continued, “Listen, if you want your jacket, it’ll still be in the dumpster outside the building. If the garbage truck has not already taken it.”
Chris breathed a sigh of relief. Thanked her and pulled me out towards the dumpster.

************

“Dude why did you pull me from there?” I asked Chris on our way to the dumpster. 
“You got your jacket. Well sort of. Remains to be seen.” He replied.
“But we don’t even know who that woman was.”
Chris had a huge smile on his face. 
“What?” I asked, “Why are you so happy?”
“Dude, that was his Sugar Mama.” 
“Sugar Mama” 
“Dude,a sugar mama is a woman who…”
“I know what a sugar mama is. Couldn’t that be his landlady.”
Chris laughed at my suggestion, “Were you even there dude? She was his Sugar mama.”
Chris liked the conclusion he had reached. I didn’t care anymore. As we reached the dumpster we saw a brand new American Tourister bag right on top. It seemed strange so we pulled. I laid down a couple of bricks to stand on to look inside the dumpster. Chris stood outside, examining the bag, “Dude, it says ‘Property of Rob Abraham’.” I looked back, got down from the dumpster. It did belong to Rob. 
I don’t think either of us planned to leave it alone. Now, I don’t consider myself a particularly lucky person. And going by the day I was having up till now, I was not hoping for anything interesting. It opened right away. She hadn’t locked it. Why would she.
Chris opened the bag. “What the fuck.” I looked at Chris and mouthed those words. His eyes went so wide that I would’ve been scared if I hadn’t looked inside the bag. “Close it. Close it.” I told Chris, “let’s go home.”
****************
“Dude. This is so cool.” this was one of my work people — Sanjay, “When did you get it.”
“Today,” said Chris looking at me. I giggled. 
“Where did you get it from,” asked Sanjay again.
Chris and I looked at each other. Blank face. We’d been playing for the past hour and a half now. Our eyes had closed from staring at the screen for so long. But for that one moment both of us smiled and said “Assholes”
I decided to throw a party to end the day. Nothing fancy. Just a few of my friends from college. An ex girlfriend. A couple of work people. We had alcohol. Music. We had finger food delivered from a budget restaurant nearby. And the best of all — we had Fifa.
“Hey can I get a turn?” asked Sanjay.
“Uhmmmm, sorry man… I ummm… don’t share my controller with anyone.” Chris replied.
“Ummm… yeah sure man. If I get beaten, you can take the controller from me,” I told Sanjay as he looked at me expectantly, “Deal?”
“Sounds good.” Sanjay stood up and went to the kitchen to fix himself a drink.

“Lose to me in the last 10 minutes?” I whispered to Chris.
“Deal.”
