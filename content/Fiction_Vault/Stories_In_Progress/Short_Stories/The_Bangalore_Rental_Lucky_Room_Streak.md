Date: 31-12-2018
Topic: You're moving into a new house; write about the people or person who lived there before you.

I love this house. Especially the big rooms. I had been looking for an apartment for 3 months now. This house was worth the wait. The balconies are spacious. The bathrooms big enough. 
There were three girls living in this house before us. I got the house through them. I had been looking for a long time. While I was looking, one of my friends moved into this house. But she decided to move out in a month, because these girls weren’t ever here. 
These girls lived in bangkok now. They had been paying for this house for a while. Which doesn’t make sense. They must’ve been doing well it seems. TO be making the kind of money to pay rent in two places. And the rent here wasn’t less. It was 14K a month. Which leads me to believe that they were either being lazy to make the decision to move out, or they had enough money to believe they could continue paying for it and have an option of returning here. 
I don’t really remember there names. I was told their stories by Bhagya, the house maid. She had been working there for a few years. The girls were friends. Two of them or maybe all three were partners in an online business. Selling clothes and accessories in Thailand. They were doing well. One of their partners lived next door. He was always out. Working. I don’t want to presume that the girls gave him a shitty deal. I don’t know enough. But in this scenario, I would want to be the one living in Bangkok.
The house was kept in decent condition. But that seems to happen when you’re not living there, and a maid comes everyday to clean. There were a lot of things in the house that they had left behind. We sent most of the stuff next door. There was a dresser, beds, mattresses. I think there was a bean bag as well. 
I never met them. I had talked to one of them a few times on the phone. Discussing the moving in/out situation, and dealing with the landlord. 
The house had been rented out for a number of years now. Bhagya was the only constant. She had told me about the people living there. I don’t remember most of it. I should pay more attention to when people are telling me stuff. It’s interesting when I think it is. Bhagya told me that my room was lucky. The girl who lived there before me wanted to get married. And she did a few months after moving into the room. A boy who was living there got a better job. Similar things happened to other people in the room. I’m not too proud of having broken the streak. When I got out, I was unemployed, broke, and no idea what to do. 
Akansha, my friend, lived for a month there. I had been looking for houses with her. Then she found this one and ditched me. But I can’t fault her for that. It had been a while. Also, she redeemed herself by telling us about this house when she was moving out. She also helped me get the best room in the house. I’m grateful for that. I would have been a stupid little puppy about it.
