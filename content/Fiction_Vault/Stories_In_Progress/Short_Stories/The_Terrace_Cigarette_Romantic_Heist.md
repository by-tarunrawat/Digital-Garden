Date: 23-11-2018
Topic: It’s Saturday afternoon. You’re not at home

I had too much to drink last night. Couldn’t make myself call a cab and go home. I was so stoned. Dangerous combination. Alcohol, weed and good company. Listened to music till late last night. 
There is this time at a party. You’re drunk. Sleep seems like a good option so you want to go home. But there are people still there at the party. They’re having fun — you too. So you stay. There is a girl at the party. She is beautiful. Especially right now. Kissing her would be the best thing right now. But there are just so may people here. And I don’t know if she would want to kiss me back. So you just think of ways to find out. I sit close to her. See her reactions. Is she getting closer to me? Is she leaning her head on my shoulders? Is she touching my arm. Where does she look when I talk to her. She is looking right in my eyes. Maybe she wants it too. Not that looking into my eyes is any kind of indication. It’s just good manners. But I see something else in her eyes. Maybe she wants me to make a move. I can’t it’s too risky. I don’t want to be the sleazy guy. I don’t want to make her uncomfortable. BUt It’s more about me than her. I care about what she would think about me. 
I shouldn’t give a fuck. Right? Does it really matter? I can ask her. When everyone is gone. But so many people are still here. It doesn’t look like they want to go yet. What should I do? I go to the terrace. Alone. With a cigarette and another drink. Hoping that she will follow me. Give me company. That’ll be the perfect signal I was looking for. But even then. I can’t be sure. Should I ask her? Look for other signals? I should just ask her right? What is the romance in that. Hey Tanisha, I really feel like kissing you. That doesn’t sound so bad. Maybe I can make it a bit romantic. I can hold her hand, and ask. Maybe. 
With this plan in mind, I go to the roof. Drink in hand, cigarette on my lips and lighter in my pocket. I look at her. Trying to show her where I’m going. Not explicitly, just by eye contact. She is talking to someone. Someone asks me “Where are you going?” I show them the cigarette. THey nod their head.
There is a nice breeze on the roof. I keep my glass on the ledge. Light my cigarette. I’m thinking of things to talk about when she is here. I don’t want to stand awkwardly not having anything to say. I want her to think I’m interesting. 
I’ve smoked half the cigarette. I don’t think she’ll come. I’m staring at the entrance. Suddenly I hear footsteps coming up. I turn around. Don’t want her to think I was waiting for her. Be chill. “Dude, can I share that cigarette with you?” It’s Roy. “Sure.”
I come down 5 minutes later. Pick up my stuff, call a cab.
