I used to spend precious time in front of the mirror. Brushing my hair. Putting oil and cream. Setting it one way then changing it to something else. Changing it back again. I was obsessed about my hair. It was as if I knew I was going to lose them. Maybe my over attachment to them made them leave me. 
I used to think my friends had better hair. They had more of it or had a better style that I couldn’t do on my hair. I used to think of stealing their hair. It was silly. I thought maybe if magic existed. And I had the gift. I could just think about swapping our hair, and it would be done. So I would do exactly that. I would sit close to a friend whose hair I wanted. Look at their head. And utter these words in my head: “I wish I get your hair”
Obviously it didn’t happen. But if it did, imagine the surprise on their faces. They would know something strange is going on. But how would they ever know what. THey could even observe something strange about my new head. But what would they say? “You stole my hair”. How? How exactly did I steal them without you knowing. 
Have I ever wanted to steal something. I watch movies like Ocean’s eleven and the Italian job. They make stealing look so cool and glamorous. Stealing paintings and money. Doing it in style. 
I’ve thought about doing something like that. Not to do it in real life, of course. I was thinking from a story perspective. A heist movie, or a con job. How would I pull it off? I have to start with what I want to steal. I’m not interested in paintings. I don’t know anything about them. Same with antique pieces. I can probably make something about antiques. It can be about history. 
How about a story about a person who has the ability to travel in time. This person — let’s call this person X — goes back in time to different time periods. Steals objects like utensils, statues, ornaments. Hides them near the area. Goes back to their own time. Digs the stuff up and sells them. 
That’s an interesting idea. Why does he do it? For money. X is smart. He has to think about keeping his secret. So, he does it discreetly. He has millions of dollars, but he lives in a smallish 2 bed room house. He doesn’t need to steal anymore. But he keeps doing it.
How would the story go. Where is the conflict? What does he want? X lives alone. Has a few friends who hang out with him. They watch TV and play video games. 
Maybe he goes back and changes something that has an effect on the present time. I’ll have to do this on a story circle. That will give me some idea on how can the story move forward. 
What are the images I see. Mohan jo daaro. Akbar. Babar. Swords. 
So this person is going to change things in the present time. By stealing something. That loses it’s value because of that change. But that is not enough. He loses something important. SO he has to go back and change things. I’ll study the story circle again and try to put it through that process.
