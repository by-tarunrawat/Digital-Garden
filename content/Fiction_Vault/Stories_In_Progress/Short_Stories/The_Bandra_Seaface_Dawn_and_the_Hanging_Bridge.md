Date: 18-04-2019
Topic: You’re asleep. You’re not at home.

I can hear people talking from time to time. I can hear the cars racing away. Even though my eyes are closed. It is almost as if I can see the streetlight over my head. I see a man who look like me do strange things. Fly. Run around a cricket ground. Sing and dance. Have sex. But my eyes are closed. 
I try opening my eyes. But the eyelids are too heavy. How did they become so heavy suddenly. I open and close them thousands of times a day. And right now, it seems like a job for an Olympic lifter. I have awareness of my surroundings. I can hear the sea crash on the rocks. Somebody just jogged past me. Maybe it was two people. I could hear the footsteps. Rhythmic. Someone must be jogging. 
I am definitely asleep. Am I awake as well? In this moment, I am both. I force my body to sit. It is easier that opening my eyes. I sit like this for a while. Eyes closed. Hands resting on the side. Trying to hold my body in place. Focus on your breathing. Breathe in. Breathe out. Breathe in. Breath out. The see air hits the back of my head. Its cold. I’m sweating. I’m fully awake now. I can open my eyes.
There is a couple sitting not far from me. I can see their faces. They are kissing. Not like a peck on the lips. Intense, passionate kissing. One that I thought you’re supposed to do at home. Or in private. Where is their home. Don’t they want any privacy. Are they okay with people looking at them. The guy, who is facing me, sees me staring. They stop. The girl lays down. Keeps her head in the guy’s lap. I look away. 
It’s 5 o’clock. Still two hours to go. More people come jogging. I turn around. I want to look at the sea. The rocks below me are barely visible. I can see a bridge. It looks beautiful. Red, yellow, orange lights illuminating it. It looks like it is hanging by threads. Tied to a single nail somewhere in the sky. The couple start kissing again. There is still some sleep in my eyes. I’m dazed. My gaze gets stuck wherever I look. I can’t help it. The guy sees me again. This time they don’t care.
