Date: 09-12-2018
Topic: The first time I wore ______

It was late 2008. I had recently graduated and was waiting for my joining to come. I was about to join one of the biggest IT companies in India. It was my first job. I had graduated as a computer science engineer from a mid level engineering college. 
Early in college — first year in a four year course — I had started losing hair. I was quite conscious about it. I could comb my hair a certain way. I was putting lotions and creams in my hair to grow them back or at east hide the bald spots. In fact I wore a cap for a full semester in college. I would wear it everywhere. In class, going out with friends, even under the helmet while riding the bike. It was ridiculous. I was so insecure about it.
So, in the late 2008, after the college was done and I was waiting, I was losing more hair than I could afford. I had been flirting with the idea of shaving them all off. I thought that will make them grow quicker. Anyway, after thinking about them for a while, I decided to go through with it. I went to the barber and got them shaved. I was so nervous. Didn’t know how will I look.
Coming back from the barber, wind touching my head felt cold. I could see a few people staring. I brushed my hand on my scalp. It felt weird. It felt nice. My hair is gone. This is what I feared most and I had gone and gotten it done myself. I was smiling. I felt so free. No more creams, and oils. No more hiding the bald spot. I don’t have to fear anyone touching my hair. I was free — of all the insecurities. I don’t care if I look good or not. I don’t. This is how I’m going to be from now on. I don’t have to set up my life around my hair routine. Yes, I had a whole routine.
I came home, and my mother had a smile on her face. She thought I looked funny. I didn’t care. I sent a picture to Saurabh, he loved it. He came home to check it out. He laughed as well, but he liked it a lot. A few years later he game me one of the best compliments I’ve ever gotten. “If I looked half as good as you bald, I would do it right away”
