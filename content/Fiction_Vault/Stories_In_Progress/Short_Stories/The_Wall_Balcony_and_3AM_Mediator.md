Topic: Write about a balcony

The view wasn’t great. There was a building right on front of it. It was shaped like a wall. Just one big wall going all the way across my balcony. A view of people’s laundry being dried. Sometimes you would hear someone flushing down their deeds. I hated it. I would take an open field or a pool of garbage spread across a canal any day over this block of concrete to block my air.
Looking back at it, I wish there were stories I could tell about that balcony. I used to sit there sometimes. It wasn’t a soul cleanser, but it felt nice. Sometimes I would sit with Pooja. Listening to coke studio or Paolo Nutini. Sometimes I would play my guitar for her — even when she stopped paying attention to me; even when she was checking her Instagram feed; even when she was deep in her thoughts that may or may not have me in them — I was playing for her. I was trying to impress her. I still am. Everyday. 
I had some plants there. There was a couple of money plants that grew to a decent size. There was some kind of flower on the ledge that my roommate and I shared. I had tried growing weed in a big pot — no pun intended — a couple of times. 
The first time it grew like I hoped it would. It had buds like I thought new buds should be like. I screwed up during the flowering. The weed plant is supposed to get at least 12 hours of darkness. I kept it inside my room, thinking it would get at least 12 hours of darkness in the room for sure. I forgot it needed proper sunlight during the other 10-12 hours as well. And also, people in my house forgot to water it when I was away.
The second time the growth wasn’t as dense as the first time. But I had a bog strong plant. There were good buds after 3-4 months. For flowering, I had made a big plastic sheet out of shopping bags. I had doubled it up with a black garbage back to block all sunlight when the plant was under it. I would put it on everyday in the evening when the sun was about to go down. I would pull it up first thing in the morning — literally as I was waking up. Didn’t work. I don’t know what happened. I didn’t get any thick flowers that I could smoke. Maybe it was the seed. Maybe it was what I did. I didn’t get a chance to experiment more with it.
There was something interesting that happened one night. It’s about 3 AM. I’m trying to sleep - which means I had been watching youtube videos for at least an hour. I hear loud shouting noises. My first thought was, somebody’s television is too loud. But, immediately I realized it could be domestic violence. So I got up with a jerk, went to my balcony to listen.
A woman was crying and shouting at a man. It was in the other building. One that is shaped like a wall. It was loud. Anything said in the middle of the night echoed between our buildings anyway. But this was actual shouting. The woman had been suspicious of the man, who was her husband. She had been observing his interactions with one specific girl, over the past few months. She had seen his messages this time, and there were some things in there she didn’t approve of.
The husband got a few chances to tell his side as well. Initially he took an aggressive approach. Denying everything, and blaming the wife for even suspecting him. He was loud in this confident phase of the fight. As the story unfolded, and the wife put forth some solid arguments, he got quieter. Now he really had to think about his answers. 
I followed the fight till it’s end. It went on for a couple of hours at least. There was no resolution. The husband didn’t accept the allegations. The wife did not concede that there wasn’t anything wrong. They decided to call in a mediator. A girl friend of the guy. 
I still remember that night. I was sitting on the balcony the whole time. Listening in. I know it’s rude to eavesdrop. But it’s not like I had to make any special efforts. Even if I was in my room — on my bed, with a blanket over my head — I could hear it. Certain words weren’t clear, which is why I moved to the balcony, but it was booming. It was entertaining. I wasn’t harming anyone. My listening to them had absolutely no effect on their marital life.
