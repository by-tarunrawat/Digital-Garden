Date: 04-03-2019
Topic: At 5 in the afternoon. (after Frederico Garcia Lorca)

I remember. I heard a knock on the door. I was making tea for myself. My evening tea. It was a boy. 15-16 year old. He wanted to use the toilet. He seemed in a hurry. I let him in. He seemed non threatening. I thought I could handle him. 
He was in there a long time. He really had to go. I readied my list of questions for him: Who are you? All questions seemed to stop there. I wanted to know how he was in the building. He must’ve known someone here to have come in. If he knew someone, then, where is their toilet?
He came out. I just stared at him. Waiting for him to say something about his predicament. 
“Thank you uncle”
Uncle? I’m 33 years old damn it. Yeah. Uncle is probably fine. “No problem. What happened?”
“Nothing uncle. I really had to go to the loo.”
“No. I mean. Who are you?”
“My name is Preet uncle.” He stood there with his hands behind him, making imaginary lines on the floor with his shoes. 
“Where do you live Preet?” I tried hard not to look irritated. 
“C304.”
I should just let him go right? It is too much effort trying to get answers out of this kid. I don’t want to lose my Patience. TEA! I forgot the tea. 
“Okay Preet.” I said “See you around.”
I hurried towards the kitchen. Tea was fine. I cooked it more than I wanted to. But this was fine. Kadak Chai.
I hadn’t heard the door close. Preet was still at the corridor. He was talking to someone outside. 
He saw me and signaled outside. A girl, a little younger than Preet ran in. She had a gun in her hand. Which she pointed at me. 
The girl made me sit down on a chair. Gesturing with her gun. It wasn’t a real gun. It was one of the steel bb guns you get at balloon shooting stands at melas. It wasn’t going to kill me. But I could still hurt me. I didn’t want any extra holes in my body. She was wearing jeans and sweatshirt. White puma running shoes. 
She hadn’t said anything to me. She was just a young kid. I could definitely take her. I was scared. I must have thought about jumping on her at least 10 times. I was too scared to do it. She had this look in her eyes. DO NOT FUCK WITH ME.
Preet was busy running around, filling up his backpack with things. There wasn’t anything valuable in the house. TV was probably the most valuable thing at home. And they couldn’t really take the TV in their backpacks.
“What’s you name?” I asked the girl. She didn’t move. “What’s her name Preet?”
Preet stopped. A backpack in his hand and a grin on his face. “My name isn’t Preet.” And he burst out laughing. Mother Fucker. That annoying laughter when kids laugh at their own stupid jokes.
There was a hint of a smile on the girls face. 
“Can I have my tea at least.”
