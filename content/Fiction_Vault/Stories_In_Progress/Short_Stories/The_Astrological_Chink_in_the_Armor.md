Date: 21-03-2019
Topic: Write about a fortune teller.

I’ve often wondered if the people who claim to know the future, believe they really can see the future. Pundits in Hindu religion are one such people. But they don’t claim to know the future. You can’t make every pundit claim that they can see the future. One of them is going to tell the truth. They’ve done a really clever thing instead. They tell people they can tell your future by analyzing the position of the stars based on your birth. This way they don’t have to claim to have the magical power of foresight. And they can say it involves math and calculations. Which somehow justifies it in the mind of people who believe in it. Most people don’t even need that justification.
I’ve had my fortune told by many pundits. Most people have said I’d do really well in life. Which is subjective really. I was studying to be a software engineer. I lived a privileged life and had good prospects. And my mom would consider a steady job with regular promotion, ‘doing well in life’. Many of them have also said that I would travel to foreign countries. Travel is getting cheaper. More and more people travel to other countries. Most of my friends have traveled to Europe, Asia, America. I doubt that their pundits would say the same for them. It’s a generic thing to assume, that most people living a certain lifestyle will travel. However, I like to prove my fortune tellers wrong. I’ve never ever been outside the country. My friends, whose pundits never predicted a foreign trip, have been multiple times.
Last year my mom wanted to get a pooja done. She had invited a famous pandit to our house to conduct pooja and read our fortune. I am a cynic. So, I wanted to test this guy out. But I had to fly to Bangalore in a couple of days. My cousins and relatives urged me to stay back. They wanted to see the exchange between me and the pundit. THey thought that either he will convert me, or I will find a chink in his armor. Even I wanted to talk to him and try to debunk him. Unfortunately I had to go. Maybe I’ll get another chance. Maybe not. I doubt he would have converted me into a believer. I don’t know if I would have debunked him or not. I’ll make a prophecy right now. Someday, I’ll meet this pundit, and I’ll find out how he makes people believe in his prophecies.
