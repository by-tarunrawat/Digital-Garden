SHe is trying to not think about work. BUt that is what she always does. SHe thinks about work while smoking. SHe is now addicted. I also see her talkign to her boss. With her head bowed down. I see her trying to get away fro her boss. I see her trying to escape his glances and looks because she doens;t want to hear another lecture.
THe initial eperience is of frustration. Its stuffy. Everything is closing down. It's like you are in an elevator and there are people entering. First there is just one. THen another. And suddenly a family enters. Then another family. Your hands are stuck to your side. You are just trying to look at the elevator cieling because you want to breathe. YOu can't move. And another person enters. And you feel like you'll die of suffocation. You can't move your hands and legs and you're at the back of the elevator. Even if it opens it'll be difficult getting out. You want to get out. You feel like you'll die and the only thing that can save you is the elevator door opening. You've decided that you'll get out no matter which floor it opens on. But it never opens.
