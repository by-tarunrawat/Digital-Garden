He checked his watch once again. 10:34 it said. He looked through the scope again. It had been almost an hour now that an car had come on the road. This seemed unusual to him. It was the highway. And although it was far from the city, there was no shortage of travelers at night. He looked through his binoculars. He couldn’t see anything approaching for miles. The watch showed 10.37.
His hand went to his pocket and took out a pouch. It had pre-rolled joints inside. He sat back on the tree putting the tree putting the joint between his lips. 
“Hey Doc”
“Hi” said the voice from the other side of the call.
“Doc, I’ve been feeling that anxiety again” stressing on anxiety, air-quoting with one hand. “And I’m about to smoke a joint. Like you said.” He waited for a response from the other end. There was none. “I’m not sure if this is a good idea. It makes me slow.”
“That’s the idea Samir” the voice said. “It is supposed to calm you down. So that you don’t get… thoughts.”
Samir knew he didn’t have to smoke if he wanted. It was only advice, not an order. But he wanted to try whatever was necessary.
“Sure doc. If you…” He saw something from the corner of his eye. He picked up the binoculars. There was a car in a distance. 4 maybe 5 kms out. 
He got into position. 
“You there? Samir…” the voice said.
“Yeah doc. Sorry. I got distracted. I… uh… I’ll call you tomorrow.”
“Listen Samir, its okay if you don’t want to do it. We can talk about it if you want. I’m sure even that helps”
His brain was now calculating all the scenarios. His heart was racing. He had a smile on his lips. This is waht he was waiting an hour for. The car will take about 4 minutes to come in my range. The wind is about 5 kms per hour. His training was kicking in.
“No doc. Okay here I’m lighting it now.” He lit the joint and took a puff. He coughed as he took his first drag.
“Haha. You’ll get used to it. Don’t worry. We can still talk if you…”
“Doc. I’ll call you tomorrow.” He hung up.
He took another drag. This time it went down smoothly. He took another and another. 
He had his eyes on the target. The car was still 3 minutes away. His finger was on the trigger. He had already calculated all the variables. He knew the exact position the car needed to be when he pulled the trigger. Now he needed to clear his mind and focus on the task.
2 minutes.
Maruti zen. White number plate. Some poor middle class guy. Does he have a family? 
Wait. No. Clear your mind. Breathe.
In. Out. In. Out.
Why do I care if he has a family. I don’t know him. Or his family. Thousands of people die everyday. Should I care about everyone’s family.
Samir. Don’t do this. In. Out.
1 minute.
What if I miss. What if he escapes. From this distance it’ll be impossible to miss. I already know what I have to do. I’ve set everything up. All I need to do is pull the trigger at the right mom.. Damn it. Why can’t I clear my head. 
30 seconds
Should I aim for the wheels. I wonder what happens if I don’t kill him. Will I still feel the need? Do I feel the need right now? I don’t feel excited. Samir. You’ve come all this way. It’s just another random person. You can’t think about these things now. It’s too late. It’s too late. IT’S TOO LATE

He pulled the trigger. A loud noise filled the highway. The car swerved and hit a milestone.
Samir sat up on his back. Took a deep breath. “Oh fuck!”
