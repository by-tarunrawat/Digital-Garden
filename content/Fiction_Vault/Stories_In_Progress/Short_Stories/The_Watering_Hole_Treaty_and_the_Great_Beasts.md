Date: 12-06-2019
Topic: Just beyond the edge of woods

There is a stream. And in that stream animals gather to quench their thirsts. Deer and buffalo. Elephant and giraffe. Lions and wolves. They drink their water and leave. Some of them talk to each other. They tell each other of the dangers in the forest. How the lions try to kill some deer’s best friend. And how that has left the poor guy in trauma. He now hunts only on the inside of the herd. He doesn’t get to eat much there. Most of the grass grazed by the deers gathered before. 
Nobody talks to the lions. They talk to you. Except the elephants. Nobody knows what they talk about. From a distance it looks very official. Like officers from two separate department negotiating a deal. Elder animals say, it is impossible for us to guess what they talk about. They are beasts of power. And powerful beasts have a strange relationship. It comes from somewhere between fear and respect. Some birds say they’ve heard them talk about the great beast. But then again. You ccan’t believe the birds. They say anything. THe greatest beasts this forest has seen are the elephants.
All animals know the lions won’t attack near the stream. A deal had been reached hundreds of years ago etween all the animals in the eoods. Lake and the stream are safe zones. If an animal, such as the lion was to break the deal. He would not be allowed to drink water from the stream. All the animals will gang up on him and drive him away. THere had been an example of a family of hyneas killed by the elephants and the buffalos. It’s a flimsy deal. One that the these cornivores can break easily. THey can get their water from any part of the stream where there are fewer animals. It’s not guarded by animals everywhere on it’s path. Or they can drink during the night. When most animals keep away. But it becomes awfully inconvenient. Most predators didn’t have any shortage of food. So they didn’t mind this minor inconvenience.
