Date: 07-01-2019
Topic: Open the box

There is light. Blinding light. I can only look from my peripheral vision at first. As my eyes get used to the light I look inside. A sun is shining. Around it the there are planets rotating. I can’t see the earth at first. I spot a blue ball in the distance. I can see the path these planets are on. Such speed. It is so fast that I can see the trace they are leaving behind them. 
Earth whizzes past me. I close the box. I can’t believe my eyes. I open it again. A full moon, with the bottom covered with clouds. It’s like a paining. I close and open the box again. I see my reflection. IN golden water. Ripples run through my face. It looks like I’m putting my head in the water. My reflection lets a scream out. A huge turbulence in the water. Bubbles everywhere. I don’t know what this means. But it’s scary. I want to know more. But I’m too scared to look. I close the box again. 
I’m not sure what the box wants me to know. It is showing me different things. Space, beauty, me. I don’t know if it makes sense. Does it want to show me my place in the universe. How small I am. Why am I screaming then. Why am I drowning out my own screams. 
I open the box again. It’s empty. There is nothing in there. A minute ago, it had the entire solar system. It wasn’t an image. It was actually there. I could see it clearly. In a distance. The real thing. I pick the box up. There is a pattern carved on the box. A design, which does not mean anything to me. 
I found the box lying at home. I had never seen it before. It was just there. I kept a pen in it. It melted and disappeared into the bottom. I put a matchbox. Same thing happened. I slammed the box shut.
