If you really want to hear about it, there are three things you should know about me first. I have only killed two people in my life: One out of necessity and one accidentally. I am ‘the Michael Jordan’ of archery. And I love Mangoes. 

I used to be the lead archer in 5th Archery Battalion of Singhrana Pratapeshwar the third. I was like a normal village kid. My dad used to work for the king, So, we were rich. My father was a finance minister in the king’s court. Although that was not his choice of career. He wanted to be a swordsman or an archer. He wanted to go to battle and return a hero. But my father, as it turned out, was a coward. This of course, was a relief to my grandfather, who wanted his son to be in a safe job. He had been teaching him business and accounts. Pitching projects to the ministers, Lords and Kings. Unfortunately for my father, he was smart. His intelligence meant that he would now have to take over the family business. His dreams stored away in darkness only to be thrust upon his son.

I was neither coward nor smart. Getting into fights all the time, with people much larger than me. My father saw potential in me and put me in Military school. I was not asked and I didn’t mind. And to be honest as a 12 year old I didn’t really know what I was getting into. 
The entire course was a 6 year accelerated leadership program. With boarding, lodging and food included in the course fee. Of course they would make us do most of the work maintaining the school premises. Bhartiya Uch shiksha Acharya Prathmeshwar Gurukul - or BUSAPG for short. Everybody called it BUSA-Pa-Ga for some reason. 
Acharya Prathmesh was some guy who started this gurukul generations ago, when it was A-Pa-Ga. A couple of generations here and there someone had the idea to offer the course for anyone who had money and it became USA-Pa-Ga. Another couple of generations they expanded into other kingdoms offering their course. It needed to have a more widespread appeal. This is the current BUSA-Pa-Ga.

I was an average student. Always showing potential in my teachers eyes, but never quite reaching them. I somehow graduated my 6 years with average marks and started applying for jobs in various battalions in the kingdom. But owing to a prolonged peace in the kingdom, the attrition rate in the armies was almost zero. I had applied to all the major warlords and lieutenants. I had applied to the Lords, but let’s face it, its nothing but a glorified bodyguard job. I was secretly hoping not to get those. Afters months of interviews and applications I decided to take up an internship with a freelance hunter in hopes of learning on the job and honing my skills.

Mahaveer Mrityunjay Lavya was a lazy man. But when Lords and rich people wanted to go hunting without having to deal with their inadequate skills, he was the one they came to. Mahaveer Mrityunjay Lavya once told me that he was from Eklavya’s bloodline. “Ek” he said, “Was the first Lavya. There have been others after him. Dwitiya Lavya, Tritiya lavya”. My father thought it was a marketing stunt. “He is a smart man. If I was in the archery business, I wouldn’t have found a better name to associate with it.” I didn;t care if it was a marketing gimmick, as I saw it, he was the best archer in the Kingdom. Maybe in the top 5 but certainly in the top 10. It was a great chance to learn from a master.
