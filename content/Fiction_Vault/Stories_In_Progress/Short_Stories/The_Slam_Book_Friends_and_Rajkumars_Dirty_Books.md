Topic: "So, these were my friends . . ." (after John Balaban)

There was a boy, Abhinav, in my class in the boarding school. He was Nepali; from Kathmandu. I was into sports — cricket, football, basketball. So, most of the friends I made in school were kids who played some sports. Abhinav didn’t. We still became friends. He was a quite, shy guy. Got along with everybody. We became friends because even I was quite and shy in those days. I think I still am. 
We used to hang out a lot. All the kids around her were boisterous and loud. There was an abuse in every sentence they spoke. All except me and Abhinav. We sat in class together. Used to talk about music and films. We teamed up for class quizzes. We made cards together for people on their birthdays and other festivals. We used to make slam books for other people. It was easy. I don’t remember doing much else with him. He used to be my best friend back then. 
There was another kid called Rajkumar, who was infamous for being a bad boy. He used to bring dirty books in our class and read them. Abhinav and I were innocent. Horrified by the sexual nature of the stories, we used to put out fingers in our ears and try to drown out the narration. It was funny, now that I think about it. I think the stories were to get a reaction out of us, rather than do everybody else a service. 
I lost touch with him when I left GRD. Back then there weren’t many ways to keep in touch. You can exchange phone numbers. But that didn’t occur to either one of us. I don’t know why. We did like each other. We would have been friends given any other environment. I would’ve like to still be friends with him. 
I got in touch with him a few years ago on Facebook. Added him as a friend. But we did’t chat much. I didn’t know what to talk to him about. He probably felt the same. Our personalities got in the way. I was never used to making small talks and catching up. He was like me so I’ll assume it was the same for him. It had been so long that we are strangers to each other now. I’ve definitely changed a lot. He must have as well. 
I went to another school after I left GRD. I lived at home now. Going to a new school, and meeting new people was nerve wracking. I sat next to this kid Deepank. He was a strange kid. But all the kids were strange. We became friends. He had a twin brother who was a bit more sensible. 
Deepank was a romantic. I don’t know how to talk about him. He liked a girl but didn’t know what the protocol was to convey those feelings. So he used to get her gifts. Say nice things. THe girl knew. Another kid told her. She was surprised by that. Which is strange because you have to be either stupid to not notice or lying. 
I’m not in touch with him anymore. BUt he is still in my life in a way. My friends and I make fun of the things he posts on his facebook page. It’s overtly romantic shit, cheesy to be exact. It’s a source of amusement for us. And an ice breaker when conversations stop in our chat group. I wonder what would he say if he knew his role in our lives. 
I feel I should talk to him sometime. I feel I should talk to Abhinav as well. I would like to know how both these people turned out. Who are they now? I hope someday I will. Why is it hard to reconnect with people. Is it like that only for me? I hope so. It would be a shame if people don’t. There are so many things that are taking us away from each other. From people we like. Or liked at some point in our lives. Shame shouldn’t be another reason.
