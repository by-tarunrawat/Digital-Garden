They shone in the sky, my father and his father before him.
They were poets but there was poetry written on them.
Absolute legends in their field. 
They expected me to write as well, and in my age become a poet of the big screen.

But my mind is in colors, violet, orange and magenta,
I love how they come together in a sunset, Its as if god is a painter.
I want to mix them too, there is a lot I can create,
I want to become god with a few colors on my plate.

I can see the disappointment in my mother’s eyes,
My fathers eyes too if he were here, 
uneducated, idiot he would have said. Writing is an intellectual art. 
I wanted you to be praised. Paint beautiful pictures with a phrase.
Here you are doing a laborer’s job. 
Painting? What are you going to do paint sign boards?
If he were here.

But I can see it in my mother’s eyes. 
It’s as if my dad is taking through them.
I’ll show them on the canvas. I can show them what I can do.
What I want to do. What I’m meant to do.
What I love. What I’ll give my life for.
If they can’t see that, then that is on them.
I can’t live on their whim.

Now what do I do, what do I put on this canvas.
What do I make that makes people notice.
A portrait? How about the sunset? An object? I can’t decide.
Everyone makes these things. What will separate me from them.
What will make me famous? What will make me rich?
I can do commissions. You know till I figure stuff out. It’ll only be on the side.

I’m not selling out. It’s what everyone has to do.
It’s a journey I’m taking. I’ll do it for a few bucks for the time.
I’ll do it for myself when I know what I want to create.
Violet, orange and magenta, I want to conjure my own sunset.
Or maybe worlds that no one has ever seen. I want to be the god of my own canvas.
Can’t wait!
