The air was colder. People were looking at me. Turning heads even. I had shaved my head. Have they never seen a bald man before? Is there something on my face. I had checked my newly shaved head in the mirror back at the barbershop. It looked okay. I mean, I wasn’t used to the face yet. It would take a few days still.
I had picked an ordinary head for the shave. I didn’t want to focus on hairstyle, so I went with one without that problem. Hair takes a lot of maintenance. All my previous heads had hair on them. I’ve had four heads in 25 years. My first was the one I came with. From god’s factory. 
My second head was a few days after I turned 14. I had wanted a new head for months now. I had gotten a personal volcano on my forehead. A pimple. I had scratched it and the scar remained. I looked like Harry potter, one of the villains from the 20th and 21st century legends of Lord Voldermort. The teasing TastySchlong_21 and his gang gave me was ridiculous. Mom was against the idea. She said she couldn’t afford it. She had some money saved to get a car. I had called the Children right protection brigade. I got my head in a matter of a few hours. She was still on her first head and didn’t understand what I was going through. Maybe that’s why she looked so - ugly. With her eyes looking in different directions and her oddly shaped head. She said we should be grateful for the body god gave us. She was on her second hand though and both her eyes were new. What did she know.

“What’s the problem… sir” the barber wasn’t sure why I was still standing outside his shop. I wasn’t sure why I was standing there. There were eyes on me. They couldn’t have recognized me though. I showed him the CR code on my arm. He looked at it with his eyes. A small lazer beam recognized the code. “Thanks for your business YourFriend56 sir”. “Don’t call me that,” I shouted. The customers in the shop turned to look at me. “I’m sorry sir. That is your name sir. Isn’t it,” the barber had that ‘what can you do about it’ smile on his face. I hated that. It seemed like everyone had that smile nowadays. Fuck that shit. 

I left without answering. Eighty five thousand rupees at a rundown barbershop. What has the world come to. My dad used to pay only twenty thousand when I was a child. Till he died. Loser. Who dies in this day and age! Loser. That’s what you get for not saving yourself on the cloud.
