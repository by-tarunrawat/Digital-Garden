EXT. LIBRARY. - DAY

Vartika(F,28) is browsing the shelf looking at books. She takes out a book and leafs through it. Diya(F,28) comes from behind and taps her on the shoulder.
      DIYA
(Whispering)
Did you find it?
Vartika closes the book and keeps it back
VARTIKA
(whispering)
Not sure we'll find anything here. (Drop the first word)
Vartika continues to look, going up and down the shelf.
DIYA
Why don't we just ask the librarian?
VARTIKA
(mumbling to herself, reading book titles)
Time dilation, Through the wormhole, temporal paradox...
DIYA
This is taking too much time
Vartika stops. 
VARTIKA
(Points to the librarian with her eyes)
Don't want him to know what we're looking for... Or anyone else.
 (Delayed Answer)
Vartika gets back to the books. Diya is still staring at the librarian.
DIYA
My stepdad is a librarian. Broke his foot once. In the Library. Can you imagine? Had to roll around in a wheelchair for a...(pauses) (Tangent)
Diya remembers something and puts her hand on her forehead.
VARTIKA
What?
DIYA
(Loud)Stephen Hawking... (Yelling) He's alive right now...
LIBRARIAN (O.S.)
(Yelling)
SILLENNNCCEEEE. (External Interruption)
DIYA
(whispering)
... and he lives here at Cambridge.
Vartika stops browsing, turns around to face Diya.
VARTIKA
Date today? (Sentence Fragment)
Diya hurriedly looks for something in her bag. She takes out a device that looks like a bracelet. It has multiple dials. She looks at one of the dials and presses a button.
DIYA
13th March.. 
Vartika has a realization.
VARTIKA
Quick. Grab your stuff. We need to hurry.
DIYA
What? Why?
VARTIKA
His health is... We only have... 
(Takes a deep breath)
He dies tomorrow. (Starts again)
Diya raises her eyebrows in shock. They run out of the library as fast as they can.
