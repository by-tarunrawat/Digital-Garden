Somebody comes home. It’s the anniversary of the day I passed. My mom welcomes them. Makes tea for them. They ask her how she is doing. She says it still doesn’t believe it happened a year ago. She feels guilty for living when her husband and elder son have both died. I wish she didn’t feel that way. These things happen. As much as we like to believe, we don’t have any control. SShe cries, there are tears rolling down her cheeks. It’s been a year now. She is even more paranoid now. She calls Mannu and Vartika everyday. If they don’t pickup the phone, she gets scared. Starts calling all of their friends. She sometimes has suicidal thoughts. But she doesn’t have the conviction. It’s the guilt that makes her think it.
Pooja has moved on. She remembers me fondly. She doesn’t think she’ll ever stop loving me. She thought we’d get married and have a family. Travel the world. Be crazy in love with each other. All those expectations came crashing down. She started dating recently. Went out with a couple of boys. Both setup by someone else. One of her best friends set her up with a guy from her office. He was a nice guy, good job, well traveled and smart. She went out with him a couple of times. She couldn’t bring herself to go out with him again. It was too hard. It had been six months. Today she was going out with Hareesh. Her mother had set her up with him. She had already gone out with him a few times. SHe liked the guy. He was funny and caring. He was sweet. He liked going out with friends to parties, meeting new people. He was social. Complete opposite of me. She couldn’t help but compare him to me. 
That night she had gone out to a nice restaurant. There was a nervousness in her demeanor tonight. It had been a couple of months that she had been going out with this guy. Her mother was keen on knowing whether she wanted to marry the guy or not. Her nervousness was for a different reason. She knew that there was an expectation of physical intimacy. Her friends had told her that she needed to do it. It had been too long. A year is a long time to have not had sex. She decided to go for it. 
At the end of the night, he had come to her apartment to hang out. It was a normal occurrence. He had come to Pooja’s apartment a bunch of times and gone home disappointed. Tonight seemed a little different to him though. She felt relaxed and open to his touches. She made him a drink and was smiling at him throughout the night. They put on a movie on the TV. He snuck in close to her and put his arm around her — making sure he wasn’t touching her. Just kept it on the sofa gently. Pooja could see what he was upto. SHe didn’t mind. She put her head on his shoulder. He put his hand on her arm. She looked at him. They both wanted it. He kissed her. Gently at first. Then hurridely they moved to her room. They made love. He thought this was the best way he could do it. She would be really happy to see him get married. He was planning to propose. He wanted to get married and have children as soon as possible. He had picked up a ring last week and was planning something special tonight. He had cooked baked chicken and bread. He had ordered a chocolate mousse for dessert. He had decorated the balcony, where they were having dinner. Somewhere in the middle of dinner he would get down on one knee and propose.
