Father name: Dhananjaya Roy/Panda


Who is the protagonist?
Parikshit Roy is a 32 year old man. He grew up in a middle class family

What is the situation? What is the hero’s personal condition at the beginning? How will that condition be changed, for better or worse, by the hero himself or by the antagonistic force?
What is the protagonist’s objective? At the beginning, what does the hero want? What moral (or immoral) choices will she have to make in her attempt to gain that objective?
Who is the opponent? Who or what stands in the way of the hero achieving his objective?
What will be the disaster? What misfortune will befall the hero as the result of her attempts to achieve her objective?
What’s the conflict? What conflict will result from the hero’s reaction to the disaster? And what is the logical flow of cause and effect that will allow this conflict to continue throughout the story?

A child is sitting in an full auditorium. He looks excited. The principal announces an award for Parikshit. Parikshit gets up and walks towards the stage. The elder man is his grandfather. He happily applauds. The principal compliments Parikshit saying how well he has done and he expects him to do great things in his life. 

Parikshit wakes up as we hear a loud banging on the door. He is afraid as he gets up and gathers his clothes. He sees a half smoked joint. He lights it and smokes while preparing to leave. The banging gets progressively louder. Parikshit picks up a bag and shoves clothes in it as he shouts, “Aa raha hun. Nanga hun. Kapde pehen raha hun” 
The banging turns into someone trying to break the door. Parikshit picks up his wallet and his phone. He goes over the balcony railings. He looks down. It’s the 4rth floor. He looks up and tries to get down on the downstairs balcony. He has forgotten something important. He comes back up. Looks at the door. It’s almost come off. He struggles with the decision whether to go in or not. HE finally goes in. He runs towards the bed and finds a bag of Weed under it. He picks it up as the door breaks. HE leaps towards the balcony. With a joint on his lips, he smiles at the two shadows barging in and with one hand salutes goodbye. But his hand slips and he falls. 
Two women barge in. Rambo is in a tshirt and jeans and Rocky in a saari. THey are disappointed as they look over the balcony. PArikshit is hanging on the ledge. The woman in the saaree offers to help. THe other woman tells her to let him fall. They’ve come here to beat him up anyway. They were told to break a few bones so, if he falls, he will break a few bones. The other one thinks that he may die from this height. THey disagree on whether Parikshit will die falling from this height. Eventually, they come to a conclusion that they can’t take a chance and pull him up with a saaree pallu. As soon as they pull him back up. THey start beating him. 
WE find out that Parikshit owes 20 lakh rupees to their boss “Bhaiya ji”. Parikshit argues that he owed money to Didi. Who is this new Bhaiya ji? Rocky slaps him and tells him that Didi is now Bhaiya ji. (Didi janam to aurat hui thi. PAr unko sareer me, mard jaisa lagta tha. Agar unko lagta tha to hum kaun hote hain rokne waale)
PArikshit is told to fork out the money or they will break his bones. They discuss which bone to break. Parikshit tells them that he has the money. He just needs a little more time to get it. He will pay them 30 instead of 20 if they give him another week. Rambo calls Bhaiya ji and tells him the deal. He tells them that his grandfather died a couple of weeks ago. And since his tehraveen has happened, his lawyer will read his will. His grandfather is rich and would have left him a lot of money. HE can pay them 30 lakh instead of 20. 
Bhaiya ji agrees, but he insists that ROcky and Rambo should accompany him. Rocky is disappointed as she will have to explain all this to her saas. She beats Parikshit a little more as he has put her in this mess. 


Parikshit reaches his hometown, Rishikesh, with Rocky and Rambo. He goes to his grandfather’s massive house. There are a few relatives still there. They ask why he didn’t come for his grandfather’s funeral. We find out that Parikshit didn’t come to see his grandfather before or after he died. 
In flashbacks we see that Parikshit and his grandfather Dhananjay had a fight. Parikshit desperately asks his grandfather to help him. Dhananjay refuses and sends him away. 
THere are pictures of Dhananjay and Parikshit on the walls around the house. They are mostly when he was a kid in school or college. THere are trophies sitting in a display with PArikshit’s name on it. Dhananjay loved him. But it looks like they had a falling out. Parikshit hadn’t talked to his grandfather in months. 

The will is being read. Rocky and Rambo are sitting next to Parikshit. They are excited. A painting willed to a duur ki bua. A sculpture willed to a duur ke chachaji. PArikshit is given an old car. His grandfather’s first car. Now the estate worth 20 crores and the rest of the wealth of 50 crores is given to Parikshit. Rockya nd RAmbo are impressed. But, the lawyer adds, Parikshit will have to get something from a village called Padmaview. Parikshit is disappointed, but Rocky and Rambo cheer him up and then threaten him to convince him to go to the village. 

The village is not on any map or anything. They have to ask people, and ride around. Hours become days, days become weeks. Rambo calls Bhaiya ji, who tells them to bring him back. They will sell his car and break his bones and make him beg on the streets till he recovers the money. Right then they find the village. Padma view. 

It’s a lonely road as they drive towards the village. There is no one on the way. They reach a pllace where a man stops them. The man takes them to a small hut and interviews them. Asking them questions like - why do you want to go? Who are you? What is your background etc. At the end of it he rejects them and asks them to leave. Not everyone can enter Padma view. 
Rambo points a gun at the man. He has to let them enter. 
As they enter, another man tries to stop them but they go past him. Some of the villagers have gathered and scold them for coming to their village. It was so irresponsible of them. Parikshit tells them that they have come here for something very important and they won’t leave without taking it. 

****
Parikshit finds Dhananjay’s house. He looks around, but can’t find anything. The house is organized. All his grandfather’s things are in place. Rocky and Rambo help him, but they can’t find the missing artifact. Parikshit wants to give up and go back. Rocky and Rambo stop him and tell him that they aren’t going anywhere without getting the artifact. They decide to spend the night there. 
Rocky tries to call Bhaiyaji, but she isn’t getting any signals. 
Parikshit can’t sleep.
