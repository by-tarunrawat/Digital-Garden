3 act structure
ACT 1: SETUP
Sequence 1 – Introduce Main Character/Status Quo
Parikshit is out of job and broke. He doesn’t even have money to pay his rent. He has gotten into gambling and owes a lot of money to some bad people. These people have repeatedly threatened him. He always tries to get away from them.
Two of the creditor’s goons, Rocky and Rambo, catch hold of him. Rocky and Rambo are two ferocious women, who are dangerous and feared by all. They take him to their boss, Bhaiya ji, a transgender man. Bhaiya ji tells them to beat him up and break his bones so that he can be put at some traffic signal to beg and pay Bhaiya ji back. 
Parikshit is scared and reveals that his grandfather died recently and he will inherit all his money and estate. He can pay Bhiaya ji back very soon. Bhiaya ji doesn’t believe him but Parikshit promises that if he lets him go to his hometown, he will pay him double the amount he owes. Bhaiya ji agrees and tells him that he will take 3 times the money. Parikshit has no choice. He accepts. Bhaiya ji has another condition. He tells Parikshit that he will send Rocky and Rambo with him so he doesn’t run away. 

Plot Point #1: Inciting Incident/Point of Attack
Parikshit goes back to Shimla for his grandfather’s tehrveen. Everyone is surprised to see him. They ask him why he didn’t come to his grandfather’s funeral. He was supposed to light his pyre. Parikshit gives some lame excuse. 
During the will reading, Parkshit finds out that his grandfather, Dhananjay, left him everything, but he has to get a family heirloom from a village deep in the mountains called Chakra. 
Parikshit refuses to dance to his grandfather’s tunes. As a child he had to do things as his grandfather told him. Live his life the way he wanted. Now even after his death, he is making his do his bidding. Rocky and Rambo threaten him to do it, otherwise they will be forced to kill him. Parikshit has to go with them. 

Sequence 2 – Set Predicament/Establish Main Tension
THe problem is, they can’t find the place. It’s not on any maps. They have some hazy directions from his grandfather. They try to find the place but can’t. Parikshit wants to give up but Rocky and Rambo push him to find the place. 

Plot Point #2: The Lock In 
THey finally find the place. They try to find the heirloom, but can’t. They look everywhere. The people in the village are behaving strangely. They ask all these weird questions about what’s happening in the world. Current prime minister, current affairs etc. They tell the villagers that they will leave the village as soon as they find what they came looking for. THe villagers laugh at them and behave strangely. Telling them, they can’t leave. Parikshit is frustrated and tells Rocky and Rambo that he wants to go back. He can’t find the heirloom. It’s not here. 
Rocky and Rambo had been given instructions by Bhaiya ji to kill Parikshit if he tries to run. Rambo shoots him. 
But he wakes up next day with a shock. He doesn’t understand what happened. THe village head explains to him that this village is cursed. Anyone who enters it cannot go out and time resets every night. SO, even though he was killed, he came back because the day reset to the previous day. 

ACT 2: OBSTACLES
Sequence 3 – First Obstacle/Raise the Stakes 
Parikshit doesn’t believe any of it. He is in shock and wants to leave as soon as possible. Even Rocky and Rambo agree with him. They want to leave as well. Their car is broken so they decide to walk out of the village. THey walk and walk for hours, until they see another village. THis one looks similar to the one they left. AS they approach the villagers, they realize it’s the same place. 
PArikshit freaks out and runs out again. But he comes back to the same place. The villagers tell him that it’s like this only in this village. Everyone here is trapped. They can enter, but they can never leave. 
Rocky, Rambo and Parikshit try various ways to get out of the place, but to no avail. Rocky and Rambo finally give up and tell PArikshit to give up too. 
Parikshit also gives up and starts living the village life. He makes friends with the villagers finding out about their life. One of the villagers becomes his friend and helps him deal with the fact that he is stuck here. Many many months, maybe years, pass while he is still there. No one ages. Rocky and Rambo have fun with the villagers. Bullying them, killing them with no consequence. ON small irritations like, didn’t like the sound of someone’s voice, someone asks too many questions. They know that these villagers will come back later. So they don’t care. 

Sequence 4 – Higher Obstacle
Parikshit finds out how the villagers live here. He gets to know their stories. He finds out that they were all brought by someone, who somehow left the place. There are people who have gotten used to this world. They don’t remember what it was like in the real world. Some people desperately want to get out. THey have children, wives, husbands, family and friends that they need to get to. Even thought they know it’s been several years since they were stuck in this place. Some are depressed. THey don’t do anything and sit around moping. Have no hope things will ever change. Then there are some people who don’t want to get out. They think that god has sent them here. But even among them, there is no consensus. Some think this is swarg, others think this is hell.

They want to know if he has mentioned any way in which they could leave too. Parikshit tells them that he has nothing left in the outside world. He was a loser there. People like these two were after his life. Nobody valued him. Here at least there are people who like him. RAmbo threatens him, but he just tells them they are free to kill him. He’ll just come back the next day.

Parikshit begs her not to kill him. Even though he wakes up from his death everyday, the act of dying is extremely painful. RAmbo tells him to take her deal and find a way through. She will make sure that Bhaiyaji doesn’t kill him. Parikshit agrees, in return, RAmbo will not kill him either in the village or outside. Rambo agrees as well.
