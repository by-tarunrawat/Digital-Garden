1. Rajkumar and Nawaz are feuding on social media. Their followers enjoy seeing them fight. It’s like a joke for them. But in real life they are friends. 

2. Fe find out that they have been in the city for 11 months now. Their parents had given them 12 months to prove themselves as influencers and become famous. But now only a month is left and now they haven’t started making even a penny yet. 

3. They find out about this influencer marketing agency that hires influencers to endorse products and brands and pays them a monthlyh fee. They go to apply, but find out that they don’t have enough followers. The interviwer tells them taht for now they can endorse their brands for free, but as soon as they reach 50K followers, they will start getting paid. 

4. This is like a ray of hope for both of them. They start getting other influencers involved in their feud as well. CAlling out and abusing popular accounts with lakhs of followers. Unexpectedly, this works. Their followers start increasing. THeir follower count reaches 48k and 49k each in just a week. THey are sure that they will have 50K followers by the end of the month. 

5. A meme account, @JamesBong with millions of followers exposes them. This person always wears a sharon stone mask. THey had feuded with the account calling him a bot account and getting fake followers. In retaliation @JamesBong makes a compilation of all their videos, showing their fakeness and finally a short video of them hugging and hanging out with each other. 

6. Their followers are angry as they feel cheated. People start unfollowing them. Their follower count suddenly goes from alost 50K to 20K and is decreasing every hour. Their dreams shattered. They want revenge. They drink together and decide to confront @JamesBong. They find out that she is doing an event live from a location. 

7. In their drunken state they follow her and go to the location. After a lot of trouble they finally meet @Sharonstoner and confront her. They take the mask off and find out that @JamesBong is a girl Vidya Sengupta. 

8. As they are talking. A couple of goons kidnap them and take them away. They were after Vidya as she was trying to expose something sinister. 

9. With the help of RAjkumar and Nawaz, Vidya manages to run away with them. She doesn’t know who they were but now all three of them are in trouble. 

10. They go to Vidya’s friend, who is the leader of the indian Wing of the hacker group @Incognito. These guys wear joker masks and expose corporations and politicians.
