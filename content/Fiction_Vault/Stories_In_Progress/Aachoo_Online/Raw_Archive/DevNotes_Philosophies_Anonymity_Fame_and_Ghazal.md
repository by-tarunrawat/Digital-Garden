Anonymity is a shield from the tyranny. 

Give me a mask and I’ll tell you the truth. 

Anonymity is the fame of the future. 
Gumnaami me hi asli shohrat chupi hai. 


Our hero wants to be famous. He needs to understand the value of anonymity. There is huge value in anonymity. 

Anonymity is the shield from the tyranny. In a world, where everyone is ready to judge you and criticize you. If the value you have depends upon your being famous, then it can be taken away from you. You need to protect your fame and popularity. To do that you will go along with anything that protects your fame. You will go along with the most popular opinion, even though it’s not what you believe in. You will agree with the powerful, because you don’t want to upset anyone who can take away the thing that you value most: your fame. You are afraid to speak the truth. You will be a prisoner of your own making. 

*_________________________*

You don’t appreciate anonymity till you don’t have it anymore.  

You don’t have to achieve fame to make a mark on the world. 

Duniya me apni chaap chodne ke liye shohrat ki zarurat nahi hai. 

Against: But it helps if you are famous. You can bring attention to the problems you see with the world. You will only make an impact if you can reach out to many, many people. You have the platform to raise the issues. What can you really do if nobody is listening to you? You can’t make a difference with just 1 or 2 people or even a hundred. You need the crowd to come together and attach weight to an issue to make something happen. People in power and authority will not listen to just one person. 
 Look at Gandhi, MLK Jr and mother Teresa. THey needed people to support them. THey couldn’t have achieved what they achieved, alone. 

For: The only change you need to bring about is in yourself. Fame is not going to change people’s minds. It might give you a temporary attention. But that attention is fleeting. Today, nobody is going to pay attention to you for a long time. There are so many distractions that people will find something else. 
If you need fame to bring about change, then that change will also be transitory. Like fame. 


Real integrity is doing the right thing, knowing that nobody is going to know whether you did it or not.

Are you doing things to actually bring about change or just bringing attention to yourself. Do you want to do something good in the world or do you want people to know you are good person? We need to be honest with ourselves about that. Most people who believe they care about an issue, do not, in fact, care about it. They just want to project an image of a person who does. You just want to be appreciated. If you really care about something. Passionately. Deeply. You will do something about it irrespective of fame and popularity. Honestly, that is the way to go about it. If people join in the way, because they care about it as well. Then good on them. But you don’t need to have hordes of people behind you to take action. You just need conviction.

Main Char 
Mai hamesha se famous hun. Bas abhi logon ko is baat ka pata nahi hai. 

Kayi logon ki shohrat is baat ka saboot hai ki log kitne chutiye hote hain.

Shohrat ka matlab sirf logon ki tareefen batorna nahi, logon ki gaaliyan khaana bhi hai. 




Na ho shohrat to Gumnami ka bhi khatra nahi hota. 
Bahut mashhoor hona bhi acha nahi hota. 

Fasadon, haadson, jungon me hi hum ek hote hain. 
Koi aafat na aaye to, koi apna nahi hota. 

Samajhne laga hai baccha duniya ko paida hote hi. 
Ab is duniya me baccha banke koi paida nahi hota. 

Ghar me andhera, roshni bahan, aisa bhi hota hai. 
Kisi ka dil hota hai bura, par chehra nahi hota. 

Mila leta hai maila haath milkar koi bhi lekin, 
Milata hai nazar wo, jiska dil maila nahi hota.
