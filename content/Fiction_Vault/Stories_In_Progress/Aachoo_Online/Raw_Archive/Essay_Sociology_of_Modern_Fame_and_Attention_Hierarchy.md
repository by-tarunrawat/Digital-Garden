Everybody wants fame. Everybody wants to be known. Family, friends, relatives, acquaintances is not enough. Everybody wants to be known by strangers. They want to be admired and respected and loved. Earlier there was only one way of becoming famous. Movies. You become an actor or a singer. People see you on the big screen or TV, hear your voice. THat’s what being famous meant. Politicians are famous too. But nobody wants to be a politician. They are not cool. THey get abused. People know they are corrupt. People admire filmstars and movie stars because they are heroes and heroines. THey have something pristine about them. THey are above everybody else. 

Today, becoming famous is easier. Social media has increased the reach people have. Anybody can be famous. You don’t need to be talented for that either. All you need is a phone. You can sing, not well. You can dance at songs everybody is dancing on, not very well again. You can model with your cheap clothes, doesn’t need fashion sense. It seems like you don’t need to be great at anything to become famous. I have to say, it has given a chance for people to do anything they want. This wasn’t available to anyone in the history of humankind. 

People had to struggle to get into one film as a secondary character. Now you can make a short movie at home and put it up. It has the potential to be seen by millions of people. Pose in new clothes, or in exotic locations, or fancy restaurants and clubs and you get a chance to be famous. People like watching other people do well in life. It makes them feel bad about their own lives. But they keep on watching these things. Maybe they think how someday they will do all this.

These social media stars are doing sponsorships, ads, collaborations. These people give their opinions on thigs and their loyal followers take it as an expert opinion. So a person who has shown ass in different clothes all her life, can comment of political or social affairs and be taken seriously by her fans. That’s why they are called influencers. THey influence public opinion, sell product and can raise status by shout outs and collabs. There is a new power dynamic in popularity. People are being valued on how many subscriber or followers they have. How many likes they get.  

This unprecedented access to people’s attention has created a hierarchy.
On top there are superstars. People like Shahrukh, Amitabh Bacchan, Salman, Deepika, Virat kohli and all. People, who if they go out, can create a massive traffic problem. 
There there is normal film stars. Like Rajkumar rao, Nawazzuddin, Kiara advani, Sonam Kapoor etc. 
Now comes the new categories of this hierarchy. Influencers. THere are levels to this also. Based on how many followers you have. How loyal are your followers. Can they fight, argue endlessly, threaten and abuse others on your behalf. Which platform are you on. Youtube has more status than TikTok. Instagram has more status than Facebook. Twitter is it’s own abattoir of sensible discussions. Least of all is whatsapp. It’s more like a fringe member of your friend circle. Useful half the time and annoying the other half.
