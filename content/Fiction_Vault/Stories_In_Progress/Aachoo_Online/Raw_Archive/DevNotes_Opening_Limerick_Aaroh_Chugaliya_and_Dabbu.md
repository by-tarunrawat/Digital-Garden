There once was a boy named Aachoo. 
He was followed by a few.
He wanted to be famous. 
He had some plans. 
Which involved a few gangs. 
“Oh yes!” He said, “I’m a genius.”


He convinced his friend. 
They would put and end. 
To their anonymous existence. 
With their wits combined, 
They surely could grind, 
And create the most influence. 

The friend called Maddy,
Was always ready, 
To make himself known to the world. 
Disguised as philosophy and politics,









Aaroh Chugaliya is a social media influencer. Runs his social media account by @Aachu. He is moderately successful making lifestyle, travel and fashion content. Followed by over 2 lakh people, he considers himself to be famous and popular. He wants to be the most famous influencer in the country. Star in movies. Being adored. He wants to see hordes of people running after him for selfies. He dreams that wherever he walks. He turns people’s heads. Even famous people like 
Lives with roommates. 

Dabbu is a political right wing social media influencer. He makes politically devisive content and gets constant hate for it online. He has become immune to it. 






I want to become famous. This was Aaroh Chugaliya’s ambition in life. He dreamed of meeting stars and celebrities. Going to parties with them. Being a household name. However, he had no talent. He couldn’t act. He could dance or sing. He wasn’t good at sports. Although, this didn’t stop him from being full of himself. He thought people should know about him. They should respect him and follow every word he says. This was not the case in real life. 

The only thing he was good at was making friends. This quality made him popular in college. He had a lot of friends. Most of them were impressed by the bullshit Aaroh spewed at them. He would lie to make himself likeable. There were some who would see through it. But he would spread rumours about them, before they could discredit him in any way. 

To remain popular, he would go out of his way to help people. Some people would take advantage of this. These were people who saw through his act, but would pretend they believed him and ask him to do ridiculous tasks.
