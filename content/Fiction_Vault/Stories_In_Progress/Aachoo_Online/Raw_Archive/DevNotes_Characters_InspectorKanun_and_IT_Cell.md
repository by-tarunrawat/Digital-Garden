WHAT IF??

What is the message I want to convey?

Fame is bad. People do crazy things to become famous and maintain that fame.
It’s Kind of, sort of the message. It has to be clearer than that. It’s not really a message anyway. It’s just a statement. 

So, the message to convey is it’s not the fame and the likes you want. It’s the love of the people around you.

Love or connection? Maybe connection.

What is it about?

Cost of fame?

My hero wants fame. 
At any cost? 
Maybe. 
He would do anything to get more likes. Fake pictures and videos. Photoshop.

What is it that he really wants?

He wants to live the life of an influencer. Fancy car, hotels, dinners. Luxurious apartments, glamourous girlfriend. Everything he sees other influencers and celebrities doing. 

Follows celebrities and mimics their life. Live the life you want - his motto. He wants to live the life of a celebrity influencer. He has 10-15K followers. He wants to live the life like Gaurav Vanarchand aur @VanGau. VanGau has 3 crore followers. He has a big car. He has a girlfriend every month. He gives to the poor followers he has. He parties at beaches with celebrities. That’s the life Acchoo wants. 

Protest. Small. Acchoo sees VanGau go to a protest. SO he decides to go to a protest.
