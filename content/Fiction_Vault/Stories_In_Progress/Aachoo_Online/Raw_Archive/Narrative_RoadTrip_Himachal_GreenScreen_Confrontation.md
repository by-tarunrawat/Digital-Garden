I have decided to keep writing. Explore various ideas. Maybe some of them will work out. 

So initially I had thought of two boys, living in a small town, wanting to be influencers. They would make these stupid videos.

Physical actions and emotional Dilemma. Theme: based on Hero’s flaw. 


Hero wants to be famous. The flaw will come from this desire only. So, what can be the pitfalls of wanting to be famous?
Becoming famous at all cost. The hero will do anything that makes people impressed by him. Will he do embarrassing things to be famous. Does he have any conditions to his desire? Is infamy okay with him. I think not. What if it is? Maybe the hero thinks that it doesn’t matter if it’s embarrassing, as long as people know him or like him, it’s fine. 
Becoming famous at all cost. Embarrassment, infamy. Does the hero think infamy is good. MAybe infamy is good but not embarrassment. Is he hardworking? Lets make the hero hardworking. 

Hero in an IT cell? What if the hero start working in an IT cell. Making posts and promoting propaganda. That will be a political type of story. Uncovering of the truth. Is that our story? No. That’s not our story. 

What if I combine my kidnapping story with this one. A girl fed up with her job and family problems runs away. On the way she finds  a couple of wannabe influencers in need of money. They find out about the girl’s story and decide to stage her kidnapping to get the money they need. This is interesting. So what will they do.

There is this character he calls himself Sosal baba. He runs a company that trains and makes influencers. Our two main characters decide to join the course that sosal baba offers. It’s 1 lakh rupees. There are 15-20 people who have joined this program. OUr heros Acchoo and Dabbu join the program. 
The program is that they will travel from Dilli to Ladakh in a bus. 
*********

Another break in writing. SHould we go back to the hero’s flaw. Achoo wants to be famous. He believes being famous gives you access to things. It solves all your problems. Even his problems will be solved if he becomes famous. Famous people are just better. They know better. They are examples for the rest of the world. He also wants to be an example for the rest of the world. 
IF that’s the flaw. Thinking that famous people are better people. If you are famous, all your problems go away. Then he should encounter situations where he meets famous people. They will all have their own problems. 
The theme then would be: Being famous and popular doesn’t solve your problems. You solve your problems by facing them head on. You have to accept your problems. You have to become a better person, who cares about others rather than others caring about you. 

This would mean that at some point Achoo will become famous, but his problems will not go away. He will still have to deal with them. 

So at midpoint, his desire will come true. Right? Let’s not think about it that way. Let’s go sequentially. 
*********

Once upon a time, there was a boy called Achoo. He was a 24 year old lazy boy. He didn’t want to work. He sat around in his house all day and roamed around with his friend Dabbu. They had other friends also, but they got busy with their jobs and didn’t have any time to hang out with Achoo. Achoo blamed them for this. He thought they are being arrogant and haughty after getting jobs. 

Achoo and Dabbu roam around town all day, making videos and reals. Posting them on social media. They hope that one of these days one of their videos will get viral and they will become famous. Their friends and family have tried getting them jobs, but they don’t listen to them. They don’t want to do a normal job. They believe that if they become famous, they will not have to work a single day in life. 

Their families are fed up of their uselessness. They don’t know what to do with them. Achoo keeps asking his father for money. At first his father would oblige, but now even his father has stopped giving his money. 
Achoo is also frustrated that his content doesn’t get likes and views. If he could get just one viral video, then he would become famous and wouldn’t have to work a day in life. 

He reads about this company that promises to make you an influencer. Achoo and Dabbu decide to get in touch with the owner. The owner, called Sosal baba, promises big things. He tells them that he can get their videos viral for 50k bucks each. Achoo and Dabbu don’t have that kind of money. They somehow take out a loan from a loan shark to pay sosal baba. Sosal baba takes the money and tells them to go and make videos. And he will get them 1 lakh followers in no time. There is another person with Sosal baba Ghoomti chappal. He has 4-5 lakh followers. He ensures them that Sosal baba is like his mentor and he is the reason he has become so famous. 

Achoo and Dabbu are ecstatic. They make many videos. Contact local shops and restaurants for branded content. These business are reluctant to offer them any money. Achoo makes a deal with a travel company owner. He will make content for him and in return he will get them many more new customers. He will do this for free. But he will take a cut in the profits that they make. THe owner agrees. 

After Achoo and Dabbu has made enough videos, they decide to go to sosal baba to get views, shares and followers. But, when they go to the office, sosal baba is not there. They find out that sosal baba is a scammer. He has taken money from others and run away. Now achoo and Dabbu are in trouble. They don’t know what to do. The loan shark, Danger singh, sends his goons to get his money back. Achoo and Dabbu don’t have any money to pay back. They get threatened and given a month’s time to pay back the money with interest. Which comes out to 2 lakh rupees. Achoo and Dabbu are in deep trouble. They have nowhere to go. Achoo asks his father for the money. But his father doesn’t have that kind of money to give him. 

Now there is a threat on achoo and Dabbu’s life. They have no other option but to look for Sosal baba. They have a clue. They decide to contact one of the influencers, that Sosal baba marketed as one of his customers. 

This guy is a travel influencer, so it is difficult to track him down. They see from his post that he is in Himachal. He will be in himachal till 4/20 to celebrate the date. It’s April 1, So, they decide to track him down in himachal. They sell some of their belongings to buy a bus ticket to Himachal to track this guy down. They have about 25K in cash for their expenses. 

They reach Himachal in Parvathi valley, where the travel blogger ghoomtichappal is supposed to be. They see that Chappal has tagged himself at a cafe in a secluded location. Achoo and Dabbu can’t believe their luck. They will find him so easily. They go to that location. It’s a place called Badnam cafe. The cafe is filled with young people. The owner of the cafe is an old guy with a long beard and flowing locks. His name is GeeGro. He tells them that he hasn’t seen the guy anywhere around. 
Achoo and Dabbu are confused. They decide to stay in himachal for a couple of days and look for Ghoomti chappal. Unfortunately, they lose their money. As they are counting how much money they have left, a gust of wind takes the money away from their hands. They are in a pickle. How will they find Chappal. Where will they stay without money? WHat will they eat. Geegrow offers them a job. He tells them that they will have to work a few hours at the cafe. He will give them 1000 bucks a day each, a room to stay and two meals a day. They can work for a week while they look for whoever they are looking for. Achoo and Dabbu are reluctant, but they don’t have any other option. They agree. Gee grow tells them that they will have to work 8 hours a day. 2 hours in the morning, taking care of his farm and 6 hours at night, when the cafe opens for business. In between, they are free to go wherever they like. 

The next day they start working in the cafe and GeeGrow’s marijuana farm and go to different places, Ghoomti chappal has supposedly visited, but no one has seen them. They decide to go to all the hotels and hostels nearby to check if he is staying in one of those places. But can’t find anything. Then, Ghoomti chappal posts about a hostel he is staying in, promoting it to his followers. 

Finally, Achoo and dabbu have a clue. They go to the hostel, but they find that he is not in their reservation/guest list. They think that because he is famous, he must’ve been staying under a fake name. So they decide to wait outside the hostel for the guy. Unfortunately, ghoomti chappal is not there. Dabbu is disheartened, but Achoo picks him up saying that Chappal is a famous and trusted influencer, he will be somewhere in Himachal. They will keep looking till they find him. 
The next day, they see an instagram live of Ghoomti Chappal at a famous picnic spot khudkhushi point. They rush to that place in hopes that now they will finally be able to talk to ghoomti chappal and find out about Sosal baba. 

When they reach the khudkhushi point, they see that no one is there. They are confused. How can this happen? Ghoomti chappal is still live on Khudkhushi point, drinking tea at a tea stall. But the tea stall is empty. They are the only ones there. They don’t understand what is happening. Discouraged, they debate what to do next. The best bet is to go home find a job and talk to danger singh to give them a few more months to repay the loan. 

Achoo is not done yet. He thinks that Chappal lives in Delhi. They can go and track him down there. Dabbu is not so eager. 1. Chappal is a traveler and might not be in delhi. 2. IT was so difficult to find him in Parvathi valley, it would be almost impossible to find him in a big city like delhi. Dabbu does have a point.

Danger singh has been looking for Achoo and Dabbu. He is under a lot of pressure himself. His own boss, Nuclear Khan, is after him. Nuclear pays a surprise visit to Danger and does a quaterly appraisal. He finds out that about a corore rupees are missing from the accounts. Danger is extremely afraid of Nuclear and tells him that two boys had taken loans from him and have now run away. Nuclear tells him to find those guys. Side note: Nuclear is a dangerous person. He is also a big time loan shark. Whoever doesn’t pay their dues, he makes them beg online. Setting up a payment account.
Nuclear tells Danger to find those kids and get his money back or put him in Nuclear’s online beggar party otherwise, he will put danger in danger. Danger singh is afraid and finds Achoo and Dabbu from their social media posts and sends his best goons, Dayan dola, bayan dola and chaudi chaati to look for them in himachal. 

DD, BD and CC go to himachal and find Achoo and Dabbu. IN trouble, Achoo and Dabbu ask Geegro for his help. Geegro gives them their salary and sends them on their way home. Telling them that he will handle the goons. Achoo and Dabbu run away. But they realize that if they go home, Danger will find them and they will be in danger once again. So they decide to go to delhi to find Ghoomti chappal and Sosal Baba. 

Achoo and Dabbu reach Delhi in hopes of finding Chappal. They find out that there is an influencer event happening. They are sure that they will find Ghoomti chappal there. The influencer event is exclusive and is only for influencers above 1 lakh followers. 
Achoo and Dabbu con their way in. Inside, they are mesmerized by the glitz and glamour of the event and the influencers. They meet some of their favourite influencers, who don’t give them any bhav. They ask around for chappal, but nobody has seen him around. Finally, at the end of the party, they are talking to one influencer and find out that this fat guy Ghoomti chappal. They corner him and ask him about sosal baba. Chappal is cornered and tries to send them away, but Achoo and Dabbu are adamant. 

Finally Chappal tells them that he will tell them where sosal baba is. He just needs time. Achoo and Dabbu are not convinced. They follow Ghoomti chappal to his house and force their way in. Once inside, they realize that all of ghoomti Chappal’s content is fake. They find a huge green screen set up in one room, where he makes all his videos. Pretending to be in places that he can’t afford. Achoo and DAbbu are heartbroken by this betrayal. Ghoomti chappal tells them to come back the next day, he will get them to meet Sosal baba. 

Achoo and DAbbu go away, but remember that they’ve left somthing at Ghoomti chappal’s home. They go back to realize that chappal is packing everything up and leaving. They confront him. Right then, Somebody opens the door to come in. It’s Sosal Baba! They have finally found him. They catch him and confront him to give them their money back. Sosal is reluctant at first and tells them that he has spent all of it. Achoo and Dabbu are not hearing it. THey want their money back at all costs or they will take Sosal to the police. 
Side note: Sosal baba’s real name is Sourabh Salil. He used to work at a marketing agency, getting fake likes and fake followers for brands. Making their videos go viral. He realized it is a great way to make money and started his own agency with Ghoomti chappal (real name: Ghulam Chawla). Initially it worked well and they had a few clients. But when sosial media clamped down on bots getting fake likes and followers. Their business tanked. Sosal realized that he can scam young people wanting to be influencers and started his scam business along with Ghoomti chappal. 

Sosal realizes that he has no way out so he tells them that he has a new job/scam. They can make a crore in a month. If Achoo and Dabbu help him with this, he will give them 10%. Achoo and Dabbu are reluctant to do it. Sosal realizes that Achoo and Dabbu are desperate and tells them that if they want their money this is the only way to do it. Otherwise they can send him to the police. They will not get their money back. Achoo asks what the plan is. Sosal tells them that the scam is to write and spread fake posts and blogs wrapped as news on social media and messaging services. They need to gather as many people against the protesters. All he needs from Achoo and Dabbu is to find 

Achoo and Dabbu are in a pinch. They tell Sosal that they will think about it. Achoo and Dabbu debate whether to go with Sosal’s deal or not. They don’t want to scam innocent people like themselves. They decide that they will confront Sosal and take him to the police. If they don’t get their money, then so be it. 

But, Danger’s goon finds them. They are not caught. To get out of the situation, Achoo tells them about the scheme. The goons don’t trust them. They call Danger Singh. Danger tell them that the money they owe will do nothing to solve his problems with nuclear. They need to get the two boys and give them to Nuclear khan.
