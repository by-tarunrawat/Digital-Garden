Mai aj bahut controversial topic ke baare me baat karna chahta hun. Police! 
An an aam insaan mai jab bhi kisi sarkaari daftar me kaam hota hai to wo kaam paise se ho jaata hai. Paisa do, kaam karawa lo. Mujhe  driving license chahiye, paisa do, license lo. Mujhe ghar ki registry karni hai, paisa do, kaam turrant ho jayega. Municipality, haspatal, registrar, school, colleges, RTO… Kya chahiye, building permit, telephone connection, bijli, business license, driving license, birth certificate, death certificate, domicile, passport… Har cheez ke liye, paisa do, kaam lo. Paisa do kaam lo, paisa do kaam lo…
Par Police… Police aisa akela sarkaari sansthan hai, jo kaam na karne ke paise leti hai. Matlab alag hi level ka arrogance hai. Paise bhi lenge, kaam bhi nahi karenge. Aapko kabhi kisi police waale ne chalaan ke liye roka hai? Is situation se nikalne ka ek asaan upaye hai… 500 rupaye… Mere twenties me 100 hua karta tha… ab zyada ho gaya hai. Anyway, aap police waale ko 500 rupaye do aur wo aapka chalan nahi katega. Jo ki uska kaam hai. Aapne policewaale ko 500 rupaye diye, apna kaam na karne ke. Kamaal hai na. 
Ye to khair mai baat ko thoda ghuma raha tha. At least, paise deke aap bach to nikle. To aapka fayeda hi hua. 
Ek baar mujhe yaad hai, mujhe kisi ne phone pe scam kiya. 10000 rupaye furrrr. Mai police ke paas gaya. 4 ghante wait karwaya. Mai jitni baar poochta ki sir, FIR likhe lo, mujhe bolte haan 5 minute baitho, sir baat karenge. 4 ghante ke baad to mujhe lagne laga ki mai hi shayad galat hun. Faaltu me Police waalon ka time waste kar raha hun. 
 Finally meri baari aayi. Maine complaint ki - Sir, mujhe kisi ne phone pe behla fusla kar 10000 rupaye ka chuna lagaya. Ye rahe unke phone number… Aap FIR likhiye aur jaldi se unhe pakad lijiye. POlicewaale ne mujhe hi interrogate karna shuru kar diya. Ek point par mujhe lagne laga ki kahin ye mujhe to jail me nahi daal denge. 
Saare sawaalon ke baad maine fir bola, sir FIR likhiye aur choron ko pakad lijiye. Mere paas unke number hain, aap number se unki location track kar sakte hain… Aap easily unko pakad… Police waale ne mujhe roka… Aur mujhe hi daant diya. Tu sikhayega ki police kaam kaise kare. Hain! 
Mujhe bola ki constable se ek khaali page le ke ek letter likh. Mujhe zyada idea tha nahi to maine poocha, kya wo FIR hoga… Policewaala fir kheej gaya - Tu likh to pehle. Mere andar to himmat nahi thi ki mai usse agla sawaal poochun. To maine letter likh ke constable ko de diya. Constable ne mujhe roka aur bola - Kaam jaldi karwaana hai ki nahi. Maine bhi bol diya - Maine aadha kaam already kar diya hai. LEtter me saare mobile number hain… Aapko bas track… Constable bhi chilla diya - Abey… Mai pooch raha hun kaam karawaana bhi hai ki nahi. Maine bola - Of course. ISiliye to mai police station aaya hun. Warna ghar me baithta. 
Constable ne sar hilaya aur aage jhuk ke, apne angoothe aur tarjani ko masalte hue bola - Kaam karwaana hai ki nahi? Mujhe ab samajh me aaya. Sarkaari kaam karwaane ke liye paise dene padte hain. Maine 100 rupaye nikal ke de diye. Constable ne muh banaya. Baraaz hi ho gaya - Le ja wapas. Bhikhari samjha hai kya. Mai ghabra gaya. Maine upar 500 ka note aur rakh diya. Isse constable ka gussa thoda shant hua - Ye kya hai… Bas itna? Maine 500 rupaye aur rakh diye. Maine socha 100 rupaye wapas le lunga. Total 1000 ho jayenge. Usne 100 ke note ke upar haath rakh diya - Rehne de. Tu ja. Hum dekh lenge. 
Mai khush tha. Ab to kaam ho jayega. Mujhe rishwat dena bahut bura lagta hai. Lekin agar kaam ho jaaye to bura lagna thoda kam ho jaaata hai. Ki chalo at least kaam to ho jayega. 
Maine kuch din baad phone kiya - Sir, mai 4 din pehle complaint leke aaya tha. Constable - INvestigate kar rahe hain. Jaise hi pata chalega aapko phone karenge. Hum wapas call karenge. Mujhe kaafi generic reply laga. Jaise ki sabko yahi bolte hon. Baaki log hello bolte hain, policewaale bolte hain - INvestigate kar rahe hain. Jaise hi pata chalega aapko phone karenge. Mujhe is point par bhi koi shak nahi tha ki police waale choron ko pakad lenge aur mere paise waapas dilwa denge. 
Ek hafta aur ho gaya. Mere apne kisi dost se baat kar raha tha, ki mere saath aisa aisa hua. MAine police me jakar FIR likhwayi. Mera dost thoda hairan hua. FIR? Unhone likh bhi li? Maine muskura ke use bola - Matlab 1100 rupaye dene pade. Lekin likh li. 
To matlab, unhone actual FIR likhi? 
Actual FIR to nahi. Maine ek letter likha… Shayad wo usi ko FIR me copy kar denge. 
SHayad? 
Matlab kar denge yaar. 1100 rupaye liye hain. FIR to likhenge hi. 
Dekh bhai, Policewaale itni asaani se FIR likhte hain hain. FIR likhne a matlab unhe koi action lena padega. 
Haan to? 
Action lene ka matlab hai kaam. Wo extra kaam kyun karenge?
Mujhe is baat par hasi aa gayi. Extra kaam thodi na hai. Chor uchakke pakadna hi to unka kaam hai. Extra kaam hota ki unhe meri presentation bhi banani padti. 
Par fir bhi… Unko already itna kaam hai. Wo FIR likh kar kyun badhayenge. 
Kyunki maine 1100 rupaye diye hain bhai. 
Thik hai. Thik hai. Tu ek kaam kar na. Kal jake Policestation me check kar le, ki teri “FIR” ka kya hua. 

Mai agle din police station me gaya. Constable tha hi nahi. To maine ek dusre constable se poocha, meri FIR ka kya hua? Usne mera naam poocha aur FIR ki date poochi. FIR ek REgister me kaafi der tak dhoondhne ke baad bola, aapke naam se to koi FIR hi nahi hai. 
ABey yaar. Chalo koi nahi. Aap abhi likh do. 
Constable ne register band kiya aur bola - Saab se baat kar lo pehle. 

Mai usi samay wahan se nikal ke aa gaya. Police ne paise bhi liye aur kaam bhi nahi kiya. 

Us din mere saath 11100 rupaye ka fraud hua. Agar mai police ke paas na jaata to fraud kewal 10000 rupaye ka hi hota.
