A police officer who is also an influencer. An officer of the law recording everything he does. Making instagram reels and videos. Hosting Instagram live sessions for his followers as he goes to a murder scenes. He has millions of followers that see his content. He makes videos of procedure. Live streams shootouts and investigations. Loves the attention. 


***********

Two shady looking people, x and y, talking in a cafe. They look out of place. They are talking about doing something right there. One of them is not sure about it. The other one is trying to convince the other. A customer overhear them and walks to the counter to tell the cashier to call the police. He thinks the two shady looking people might be thinking of holding up the cafe for money.


X and Y are gangsters. X wants to get out of the business and become an influencer. 

***********

Names: 

Sosal Baba. 
Ghoomti Chappal
Danger Singh
Nuclear Khan
Achraj Bhai
Jee Grow
Aachhoo
Dabboo

***********

Acchoo goes to an Indian Idol Audition. Dabboo goes as his beatbox. 
Inside the audition. Dabboo drags a table from behind the Indian Idol Standee making a harsh, cringy noise. 
Acchoo sings a song. Not very well. Dabboo plays tabla on the table and makes makes sounds from his mouth to accompany Achoo’s singing. The result is a ridiculously bad song. Achoo is rejected. He bids farewell to the judges. Saying he will see them next year. It seems he does that every year. 


After this he plans to go on the roadies Audition. His aim is to become famous. It doesn’t matter if he has talent or not. He figures if he gives a funny audition or something. That will come on TV and he will become famous from that. FOr some reason, as ridiculous as his auditions are, they never manage to get on TV. 

*************
 
Politician/Strongman trying to gain power. Misinformation as a tool to gain popularity and dismiss his rivals. 

**************


So what’s the story here. The gangster, lets call him BD, wants to leave his job and become an influencer. He tries to follow trends and make content but doesn’t get followers. His content is unoriginal, low quality and boring.
