What is the argument of the piece? The theme?
The hero believes in fame and popularity. He believes that the only thing that matters is how many people know you and follow you. Paisa aur power sabse zaruri cheez hai. Aur dono hi tab aate hain jab tumhare paas fame ho. If you want money and power, then you need to be famous. It is easy to become famous. Wants easy fame. 

The theme has to be ironic. In complete opposition to the hero’s belief. 
So… What can it be? 
You don’t need popularity to be successful? 
Love and cooperation is what moves the world?
[If you come to fame without looking understanding who you are, it will define who you are.
Sooraj ki tarah chamakne ke liye pehle sooraj ki tarah jalna seekho]


Theme: Sooraj ki tarah chamakne ke uski tarah jalna padta hai. 
Anti Theme: Easy fame. Fame depends on how many people follow you. How many people like your pictures and videos.

Let’s lock that for a moment. 

Now design a character that embodies anti theme. Show why is he the way he is. Make us feel about the person. 

So the character is after easy fame. He thinks getting famous will solve all his problems. He wants fame at all cost.
Why? What happened that made him feel this way? Why is this his goal? 
What circumstances made him this way?
Something happened. Something bad. Something sad. Was he humiliated? Humiliated. Humiliation seems like a good motivation. He has something to prove. What if someone died because of that humiliation? That might be good. But how did that happen. 
Let’s say he lived in a small town. Like any other young boy. He had a father and a mother. He was quite well known in his town.

What else? He was in college. The loser of the college. Liked a girl. The girl humiliated him, along with other popular boys. VEry cliched. 
It’s a common trope right? Loser boy likes a girl. The girl pretends to like him. The boy gets humiliated on his big day. 
How can we change the cliche. What if the loser boy likes the girl. The girl is the hottest and most popular in college. She likes him as well. They are always together. The loser boy is jugaadu and he does things to impress the girl. THe girl likes the boy because he is not surface level. He talks straight. As it is. As they get close, the boy has insecurity about why the girl would be with him. He is not that great looking. He isn’t intelligent. It must be like a project or worse, a prank. These questions keep haunting the boy. His friends and others don’t believe him when he tells them that she is his girlfriend. He puts pictures of them online, to make people believe him. 
Rumors start spreading around the college. The girl is only with him because he is paying her. The girl is humiliated. Acchoo has hell to pay. What rubbish. 

Something else. Why does he want fame? Aisa kya ho gaya uski life me? Itni buri tarah chahiye. Kyun chahiye hota hai logon ko fame. GHar me pyar nahi mila. To wo bahar strangers me khoj rahe hain. Jitne log tareef karen utna pyar. Lonely existence. You require other’s approval to be complete. What does it bring really? IS it fulfilling? 

I’ll ask again. Why does he want fame? Why? THe parents not loving him angle also sounds cliche. It’s not my style either. What’s my style. WHy does he want fame according to my style? WHat can I come up with? 

WHat is his world exactly? 
Where is he now after he decided to become an influencer? 
What does he do everyday?
Where does he live? 
Does he work?
He has to work no? 
Maybe he works at a appliance store? Maybe McDonalds? Maybe he is a delivery guy? 

He is not comfortable in his situation. In this case. Sometime has passed. Acchoo hasn’t become an influencer. HE is working at a shitty place, in a shitty job. Just to get by. He makes trending dance reels in his free time. Doesn’t get any following. It’s this shitty situation. THen he gets a chance to finally realize his dream. But he has to go and…

2nd situation is when he is comfortable. He has finally achieved some level of fame. He is at 50K followers. People sometimes recognize him. He is making money by cheap brand sponsorship, like dogra shoe mall and Universal Parchoon. He also does something on the side. It has to be something that should lower his status in any way. HE has to save face, right? He works as a social media manager somewhere? 
He is proud, almost arrogant of his popularity. Brags about it all the time. Brings it up in conversation. Really annoying about it. Now, the things he is proud of are taken away. Something he says on his social media, gets him cancelled. He loses his job. His followers start dropping. He tries to issue apology, but his account gets blocked. 
He says nasty things to his friends, but they leave as well. His roommates stop talking to him. 

Now that he has lost everything. He has to go get it back. What does that mean? He thinks he has lost respect and fame. He needs to get back his fame to gain respect. Reinvention? Hel[ing people? Internal mental health issue. 

We need to design a situation where we hit one of the three axes and should be an antithesis. 

What is that situation? What is the antithesis. Fame is everything. Easy fame?

There is an opportunity. Acchoo isn’t recognized. He has to work the same as other. But finally.
