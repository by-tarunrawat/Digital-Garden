Romila Joshi’s father fixed her marriage to a well to do business family, when she was only 17. She was to be married next year, after she finished school and turned 18. 

Romila had been in Dehradun all her life. She wanted to see the world. Meet new people. Travel and live. She didn’t want to be tied down to a family she didn’t know. 

Her mother was sympathetic to her dreams. But her father had his own dreams to see his daughter settled down in a good family. Romila fought and argued. But her father didn’t move. She ultimately surrendered to his will. 

However, Her father had a severe heart attack and died. As the doctor announced this in the hospital, she felt relief instead of sadness. Maybe her dreams will come true. IN her head she was already building grand plans of what she was going to do. 

But this lasted only 8 minutes. Like a miracle, her father came back to life, 8 minutes after being declared dead. The doctor was excited, the whole family rejoiced, except Romila. All her dreams came crashing down. The first words her father uttered were, “Shukra hai mai Romila ka byah dekh paunga.”

Romila’s head was exploding. She decided, she can’t wait for her father to die to live her life the way she wanted. She decided that she would save up her pocket money and run away as soon as she had enough. 

She dilligently put any money she saved in her gullak. Being stingy and living an ascetic (Jogan) life style. A few months later, on the eve of her last board exam, she counted the money she had saved. It was under 7K. But it was enough for her. She left that night. Not even finishing her board exams. She left one note. “I’m not sorry.”
