Scene 1: Small room in a Delhi. 
A man about 33 is sitting on the bed looking at his phone. On his screen, his bank balance, 3,253 Rs. He closes the window and opens a food delivery app. Total cart vale: 264 Rs. He removes several items like omelette, mango shake and chicken puff. Only one item remains. Bun Samosa. Total cart value: 52 Rs. 

Scene 2: Flashback: 3 years ago.  
A party going on at a bar. It’s Aaroh’s Farewell party. We see he was in a corporate job, with several colleagues revealing their envy at Aaroh leaving the corporate job. They tell him they could do that as well. Aaroh laughs. He looks happy. We find out that Aaroh has already lined up a couple of regular gigs. Everybody has to start small, he says. The goal is to have my own album. The bill comes. It’s 12K. Everybody starts calculating how much they each owe. Aaroh magnanimously takes the bill and pays it. A notification comes, he has more than 10 lakh rupees in his account. 

Back to Scene 1:
Aaroh takes a breath and orders the bun samosa. He packs his guitar in a case. The name Aaroh Chauhan written on top.
He goes out of the room, into the living room looking at his bank balance now: 3,199 Rs. 

Living Room:
A man dressed in formals is sitting on the couch. Some mindless youtube video of a vlogger playing on the 29 inch LCD TV on the wall opposite the couch. The man, Mohit Bhandari, is scrolling on his phone. He sees Aaroh, urf Aachoo, as he calls him, and tells him, tu ghar pe hi hai na aj. Ek list daali hai fridge pe, le aana. Acchoo looks at his balance, looks at Mohit and says, meri meetings hain. Mohit is suspicious and asks kaunsi meetings. Aaroh lies. Mohit sighs and nods, thik hai mai Dilbagh ko bol dunga. Another roommate, Dilbagh Khan, comes out of his room and plops down on the couch next to Mohit, grabbing the remote from him and changing the video. Mohit is irritated and tells him that he was watching that for work. Dilbagh refuses. In their argument we find that Mohit works in a job in marketing and works with influencers. Mohit snatches the remote back and puts the previous video. Dilbagh shrugs. Acchoo is about to sit when Mohit reminds him that he has a meeting. Aachhoo remember the lie he has told and putting his guitar case on his shoulder, he leaves. As he is going Mohit calls him out again reminding him to pay him his share of the rent and the rest of the expenses for the month. Aaroh nods and leaves. 

Scene 2: The street in front of the building. 
Aaroh comes out of the building and takes a sigh of relief. He takes out tobacco and paper and rolls a cigarette. He lights it. Taking a drag, he looks at his phone and sees the food delivery guy almost reaching. He takes a couple of more drags and throws the cigarette down as the delivery guy comes. 
Beatboxing in the background. Aaroh takes the packet from the delivery and takes a left.
