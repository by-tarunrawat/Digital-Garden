Two good for nothing influencers, have to become super famous in two months to get into a prestigious creators conference.

1. Nawaz getting out of his room in stealth mode. It’s almost as if he is afraid of something, going into the battle field. Dodging imaginary mines and looking for snipers along the way. The audience sees it through a recoding someone is making on their mobile phone. It’s a prank (something falling on top of his head). Raj comes out from behind and reveals that this is another Pahadi Boyzzz successful prank. Nawaz and Raj then watch the video. Nawaz spots a problem in the video. They decide to do it again. THus revealing the fake nature of their “pranks” 

2. Raj is pretty happy with his follower numbers and views. He thinks this should be enough to get into the creators conference. Raj wants to talk to one of the organizers of the creators conference. He DMs him. But there is no response. Repeatedly. Raj and Nawaz decide to stalk him from his stories and bump into him “accidentally”. 

3. But this became tougher that they thought. The organizer would post that he is at a pub called Diesel. But when they reach, he is not there. Even the ambiance of the pace looks different. This would happen a lot. He would post a story about going to a place, but when Raj and Nawaz would reach, he is not there and the place looks a little different. They don’t get what is happening. One day they see a story that he is in france. They lose all hope. If he has gone that far, he must’ve gone for long. They are sad and dejected. They decide to go to a new pub, where they could get some content for their instagram. By chance the organizer is also there. When asked about the France post, he reveals that he went there a couple of years ago. He clicked so many pictures that he posts them every now and then. He doesn’t go out anymore. It’s too hectic. He mostly uses old pictures for posts. Sometimes he goes out when he is running out of content. 

4. They demand that they be a part of the creator’s conference. He tells them, tickets are available in a couple of months. THey can buy those and be a part of the creator’s conference. Raj: Ticket nahi chahiye. Hame part hona hai… Matlab. NAwaz: Matlab panel… Panel speaker. RAj: Haan. Aur Bina panel ka bhi agar ho sakta hai to wo bhi chalega. Nawaz: Keynote speaker. Raj: Haan wo bhi chalega. 
The organizer laughs. He tells them that to be in the panel for creators conference they need to be significant creators. Raj: Arey. To pichle 2 saal me 136 videos banaye hain hamne. trending posts alag. Baaki selfies, wanderlust, sab mila milu ke 1500 ke aas paas post hain hamare. 
Organizer tells him, this doesn’t matter. You need to get views as well. Nawaz: Views to bahut aate hain. Pichle waale video me 2 Lakh views the. Use pichle waale pe 2 Lakh 1 sau 18. Aur usse pichle waale pe 2 lakh 3 sau pachp…
Organizer tells them this is not enough. You need to 100 times more views than that. TO get on the panel they need to get at least 2 crore views regularly on all their videos. All their videos should go viral. 
Raj and Nawaz are a bit daunted. Organizer insults the content they have on their profile and tells them that for keynote, they will have to be Rohit Shetty, Karan Johar, Roma Jogan level. RAj and Nawaz absolutely adore Roma Jogan. She is the rockstar of podcasting. Her podcast is viewd by 20 crore people every month. Most of her listeners are from Small towns and villages. She speaks in Proper hindi. Smokes and drinks on her podcast. Has the most interesting people. Most celebrities vie to go on her podcast. But she is extremely selective about who she calls on her podcast. 
The organizer tells them that they have two months to become a viral influencer. Maybe they get lucky. Or they can try again in 2 4, 20 years and most likely never. 

5. Raj and Nawaz are depressed. They don’t know where to go from here. They drink. When drunk they meet a man who is also drunk. He is drinking because his mother died. Raj tells him their problem is bigger. They Have to go from 2 Lakh to 2 crore views in 2 months. *Comedy: Contrast the misery of mother dying with not being able to increase followers* At the end of the night. The stranger tells them that there are companies that you can pay to increase your followers. You can just google it. 


Raj and Nawaz decide to do their pranks on strangers for some reason. Their pranks are cringy at first. People start following them just to get a laugh at their expense. They think people are following because they like their content. They make more of such cringy videos. More and more people start following them. THey become a meme. 
Raj and Nawaz google the websites and find several. Most website offer 1 to 10 Lakh followers. And even this is quite expensive. Raj talks to his mom, who tells him to do bargaining from the websites. Raj thinks this is a good idea. THey find the address of the company and goes to talk to someone incharge. They tell him that they need at least 2 crore views on their videos. The person tells them that this is highly risky, because social media sites can find out and ban your account. The person tells him that it will be expensive. *Comic exchange where RAj and Nawaz are trying to haggle with the person, and the person is trying to tell them they can’t reduce the price.* Eventually they reach a deal where Raj and Nawaz will work for them for two months without any salary. But during this time they will get 2 crore views on each video as well as more followers. The person incharge agrees. 
There is a section of people who think that Raj and Nawaz are doing social commentary with their pranks. This is art. What Raj and Nawaz are doing is exposing the hypocrisy of the world and the corruption that lies within each of us. Certain sections start thinking of them as modern day philosophers. Gaining even more followers. Their videos start getting viral. Their comment section is divisive. Some people think they are useless clown, while the other think they are some sort of genius. COmparing them to the modern socrates. 
They start making videos. Which they make viral themselves. Whenever people criticized them. They would criticize them back. Talking about how this is art. Other people also start believing this is art. After that is starts growing organically. A section of people think this is art another section thinks it’s cringy. THere is always a fight in comment section. *Somebody advices them. It’s okay if they are abusing you. As long as they keep on abusing. Because when the abuse stops, is where the money stops. ABuse is engagement.” 




Roma Jogan. Talking to a world renowned psychologist. She dreams of buying 150 acres of land to build a house for herself and her 41 kids. Over the years she goes back to a small place called Kasar in Almora. She wants to buy a piece of land. Farm Pears and Peaches. She wants to have 4 dogs and 50 kids. She dreams of opening a school for her kids. *At some point during the conversation the guest jokes how will she get her guests to come all the way to Almora for these podcasts. This makes Jogan think for a bit.*  

We see her with those 41 kids. It’s clear that she loves them and they love her. They all call her Roma. We also see that the kids don’t have enough space in the house. Jogan promises them that soon they will move to a new house which will have a room for everyone. And loads of area to play. 

We find out in another scene that she is about to do a 100 crore deal with a music and podcasting app swar app. Jogan finds out that the land she wants to buy has other interested parties who are willing to pay 65 crore for the entire land. Jogan tells the owner, if he waits for a month, she will pay him 15 crores more than the other parties.  

Jogan’s producer tells her, “Agar 80 crore use de doge to bachega kya.” He tells Jogan to try and get 150 crores in the deal. She are going into a meeting with the swarapp executives. Jogan asks if she can smoke. THe executives tell her yes. JOgan rolls a joint in front of them and smokes it. The executives are reluctant about even paying 100 crore for 3 years. THey think that Jogan can’t walk away from this deal. Jogan ups the price and tells them that now she is demanding 250 crores for 3 years. The executives laugh at her. She takes a drag and tells them, “Mere average listeners har month 4 crore hain. Isme se 20 percent mere exclusive hote hi tumhare app par aa jayenge.” THe executive laugh at the 20% number. Jogan calmly tells her that 20% is a lower estimate. She has had a 30% conversion rate for all her endorsements. The executives shut up. Jogan then calmly explains that she is willing to shave down a further 5% for the overlap (Highly unlikely, because they only have only 5 Lakh paying subscribers right now which means their yearly subscription revenue is 60 crores) that some of her listeners might be on their app. So if they have to pay 100 bucks a month for subscription. Then this deal will make them 700+ crore a year and 2200 crore in 3 years. 250 crores is only gyarah dashamlav something something of that. 
The executives seem impressed. Some of them also seem high. But it looks like they like the deal. They tell Jogan that they will have to confer with their investors. But this looks like it will go ahead. Jogan tells them to add a helicopter in the contract. The executives are confused and ask why. Jogan tells them to bring my guests to Almora.

At some point in the story. RAj and Nawaz go to meet Jogan. BUt she ignores them. Then RAj shouts, hum KAsar se hain. Jogan stops and takes them in. She talks to them about KAsar. THey exchange notes. Talk about people they both know. THen RAj tells Jogan that he wants to come on her podcast. Jogan laughs at this. She tells them to fuck off. RAj is adamant and asks Jogan why can’t they be on the podcast. Jogan tells them that for them to come on her podcast they need to be some expert or artist, or should have a great story to tell the world. OR reveal corruption or something. Raj tells her that he knows about a troll farm. He knows how it works. Jogan tells him it is interesting. But it’s not enough. THey need to get more. IF they can have hard evidence. Videos, voice recording, pictures. She could maybe think about it. 
Raj and Nawaz will take this up and do a sting operation to get dirt on the troll farm. Make this funny. Put jokes and gags.
