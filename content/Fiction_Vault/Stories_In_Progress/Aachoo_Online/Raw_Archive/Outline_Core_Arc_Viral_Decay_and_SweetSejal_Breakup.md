Acchoo is an influencer wannabe. 2 years ago one his idiotic dance video got viral. He gained thousands of followers overnight. He thought he had become famous. But his fame lasted for 2 seconds. For that little time, suddenly he got endorsements, he was being called for events, he made a bit of money. He left his job unceremoniously, closing all doors to ever go back. 

For a while people enjoyed, laughed, copied his viral dance, but soon they got bored. Acchoo had nothing to follow up his tiny success. How long can people really enjoy nonsense. He tried everything to keep his followers. He made skin care tutorials, travel videos, followed all the trends, took up all the viral challenges. Nothing worked. His followers slowly went away. At it’s peak, he had more than 2 lakh followers, but now he has 12,383… And decreasing further.

He desperately wants to become famous, because he believes they live such lavish, adventurous and unique lifestyle. All common people do is follow a pattern. Get up at the same time everyday, go to the office, come back home, eat dinner and sleep. Repeat the same thing over and over. While famous people live such fabulous lives. Viewed and loved by millions. He wants to do the same. 

He heard someone once say, fake it till you make it. He follows that principle to pretend he is what he wants to be. Rich and famous. He will go to any lengths to achieve that. Go to fancy restaurants and hotels to pretend he is staying there. 

He has a girlfriend, SweetSejal, who has over 2 lakh followers. SweetSejal, is a fashion and lifestyle influencer. Her goal is to become a heroine in Bollywood. Sejal initially dated Acchoo because she thought he had great prospects, but now, with his followers at 12000, she want to break up with him. 

Acchoo doesn’t have new content to post. He has been posting the same thing over and over for a while. He has to beg for likes from people he knows, how can he expect other people to like his content. He has to do more. He realizes he needs to do exciting things to become famous. 

Acchoo goes to an agency to ask for a collaboration and endorsements. The agency guy, his friend, tells him that he has too few followers for any collab. For him to work with them, he will need to have at least 2 lakh followers. He suggests Acchoo to become socially conscious. Try to post about the things going on in the country, take a stand. 

**********
Dabboo is Acchoo’s roommate. He works in an IT company. He is bored at his job. He finds Acchoo’s life really exciting. At office he is like a drone. Doing what he is told. Same kind of work again and again. He hangs with Acchoo to get a taste of the life he wants. 

**********
