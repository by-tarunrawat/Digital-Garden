Chapter 1: Vigyapan, Shohrat aur baaki Drugs

“Mai famous to hun, par abhi sabko batana baaki hai”
- Aaroh Chaurasia (Deleted)
- @AachaGuy1999

1. The camera explores the dingy bathroom, a cacophony of sounds. Dance music, Slow Music, Comedy clips with laughter tracks, Someone’s skincare routine (Lingers), sponsored post. The camera finally stops at Aaroh, sitting on the pot, scrolling through his phone. He keeps scrolling with a disinterested look. But he keeps scrolling. The speed of his scrolling increases. It seems like it will never stop. And we keep staring at him doing this mindless thing to the point of irritation. THe scrolling picks up speed. He doesn’t stop even for 3 seconds. It looks like he is in a trans. 
Door banging. “Kar Li potty?”
Aaroh’s trans is broken, he puts the phone on the basin and puts pressure on his sphincter. He shouts, “Ho Gaya. 5 second.”
He takes the handshower to clean his ass. 

The door opens. Aaroh nods to his roommate, Kungfu. Kungfu has a glass of protein shake in his hand. He telle Aaroh to cut some fruits for breakfast. Aaroh tells him okay. 
He goes to the kitchen. Picks one apple, two banana’s, an orange. His phone beeps. It’s a message on a whatsapp group. He opens it. It’s a video of a Anjali Baba teaching ways to get amazing shiny hair by natural means. You need to use Reetha, Sheekakayi, Neem and Cow urine and Anjali baba ji’s saliva. Mix it all up and wash your hair with it. But Thankfully, Anjali Baba has already done all the work for you. Presenting, Baba Anjali’s Thookesh Kranti Shampoo. He is about to click on the link. 
Kungfu comes back, “Tune ab tak fruits nahi kaate?”
“Research kar raha hun.”

2. Profound podcast. The ingenuity of it all. Manaviya avishkaron me sabse behtareen. Mardon ki chaddi. Aur elastic to kamaal ka avishkar hai.
