A billionaire is an influencer. Influences decisions on a global scale. They are controlling health policies, economic policies, and lives of not just countries but continents and the world. 
For example, Bill gates has a charity that supposedly help eradicate malaria in poorer countries. We assume that he is a philanthropic and charitable man. But what if he is not. Can you really trust an individual person to think about the group and other people before themselves. Because to reach that stage where you have made billions of dollars, you need to think about yourself. How can you profit from the market, the things you’ve invested in. What is the worth of YOUR time. You can’t get that rich without thinking about your own personal gain.
You’re giving vaccines and medicines to the poor. Everybody praises you. You’re the new messiah. The people you’re helping are happy. They think you’re the reincarnation of whatever god they worship. You’re a media darling. You come on television interviews around the world talking about the good you’re doing. 
Behind the scenes, you have a huge empire that is growing even quicker because of this. You have invested in companies that are providing the medicines that you’re buying and supplying. Because this the stock of the company goes higher. The PR it gets from your philanthropic activities and association sends the stock further flying. You’re even richer. Even though you’ve spent half your wealth towards charities.
You’ve now invested in health organizations around the world. Global organizations like WHO and doctors without borders. This helps you get the endorsements of these organizations that everyone trusts. There are some shady deals going on. You’re paying people off in addition to funding their organizations. They don’t oppose you and endorse your work and your opinion. 
If today you say a certain medicine is required, these organizations with agree with you. A loarge stock of these medicines will be ordered around the world. If you see sales dropping, you can get vaccines mandated. Changed government policies around the world so that it is compulsory for citizens to take those vaccines in order to live their lives. They can’t go to public places without it. They can’t take flights, trains and any public transports. You’ve made sure that the vaccines are ordered around the world on your say so. People don’t know this, but you’ve done this behind the scenes. Paying off WHO executives and state heads. In turn, you’ve gotten richer than anyone else in the world. 
This is TRUE INFLUENCE. Influencer with millions of followers and fans, celebrities, experts think they have influence and say over general public. They think their followers will do whatever you want them to do. THey think they have power over them. But do they really. It takes one old post, one slip of the tongue, one offended person to crush your influence. The billionaire’s influence is not like that. It’s real. He can sway countries, continent and the world to do whatever you want. 
But are you satisfied with all this? Of course not. This is a game for you now. You want to make as many points (money and wealth) that you possibly can. You want to win. You will do anything to win. Rules of the game don’t mean anything to you now. You make your own rules. If there are rules and laws that stop you from accumulating more points, you just disard them and pay the rulekeepers to make new rules. 
*******
Considering all this, our character, Bilas Gautam, is an Indian multibillionaire. He is in the top 5 richest people in the world. He wants to be at the top. This is a game for him. He wants to win and accumulate as many points he can. 
Bilas Gautam came from an upper middle class family. HIs father was a businessman, so Bilas got a good headstart. After graduating from London School of business, he started his own business, manufacturing and selling computer chips and hardware. After the company became successful, he started making software. His software became ubiquitous. One out of 4 people in the world have his software on their phones and computers. He retired when he was 50. He realized that the company ran itself, and he didn’t need to be there. HE can make more points working somewhere else. 
He announced that he will donate more than half of his wealth to the poor in his own country India, and around the world. He created a Gautam foundation, in which he put more than 50 billion dollars. He started with providing medicines and vaccines to developing nations for free. IN the meanwhile investing in the companies buying the vaccines from. He made loads of money doing that. But people didn’t really know that. 
Then the pandemic hit, Bilas had the opportunity of a lifetime. He bought even more shares in the pharma companies around the world and pressured them into developing the vaccines. When the vaccines were made he pressured WHO and heads of state to endorse the vaccines. When people protested and questioned them, he simply pressured the same leaders into making the vaccines compulsory. 
THe pandemic made him the 3rd richest person in the world.

He developed world class research facilities, where he employed the world’s experts to develop medicines and vaccines. The pandemic taught him one thing: He can’t rely on a global catastrophe to make money. He needs to be more proactive. He needs to find out what are people’s ailments. What are they likely to suffer from. 
He has an idea. He wants to develop a vaccine that can insert a microchip in people. This microchip can not only track people, but can be programmed to find out other things. It can find out people’s blood pressure, heart condition. By sending signals to different parts of the body, it can find out what will people get sick from. 
He has developed the vaccines and tried it out of humans as well. Although this was done secretly. He paid poor individuals to be a part of the trial. A lot of people died at first. But now slowly but surely he has perfected the vaccine. 

Now he is driving a propaganda campaign to get people injected witht he microchip. He has already tested the whole thing in Somalia. A lot of people died at first. But now he can see his microchip working perfectly. He is now bringing it to the rest of the world. 
He has not told anyone what this is. He has published research on a generalized vaccine against all viruses. Articles have been published in magazines and research journals. The scientist who questioned the papers have been paid off. USA and Europeean countries are willing to vaccinate their population. India has been a little difficult, but that has been taken care of. 

The only problem remaining is the people. They are divided. Half of the people around the world are willing to get the vaccine and endorse it. But the other half don’t. They question the need to get vaccinated with somehting they don’t really need. There are conspiracy theorists in these as well. But nobody takes them seriously. Even people who are against the vaccine. 

When the story starts, Bilas Gautam knows that he can’t execute his plan unless he gets people on his side. His plan is to buy influencers and endorse getting vaccinated, and discredit people who don’t by calling them anti vaxxers.
