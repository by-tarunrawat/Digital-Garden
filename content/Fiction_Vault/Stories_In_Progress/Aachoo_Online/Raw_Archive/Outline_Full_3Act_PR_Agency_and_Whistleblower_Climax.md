Fame is everything. It is the pinnacle of human existence. It is a rush to be known by thousands of people. To be adored. To be loved. If you’re famous, opportunities just fall on your feet. Fame opens closed doors. You get respect from people if you’re known by hundreds of thousands of people. 
And it’s not just for yourself. You can really make a difference in the world if so many people are listening to you. You can achieve good in the world. Ordinary people can’t do that. Even if they try to do something good, there is no one watching it. No one is getting inspired by it. What’s the point of that? If people don’t see what you’re doing then is there even a point to doing it? 

Opening:
Acchoo is a small time influencer. A few thousand followers of his vlogs. He pretends to have a great lifestyle. Eating fancy food, staying in expensive resorts, driving SUVs and stuff. He justifies it as giving aspiration to his followers. If they see him live this life, they will work hard to achieve it. 

He has other influencer friends, who have more followers than him, drive fancier cars and wear expensive clothes. Acchoo feels less-than with them and wants to become a bigger influencer than them. 
1. Sneaking his way into a hotel, clicking pictures and running away. 
2. Going to the same cafe because people recognize him there. 
3. Convincing people into doing what he wants
4. Fake gym shots. 
5. True support from family and friends. Large number. Some incident happened. He live broadcasts and 100s of people show up. 
6. Dissatisfied. He sees other influencer living their best life and his own life feels empty. He is faking everything while other people are actually living. 

Inciting incident:
He is in an event where an MP has come for inauguration. 
Protesters surround the area, demanding explanations from the MP for a scandal he is supposed to be involved in. The MP is blocked inside the event and can’t go out. 
Acchoo comes to his rescue convincing the protesters to let the MP go. 
MP tells one of his advisor, sosal baba, to talk to Acchoo. 
Sosal Baba approaches Acchoo and offers him a great opportunity. To really influence people, since he wants to be an influencer. 
Acchoo refuses, but sosal baba tells him that he needs people like him who can really make a difference in the country. 


Acchoo goes back home. His interaction with the protesters has been recorded on video and is getting viral. 

At first Acchoo is excited by this. But then he sees abuses being hurled at him in the comments. He is being heavily criticized. His followers start dropping. He panics and posts an apology video. The video is even more offensive and puts him in more and more trouble. He loses most of his followers.



First Act break:
Acchoo has no idea what to do. He panics. He DMs his influencer friends, but they don’t reply and block him. He has no other options left.  Then he remembers sosal baba’s offer. He gives sosal baba a call. Sosal baba asks Acchoo to meet him tomorrow. 

Acchoo meets sosal baba at an office. Sosal baba shows him around and tells him that this office that they are about to start, it has a studio to shoot videos, equipped with the best system. He tells Acchoo that this is a Communication and PR agency.
Sosal baba wants to hire Acchoo to work for him in the agency. Acchoo is reluctant and tells him, “Office me hi kaam karna hota to influencer thodi na banta” 
Sosal baba explains, “Tujhe content banane se koi nahi rok raha. You’ll still make content. You can make content in the studio. There is a whole production team to help you. Here you have a chance to influence lakhs, even crores of people. 
He introduces Acchoo to one of the employees. Acchoo recognizes him. He is an influencer called Bollybuff. 
BollyExplains that when he joined, he had 4000 followers. Now he has 2 lakh. 
Acchoo doesn’t understand what is the catch. Sosal baba tells him that there is no catch. THe videos he has to make will be for the clients of the agency. Like Bolly buff’s clients are Akshay Kumar and Bobby deol. So he promotes their movie, their brands. FOr example when there was a controversy about Akshay endorsing a gutkha brand, then Bolly buff made a series of videos defending him that went viral. You just have to think of it as, the client is the brand you are endorsing and make sponsored posts. 
Acchoo understands this. This is a win win situation for him. He agrees. 
Sosal baba is happy and shows him the IT department. IT’s the most important part of the office. These are the people that make your videos viral. 

2nd Act:

Sosal baba tells Acchoo that his first client is the MP that he helped at the inauguration. It is only logical that Achoo would talk about him after the whole controversy. 

Acchoo makes a video praising the MP and defending him from the protesters. Making shit up, and lying in the video. Sosal baba is impressed. 

Acchoo goes home. While watching TV, his phone starts buzzing. He thinks it’s a call. But it’s notification coming one after the other. His video has gone viral. Viewed by lakhs of people, and liked by thousands. Aacchoo Is ecstatic. 
He makes more videos. Defending other clients. Telling lies. 

His follower count is also increasing. He is being followed by lakhs of people. He is being called to collab with other big influencers. He moves into a better house and now can post real pictures of his house. 


But apart from the love he is getting from his followers, he is also getting a lot of hate. This starts bothering him. 

He shrugs it off as now he is being called on news network to give his opinion, as a youth thought leader. Acchoo is loving it. 

He finds out about one of the incidences he was involved in where the people got seriously hurt. This affects him. HE starts questioning what he has been doing. 

Midpoint:
He talks to sosal baba, who reveals his dark side and tells Acchoo to continue with the work he is doing. 

Acchoo is disturbed and visits the protesters against whom he spoke and other victims.

He then sees the IT department and how it works by inflating followers and likes by using fake accounts and bots. There is a whole sub division of junior IT associates, whose job it is to start arguments in comments and troll people. These trolls are dissatisfied with their lives and disillusioned. 


Acchoo realizes the folly of his ways.  

He hatches a plan. He decides to use the agency’s resources to make a series of videos about the plight of the victims and the whole manipulation game. 
The IT department, doesn’t see what the video is. So they will make it viral. More and more people will see the real issue. 
He makes the videos. IT causes even more chaos and riots.

2nd Act break:
Sosal baba realizes what Acchoo is doing and stops him. Sosal baba’s boss, Mr Jagani talks to Achoo. He gives him a spiel about the principles the world runs on. There is a queen bee, who everyone follows. Whatever they will do is determined by the queen bee. That is their role. To tell the bees of the world what to do. If people are not told what to do or think, then things like this would happen. So, we have to do our role so that they know what their role is. 
Acchoo doesn’t have a response. He has lived all his life on the same principles. How can he say otherwise. 

3rd Act: 

Mr Jagani gives Acchoo a chance. He needs to make another vlog and tell the world what he did was fake. If he doesn’t then, Acchoo will lose all his followers. It will not be a problem for someone with his resources to suspend his accounts permanently. His entire existence will be wiped clean from the internet. 

Acchoo doesn’t have a choice. He has to do what Jagani has said. 

Something makes Acchoo remember the words his mother said before. It’s not about influnce or following, it’s about trust. How many people trust you. Trust in you that is important. Because that is the hard thing to do. Even if 1 person trusts you. You are extremely lucky. 

Acchoo has many people, the victims and the protesters who trust him to do the right thing. He goes to the studio for a live broadcast on his mobile and reveals the entire plan and the agency. 
He runs around the office with Sosal Baba, Mr Jagani and other goons in tow. He records and broadcasts everything. Revealing the workings of the entire office. 
Sosal Baba finally catches hold of Acchoo. Right at the time, police arrives. Acchoo is relieved that the real culprits will be caught now. 
At the end we reveal that Acchoo is in jail, More famous than ever. 

The last shot of him going to the jail, with several people crowding him and clicking pictures from their phones. A slight smile on his face.
