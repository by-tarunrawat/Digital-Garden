HE wants to be a brand ambassador. He finds an opening on a job portal and applies. He gets the job. It’s 30K per month. HE goes to delhi. 
First day on the job. HE is dressed funky and fashionable. He realizes it’s a sales job. He decides to leave the office. HIs parents call and ask about the first day at the job. He lies to them and tells them that his first day has been good. He has to make content for the company. 

He goes back and begs for the job back. 
 
Dhenpu tells him that nobody knows that he is working as a salesman. He can do posts about the product as brand endorsements. 


***********

What if he gets followers pretending he is somebody else. Maybe he pretends he is gay, Or maybe he thwarts a terrorist attack. 

He gets followers because they think he did something brave and heroic.

His followers start increasing. 

He becomes the face of anti-terror and starts getting brand endorsements. 


***********

Everyone wants likes. An event, a celebration, a moment hasn’t even happened if you’ve not captured it on your phone and posted it on social media. Likes, shares, comments gives it meaning. If people haven’t liked the photo that you’ve posted, you have failed in this social struggle to have more followers, make viral videos, capture every moment that you’ve lived. But it’s okay, there is always another moment to create. 

Another moment to create. Everyone is creating moments, not living them. Doing things because they will get more likes than something else you might have wanted to do. Have opinions about everything. And you must have the right opinion. Your opinion can’t be too different from the people who follow you. Otherwise you will be cancelled, your followers will leave you. That would be a disaster. You have become a social pariah. You might even lose your job. 

Can you really have your own opinion if there are such severe consequences? 
________

Influencers, thought leaders, celebrities. Everybody loves them. They have followers who would fight, threaten and harass each other. Who is this person they are fighting for? What has this person done for them? Sure, there might be a few stories where someone was truly, truly impacted by a celebrity. It changed their lives. Helped them get through a difficult time. But for most, it seems they’ve decided someone they idolize and would not hear anything against them. 

How far would you go for your idols?
_____________

Followers believing your every word. Ready to fight for you. What power to have. Some would say it’s also a responsibility. Those people underestimate the human tendency for idol worship. There are so many examples of fans/followers going after critics. INdia’s PM has built a huge cult of followers that is fueled by manipulating opinions on social media and news. That’s real power and influence. To have an entire population stand behind you to kill or to die. 

Can you let people die to gain this power? Can you let injustice run rampant?

Story of the influncers and the lengths they can go to maintain their influence over thousands and millions of followers. 

Story of common people who get sucked into this vortex of this aspirational celebrity and fame. 

Story of the lengths powerful people can go to maintain their power over people.
