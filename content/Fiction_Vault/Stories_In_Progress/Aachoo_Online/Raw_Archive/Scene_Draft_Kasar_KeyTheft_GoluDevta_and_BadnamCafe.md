Acchoo wakes up in the morning and with tiptoes goes to his father’s room and steal bike keys, tied to his naada. 

Acchoo goes to the gym, hoping to convince the gym owner to let them in. After a lot of negotiations on how many equipments he can touch, the owner agrees. Achoo changes into gym clothes and does a photoshoot with dumbbells. Posts it on his social media, with an inspiring caption. 

Golu devta, for followers. He gets flocked here. Lots of people crowd him. He feels good about that. Young girls and boys want to get their selfie clicked with him. This makes him feel good. He comes here especially for this adoration. 

Later, Acchoo and Dhenpu discussing what place to go for breakfast. They’ve already gone to every place in the entire district too many times already. Now no one will offer them free meals anymore. They decide to go to their usual adda Badnam cafe and convince GeeGro Saab to give them a free breakfast. 

At Badnam cafe, Achoo tries convincing Geegro. But Geegro is not ready to give them anything for free. So, Achhoo offers to work for a day at the cafe. Geegro agrees. 

Acchoo clicks a few breakfast pictures. #Naashta #Influencer life. Now Achoo has to work at the cafe. His friend Dhenpu refuses to help and makes fun of him all day. Achoo also tries to sneak a few pictures with other people’s food, but Geegro is keeping an eye on him. 
Now, because Achoo has posted pictures of himself on the social media, more people start coming in to the cafe, increasing Acchoo’s workload. 
Acchoo doesn’t want people to know that he is working at the cafe. He pretends to be hanging around, and making lame excuses for why he is bringing people food, and cleaning tables. At the end of the day, Achoo and Dhenpu get another meal. Achoo is so tired that he doesn’t even bother to click photos. 

When he comes home, expecting a scolding, his father is drinking. His father offers Achhoo a drink as well. They talk about Achoo’s future. His father asks what does he expect to do in the future. Dad gives him the freedom to choose what he wants to do. Even whatever this influencer thing is. He believes a person should be free to choose what they are going to do for the rest of their lives. And Achoo has to decide now. He can be this influencer, digital creator, but there needs to be a plan. It cant go on like this. His dad wants him to be active and go after what he wants.
