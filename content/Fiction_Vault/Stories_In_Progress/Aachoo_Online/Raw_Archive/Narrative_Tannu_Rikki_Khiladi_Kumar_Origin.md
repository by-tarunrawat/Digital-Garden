Rajkumar and Nawaz are two friends in a small village. Their parents are farmers and well to do. Rajumar and Nawaz were 16 years old and had passed 10th when they both got smartphones as their dads promised. They loved their village, but there was nothing to do. 


are both addicted to social media. Rajkumar and Nawaz think they are social media influencers. The village has 900 people but Rajkumar has 1536 followers and Nawaz has 1200 followers. 

**************





Act 1:

1. Opening Image (1)
A village. Tannu(24) and Rikki (26). Social media obsessed. They always fail at whatever they are doing. THey try to do a stunt. But are both scared. Eventually, in attempting the stunt they destroy someone else’s property. The villagers are angry. THey’ve done shit like this before as well. Tannu and Rikki tell them that they are trying to make a viral video to become famous. 

2. Theme stated (5)
The villagers are fed up of Tannu and Rikki’s desire to become famous. The villagers tell them that you need to have talent to become famous. Rikki is worried that they have no talent and asks Tannu about it. Tannu tells him that the villagers are wrong. You don’t need talent to become famous. You can become famous overnight in todays world. 

3. Setup (1-10)
Tannu and Rikki’s family is fed up with them. They do nothing all day, but watch tik tok videos and social media.
Tannu and Rikki are delusional that they will make silly videos and become famous. After that money will pour in and they will just endorse brands and these companies will pay them money. 
Rikki is older but always follows Tannu’s lead. Tannu and Rikki were childhood friends. Rikki was given the responsibility as the elder to take care of tannu. But it was always Tannu who would assume the leadership position and get rikki in trouble. 
Once when they were 10-12, Tannu convinced Rikki to release Sushma aunty’s cow, so they could ride it around the village. They got caught and Rikki got the beating of a lifetime. Tannu could’ve run. But he stayed back and threw rocks at the Sushma aunty. He eventually got caught as well. They were both beaten, then taken to thier families and then beaten by their mothers as well. 
Tannu and Rikki became best friends. Rikki always knew Tannu is going to get him in trouble. But it will always be fun and he was sure Tannu would be right in the middle of it with him. 
Tannu’s mantra to making decisions is, WHAT WOULD KHILADI KUMAR DO? 
Tannu’s dad was an akshay kumar fan. He had all his movies on DVD and would watch the khiladi series, mohra, Akshay kumar fighting wrestlers, riding Airplanes. Tannu became an even bigger fan. His mantra to decide anything is what would khiladi kumar do? He sees a wall and instead of going around it, he would think, what would khiladi kumar do? And then try to jump over the wall. He was never able to do it. He wasn’t particularly athletic or anything. He always failed at these silly stunts, but he never stopped trying. 
As a kid also, he started having a reputation in his class of a spoilt child. It wasn’t Tannu’s fault. He always did what khiladi kumar would do? Homework over cricket? Khiladi kumar would go play cricket. Studying over TV? OF course khiladi kumar would watch TV. Teachers were fed up and parents didn’t want their kids to be friends with him. 
This is when Tannu met Rikki. He thought Rikki was funny, how he says yes to everything. Tannu never told Rikki about his mantra. He wanted Rikki to think he was cool. But he always got Rikki in trouble and always felt bad about it. But Rikki always kept coming back, like a protective big brother. Tannu always thought that because he is so stupid, Rikki is always around him to save him.  
Their families are fed up of the fact that even thought they are 24-26, they don’t work and earn a living. They are becoming a burden on their families. 
They are becoming a nuisance for the villagers, who are fed up of their stunts. They decide that it is time Tannu and Rikki are sent out to the city to make it on their own. 

4. Catalyst (12)
During one of their stunts. They accidentally damage the dam and put the entire village in danger. 
The villagers finally have lost patience with them and tell them to stop their stunts or they will kill someone one day. 
Tannu is also fed up of everybody complaining. He gives a big speech about how everyone is jealous of him. He tells them that he will go the city, become an influencer and get famous. Rikki is taken aback by this. The village is the only place he has known. Tannu convinces him that this is going to be great. No family nagging, no complains from the villagers. Like always, Rikki agrees.
