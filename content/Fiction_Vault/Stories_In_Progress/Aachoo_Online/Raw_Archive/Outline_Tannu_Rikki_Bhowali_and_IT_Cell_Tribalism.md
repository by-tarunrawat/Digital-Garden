2 young boys, wannabe influencers + are kicked out of their village because of their actions + they vow to become famous and go to the city + only to find out they have no talent or skills to do so.

2 young boys, who want to become influencers, and do crazy things to become famous + They are kicked out of their village after a stupid stunt goes wrong + They go to the city with a dream of becoming famous + only to find out that it takes more than talent to make it. 


************

Tannu and Rikki are friends, living in village Bhowali. They both live with their parents. They do nothing all day. They are Vloggers without too many followers. Their parents are fed up of them doing nothing but recording themselves all day. Tannu and RIkki tell them that they are going to become influencers and get famous. Rikki is scared and wonders whether they can become famous. It’s not like they have any talent. Tannu tells him that today you don’t need to be talented to become famous. 

Both, Tannu and Rikki’s parents plan to get rid of them. They give them some money, book them a ticket to Dehradun and throw them out. They tell them to find jobs or become influencers or whatever, but they need to fend for themselves. 

THEREFORE

Tannu and RIkki go to Dehradun. They find a PG to live in.  There are other kids around them, who are going to college and trying to get jobs. They try to do one of their videos. But fail and cause a lot of damage to the PG. The landlady warns them and tells thenm that she will throw them out if they continue this nonsense. Now they don’t know what to do. One of the kids in the PG tells them that a local agency is hiring influencers and will pay according to their following. This seems like a job that they are willing to do. 

Therefore

They go to the agency and interview for the influencer position. The interviewer sees that they only have 1200-1500 followers and laughs them off. He tells then that they won’t be hired with these many followers and will have to be at least at 10K.

But

 Tannu and Rikki are desperate and tell the interviewer that they are willing to do anything. Endorse any brand, say anything they want them to say. The interviewer tells them that he might have a job for them and calls one of his subordinates. He tells the subordinate to put them in the budding influencer IT Cell. 
The subordinate, Dinesh Negi, shows them around the office. There are cubicles, but they are segregated based on teams. A celebrity team (Farhan khan - big team, Reetika RAducone - medium team), Political team (Indian people party (IPP) - big team, Bhartiya RAshtriya conference (BRC) - big team, and Common man party (CMP) - one person) 
Dinesh gives them their cubicles and tells them that they will now be a part of CMP IT team. Their job will be to track all the conversations around them and reply back. Share articles about the party. 

Tannu and Rikki learn the tools of the trade. THey see how the place is working. There are rivalries between teams about their celebrity/political party. The rivalry turns to sabotage. Everyone is trying to get dirt on the other. Tannu realizes that you can lie online, say anything you want without any concequences. One time, Tannu gets too far. But his boss is impressed that he took initiative. The boss puts him in the CMP team. 

Tannu is happy, They lie about the other parties. Sudden;y everyone is talking about CMP. Tannu has done a great job. 

The local leaders of BRC and IPP come to the agency and complain to the owner. THe owner shows them that he is not favouring CMP. The team still has only 3 people. The leaders are not satisified and want Tannu on their own team. There is a fight to get tannu on their team.
