Acchoo is a podcaster and an aspiring influencer in Delhi. He lives in a small room, in a 3 room house with 2 other flatmates. He is broke and struggles to pay the bills and his share of the house rent. His roommates give him an ultimatum to pay up his previous dues or leave the house. They already have a replacement for him.
Acchoo has been trying to get sponsors for his social media and podcast. But he doesn’t even get a meeting with the brand managers of any companies. After he gets the ultimatum he decides to sneak his way into a meeting with a brand manager. The brand manager is somewhat sympathetic and tells Acchoo that he will need at least 100K followers to get a sponsorship from them. 

Acchoo is desperate and goes to a man called Sosal Baba. Sosal baba is known to increase followers and get engagement for celebrities and politicians. Acchoo makes a plea to sosal baba to get his followers to 1 lakh. 1 lakh is a small number for sosal baba. He is used to dealing with millions of people. But Acchoo is desperate, so he tells him to fork over 5 lakh rupees. Achoo doesn’t have that kind of money but wants the followers. Sosal baba gives him a deal. He asks Acchoo to make the content Sosal baba tells him to and he will get him more than a lakh followers on his social media. Acchoo agrees. 

Acchoo is handed over to another executive Achraj bhai who will be helping him. Achraj bhai gives him a spiel about what kind of content sells. “Fasadon, haadson, jungon me hi hum ek hote hain. Koi aafat na aaye to, koi apna nahi hota.” He tells Acchoo to either be a victim, or controversial. He has an army of people who will share his content everywhere. He will get likes and followers. 

Acchoo goes back to make controversial content. What he says is pretty innocuous. He makes videos with mild criticism or offense. Like irritating people on the road in the name of playing pranks. But, he doesn’t get any likes or new followers. Achraj bhai is under a lot of pressure from Sosal Baba already from not meeting his targets. He tells them to do something big. Dramatic. Filmen wagerah nahi dekhte kya. 

Dhenpu has an idea. He tells Acchoo that they should hire actors, who will pose and goons and Acchoo can beat them up. It works on heroines in movies. It will work for him too. Acchoo agrees. 
Acchoo aconvnices his two roommates to do it. After a lot of negotiations, they agree.

On the day itself. Acchoo’s roommates come in a disguise. One wearing a beard and the other a burkha. Acchoo is horrified to see the guy in a burkha. He tells them that he can’t beat up a woman. It will look bad on the video. The roommates are irritated by Acchoo’s unreasonable demands and beat him up instead. In the chaos of it all. One of the guy’s mustache falls off. He looks like a maulvi. The whole video is recorded. 

They show it to Achraj Bhai. Disappointed that they have failed. But Achraj tells them that this is gold. He tells them to post the video. Acchoo does. 

The video goes viral. Acchoo makes more videos. Talking about the trauma of the whole situation. Everyone is talking about it. Aacchoo is praised as brave. His followers increase to higher numbers. Almost 5 lakh. 

The video catches national attention. Abhijeet Rane, news anchor manipulates the narrative to make it a hindu muslim issue. 

Acchoo is called to studio to share his views. He has truly become famous as his follower count is still shooting up. HIs relatives are calling back. People in the street are recognizing him. His dreams are coming true. 

He is on TV, Ads. Making appearances on daily soap, saying his catch phrase.

He goes on the show love beach for an episode as a special guest. There he meets Priyadarshini. Who gives him a piece of her mind, how fake he has become.  
He tells her that she is fake as well and she has no right to tell him anything.
