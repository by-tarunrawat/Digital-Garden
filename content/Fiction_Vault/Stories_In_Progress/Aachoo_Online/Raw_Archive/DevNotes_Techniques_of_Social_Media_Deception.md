What does everyone wants from social media and the internet?
Fame. Likes. Followers. Money. 

How far will I go to get these as a normal person? 
1. I will post regularly. Document everything I do. Give it some kind of spin to make it look cool. I would misappropriated shers and poems of others and post it as if they are my own. 
2. Maybe post some old videos or photos of travels I’ve been on with some poetry or something. If I’m okay with slight deception (mamuli dhoka), I would post some of those pictures making it look like I’m there now. I can use and reuse pictures to show how well traveled I am. What a life I’m living. 
3. I will post food photographs. Anytime I go to a restaurant, the first thing I would do is post pictures. For this, I would have to convince my friends to come to these fancy places. I don’t have money. So, I would order something cheap like, fries. But, I would take pictures of my friend’s food and post it as if my own. This way I can post one now and another later to show I go to these fancy places every other day. I would find opportunities to hold their drinks and click pictures to show how much fun I’m having. 
4. I will post photographs of nice clothes. Whenever I buy something new I would click a picture. Sometimes I go to the store to try on clothes, then click selfies of me wearing the clothes I’m trying and post it as if they are my own. I borrow clothes from friends all the time. Some of them have branded shirts and T shirts. Sometimes I would tag the brand to show that the brand has ‘maybe’ sponsored me. I would often give a coupon code, Accho30. As if using that coupon will get you a 30% discount. The coupon is fake. 
5. I have a morning wake up routine. But I can’t look like how I actually look like when I get up. So I have to wake up early, bathe, dry my hair and comb them, apply moisturizer. Then I would get into bed to show #wokeuplikethis. 
I go to the gym. Because I don’t like working out, I don’t really have a membership. So, I have to sneak in. I spray some water on my face to show sweat. Pose with dumbells and exercise machine. 10 minutes and I’m out. 
6. If I had some kind of talent like music, art, pottery, etc. I would have an easier time making the content. If I’m a musician, I can post original tunes. If that doesn’t work, I could post popular song covers. Same with art. I could post whatever I’m draing or painting. Post BTS/process. If that doesn’t work, I could post paintings of celebs and famous people. I would tag them to get likes from their fans. This is not exactly shady. Is it selling your soul even. Not that big of a deal, right? I mean, it’s a way to sell yourself. Although, what if that doesn’t work either? Even if I’m a good singer or a painter, I don’t get followers or likes. How far would I go now? I don’t have any integrity left. I don’t have money. I don’t have any prospects. I will associate my work with political and civil causes. I will keep it vague so as to not piss of either groups. Still dedicate the piece to the cause and people associated with it. That might get me a few more followers. 
7. If I was how boy or a girl, there are a few more options apart from talent. I could always be a thirst trap. If I have great body. I would flaunt it. Any excuse to wear skimpy clothes. Pose seductive pictures. Post seductive dance videos. Post naughty captions, leaving hints how I’m looking for someone. I want my followers to think I’m available for them. They might have a chance with me. I would post things like looking for a guy/girl who is honest, reliable, and other generic qualities that everyone thinks they have. This would make me think he/she is attainable. If that doesn’t work, I will go full vulgar. I will have to show more skin. Post suggestive pictures and videos. It will be a sleazefest.
