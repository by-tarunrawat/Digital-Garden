1. The dream. 
	A. Aaroh is going on his Scooty, when he sees a car broken down on the side of the road. He stops to investigate and finds Juhi Chawla. “Aila Juhi Chawla”. Juhi tells him that her car has broken down and she is stuck in the middle of nowhere. She asks if Aaroh would give him a lift. Aaroh first asks if he can take a selfie with her. Juhi agrees. She gets on the scooty, but Aaroh is still posting the selfie on the internet with hashtags and stuff. 
He drops Juhi at an ad film location. 
	B. Aaroh and Juhi out for a coffee. Where Juhi is pouring her heart out. Aaroh is taking picktures of the croissants. Juhi is slightly irritated. 
	C. Aaroh and Juhi at a KKR game. He meets Shahrukh and boasts about himself. During the match Juhi takes selfies with Shahrukh, celebrating KKR win. This irritates Aaroh
	D. Aaroh and Juhi meet outside, where Aaroh tells her that she should take selfies with only him. Juhi proposes that they get married. Aaroh has a lightbulb moment. He agrees. 
	E. Juhi and Aaroh are at a mandir, They are about to put varmalas on each other, but the camera guy is fucking up. Aaroh stops the ceremony to fix the camera. They are about to put varmalas again, but the lighting is wrong. He fixes the lighting. Now everything is set. They are about to put varmalas on each other when suddenly. 

2. The reality. 
Aaroh is being woken up by his father. He is disappointed that he was about to become famous and get loads of followers. His father doesn’t understand. He tells him to get a job. Aaroh tells him he already has a job.
	A. Aaroh washes his face. Applies cream and makeup. Then goes back to bed again. HE pretends to wakeup and on his camera he tells his 2000 followers that he has just woken up. He boasts about the glow he has in the morning because of his nightly routine, that he will post later. 

3. Aaroh’s life/ Day to Day
He is on his phone all day. His mother and father keep telling him to do some house work, but he tells them he is working. He keeps messaging big influencers for collab opportunities. He has sent hundreds of messages to people, but without any reply. 
He has a few friends that he has convinced that he is a big shot. In fact, for them, he is a big shot. These are young men with 100, 200 followers. So Aaroh with his following of 2000 people is a big deal for them. He believes that he will become an even bigger influencer. The only problem is his circumstances. He is in a small town with limited opportunities. If he was in a city like dehli or Bombay he would do much better. He tells people that he will go to the big city, when the time is right. In reality, he has no money to go anywhere. 

4. Sponsorship. 
Aaroh doesn’t have money. But he likes to go to restaurants and cafes. He often works out deals, where he gets free food and drinks in exchange for posting about the resturants online. In reality, he cheats. 
For example: He goes to a mountain view cafe and talks to the owner about a brand collab. The owner doesn’t need it. IN fact, he believes that Aaroh can’t possibly help his business. Aaroh tells him that he can show the owner the power of his influence. He posts online that he is at the cafe. Soon enough his friends show up. Telling the manager/owner that they have come here on Aaroh’s recommendation. THe manager is impressed, but still reluctant to offer free stuff. Aaroh finally threatens him. He tells him that if he can recommend the business, he can very easily destroy it also. He can cancel the restaurant. They have a showdown, where, Aaroh writes a post criticizing the cafe. HE tells the owner that he is going to post it online if he doesn’t get the free service. THe owner finally relents. 
Aaroh takes pictures and posts them online. Showing what a lavish lifestyle he is living. 

5. His detractors
He is often criticized by his relatives and neighbors. In response, he cancels them. You and I would think, what would that even achieve, but in his world, by canceling people like that, they in turn are criticized and shunned by people in their circle. 
For example: His chacha comes to his house, bringing a job opportunity for him, in Delhi. This irks Aaroh. Not only does he refuse the job, he even cancels his chacha online. His chacha now, is treated badly at home. He is refused ration at the local general store. Even when he goes to a restaurant, he gets poor service. 
Finally his chacha has to apologize to Aaroh. Of which Aaroh makes a video and posts online. 

6. The girlfriend. 
Aaroh is in a relationship with Stuti. He is planning to propose to her. He calls Stuti to the lovers point for a date. But hte whole thing is staged. Stuti starts the conversation about Aaroh and her future. Aaroh decides, this is the right time to propose. He has his friends ready to take a video of the whole thing. He goes down on one knee and proposes to Stuti. He shows her  a ring and tells her to marry him. But he fumbles. He is extremely irritated and throws a fit. He tells her that he has to do it again. Stuti looks at the ring and asks Aaroh how much did it cost. Aaroh tells her that it’s his mom’s ring. He has borrowed it only for this proposal video. He needs to return it to her. Stuti is shocked. Aaroh does another take but Stuti stops him. SHe breaks up with him telling him that he needs to grow up. This influencer business is not his career. It isn’t even his real self. She can’t be involved with someone who pretends to be someone else all the time. Does things to show other people. Aaroh is shocked. As Stuti is leaving, he stops her and tells her that it’s fine if she doesn’t want to marry him. She just has to say yes. He has done a lot of preparations for this Proposal video. HE has a great idea for a background score. All he needs from her is to say yes, right now, then she can break up with him. Stuti is flabbergasted and leaves.
