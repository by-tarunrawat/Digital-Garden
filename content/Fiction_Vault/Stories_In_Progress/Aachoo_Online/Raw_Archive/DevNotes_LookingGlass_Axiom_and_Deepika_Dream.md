Building character. Acchoo. 

Famous hone ke liye, influencer banne ke liye, dikhana hota hai ki tum successful ho. Tumhari life bahut achi chal rahi hai. Professionally to tum successful ho hi. Tumhari personal life me to bahut aish chal rahi hai. Fancy restaurants, holidays, girlfriend, clubs, new clothes and gadgets and parties. Ye dikhana padta hai, tabhi to log kahenge, bhai jiyo, to iski tarah jiyo. Unko yahi pasand hai. Wo tumko ek khoobsurat ladki ke saath kisi beech par beer peete hue dekhte hain to unhe lagta hai kaash wo bhi ye kar paate. Wo tumhare through apni life jee rahe hain.
