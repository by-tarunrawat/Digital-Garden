Aaja mere yaar… chal party me jaate hain. 
Whiskey, beer ke saath thoda cocaine milaate hain. 
Daaru, ladki, drugs, sab tere liye hai bhai… 
Tu bole to party me karaoke gaate hain. 

TRUST ME BRO!! Entertainment bhi poora hai. 
TRUST ME BRO!! Scene tujh bin adhoora hai. 
TRUST ME BRO!! I’ll take you in my AC car. 
TRUST ME BRO!! Tera bhai bol raha hai yaar. 

Tu chal ke dekh… Wahan snow mountains ki rasam hai. 
White, blue, green, red… Saari goliyon me dum hai. 
Kuch nahi to whiskey, vodka, gin se chala lenge dost. 
Nahi chahiye to bhai, saada ganja kaunsa kam hai.   

TRUST ME BRO!! Saari bottalen nipatayenge. 
TRUST ME BRO!! Wapas taxi me aayenge. 
TRUST ME BRO!! For you my love is Shalimar.  
TRUST ME BRO!! Tera bhai bol raha hai yaar.
