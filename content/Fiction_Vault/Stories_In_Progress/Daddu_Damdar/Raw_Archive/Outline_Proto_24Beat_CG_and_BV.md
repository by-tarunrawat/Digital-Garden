Rajkumar and Nawaz. 
1. Living in the city for a 9 months. They have a individual social media accounts where they are in constant feud with each other. They try to one up each other, or bring each other down. They fight publically, calling each other names. Criticizing their posts. Both of them try to flex to each other. But in reality it’s all fake. Their posts are fake, their rivalry is fake. In fact they are old childhood friends. This is a strategy that they are employing to boost their following. 

2. They are not satisfied as they are not making any money. We see them make videos and posts of fake places. For example, they would put a huge poster of a beach behind them, wear swimming trunks and make videos. THey also make videos to defend people they are following like @Indian_Bhai - who does right wing type videos, @thesassygirl - does lifestyle and fashion videos (Rajkumar has a crush on her.) 

2. They don’t make any money. Rajkumar keeps on assuring Nawaz that soon they will get endorsements from brands, but he doesn’t believe it either. When Nawaz tells him that the villagers also told them that they will never become famous because they don’t have any talent, RAjkumar tells him that you don’t need talent to be famous nowadays. 

3. But they are getting desperate. THey are running out of money, and their parents are forcing them to come back. They had asked their parents to give them a year to prove themselves. IF they don’t become famous and start making money from it, they will come back. Rajkumar will get into farming and NAwaz will marry whatever girl his parents put in front of him. The year is about to end. They have only 3 months left.

4. They find out about this social media and influencer agency that is paying influencers to endorse things for a monthly payment. Rajkumar is deperate to join this agency. They both go and talk to the hiring manager, but the manager shoos them away. He doesn’t think they are popular enough to be hired for something like this. They will need at least 50K followers to get hired. 

5. Rajkumar knows that it is impossible to get that many followers this quickly. So, he decides to do something different. He starts picking up fights on social media. Diss people, abuse them and fight with them. He starts getting engagement and followers. But still not enough. 

4. They find out about this influencer agency that hires influencer and pays them on a monthly basis. 
4. A social media meme page called @SharoneStoner exposes them and 
4. A group called incognito is creating havoc around the world. It’s an anarchist group that makes videos wearing a joker mask, threatening corporations and politicians around the world. They are now after politicians and GHAI (Global health advancement Institute) who are pushing for a new vaccine to be mandated around the world. They have hacked emails from a politician Mickey Chump, Anuj Sahi and GHAI executives, that has created doubts over the vaccine. They have also found emails from a  notable scientist Dr Raja Pancho to the GHAI, where he has expressed doubts over the effectiveness of the vaccines and lack of testing. Soon after this Dr Raja PAncho has disappeared creating even more doubt in their minds. 
They have leaked the emails about these people taking money from a shell company to mandate the vaccines as soon as possible. But because of lack of evidence, nobody takes it seriously.
