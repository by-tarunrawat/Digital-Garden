Akshi Gupta is a poor girl in a rich school. Her parents are lower middle class people. Mom works as a maid/nanny in other people’s houses and dad is a low level government servant. She is smart but regularly gets in trouble at her school, usually because she has gotten into a fight with someone. She always believes she is doing the right thing, but it always backfires. Akshi has a crush on a boy in class but she doesn’t have the courage to talk to him. She has insecurities about her social status. She has a friend, Naomi, in class who encourages her to talk to the boy. Even creates situations for her to get close to him. But Akshi always gets tongue tied. 
Akshi has told people at school that her mom is an interior decorator and her father is a high ranking police officer. She saves money by hustling people and maintains the charade that she is well off. But even with the money she saves she cannot compete with other students in the school. SHe often has to refuse offers to go to clubs and weekend getaways. So she has become an outsider. SHe thinks that if people found out about her she will lose their respect. They will treat her as garbage. 

Akshi meets this strange old homeless man near her house. She talks to him out of curiosity. The old man is cagey about who he is and where has he come from. Akshi starts calling him daddu and develops an affection when she sees how helpless he is. She sees that he also gets these attacks where he forgets who he is. Daddu realizes that the Akshi is a sweet girl and tells her that he is a superhero, but, due to old age, his powers don’t work the way they used to. Akshi, like any sane person would, thinks he is crazy. She decides to stay away from him. 
One day, she sees a few boys bullying him. They have taken down his tarpaulin tent and are kicking him. Akshi cannot stand this and confronts the boys. The boys rough her up as well. Daddu calls them out and tells them that now he will teach them a lesson. HE shouts at Akshi to run. Akshi runs away to get the corner traffic police. WHen she returns the boys are gone and Daddu is on the ground in a fugue state. Akshi takes him to a government hospital to tend to his injuries. He has none. The doctor tells her that he has dementia. Akshi feels bad for him and takes him to his tent and puts it back up for him. She gives him her blanket to sleep comfortably. 
When Akshi returns back to school, she finds out that there is a school trip arranged. All students have to bring one guardian along to come. Naomi encourages Akshi to somehow bring any relative. This is a golden chance to get close to Vibhor. Akshi is in a pickle. His mother and father both refuse. They can’t take leave from their jobs. Akshi decides to bring Daddu. She gives him a bath and a haircut and takes him as his grandfather. 
At the zoo, Akshi talks to Vibhor. Vibhor is dumb and sweet. Vibhor’s friends take him away. Naomi goads Akshi to talk to Vibhor. She forgets about Daddu to go talk to him. She attempts to get into the group to get close to Vibhor. Vibhor talks about how he wants to become a police officer, but his parents keep telling him that he has to take over their multimillion dollar business. Life is so hard! Tch tch tch. Akshi blurts out that her father is in the police. Akshi lies that her father is a top ranking official and he has caught many big name criminals like, (made up names) Pappu dadiyal, Danger kaana, Bahut chota rajan etc. Vibhor is impressed. He is behaving nicely towards her but she can’t get any time alone with her. At the end she makes a fool of herself and runs away when people are laughing at her. 

She walks around the zoo alone, vowing to get back at everyone. At the end of the trip, when everyone has gone, Akshi panics when she can’t find daddu. Vibhor sees her and offers to help. THey look around the zoo. An aunty standing alone outside the bear enclosure sees daddu inside. Akshi sees that daddu has opened the gate of the enclosure. She sees that the lock is crushed and daddy is inside in his fugue state. She sees that the bear has not yet noticed daddu so she can probably go in and bring him out. She tells the aunty to get some zoo authority. Aunty is panicking at this point. She runs to get someone. 

Akshi walks carefully, but she accidentally steps on a twig. This catches the bear’s attention. He first notices daddu and goes to check on him. Akshi panics and makes a noise to get the bear’s attention. The bear runs towards her. She runs towards the gate. SHe runs around the enclosure trying to get rid of the bear and telling daddu to go out. Daddu isn’t listening. THe bear finally traps her. He is about to attack her. In a second, she is out of the enclosure, with the bear fallen on the ground. Daddu is panting next to her. She can’t believe it. Daddu does have superpowers. The aunty returns with the someone and is relieved to see both outside. She announces that Akshi has saved the old guy from being eaten by the bear. Vibhor has also come and seen the bravery Akshi has shown. He praises her and wants to talk to her but Akshi is freaked out and leaves with daddu. 
Akshi asks daddu about his superpowers and he tells her that he had been saying this all along. Akshi doesn’t now how to process this. Next day she goes to the school and everyone is praising her. They’ve all heard how she bravely saved her daddu from the bear. Vibhor has told the entire school about it. Vibhor is also interested in Akshi. Akshi likes this new found attention. The school bullies are also afraid of her. 

Akshi goes to daddu after school and tells him about it. She asks what kind of superpowers does he have. She asks about why has nobody heard about him. Daddu tells her that he was active when internet hadn’t taken off yet. Phones didn’t have cameras. He didn’t want any atttention either. There are a few articles about him in local newspapers. He takes her to a library. WE get a sense that the librarian knows him. She has all the newspaper prints where he was mentioned. THere were aonly a couple of blurry photographs. This had become an underground legend/myth about this superhuman person, who was going from city to city and saving people. Serious journalists had dismissed this as a rumor, likening it to a Yeti. Akshi is fascinated by all this. 

SHe asks him why has he stopped saving people. HE tells her that his body and memory had deteriorated. He didn’t want to put lives in danger after he She pitches him ideas that they can use to make money. Daddu rejects all of them. Daddu doesn’t want to use his powers for selfish reasons. Akshi tells him that if he can’t use his powers for himself, then what good are the powers. Daddu believes that a superpower comes with certain responsibility and someone who wields it cannot be selfish. 

A few days later, in school, Vibhor brings a girl to Akshi. THe girl is crying and tells Akshi that her father is having some problem with a loan shark. He had taken a big loan for his business, from someone. Now that person is sending gundas and harrassing them. THe other day they beat up her father in front of her. SHe begs Akshi to talk to her dad and get this gunda behind bars. Akshi panics. She can’t do anything. But Vibhor also pleads and she accidentally agrees. 

She goes home and asks her dad about the goon, Anil Rai. Her dad tells her that Anil RAi is a dangerous guy. The police has several cases against him, but can’t arrest him. THe police is afraid of him. AKshi doesn’t know what to do. She asks Daddu to help. But Daddu refuses. He has stopped his superhero activities and doesn’t want to start. Akshi pleads, but Daddu is adamant. 

Akshi deides to tell the girl that her father is busy in other big cases and won’t be able to help. But the this time the girl also has bruises on her face. She tells her that her father has decided to help her. 
Akshi goes back to daddu and manipulates him into going to Anil rai’s adda. Daddu is angry that she is trying to make him to this. HE tells her that he will under no circumstance go inside to fight the goons. Akshi runs inside and challenges the goons. Daddu has to go in and save Akshi. THey tie up all the goons. Akshi calls her father to the location and hides. Her father catches the goons and gets all the credit. 

Akshi is happy and excited. Daddu is angry. Akshi takes him shopping. She buys a dress for herself. Buys icecream for daddu. Daddu is surprised that Akshi has so much money to spend. Akshi tells him that she swiped some money from Anil Rai. Daddu is angry and disappointed. Akshi tells him that it was only 5-6K. There was so much money in that adda. BUt she only took what she needed. The money is going to waste anyway. Daddu disagrees. The girl calls Akshi and thanks her. Daddu is somewhat glad that he helped the girl. 

Akshi is popular in school. Everyone knows what she did for the girl by getting her father to arrest the bad guys. Akshi likes this. Vibhor is also interested in her. He wants to meet her father, the hero. Akshi tells him that he is busy and travelling most of the time. But she will soon get him to meet him. She has money to go clubbing with her classmates. She wears the new dress she bought. BUt the girls already know that even though it cost 2000 bucks for her. It is a cheap dress. Akshi feels bad about it and leaves. 

**************
WE have a baba. He is called Baba Damdam. He has big satsangs, where he teaches fitness to young boys and girls. He goes on stage with thousands waiting to see him. Then at the same time everyone exercises with baba as he is teaching them about morality and justice(nyay) and Injustice(anyay) and motivating them to take action. These boys and girls are ready to do anything at his command. He has millions of such followers in different cities. He is a believing atheist. He believes that god exists. But he has put humans on the earth to decide right from wrong and give punishments. He performs feats of extraordinary strength on stage. 
His story is that he was a straight, normal kid. But at 15 something happened that gave him a purpose. So he started teaching his values to boys eventually became this hugely popular baba. 

**************

Akshi wants to do more, daddu is reluctant. Akshi tries to convince him that he only said that this power comes with a responsibility. Now it’s his responsibility to use it for good. Daddu reluctantly agrees. 

Over the next few days. Daddu and Akshi catch several gangs, drug dealers, mafia and all. Each time her father is the first on the scene. The newspapers are calling it the miracle of dharampur. The criminals confess that it is a little girl and an old guy with a walking stick that beat them up. But nobody believes them. THe reporters are calling them crazy. Akshi is making money out of these raids. Her father also gets promoted to head constable. 
*********

Baba damdam hears of this. This gets his interest and he asks someone to find the little girl and the old guy.

After one of their superhero activities. DAddu and Akshi have tied all the goons. Daddu is struggling. All these activities has had an adverse effect on his health. A nosy journalist clicks pictures of Akshi tying up the goons and taking care of daddu. A few boys 20-24 show up and respectfully ask them to come with them. Daddu is in no position to fight so they have to go with them. 
Daddu and Akshi are waiting in a room. Baba damdam shows up. He is excited to meet Daddu. Daddu has a faint memory of him. He doesn’t remember much. Baba is polite and grateful for the work daddu is doing. He tells Daddu that daddu saved him when he was 15. After that he looked everywhere for daddu, but couldn’t find him. But he had found his purpose. He had seen the power of gods in daddu. And since then he has carried his work forward by helping the needy and punishing evil. 
He asks them to join him. People send him complaints from all over the world of bad men making their lives miserable. THey need justice as well. They can at least start from their own country and get rid of evil. He tells them to take their time and think about it. 

*********** 
Daddu’s condition is getting worse. He thinks he won’t last much longer. He tells her that he has chosen her as the next superhero. He will soon transfer his powers to her. Akshi is excited by this and tells him that in that case they should accept Baba Damdam’s offer. This will give her practice to learn how to do superhero stuff. Daddu doesn’t believe in working under people. That’s the reason him and his predecessors never worked for kings and governments. Akshi somehow convinces him to accept Damdam’s offer. 
************

They bust a couple of smuggling rings in other cities. The same reporter who had taken their pictures,follows them everywhere and has taken loads of pictures. Their involvement becomes national news. Everyone wants to know who these people are. THe police is after them. But don’t have a clue. 


************
Akshi’s photographs have come out in the newspaper. Everybody thinks she is doing something to catch these goons. DOes she have some kind of power. Were the goons telling the truth about a little girl and an old man kicking their butts. All these questions are floating in the media. Akshi goes to school and everyone is looking at her differently. Some are scared of her, most want to know what is she doing? How is she beating all these villians? Student’s parents are crowding the school, asking her to solve their problems. THe media is outside her school. SHe has become famous. 
Her father is arrested in alleged involvement and she is taken into custody as well. Her father is charged with corruption and conspiracy. Akshi is interrogated to find out who is the other person with her. She refuses to tell. Because she is a minor, she is let go. 
Akshi goes to daddu for help. She wants him to break out her father. But daddu refuses as it is against the law. Baba damdam brings them over to his ashram. He tells them that he has gotten her dad out on bail. His top lawyers will be on the case and will ensure that nothing happens to her dad. Akshi is happy and grateful to damdam. Damdam then tells her that unfortunately, because of this she will have to recuse herself. 

************

The raid goes as planned. Baba damdam accompanies Daddu. He captures all the goons alone. Daddu doesn’t have to do anything. Daddu is quite impressed and asks him how is he so strong. He takes daddu to one of his ashrams and the good work they’ve been doing with the young boys and girls. Making them fitter. Giving them a purpose. He also shows Daddu his ayurvedic lab, where he makes ayurvedic protein powders and other medicines. He then takes him to another room, where he has a strength enhancer. He takes it everyday to increase his strength. He also gives it to his disciples. 

Later Damdam reveals to daddu that he knows that daddu wants to transfer his powers to Akshi. Daddu is surprised and asks how he knows this. Damdam reveals that 25 years ago, he was the boy Daddu had chosen to transfer his powers. HE was training him. But one day he just left. Without saying anything. Damdam searched for a long time, but couldn’t find him. After that he tried to spread daddu’s message of justice. 
He tells daddu that by giving Akshi these powers, he will make her life hell. She will have to make sacrifices that she doesn’t know about yet. She will never be able to live a normal life. Her life has already had so much chaos by the rumour of her having some powers. It will be even more difficult if daddu gives her this responsibility. Daddu realizes that this is true. He hasn’t this. Daddu remembers fragments of his time with damdam but can’t remember everything. He is suspicious of his intentions. He thinks that Damdam is saying all this because he wants the power for himself. Damdam tells daddu that this is not the case. He wants daddu to not transfer the power to anyone. AS he has demonstrated to him, he is carrying on the work on his behalf. He is trying to create a world where his power doesn’t exist. What if it falls in the wrong hands? What he wants from daddu is to keep his power to himself so that it dies when he dies. Daddu is surprised by this admission. He considers doing that. Goes to the librarian and tells her that he is considering this. The librarian tells him that the decision is upto him. She advices him against it, telling him that it is a divine power and meant to be passed on. It won’t let him die otherwise. He will live a miserable life aging till eternity till the power finds a new home. 


**********

Akshi is enjoying the popularity she is getting in the school. Everyone wants to know how she did it. How she caught so many bad guys. Akshi has no answer. She misses daddu. She hasn’t seen him for a few days now. She goes to his tent but he is not there. SHe calls Damdam. HIs secretary picks up and tells her that baba damdam is busy with a satsang. She wants to talk to daddu. But the secretary cuts the call. 

Akshi goes to the ashram to see daddu. SHe is intercepted by damdam. She blames him for trying to keep daddu away from her. Damdam assures her that he isn’t doing anything like this.
