The character arc of the 10-year-old girl in your story could take many different forms, depending on the specific details of the story and the direction you want to take. Here are a few suggestions for potential character arcs for the girl:

The "reluctant hero" arc: In this arc, the girl starts out as someone who is content with her ordinary life and has no interest in being a superhero. However, circumstances (such as the discovery of her own powers, or a call for help from the elderly superhero) force her to become a hero and use her powers to save the day. As she faces challenges and battles, she grows in confidence and learns to embrace her role as a hero, and ultimately finds fulfillment and purpose in her new identity.

The "coming of age" arc: In this arc, the girl is a young person who is just starting to discover her powers and potential. She is eager to learn and grow, and looks to the elderly superhero as a mentor and guide. As she faces challenges and battles, she learns valuable lessons and experiences significant growth, both as a person and as a hero.

The "sidekick" arc: In this arc, the girl is a young hero who teams up with the elderly superhero to take on battles and challenges. She is eager to prove herself and prove her worth as a hero, and is willing to take risks and make sacrifices to help the elderly superhero. As she works with the elderly superhero, she learns new skills and gains confidence, and ultimately becomes an equal partner in their adventures.

Overall, the character arc for the 10-year-old girl in your story can take many different forms, and it is up to you to decide which one will be the most effective and meaningful for the story you want to tell.
