Backstory: She was born in a poor neighborhood. At a young age she was told by her parents that she has to live within her means. Her parents also told her that honesty isn’t always the best policy. You have to be clever in this world to survive. As long as you aren’t caught doing something, you are not wrong. So the key is to not get caught. 
She used to study in a public school, with students from her own social level. But students were into gambling and cards and would often get into trouble. Her parents wanted her to get a good education and a good job so they admitted her to a private school, with a high fees, through a needy student scholarship. 
In this new school, she felt out of place. There were students who would come in cars, their own scooters and bikes. She would walk to the school to save the bus fare and would often get late. She always tried to hide the fact that she was poor. She told students that her father was a high ranking police officer and her mother was an interior decorator. She would hand out with other students, but could never go to the places where they would go, like clubbing, weekend getaways and parties. Pretty soon she became an outsider. Always in the periphery but never in the inner circles. 
She liked this boy Vibhor Bora. He is the football team captain and one of the most popular boy in school. Everybody likes him and invites him to any gathering or activity they are going to. She wants to be one of those people who are invited to everything. So she wants to date Vibhor, so she could also be popular and known.

Akshi Gupta is a 16-year-old girl. Rich family. Dad owns factories. In school. Class 11th. Not a popular kid. SHe is not that great in studies. Smart kid. Gets into trouble all the time. 
Old homeless guy. Thinks he is a superhero. Probably 75-80. He doesn’t know how old he is. Early onset of dementia. Forgets things. 
Akshi is like the gunda of the class. She sells cigarettes to boys in class. Netflix subscriptions - one time payment. SHe gets into fights. Gets punished, suspended. 
She has to spend time at home. Her father has made donations to the school that’s why they haven’t rusticated her. They’ve taken away her credit cards as punishments. She calls a couple of her friends to hang out, but they are disappointed in her for using violence. She is stuck at home. She sees this old homeless person across the street, living in a house made of tarpaulin and sticks. She sees him behave strangely. Trying to uproot a tree with his bare hands. Trying to jump off of a wall and falling flat on his face. She becomes curious when she sees that the guy is doing it again and again. Failing but now stopping. She talks to him and observes that he zones out from time to time and calls himself a superhero. SHe thinks he is a crazy guy and decides that he is not worth the time. 
One day, she is walking around her neighborhood. She sees the old guy practice his jumps off the wall. She stops to observe. A few boys come around and make fun of the old guy. They push him around. He warns them not to bully him, or it will turn ugly for them. They laugh at him and destroy his house. This angers Akshi and she decides to butt in. She tells off the young boys, who now turn their attention to her. She fights a couple of them off, but they over power her. The older guy now puts his torn coat as a cape and tells the boys to get away from the girl, or he will have to do it himself. THe boys laugh at him and decide to finish the job by beating him up.
The old guy doesn’t remember where he is or who he is. He is lying there beaten and battered. Akshi takes him to the hospital to get him checked. The doctor tells her that the old guy has dementia.

How did this become his purpose? Did someone give him this job?
