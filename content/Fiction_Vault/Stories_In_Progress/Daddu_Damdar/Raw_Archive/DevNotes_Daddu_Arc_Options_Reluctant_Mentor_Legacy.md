The character arc of the elderly superhero in your story could take many different forms, depending on the specific details of the story and the direction you want to take. Here are a few suggestions for potential character arcs for the elderly superhero:

The "reluctant hero" arc: In this arc, the elderly superhero starts out as someone who has retired from their superhero days and wants to live a peaceful, quiet life. However, circumstances (such as the appearance of a new villain, or a call for help from a former ally) force them to come out of retirement and use their powers once again. As they face new challenges and battles, they rediscover their sense of purpose and heroism, and ultimately find fulfillment in their role as a superhero.

The "mentor" arc: In this arc, the elderly superhero is a wise and experienced hero who has been around for many years. They take the 10-year-old girl under their wing and teach her the ropes of being a superhero, while also facing challenges and battles together. As the girl grows and learns, the elderly superhero learns new things as well, and their relationship deepens and strengthens.

The "legacy" arc: In this arc, the elderly superhero is the last of a long line of heroes, and they feel a strong sense of responsibility to carry on their family's legacy. They are determined to pass on their knowledge and skills to the next generation, and see the 10-year-old girl as their successor. As they work together and face challenges, the elderly superhero helps the girl to become a powerful and capable hero in her own right, and ultimately passes the mantle of hero onto her.

Overall, the character arc for the elderly superhero in your story can take many different forms, and it is up to you to decide which one will be the most effective and meaningful for the story you want to tell.
