1. Ext. Market - Day
A Sunday market crowd. Pop ups and shops filled with customers. We see a family. Akshata and her husband Himanshu, each holding an arm of their 6 year old son, Abeer. They seem to be fighting over something. Akshata tells Himanshu - Mai kisi bhi haal me nahi uthungi. Chai tumhe chahiye hoti hai. You wake up early and make it yourself. Himanshu is pleading - Please yaar. Mai breakfast bana ke to jaata hun na tere liye. Tu ek cup chai nahi pila sakti… Akshata also pleads - Yaar… Wo subah extra aadhe ghante ki neend itni achi hoti hai na… Please yaar Himanshu… Abeer sees something that he likes. He tries to get his parents attention by pulling their arms. He points at the toy. Himanshu refuses - pehle hi aap ke paas bahut toys hain… Akshata - Acha… Kal movie me kya hua? Himanshu - tune kahan tak dekhi? Akshata - Ummmm… Jab Ajay Devgun ki family ko… Aneer is angry at being ignored like this. He wants something, and it’s not even being given a consideration. He pulls his arms away from his parents and runs to the stall. He has eyes for the pink fluffy elephant. He already knows he will put the elephant next to his pillow. He will hug him every night to sleep. His eyes fill with tears in anticipation. His parents follow him to the stall and Akshata yells - Abeer! Abeer turns. His tears now rolling down his  cheeks. It’s Himanshu’s turn next. He is already pissed that he has to get up 30 minutes early to make his tea, because without it, he cannot shit! He takes Abeer’s hand and pulls him away from the stall. Abeer is now bawling his eyes out. His voice fades away as his parents drag him through the market. 
We stay on Pradeep. The 21 year old boy running the toy stall. He is used to seeing kids like these come to his stall and throw all kinds of tantrums. He turns to Waseem and asks - Record kiya? Waseem giggles - Haan kiya na. Pradeep - Mujhko pata hi tha ki ye to rone waala hai. Ganesh ko dikha. Waseem turns his neck to check Ganesh out. Ganesh is talking to a girl. The girl looks like a customer, but Ganesh is flirting with her. Waseem turns back and chuckles - Uska to fantubaaji chal ra hai bro… Pradeep laughs - chal koi ni. Tu isko music laga ke pit pot pe daal de. Waseem nods going back to his phone. Suddenly, they hear a buzzing sound in the air. They both look around, looking for a bee. Waseem spots something - Oh Bhai wo dekh. There is a drone a few feet above their stalls. Waseem tries to remember, what it’s called then says - Chota helicopter. Pradeep is amazed - Chota Helicopter! The drone is systematically turning in all direction. Ganesh joins them following the noise - Abey ganwaron. Ise drone bolte hain. Ganesh looks at the drone suspiciously and whispers to Pradeep and Waseem - Isi se sarkar hampar nazar rakhti hai. A realization comes upon both boys - Oh! Aisa kya! The drone leaves. 
We follow the drone as it flies a little over the crowd. It zeroes in on a man lying down on the ground. On the first glance he looks like a homeless person. Maybe a drunk. The person gets up. He is wearing formal clothes. Shirt, pant, leather shoes. He looks like a middle class man on a working day. The man opens his eyes. The sun is too bright for him. He closes his eyes forcefully. His ears twitch. He can hear everyone clearly all at once. He puts his hands on his ears to shut out all the noise. But it’s too much. His every sense exaggerated. He tries to move through the crowd. The drone follows him from a distance. HE can hear the buzzing sound clearly. It is piercing his ears. He is confused and disconcerted. Staggering and stumbling, he bumps into someone. It’s a huge dude. It was a light bump, but the dude went through a fruit stall. He gets up threatening to beat the man. The man gets scared. He takes a step back and runs. In an instant he is a the other end of the market. Near Pradeep’s stall. He doesn’t know what happened. A second ago he was there. Now he is here. He is even more confused. Pradeep sees this strange man in front of his stall and asks - Bhai pura daaru ka adda pi gaye kya? Waseem laughs. The man comes closer to Pradeep’s stall trying to zero in on his voice. Pradeep thinks he has come to have a fight. He is ready for it. He taps Waseem - Ganesh ko bula. Waseem turns and shouts - GANEEEESSHH! Lafda ho raha hai! Ganesh comes back running. 
Himanshu is annoyed as he and Akshata come back with their jubilant son. Himanshu stands next to the man and takes out his wallet, irritated, he asks - Kitne ka hai! Takes out some money and tells Pradeep - Wo pink haathi. Pradeep is engaged with the man and almost ignoring himanshu tells him - 500 rupaye. Then to the man - Kya chahiye bhai. Hum teen hain tu ek. Lafda karna hai to koi aur pakad. The man is in pain something is happening to him. He staggers back and bumps into Himanshu. Himanshu is already irritated and tries to shove the man. The man swings his hand to push him away, but Himanshu goes flying into a stall 20 feet away. The man is surprised and looks at his hands. Askshata is horrified and screams at the man. She takes stuffed toys from pradeep’s stall and throws them at the man. Pradeep looks at this and tries to stop Akshata - Madam. Please! Rukiye madam. Waseem hands him a stick. He hands it to Akshata. Akshata takes the stick and hits the man repeatedly. But nothing is having an effect. It’s a minor irritation. He holds the stick and pushes it back. The stick hits Akshata on the head. Blood drips down. Seeing this, Pradeep, Waseem and GAnesh rain down on the man. Abeer starts crying again. The man pushes them back. They fly off and fall far away as well. The man flails his hands and legs around in frustration. Maybe, he is going mad. Everything he hits either breaks explodes or flies off. He has super strength. Everyone is stunned. What’s going on? A few people approach the man to hold him down. The man himself starts crying. He doesn’t know what’s happening to him. He goes on the ground. Sobbing like a child. A completely free from shame, innocent child like bawl. 7-8 men come forward and hold him down. This time, he doesn’t retaliate. Akshata has now regained consciousness. Somebody calls the police - Hello! Police! Haan ye Gandhi market aa jaiye… PAta nahi.. Ek aadmi sabko maar raha hai… aur… Her eyes go wide as she sees the men get up suddenly. The man is burning hot. His body is glowing from the inside. It looks  like he has a sun inside him. Himanshu has now gotten up. He curiously and cautiously takes a few steps towards the man to see what’s happening. Silence for a long beat. Then… BOOOOM! The man explodes like a bomb. Body parts flying everywhere. Screams and mayhem. The drone has recorded everything. It goes up to get a birds eye view of the explosion. Recording a few seconds of the aftermath. The drone turns around and zooms away. The screen is left with a beautiful sunset and screams in the background.
