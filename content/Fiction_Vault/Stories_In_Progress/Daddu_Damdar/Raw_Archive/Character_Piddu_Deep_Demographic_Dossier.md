Padmalakshmi AKA Piddu never really thought about her class and social status till her parents admitted her to a private school. She had always lived in her small 2 bedroom house that was too small if more 2 guests came over. 

physical attributes

Gender: Female.
Age: 16
Ethnicity: Indian
Height: 5’6”
Weight: 52
Hair color: Black. Long.
Eye color: Brown
Complexion: Wheatish
Teeth: White and normal.
Eyesight: Normal. 
Physical attractiveness: Good looking, but doesn’t have the means to do makeup or dress up.
Any deformities, handicaps, or distinctive marks: None
Hair style and color: Ties at the back. Mother puts oil on it every morning before sending her off to school.
Facial hair: None
Clothing style: Wears clothes handed down by her relatives. Her mother brings hand-me-downs from the houses she cleans and cooks in. Akshi has started doing small hustles to earn some pocketmoney. Selling weed to kids at school. Even teachers.
Accessories: Ear rings. Nose ring. 
Jewelry: Ear rings given to her by her mom. Nose ring. 
Tattoos and/or piercings: Ear pierced. Nose pierced. Wants to pierce her lips too. Mother has threatened her not to do it. 
Hygiene: Clean. She is used to keeping things in place in her small house. Her mother says, it makes the house look bigger. 
Posture: Straight. Not really looking people in the eye. She fidgets a lot. She is always observing her surroundings. 
Contacts or glasses: None. 
Type of vehicle he uses: None. Public transport and walking. 

Background

Birth order: Only child. 
Number of siblings: 0
Parents and their occupation: Father is a constable in police. He is tame and hasn’t had a promotion in years. Mother is a maid/nanny. Earns her living by cleaning houses and cooking. Used to be a Senior Inspector. Caught a 
What was important to the people who raised him: They tried to save every penny they could. Mother would always tell the father to take bribes or get better at taking bribes. SHe would compare him to other hawaldars who have been taking proper bribes and have made bigger houses. Father is kind and empathetic fellow. He tries, but ends up giving money rather than taking. 
THey have made sure that their daughter studies well. They put her in a private school, even thought the fees was out of their budget. They have saved up 2 lakh rupees for her education. 
Level of wealth or poverty: Poor. Lower middle class. 
Whether she lived in the city or the country: North. Dharampur (Fictional small town) 
Marital status of parents: Married and happy. 
Culture: Indian. Hindu. Mother is religious. Akshi not so much. 
Emotional climate in the home: Happy. But tensions because of poor financial conditions. Her parents can’t get her even essential things. SHe has to make do with make shift objects, or hand me downs, or second hand from kabadi. 
Education: In class 12th. Intelligent. Doesn’t show. Looks like she doesn’t pay attention in class. She doesn’t really studies at home. But she has been in top 3 rankers in her class. 
Relationship with parent of the same gender: Her mother is the disciplinarian in the house. SHe keeps both Akshi and her father in line. 
Era: Contemporary. 2022. 
Societal backdrop: NA
Country and region: India. Northern part. Uttarakhand. 
Politics: She isn’t into politics. She knows that all politicians lie and steal, so she is against all of them. But her political knowledge is limited. Nrither is she interested in it. 

Inward Attributes

Intelligence: Top 3 in her class. She is street smart and book smart. 
Natural gifts or talents: She is athletic and fit. 
Love language:Acts of service. Gift giving. 
Self-esteem: Low. SHe has insecurities about her social status and wealth. Everyone in her class comes in a car or a bike. She walks more than 6 kms to school. Always getting late. 
Zeal and/or religiosity: Not religious at all.

Other Details

Friends: A couple of friends in her colony. They’ve known her for a long time. They used to play football in the colony. One friend in school. She likes the strange and rough nature of Akshi. But she doesn’t know much about her. She has told her that her father is a high ranking police officer and her mother is an interior decorator. She also has acquaintance with drug dealers. One of the dealer is her old childhood friend. She buys weed from them for 1000 bucks a packet and sells it to students in her school for profit. She makes about 10K every month doing this.
  She is very popular in college. People think she is the hippest person. She wears all these torn and rugged looking clothes. People think this is the new hipster fashion. SHe is basically fooling everyone with this. THat’s why she doesn’t have too many close friends. Because she cant share her real life with them. She has a boyfriend in 1st year college. He is a lazy guy. She basically uses him to get in the hostel and sell weed to college boys. This is how she makes money to somehow maintain the illusion of being hip and trendy. 
How do people view this character: She seems mysterious to people. No one knows that she sells weed. She doesn’t talk about her life with anyone. She doesn’t hang out with them. She can’t afford to. 
Lives with: Her parents. 
Fights with: Her mother. Akshi has gotten emotionally distant from her parents. She always stays in her small room. Her mother was afraid that she might fall into bad habits and might mingle with wrong people. That’s why she put her in a private school. So that she would be with the right kind of children. Something she could aspire to and be ambitious about. But ever since then Akshi has become even more distant. 
Spends time with: Alone. As the story starts, she becomes friends with Daddu. 
Wishes to spend time with: Vibhor Bora. A boy in her class. He is extremely popular. But Akshi can’t seem to talk to him properly. She thinks that he is too good for her. 
Who depends on him and why:  Usually her father does whatever her mother tells him to do. She is the decision maker. But sometimes they disagree on somethings. Whenever her parents have an argument, she is the deciding vote. They know how smart their daughter is and they, especially her father, value her opinions. 
What people does she most admire:
Enemies: There is someone at school who suspects her. She doesn’t know what Akshi does, but she knows that there is something strange about her. 
Dating, marriage: None
Children: None.
Relationship with God: Almost non existent.
Overall outlook on life: People with money have it easy. If you don’t have money you have to struggle in life. So, you should make money if you want to be happy in life. 
Does this character like himself: Yes. Kind of. 
What, if anything, would he like to change about his life: She would like to change the financial aspects of her life. She has had to pretend to be something she is not to fit in. If she had money, she wouldn’t have to pretend. 
What personal demons haunt him: She is pretending to be someone she is not. This takes a big toll on her emotionally and physically. She is always afraid of someone finding out her truth.
Is he lying to himself about something: She is lying about her financial condition to he friends and school. SHe is also lying about where she makes money. She tells herself that if she is cool and popular people will like her. If they find out that she is poor, they won’t. She ties her self worth to her net worth. 
Is he optimistic or pessimistic: Optimistic. 
Morality level: Flexible
Confidence level: High
How is he viewed by others: Mysterious and cool. Students in her school think of her as someone who is cool as fuck. There are a few people who can, sort of, see through her charade. But most people believe her lies.
