Episode 1:
Prologue: Something that happened years ago: Option 1: 15 years ago, someone escaped a high security establishment. Dangerous predicament. Action. 

Piddu: 15 years old. In class 10th. Her father is a police constable, mother works as nanny/maid. She is in a reputed school. All her classmates have rich parents. She doesn’t want to reveal that she is poor, so she has been lying to her classmates that her father is a high ranking senior police officer and her mother is an interior decorator. There is a school trip to the zoo where the students have to bring a parent/guardian with them. Piddu can’t get her father or mother. Her classmates will know she is poor and has been lying to everyone. 

Daddu: 80 years old. Frail and homeless. Begs for money, eats out of garbage. A vadapav guy sometimes gives him something to eat. People push him around, berate him etc. He doesn’t mind. Everyday, he tries to hug a tree, that he lives under and get on a platform to jump, get in front of moving cars. 
He lives around Piddu’s house. Piddu sees him and finds him weird. But she is curious. She also feels bad for him. 

She doesn’t want to eat her tiffin in front of his classmates. After school, she enters her colony and before going home, she eats her tiffin. Here she sees Daddu, feels bad for him and gives him some of her food. Daddu reveals, he is a superhero. Piddu realizes he is crazy, so doesn’t believe him. She asks him to show his powers, but he is unable to do so. 

Piddu has decided to tell her teacher no. A boy who she has a crush on also shows interest in her. He is looking forward to sees her at the zoo and spending some time with her. Piddu is escited, but she can’t go. 
Sequence: Her struggles with what to do about it. 
She finally decides to take daddu with her. Disguised as her real grandfather. She finds her fathers nicest clothes, gives Daddu a simple makeover. (Bath, clothes, shave and haircut etc)

At the zoo, her plans to be with the boy doesn’t work. She loses Daddu. Animal enclosure scene. Daddu saves her, revealing his superpowers. THe boy, Vibhor has also seen it. 
They make their way back home.

Episode 2:

Prologue: Daddu’s early history. Fighting something/someone/natural disaster. 
Piddu and daddu think about what to do with the superpower. They realize that he can’t use it at will. THey don’t know how it works. Piddu thinks that it will work only when it’s need arises. Like it did when Piddu was in danger. 

Piddu and Daddu decide to use his powers for something good. They don’t know what. Piddu has seen movies and read comic books. She thinks that superpowers should be used to fight crime. 

Need to introduce Jass Dhillon. Show him in good light. He is fielding questions maybe? Inaugurating a cancer research facility? 

Argument about secret identity. Daddu doesn’t want a secret identity. Piddu does. They compromise. Daddu goes as he is. Piddu goes in a mask. 
They try to find crime but can’t find any. They go to crowded places, cinema halls, malls. But can’t find any crime. THey get into trouble a few times mistaking things for crime. 3 times we’ll do this. 
They finally find someone stealing something and try to catch him. The thief has only stolen a toy. He is poor and can’t afford toys. So this is the best way to get it. They decide it is useless to look for crime like this. They should follow her father from now on, to find crime. They find small pickpockets, and people who pee on the side of the road.

  Piddu asks her father. He makes a joke about it. THey decide to follow him. Piddu’s father encounters criminals/bodyguards of an evil guy. 
Piddu and Daddu save the day. Piddu’s father gets the credit. 

Daddu has now used his powers twice. Each time he used it he got a flash of his past. He starts remembering stuff.
Where should we start? He maybe sees a village. Sees the village’s name or something. And decides to go there. 

Damdam finds out about it. Sends men to investigate.
