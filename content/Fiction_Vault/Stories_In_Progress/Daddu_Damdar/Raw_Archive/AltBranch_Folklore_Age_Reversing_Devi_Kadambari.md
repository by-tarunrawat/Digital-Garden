She was a strange girl. She was 12-years-old but behaved like she was 40. Ordering everyone around. Scolding people when they weren’t doing things how they were supposed to be done. She lived in an orphanage in a small town in the hills of Uttarakhand. The town was isolated by the other towns and villages, separated by a small bridge. The bridge was narrow and only one car could pass at a time. The girl was called Piddu wielded such power over everyone that the Manager and the staff would need her permission to do things. Even in the town, she was known by everyone. Residents, shopkeepers, labourers. Everyone was also very protective about her.
But she had a secret. She never aged. She had been 12 for the past 50 years. She was brought to this village 50 years ago by an older man. He was dying and carried a woman in his arms. The woman was wounded as well. The woman seemed to be about, 30 years old at the time. She was handed over to the village head. The woman remained in coma for 18 years. No doctors or medicines really worked on her. She didn’t need water or food. All she did was slept. 
Her injuries, though severe, did not kill her. They slowly got better on their own. But, it came at a cost. Her body started changing. She was getting younger and younger every year. Her physical body was changing. Her limbs getting smaller and smaller. The village head, Mananiya Sri Bisnu Chand Joshi, showed her to the district doctor, who was his tai’s nanad’s (sister in law) son. The doctor tried for a month, but couldn’t help the girl. He advised Bishnu to send her to a hospital in the city. Like Dehradun, maybe even Delhi. The doctors there could test her and find more about the girl. 
Bisnu was a simple man. He decided to take the doctor’s advice. The day the girl was supposed to be taken was October the 12th. The winter had started to set in. Temperatures were around 1-2 degrees. It was sunny. Bisnu had arranged a van from the nearby town. The bus already had a flat tire on the way over to Bisnu’s house. It had started raining heavily. The cold rain was hitting the body like cold ice needles. Almost piercing the skin. When it was time to take the girl to the van, Bisnu couldn’t pick her up. It was as if the girl had suddenly gained immense weight. Bisnu asked the driver for help, then Bhuppi, gyani and Jeetu. An hour later there were 20 people trying to lift her. But they couldn’t move the khatiya. They tied the khatiya to a rope attached it to the van, but the van couldn’t move. Bisnu took it as a sign that there was some godlike power at work here and let the girl remain in his house. 
THe girl was given a room. SHe required nothing. No food, no water. Just a khatiya and a blanket. The room became like a shrine. The villagers would come and light dhoop and agarbatti. Leave flowers outside the room as a sign of respect for what they thought was a Devi. Bisnu would come and visit the girl everyday. He took care of her for 15 long years. He didn’t have to do much. Then he died of an illness. His son, Mananiya Sri Jainendra Joshi, took over the care of the woman, whose body was now of a 20 year old girl. Jainendra himself was about 38. He was younger than the girl when she was brought to his house. He was upset with her because she took his room. But eventually he felt pity for the girl. Her age was reducing every year. No one knew what was going to happen to her. If this kept on going she would become a baby one day. What would happen after that? No one knew. 
The villagers kept it a secret among themselves. They didn’t want anyone to take her from here. As the years passed, the girl’s body kept shrinking. She eventually became a baby 20 years later. The prayers intensified. They all thought that now the baby is going to die as it’s body was shrinking to a new born baby. The day was arriving. The doctor told everyone that the girl was now a prematurely new born baby and can die anyday. A jagrata was help. People singing songs. Playing dholaks. Jainu (Jainendra) sat next to the khatiya all this while. At some point during the jagrata and songs. The baby’s breathing stopped. Jainenedra checked the heartbeat. It wasn’t there. Jainendra had gotten attached to the little baby, like it was his own. He wept. His sobs drowned in the noise of the songs being sung by the villagers. Slowly the news of the baby’s death spread. The singing stopped. Silence all overthe mountain. A few sobs could be heard as everyone wanted to get a glimpse of the baby’s body. 
Then suddenly, the baby started crying. It was awake. For the first time in 38 years. It was awake and crying. The whole village became silent, then erupted in joy. Jainendra couldn’t believe his eyes. He took her in as her own daughter and named her Kadambari (kaddu for short). 
She was now a normal child. Growing up normally. No abnormal behavior. The villagers knew what a miracle this girl is and always treated her special. Jainendra spoiled her rotten.
