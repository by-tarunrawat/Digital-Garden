> **Project:** [[00_Story_Bible|Daddu Damdar]]
> **Core Focus:** Establishing Akshi's working-class family (clerk father, maid mother), the fake IPS officer lie, and the hospital dementia diagnosis.

1. Akshi Gupta is a 16-year-old girl, lives in a small house with her mom and dad. Her mother works as a maid in people’s houses and her father is low level clerk in a government job. Although they don’t earn too much, they save money to send Akshi to a private school. Both of them struggle to make ends meet. Akshi is in a school filled with rich kids. SHe feels out of place. She doesn’t have too many friends here, neither does she have enough money. She does side hustles to make some pocket money. She sells cigarettes and alcohol to boys in school, charges money for forging the principal’s signature on other kids permission slips. She also teaches karate to kids in the evening. She has one friend, Naomi Joseph. Akshi has a crush on a boy in her class, Vibhor Bora. He is a handsome jock. Dumb but sweet. She doesn’t have the courage to talk to him. 
She sees one of the boys she signed the permission slip for, harassing a girl in the school. She tells him to stop and walk away, but the boy refuses. They get into a fight. She is brought to the principal’s office where the boy rats her out for forging signatures. She gets suspended for 2 weeks. She is scared that her parents will be disappointed with the suspension. So she doesn’t tell them. 
Next day, she pretends to go to school, but goes to a park near her house. She is thinking of ways to pass the time when she sees this old homeless person across the street, living in a house made of tarpaulin and sticks. She sees him behave strangely. Hugging a tree as if trying to pull it.  is doing it again and again. Failing but now stopping. When not doing this, he is checking out people passing by. Sometimes getting too close to sniff them. She sees that he is tired and exhausted. She shares her tiffin with him and asks what he was doing. He looks at her suspiciously and gives her cagey answers. I was exercising, I live that tree. Akshi can clearly see that the old guy can barely walk straight. This becomes her routine. She goes everyday to the park and meets the old guy, sharing her tiffin. The old guy is also opening up. Akshi asks what he does everyday, hugging the tree and jumping off of a wall. He tells her that he is really exercising. He is a superhero, but because of old age, he is not able to do things that he used to, like flying. His strength is also decreasing. Akshi doesn’t believe him and thinks he is crazy. SHe decides not to engage with the old man anymore. 

2. It’s sunday. Akshi is at home. She has lunch with her mom and dad. She remembers that the old guy wouldn’t have eaten anything. She decides to save some food and wraps it up. She goes to see the old guy. But there are a few boys beating on him. Akshi runs to them and tells them to leave the old guy alone. The boys laugh it off and keep beating the old guy. Akshi karate chops one and kicks the other. The boys turn their attention towards her and grab her. The old guy gets up and shouts at them to stop or he will beat them. He shouts at Akshi to run. Akshi gets free from the boys and runs away. She is hysterical. She could have gotten severely injured. She remembers the old guy. She runs to the road and finds a constable there and takes him to the old guy. 
She reaches there with the constable. The boys are gone. The old guy is on the floor beaten and battered. He is in a fugue state. Not knowing where he is. Alshi takes him to a government hospital, where his wounds are attended to. The doctor tells her that the old guy has dementia. Akshi is worried about him. THe old guy tells her not to worry. He will be fine. He just needs to finish his one last duty as the superhero. Akshi takes it as ramblings of a demented man and takes him to his place. Fixes his tarpaulin house, makes it more comfortable for him. 

3. Akshi’s suspension is over. She goesback to school to find out that there is a school trip in 2 days and she has to get a parent of a guadian to go. Vibhor talks to her for the first time and asks if she is coming to the school trip. SHe says yes. She goes home to ask her parents, but they refuse. They can’t take a leave from their jobs. Akshi has an idea. She decides to take the old guy as her grandfather. She gives him a haircut and a shave and some clothes stolen from a neighbor. She takes him tot he zoo as his daddu. 
At the zoo, she gathers some courage to talk to Vibhor, who has come with his older brother Kuber. AS they strike up a conversation, daddu pops out. Interrupting them. Akshi tries to avoid him but he is everywhere. Finally she takes him to an enclosure and sneaks away from him. She talks to Vibhor and they have a good time. At the end of the day, the zoo has emptied. The students are leaving. Vibhor asks Akshi about daddu. Akshi panics. She runs to find him. Vibhor and his brother come with her. She goes to the moose enclosure where she left him. She sees that he is inside the enclosure in his fugue state. Trying to recognize things. Akshi tells Vibhor that Daddu has dementia. Vibhor is now full on panicking seeing this. Akshi decides to go inside the enclosure and get daddu. The moose is nowhere to be seen. She gets into the enclosure and walks quietly. Kuber starts recording. As Akshi is walking, she makes a little noise. The moose comes. She freezes. The moose sees daddu and goes towards him. To save him, Akshi gets the moose attention and runs away. The moose runs after her. He is almost about to hit AKshi. But in another second she is out of the enclosure. Daddu has saved her. Akshi is in shock. What daddu was saying was true. HE is a superhero. 

4. They take daddu to Vibhor’s house.
