4-6 stakes
Act 2: 7-12 success - 12 is midpoint. Disillusionment.
Proactive. Strength
13-18 Downward spiral. - Broken, lost all hope, sabotaged.
Reactive. Weaknesses
18 - Forced to see a secret path that they couldn’t see before. Often something that their character flaw prevented them to do. (Enlightenment). They make a clear, extremely risky plan. (Final strategy)
Act 4: 19-24 Transformation.
Emotional truth. Embracing the truth. 
Stakes should staring be right in the face.
All wants replaced with needs. All fears are faced. All lies revealed. Every choice leads to a single Climax.
22-24 - New world. Character or world has changed. New perspective.  Or adapted in a unexpected way. 

Superhero story about an 80 year old homeless guy suffering from dementia, who thinks he is a superhero and a 12 year old girl who can’t remember her past feels a strange connection with the guy and decides to help him. 


1. Hook> PP>> 12-year-old CG sees this old homeless guy living under the bridge, and is always curious about him. She lives in the building in front of the bridge and sees him everyday. He punches walls and in turn injures his hand, he stacks up boxes and tries to fly, but falls down. He does this almost everyday and fails, but he never fails to do these things. One day CG sees that a bunch of boys are bullying him, she feels entitled to help him and goes down to shoo the boys away. The boys turn on her and will probably beet ehr up, but the older guy stops them and says that he is a superhero and will beat the shit out of these boys. Ofcourse he fails again and gets beaten up. CG feels indebted to him as he saved her from the boys. 

2. The girl takes him to the hospital, where some tests are done. They are told to come back later to find the results. The girl takes her home to get him cleaned up and feed him. She hides him from her parents at first. She can’t let him go to the streets. The old guy tells her that he is a chiranjeevi, but he can’t remember which one. He has trouble with his memory. He can’t even remember his name. CG doesn’t believe that this guy is a superhero. She thinks he is just a crazy old person who needs help. She plans to keep him hidden in the house. The old guy’s wounds heal very fast. In 2 days the wounds are gone. This surprises CG and she is in doubt. But her parents find out and throw him out of the house. He goes back to the street. CG feels bad for him, but she can’t do anything. 

3. Impetus> PP>> CG goes to the hospital to collect the old guy’s reports and finds out that he has cancer. He doesn’t have too long to live. CG is sad. She goes back to talk to the old guy. She tells him about his reports. He tells her he knows that he is dying. But he only needs to get his powers back, so he can heal himself. CG thinks it’s his craziness talking. She brings him back to her home telling her parents about him. He talks to the parents and tells them that he is a chiranjeevi. CG’s father is an expert in myths and pauranic legends. He tells them that there were 8 chiranjeevis. But there was supposed to a 9th as well. About 3000-4000 years ago. But this one was unique in some way. There is very little written about this chiranjeevi. He was given his powers to save humanity. Gods had given Humans full autonomy. Kaliyuga. Before leaving the world for humans, gods gave one of the humans the power to live forever and help and save humanity. She gives him her tent. He lives outside, but more comfortably. She is giving him food and water and talks to him everyday. He talks about stories where he had defeated people over the years. Like in 1500 BC he might have saved some king from danger or something. He took part in wars etc. SO what happened? CG asks. The old guy, let’s calls him BV, tells her that something happened 80 years ago. His powers went away. His memory as well. He just remembers fragments of it. For fun, she decides to entertain him by pretending to train him with different exercises to bring his powers back. THey bond over this. BV doesn’t really get his powers back but he likes spending time with CG. 
The girl knows he is only going to be alive for a few months. His health is deteriorating. She sees him cough blood. She desperately wants him to be healthy again. BV keeps telling her that if he gets his powers back, he will heal himself and become as good as new. CG doesn’t really believe that his powers will return, so, she is sad that soon BV is going to die. One day, during a superpower training exercise, CG’s life is in danger. BV saves her. There is a glimpse of his power as he shows his strength. This makes CG believe that there might be some truth to BVs claims. This means that if he is telling the truth then he probably will get better if he finds his powers. This option seems to be working. 

4 She asks her dad how can a chiranjivi die? If that was a real thing, how would she be able to save a chiranjivi. Her dad tells her that Chiranjeevis live forever, so if a chiranjivi is dying, that means that the boon came with a curse. The chiranjeevi broke some rule in the chiranjivi boon, that got triggered. CG asks BV if he remembers something about this. BV doesn’t have any memory of it. Her dad tells her that the only other way to find out is by finding Chitragupta. He has records of everything. He will know how the CHiranjeevi lost his powers. But where to find Chitragupta?  The only way to find Chitragupta is to die. He will meet you after your death and give you an account of your life. This sounds like a dead end again. What good would it be if they had to die to meet Chitragupta. 
Can they die for a minute and come back. They would have to go to a tantrik to do this. They go to a couple of tantrik’s who try to con them out of money. But during one of the rituals by a fake tantrik, Something triggers and an explosion happens. THe tantrik sees something in both of them and gets scared. He refuses to do anymore rituals. He tells them about another tantrik. A real one. They should go an talk to him. 
CG has something to explore, she asks BV what could he have done? BV doesn’t quite remember. There must be someone they can ask. Someone who might know from that long ago. BV doesn’t know anyone. BV has a locket that his mother gave him years ago. He still keeps it around his neck. He has forgotten his mother. 
5. They are scared of this tantrik, but when they meet him they find out that he is a ganja smoking, guitar playing, cartoon watching, tattooed 40 year old guy. They are apprehensive if he will be able to do antyhing. But He promises that he can arrange a meeting with Chitragupta. Should they do it? THey do it. Of course they do it and they meet Chitragupta while they are at it. 
I imagine the scene with Chitragupta in a huge library. CHitragupta is a fun guy, who has been trying out humor for the past 2000 years, but failing miserably. These two meet him and ask him about BV’s curse. CHitragupta is confused, but he tells that the 9th chiranjeevi lost their power in 1947. The chiranjeevi was supposed to be save all humans from calamities and evil. This was the chiranjeevi’s sole purpose. He was supposed to do this till the end of the yuga, which is about 4,30,000 years, when the dashavtar comes and ends the Kaliyuga. But the lord of the time, Demon Kali has many servants all over the world. One of them, a powerful demon, called Manoj, found the chiranjeevi, but he couldn’t kill him, so with a ritual of some sort, he locked his power and made him forget. SO how do they get the power back? They will need to steal the gem in which he has stored the memories.
