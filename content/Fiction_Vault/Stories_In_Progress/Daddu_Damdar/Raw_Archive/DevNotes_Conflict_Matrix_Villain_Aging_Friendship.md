The conflict between the elderly superhero and their own mortality: In this conflict, the elderly superhero is getting older and starting to feel the effects of their age. They may be slower, weaker, or less able to use their powers effectively. As they deal with their own mortality and their declining powers, they must also find a way to continue being a hero and making a difference, even as they face their own limitations.

The conflict between the elderly superhero and the 10-year-old girl: In this conflict, the elderly superhero and the girl start out as allies and friends, but eventually have a falling out. This could be due to a misunderstanding, a clash of personalities, or a disagreement about how to handle a particular situation. As they deal with the conflict and try to resolve their differences, they must also continue to face villains and challenges, and ultimately learn to trust and rely on each other once again.

Overall, the conflict in the story about the elderly superhero and the 10-year-old girl can take many different forms, and it is up to you to decide which one will be the most effective and meaningful for the story you want to tell.
