Logline: When an old homeless man with memory loss, claiming to be a superhero, saves a 16-year-old girl, she must help him awaken his powers and save him from powerful people out to capture him. 

Genre: Comedy, superhero.

Duration: 30 min. 8 episodes.

Synopsis: Akshi is a 16-year-old girl who lives in a poor neighborhood with her parents. Her father is a constable and his mother works as a nanny in rich people’s houses. Her father used to be a hero cop but had to take multiple demotions after he pissed off a senior official in police. 
Akshi lives a normal life of a school girl, having crushes on boys and insecurities about her appearance. She is also ashamed of her financial condition and has told her classmates that her father is a high ranking police officer and her mother is an internal decorator. Akshi has a strong distaste for authority and authority figures. She gets into fights with students and teachers in school whenever she sees someone misusing their powers and influence. She often gets suspended for her behavior. Her parents try to teach her to be flexible and let things go, but Akshi is strong minded and stubborn. 
Akshi meets a mysterious, old homeless guy living on the streets. She sees him doing strange things like climbing a platform and jumping (as if trying to fly), and hugging a huge peepal tree. She sees him being beaten up by street bullies and saves him. She finds out that the old guy has severe memory loss. He can’t remember things that happened to him before 25 years ago. He sleeps on the streets and carries a ‘jhola’ that has a binder with newspaper clippings of miracles of a dmamdar aadmi that have happened many many years ago. When asked about it he claims to be a superhero who has done all those things. Akshi takes as ramblings of an old man. 
The old man has lived in this condition for over two decades now, so he has rules to live by. For example, Rule number 1: Always find food before doing anything at all. Rule number 2: Don’t steal, unless you can’t find food till 9 at night. Etc. Akshi doesn’t agree with these rules. She thinks it is wrong to steal in any condition. The old guy tells her that this is not the case. Sometimes things go so bad that if he doesn’t eat, he dies hungry. Akshi takes pity on him when she saves him from street bullies. And starts sharing food from her tiffin and makes him a temporary shelter made of tarpaulin. Lovingly, she starts calling him daddu. 
Daddu is frustrated by his lack of memory, he can’t use his powers, so he questions whether he really is the superhero that the binder and the newspaper clippings says he is or has he made a story for himself. Things take a turn when Akshi is in trouble and the old guy saves her using his superpowers. He has not used his powers in 25 years, so this surprises and excites him. He tries to do it again, but he doesn’t know how to. Seeing his powers for herself, Akshi decides to help him awaken his powers. She thinks that his powers can be used to help the poor and needy against powerful people who use their influence and power to do bad things.
This begins their journey to discover Daddu’s superpowers to help the poor and needy. They start discovering his past. Meeting people who have seen him in action in his youth. Their activities make them famous and bring them on the world’s Radar. People calling the duo Daddu Damdar. Daddu comes on the radar of billionaire moghul, Jass Dhillon and a godman baba Damdam. They both have a history with daddu, that we will uncover through the story. 
They both have their own agenda with him and  send their men to find and capture him. Akshi and Daddu find out that the men after them are physically powerful and have the ability to capture Daddu. Daddu puts his life on the line to save Akshi and is almost about to get captured. But Akshi is stubborn and somehow manages to save daddu. Now, they have to go on a run to hide from these people. 
Akshi decides that if they want to find more about daddu’s life and why these people are after him they will need to go to the places mentioned in the newspaper clippings. They go on a journey to find out about Daddu’s history through towns and villages where daddu had saved and helped people many many years ago. We also find out that daddu’s superpowers are ancient and have been passed on generation to generation. Daddu also has to transfer his powers to someone he deems worthy.

We also reveal that Jass Dhillon wants to extract daddu’s powers for himself. He had captured daddu 25 years ago, to do experiments on him and has created a pharma empire from synthesizing his blood and creating performance enhancing drugs. He also has a special drug made out of daddu’s blood that gives people tremendous physical powers, but only for 20-30 minutes. He uses these drugs to fight and capture daddu. 
We also see that baba damdam is a sanyasi physical trainer, who gives ‘pravachan’ while excercising on stage. He is tryng to create the next generation of physically fit, morally strong men and women. WE reveal that Baba damdam was the guy chosen by daddu 25 years ago as his successor. When daddu disappeared, damdam started his own ashram to teach physical education and daddu’s ethics of saving people in need. 
Damdam has a secret agenda. He thinks he is now too old to receive daddu’s powers. So, he has created an army of morally strong men and women for daddu to choose from to transfer his powers. Akshi doesn’t agree. She thinks that no one should get this power. It will eventually corrupt whoever gets it, like how any kind of power corrupts people. 
 Will Daddu get his memory back and find out how he helped the world? Will Akshi be able to save Daddu from Jass Dhillon? Will damdam be able to provide a successor to Daddu?
 
Episodic brief:

Ep1: Introdcution to Akshi and Daddu. Revelation of Daddu’s superpowers. 

Ep2: Introduction of Jass Dhillon and Baba Damdam. Akshi and Daddu trying to figure out how to use the powers. 

Ep3: Akshi and Daddu decide to use the powers for good. They fight local gundas creating problems for the poor bastiwaalas.  At first daddu fails, but eventually Akshi helps him figure out how to use his powers. We see a hint of Jass and Damdam’s history with daddu.

Ep4: Akshi and Daddu become famous. Their deeds are in the newspapers. Jass and Damdam find out and send their men to bring him to them. Akshi and Daddu’s confrontation with the men. They find that these goons are super powerful and have to run from them. 

Ep5: Akshi and Daddu run from the goons. They go to places in daddu’s binder to find out more about his past. They meet people who daddu has helped many years ago. THey can’t seen to find what happened 25 years ago that daddu had to stop. 

Ep6: Daddu and Akshi are captured by Damdam. We reveal that damdam is a good guy. He was the successor chosen by Daddu many years ago. But he has a secret agenda. An attack happens at Damdam’s ashram. Daddu is captured by Jass Dhillon and taken to his secret lab. 

Ep7: Akshi and Damdam plan to free daddu. The story of what happened to daddu 25 years ago is revealed. Jass had performed experiments on him and created new performance enhancing drugs to build his vast empire. Akshi infiltrates Jass’s lab to free Daddu, but both of them are trapped. 

Ep8: Akshi and Daddu are trapped. Damdam comes to save them. Akshi is able to free daddu. But she dies when she goes back to destroy all the samples collected by Jass Dhillon. Daddu saves her by transferring his powers to her.
