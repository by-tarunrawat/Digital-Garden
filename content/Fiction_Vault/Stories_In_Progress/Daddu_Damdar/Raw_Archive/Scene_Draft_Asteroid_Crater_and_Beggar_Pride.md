Piddu: What will she become. She will have to start from the opposite end. 
What does she want?
World peace. To change the world. To get somthing done. Something specific.
Should she have a cause. Like One single mission. To save someone. Or to get justice for something. 
One single mission:
She wants to get her dad promoted. Maybe she wants something for herself. Not a cause then.
How will she go about getting her dad promotion if she had a superhero?
One of the plans I initially thought was catching bad guys. Getting his father to the scene to make the arrest. Her father gets all the accolades. Seems like a reasonable plan. 

Let’s say this is the case. Then Daddu needs to have a want as well. Hopefully opposing. He is trying to find his identity. Maybe he has been looking for it for a while. He broke away from the lab 25 years ago. When he came out, he didn’t remember anything. But he had a powers. He remembers flying away. He remembers falling to the ground clearing trees and creating a small crater as if an asteroid had fallen. He remembers this and thinks that he has powers. But he can’t use them because he doesn’t know how to. 
He wanted to find out more about himself. Who was he? Where did he come from? Did he really have powers? Was that a hallucination? 
For the first few days, he didn’t know what to do. Scared of what might happen. He went back to the crater. HE looked around to find a huge structure, completely burned down. He didn’t know if this was the lab or not. He went inside to check. Everything had been burned out. He looked around 

He was tired and weak. He wandered around the town wearing dirty and torn white overalls. Looking like a beggar. He hadn’t eaten for a few days now. He thought about asking someone for some money or food. But couldn’t bring himself to do it. Although he didn’t remember anything, he still had some pride left in him. 
But this broke too. Having not eaten for days, he collapsed on the ground in the middle of the market. Ongoing pedestrians thought of him as a homeless beggar, threw food and money at him. He survived eating the leftover rotis. He discarded his ego and decided this to be his way of living. He found a bag in the garbage along with other things that he would carry from town to town. Living off of kindness of strangers. If that didn’t work he had to beg. If that didn’t work he had to pick stuff out of the garbage. 
He was living an aimless life. He didn’t know who he was. People would call him “oye”, “buddhe”, “baba”. He didn’t name himself anything. He had tried to remember but couldn’t remember anything. He remembered fragments of memory. Couldn’t make sense of it at all. He remembered a big lab of some kind. He remembers an explosion. He remembers his flying out. He remembers crashing into a tree that broke in half. THen he remembers waking up to see that tree broken.
