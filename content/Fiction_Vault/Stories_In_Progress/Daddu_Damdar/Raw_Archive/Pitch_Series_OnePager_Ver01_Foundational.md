Logline: When an old homeless man with memory loss, claiming to be a superhero, saves a 16-year-old girl, she must help him awaken his powers and save him from powerful people out to capture him. 

Genre: Comedy, superhero.

Duration: 30 min. 8 episodes.

Synopsis: Akshi is a 16-year-old girl with a strong moral sense. She gets into fights with students and teachers in school whenever she sees something wrong happening. SHe often gets suspended for her behavior. Her parents try to teach her to be flexible and let things go, but Akshi is strong minded and stubborn. She meets an old homeless guy, being beaten up by street bullies and saves him. THe old guy has severe memory loss. He can’t remember things that happened to him before 25 years ago. He sleeps on the streets and carries a ‘jhola’ that has a binder with newspaper clippings of miracles that have happened in the past 50 years. 
The also claims to be a superhero, which Akshi takes as ramblings of an old man. Frustrated by his lack of memory and believes that he will die without making any difference in the world. The old guy is She takes pity on him, and takes him under his wing. Protecting him from street gundas and providing him food and a temporary shelter made of tarpaulin. She starts calling him daddu. Things take a turn when Akshi is in trouble and the old guy saves her using his superpowers. Even he doesn’t know how to use them. 
This begins their journey to discover his superpowers to help the poor and needy. They start discovering his past. Their actions make them famous and bring them on the world’s Radar. People calling the duo Daddu Damdar. This includes a billionaire tech moghul, Jass Dhillon and a godman baba Damdam. They both have a history with daddu and their own agenda. 
They both have their own agenda with him and want to find him and send their men after them. Akshi and Daddu find out that the men after them are physically powerful and have the ability to capture Daddu. She wants to save daddu, so they go on a run and find out daddu’s history as they travel through towns and villages where daddu had saved and helped people many many years ago.
Eventually we find out that Jass Dhillon wants to extract daddu’s powers for himself. He had captured daddu 25 years ago, to do experiments on him and has created a pharma empire from synthesizing his blood. We also find out that Baba damdam was the guy chosen by daddu 25 years ago as his successor. We reveal that daddu’s superpowers are ancient and have been passed on generation to generation. Daddu also has to transfer his powers to someone he deems worthy. 
Will Akshi be able to save Daddu from Jass Dhillon? Will baba Damdam be able to become Daddu’s successor? Will Daddu get his memory back and find out how he helped the world?
 
Episodic brief:

Ep1: Introdcution to Akshi and Daddu. Revelation of Daddu’s superpowers. 

Ep2: Introduction of Jass Dhillon and Baba Damdam. Jass and Daddu trying to figure out how to use the powers. 

Ep3: Jass and Daddu decide to use the powers for good. They fight local gundas creating problems for the poor bastiwaalas.  At first daddu fails, but eventually Akshi helps him figure out how to use his powers. We see a hint of Jass and Damdam’s history with daddu.

Ep4: Akshi and Daddu become famous. Their deeds are in the newspapers. Jass and Damdam find out and send their men to bring him to them. Akshi and Daddu’s confrontation with the men. They find that these goons are super powerful and have to run from them. 

Ep5: Akshi and Daddu run from the goons. They go to places in daddu’s binder to find out more about his past. THey meet people who daddu has helped many years ago. THey can’t seen to find what happened 25 years ago that daddu had to stop. 

Ep6: Daddu and Akshi are captured by Damdam. We reveal that damdam is a good guy. He was the successor chosen by Daddu many years ago. But he has a secret agenda. An attack happens at Damdam’s ashram. Daddu is captured by Jass Dhillon and taken to his secret lab. 

Ep7: Akshi and Damdam plan to free daddu. The story of what happened to daddu 25 years ago is revealed. Jass had performed experiments on him and created new performance enhancing drugs to build his vast empire. Akshi infiltrates Jass’s lab to free Daddu, but both of them are trapped. 

Ep8: Akshi and Daddu are trapped. Damdam comes to save them. Akshi is able to free daddu. But she dies when she goes back to destroy all the samples collected by Jass Dhillon. Daddu saves her by transferring his powers to her.
