Old superhero, daddu, meets a little girl. Why am I so excited about the idea? What do I want to see? It will be interesting to see an old guy come to beat people up. The combination of old guy and little girl is interesting. DOes that excite me?
What else? Hero vs people? Power to many vs power to one? Seeing the power withing you? Wish you could fight every injustice in the world? You see someone kicking a dog, or being mean to it. You want to slap that person. But you can’t. People being horrible to others. You can do it now. 
What’s wrong with superhero movies. Superpowerful heros. I like that part. What about a superhero power that changes hand from one to the other. THere have been 100s of such superheros in history. 
I’m quite excited about the history of the superheros. 
What about the girl? What do we want to see from her? A mentor? We’ve seen mentors. Even the novelty of the mentor being a little girl will be short lived. Friend? A little girl friends with a superhero? It’s interesting. But not exciting yet. How can this be exciting? Something about the girl. How is she impactful? Memorable? Is she feisty? WHat difference does that make. She can boss superhero around. WHat will that achieve? Maybe he will start using his powers again and do what he was supposed to do. But that’s not exciting still. 

Something about the girl. The girl is shy? THe superhero will protect her from things. SHe will learn to have confidence. Not exciting. 

The girl is a thief. She might use the superhero to steal. She will have to manipulate him or something like that. 

What do I already know about this?

The superhero is old. 75-80 years. He has superstrength, can run fast and fly. He can’t fly anymore because of old age. 
Comtemporary story. IN the world of anytime anywhere mobile cameras, social media, TV news and virality. 
He will not be able to hide well.
