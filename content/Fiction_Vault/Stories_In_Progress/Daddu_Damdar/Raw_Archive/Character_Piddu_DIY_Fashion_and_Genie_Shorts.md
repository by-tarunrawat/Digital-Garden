Logline: When an old homeless ex-superhero, with memory issues and no powers, saves a 16-year-old skater girl, they go an adventure to rediscover his superpowers. 

Genre: Comedy, action, superhero

Duration: 30 min, 8 episodes

Synopsis: Padmalaxmi AKA Piddu is a 16-year-old, in the final year of her school. She is a small, frail but fiery girl. She doesn’t let her size get in her way when it comes to standing up for herself and others. Her father used to be a hero cop, who was demoted when he got on the wrong side of some powerful people. Now he is a constable. Piddu’s mother works as a nanny to make ends meet. This lack of money and his family’s situation embarrasses Piddu. She gets into fights regularly when she is teased about this. Piddu’s father has had enough of that hero cop life. He wants to live a peaceful life with his family. He has been saving money so that he and his family can retire and go back to their family home, where he plans to open a general store. Piddu is not satisfied with this and keeps  prodding her father to do something about his situation. Catch some bad guys, solve murders so that he an get a promotion. But it keeps falling on deaf ears.

Piddu is bothered by the lack of money in the house. Having to cut down costs. She can’t buy new clothes. So, she has to wear either her old clothes, that are too tight or torn, or she has to wear her father’s hand me downs. Tshirts, shirts. She wears these clothes creatively to create an illusion of style. For example, wearing a Tshirt torn at shoulder, she would tear an exact hole on the other side, then stitch it up neatly, even paint to create a unique look. Another time she got a trouser from her father, who was 96 Kgs, 6ft 3 to Piddu’s 45 kg, 5ft 2, skinny girl.  Piddu cut of the trouser at the thighs, then sewed the bulky part on the sides to the waist and called it the genie shorts. She would wear it with her father’s belt with a huge buckle. 

Piddu had created an image of this girl who doesn’t give a fuck about anyone. She does care about what’s fair and what’s not. She thinks that she and her family haven’t been dealt a fair hand. So, she often gets into a fight when she sees someone bullying someone else, or someone doing something unfair. Despite her size people were afraid of her because she wouldn’t hesitate in confronting people. People know her as someone who will get into fights for small things. So they stay away. Some boys and girls do try to instigate her to have fun. She gives in to their taunts. She has a crush on a boy at school. Even he is afraid of her. She tries to act normal in front of him, but it never works.
