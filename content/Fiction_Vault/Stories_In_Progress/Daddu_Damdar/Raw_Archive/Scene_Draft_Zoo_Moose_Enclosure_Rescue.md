Act 2: 7-12 success - 12 is midpoint. Disillusionment.
Proactive. Strength
Act 3: 13-18 Downward spiral. - Broken, lost all hope, sabotaged.
Reactive. Weaknesses
18 - Forced to see a secret path that they couldn’t see before. Often something that their character flaw prevented them to do. (Enlightenment). They make a clear, extremely risky plan. (Final strategy)
Act 4: 19-24 Transformation.
Emotional truth. Embracing the truth. 
Stakes should staring be right in the face.
All wants replaced with needs. All fears are faced. All lies revealed. Every choice leads to a single Climax.
22-24 - New world. Character or world has changed. New perspective.  Or adapted in a unexpected way.

1. Akshi is like the gunda of the class. She sells cigarettes to boys in class. Netflix subscriptions - one time payment. She has one friend, Naomi Joseph. SHe has a crush on a boy in her class, Vibhor Bora. He is a handsome jock. Dumb but sweet. She can’t seem to be her confident rebellious self around the guy. She sees a boy harrassing a girl in the school. She gets into a fight. Gets punished, suspended. She is happy with the suspension as she thinks it is going to be fun time. 
When she gets home, her parents scold her for being so irresponsible. Then go back to their lives. THey are not very attentive parents. She is usually left on her own. So this time as well, she has to spend time alone at home. Her father has made donations to the school that’s why they haven’t rusticated her. They’ve taken away her credit cards as punishments. She calls a couple of her friends to hang out, but they are disappointed in her for using violence. She is stuck at home. She sees this old homeless person across the street, living in a house made of tarpaulin and sticks. She sees him behave strangely. Trying to uproot a tree with his bare hands. Trying to jump off of a wall and falling flat on his face  is doing it again and again. Failing but now stopping. She talks to him and observes that he zones out from time to time and calls himself a superhero. He tells her that he is building his strength by trying to uprooot the tree and learning to fly. She thinks he is a crazy guy and decides to stay away. . 

2. One day, she is walking around her neighborhood. She sees the old guy practice his jumps off the wall. She stops to observe. A few boys come around and make fun of the old guy. They push him around. He warns them not to bully him, or it will turn ugly for them. They laugh at him and destroy his house. This angers Akshi and she decides to butt in. She tells off the young boys, who now turn their attention to her. She fights a couple of them off, but they over power her. The older guy now puts his torn coat as a cape and tells the boys to get away from the girl, or he will have to do it himself. THe boys laugh at him and decide to finish the job by beating him up. The old guy has an episode of complete memory loss. He gets beaten up pretty badly. The old guy doesn’t remember where he is or who he is. Beaten and battered. Akshi takes him to the hospital to get him checked. 


3. Impetus>> Akshi develops an affection towards the old man. She makes him comfortable. Gives him her tent and a water cooler. She keeps an eye on him to make sure he is fine. Her suspension is also over. She is excited to go back to school. She wants to talk to Vibhor and ask him out on a date. She goes to school, but in the two weeks that she was suspended, the girl she saved has started dating Vibhor. She thinks that there is no benefit from doing good for someone. It always bites you back in the ass. Even helping this old man might come back to haunt her. She decides to not engage with the old man anymore. 
The old guy greets her every time he sees her pass by, but Akshi doesn’t respond to him. He carries on his daily routine of trying to uproot the tree. She feels sorry for him. One day there is a school trip to a zoo. One of their parents of guradians have to chaperon them. Akshi wants a chance to get close to Vibhor, but she can’t take her parents. She doesn’t even want to ask, because she knows they will be too busy. The night before the school trip, it’s all but decided that she will not be able to go. Then an idea pops into her head. She can dress the old guy up and call him her grandfather, her daddu. She cleans him up, gives him a shave and buys him a walker. 
At the zoo, she is looking for any chance to talk to the guy. Get close to him. She introduces her daddu. Daddu is cockblocking her all the way. Whenever she gets a chance to talk to vibhor, who has come with his older brother, Daddu pops out of nowhere and ruin their conversation. Akshi has to give him a slip to get some alone time with Vibhor. She ahs a good time with Vibhor. He is sweet and funny in a dumb way. She finds out that Vibhor is not really dating that girl. He just went on a couple of dates, and she is too smart for him. She makes him feel dumber than he really is. As they are walking around the zoo, most of the students have gone home. The teachers tell the students left to be with their guardians as they are also now leaving. Vibhor calls his brother on his mobile to meet him at the gate. Vibhor asks that he hasn’t seen Daddu anywhere around. Akshi realizes that he is an old demented person and he could be in danger. She and Vibhor look for him around the zoo, but can’t find him. A woman spots an old man in one of the enclosures. Akshi already knows who it’s going to be as she turns to look. Of course, no surprises there, it’s Daddu. She screams at him to get back. But he is in his fugue state, where he is just trying to recognize things around him. It’s a moose enclosure. Akshi and Vibhor scream at daddu but he doesn’t move. When Akshi sees that Daddu isn’t listening, she climbs in to bring him back herself. Her jump crackles a trig and a moose hears it at some distance and decides to check. Akshi runs towards Daddu, but sees the moose and stops. She remembers a tour guide telling them that moose have poor vision, so the key for a lion to kill it to stay still. The moose only sees movement. THe moose comes to check even closer. Vibhor is shouting at her to run back, but she doesn’t move at all. Daddu picks the worst moment to come to his senses, he sees Akshi, waves and calls out to her. The moose runs towards him. Akshi seeing this screams to get the moose’s attention and runs back. The moose sees her run and runs towards her. Daddu sees Akshi telling him to run the other way and save himself. The moose quickly gains up on Akshi and is about to hit her. Akshi stops as she is out of breath. She puts her hand up and shouts at the moose to stop. The moose actually stops. Moose looks at her with curiosity, but as she is catching her breath back, the moose is about to get aggressive again. Akshi knows this is the end. Suddenly something catches her and in a second she is out of the enclosure. She is both scared and dumbfounded to what happened. She catches her breath finally to see that it’s Daddu. Mind blown. His superpowers are real. Vibhor and his brother are gobsmacked. Vibhor’s brother, Kuber, has recorded everything on his phone. A couple of other people have seen it too, so Akshi quickly tells everyone, they should leave. 
 
4. They all come back to Akshi’s house. Kuber is losing his mind seeing the video again and again. Akshi can’t believeit and tries to rationalize, but Kuber shows him the video. In the video, Daddu notices Akshi in trouble, and in a blur runs towards her picks her up, while pushing the moose, jumps the fence and lands in a heroic manner. Vibhor is confused, why does Akshi’s grandfather have superpowers. Akshi tells them the truth. Daddu is a homeless old man who lives across the street. Daddu tells them that he told Akshi he was a superhero. Vihor and Kuber ask him all these questions about his superpowers, and his adventures. Daddu tells them that he doesn’t remember anything. He only remembers bits and parts of his old life. They decide to test his superpowers, but he is really weak, he can’t lift a table. They try to make him run fast, but he can only walk using a walker. They don’t understand how this is happening. Akshi thinks it might be because of his memory loss. If they could find more about his earlier life then maybe he could remember and get his powers back. Vibhor and Kuber take up the challenge of finding out more about Daddu, from old news or something. If he was a superhero, there must be some articles about him. They can not find much on google. They also put it up on social media if someone remembers seeing a superhero, or had an experience with superpowers. 
In the meanwhile, they also decide to start his training. They take him to a gym, where he is useless. Can barely pick up 5 kg dumbbells. His walker doesn’t fit the treadmill and so on. THey aren’t having much success. They are getting a lot of responses from people on social media, but they are mostly useless. 

5. Daddu spends his time either with Akshi and her friends, or at his tent. Vibhor gets a response from one of his social media followers, whose mom had seen something 40 years ago. He gets her number and goes to meet Akshi. Akshi goes to meet Daddu. The boys who had beaten Daddu earlier come to bully him again. Daddu tells Akshi to run and get help. But Akshi stays to fight back. The boys make fun of them and beat up Daddu again. Akshi yells at Daddu to use his superpowers. The boys think that Akshi has also gone mad. They beat her up. Daddu sees this and in anger gets up and beats up the boys. Akshi seeing that allt he boys are down, tries to stop daddu, but he is in rage. He takes out his anger on one particular boy and is about to hit him really hard, which would kill the boy for certain. Akshi finally manages to stop him, but she is shocked by this incident. She goes home, where Vibhor is waiting for her. He tells Akshi about the lead he has. They call the woman together. Who tells them taht she had seen a man. She describes him exactly like a younger Daddu. She tells them that he is not a superhero, but a supervillian. He killed 20 school girls by drowning their school bus. Together with the incident earlier and the woman’s story, Akshi starts doubting Daddu.

6. Dramatic question>> Is daddu a superhero or a supervillian? Good guy or bad guy? What will they do to find out? Vibhor rationalizes the woman’s story, that she might have been mistaken. Vibhor thinks that Daddu should fight crime or something if he has the powers. Akshi is not sure if that is a good idea. Daddu is into the idea of fighting crime. THis sense of purpose is the only thing he properly remembers. He knows that his powers are only to help people. Akshi gets convinced. But they still have to figure out how Daddu’s superpowers work sometimes but not always. 
**********************
7. They think that both times when Daddu showed his powers was to save Akshi. Maybe, his powers come back when he has to help someone. They decide to test this by finding trouble. They go to a shady part of town and try to find trouble, but they see nothing. Finally they find a few gundas harrassing girls. Daddu goes on his walker to confront the gundas. THe gundas laugh at him and push him away. Daddu retaliates and weakly slaps one of the gundas. The gunda gets angry and beats him up. He takes pity on the old man and lets him go. Daddu comes back beaten. Akshi thinks this was a mistake, they should go and get daddu to a hospital. As they are leaving, the gundas grab a girl and are forcing themselves on her. Akshi can’t see this and goes to save her. Vibhor and Daddu try to stop her, but she can’t be stopped. She confronts the gundas by hitting one of them in the balls with a stick. Another gunda comes her way she hits him on his head. The girl runs away. The gundas turn their attention to Akshi. Akshi tries to run, but the gundas grab her and pick her up. Akshi tries to hit the gunda and run away, but the gunda slaps her. She starts bleeding. Daddu is seeing this, his powers come back. He beats the gundas and ties them up and hangs them in their underwear. Vibhor and Akshi are ecstatic. Vibhor figures that the only time Daddu’s power comes is when Akshi is in trouble.
