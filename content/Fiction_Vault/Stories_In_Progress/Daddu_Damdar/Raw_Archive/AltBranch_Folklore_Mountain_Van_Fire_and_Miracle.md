Some back story with god themes. 
Bisnu didn’t know what to do. He prayed to the gods, he begged the girl to help him. They tried one last time. This time the khatiya moved. Everyone who saw this was stunned. They believed that the girl was some kind of devi. Bisnu didn’t agree. He wanted to get rid of his troubles. He sent her to the city hospital.
That night, the rain was incessant. It got so cold that all the quilts could not keep the villagers warm. Bisnu was restless and tried sleeping. But the cold was too much to bear. Suddenly the earth started shaking. It was an earthquake. Bisnu ran outside to see all the villagers gathered on the road. Bhuppi’s mother shouted that this is all because the devi was sent out of the village that all these calamities are happening. People standing on the road, in the cold winter rain. Shivering and shaking like the earth a few moments before. Bisnu started to believe that the girl was indeed a devi and needed to be brought back. He took bhuppi on his scooter to go and get the girl back, although he knew that there was no way he could catch upto the van. 
Bisnu was determined. Dehradun was 12 hours from the village. He knew that the van would reach Dehradun by 8 in the morning. WIth the pace he was going, he could reach Dehradun by 10 then take the girl and come back to the village by 11 in the night. It might be too late by then. He rode his scooter fast. AS he got out of the village the weather was nice and cool. No sign of rain here. He twisted the accelerator and rode even faster. 
It was 3 o’clock. Bhuppi was dozing on the back seat. Although Bisnu himself was sleepy, he kept telling Bhuppi to stay awake. Bisnu’s eyes were closing and he could be forgiven for not seeing a man waving in front of his scooter. For a split second that he slept, he had a dream where he was going somewhere and the girl grabbed his hand. She told him to take her with him. Bisnu refused. The girl was begging almost. Bisnu jerked his hand away. He turned and walked, the girl appeared in front of her. Her hair flying. THe silhoute of her body immense from up close. Everything black, except the eyes. The eyes were wide and looking at Bisnu.
This woke up Bisnu from his sleep and he saw the driver of the van waving in front of his scooter. The scooter skid and hit the mountain. Luckily Bisnu and Bhuppi were fine. They had minor scratches. The driver thought that Bisnu is going to yell at him for causing the accident, but he was relieved to see him. He asked where the girl was? Where was the van? The driver tells him that ever since he left the village, he has been having trouble. His van sputtered to a halt. His fuel was leaking and his tire got flat again. So he had to stop. He got out of the van to check the tires and the leak. The van started moving down the slope and went down the mountain. 
They looked down the mountain and saw the van burning in flames. Bisnu was filled with guilt. Devi or no devi, his decision had led to this girl’s death. The three of them went down the mountain to have a look. Bisnu wept seeing the van, thikning about the tragedy. They sat there till the fire went out. Most of it out of guilt, but also because the fire provided them with some warmth. As the sun started rising around 6. The fire also slowly went out. Bisnu and Bhuppi had slept for a bit till then. IN the morning Bisnu wanted to check if there are any remains of the girl to take home. He looked inside the now burnt van. He almost shat his pants seeing the girl still alive. The clothes on her body were burnt. THe previous injuries were still there. But she was otherwise unharmed. It was a miracle.
Bisnu covered the girl’s body in his shawls and blankets and brought her back to his village. The sun shone brightly and the village was back to normal.
