Memories are strange things. Things we remember exactly as they happened may not be the way someone else might remember it. Details change, perspectives shift, and suddenly the main character is someone else. Sometimes you build your own narrative around how things happened. Fill gaps that you don’t remember with things that give shape to that narrative. Making you the hero of the memory. 
So many things happen to us throughout our lives. We don’t remember most of them. Humans forget more than we remember. Our brains are wired to filter out useless information. Memories and details that are not used over time. Even everyday details are forgotten. It’s a great credit to our species that we’ve managed to remember and pass on so much crucial knowledge. Writing is a crucial part of it. Writing and passing off knowledge. If someone is in the habit of forgetting then a system must be devised for them to remember things.
Imagine if a superhero forget who he was. Will he even remember that he was a superhero? He will go through his life trying to find out who he was before he forgot. There hasn’t been anyone who could come and tell him. No one to guide him. He will be alone. Wandering the cities and villages. Where would he even start? What does he remember? If there is a sense of duty in him. Saving people. Stopping crime. Would he go about doing that even if he doesn’t have powers? At some point it would be a hindrance to him. Getting beaten up, insulted, pushed and shoved. Would he not question his sanity? Would he not start thinking he isn’t who he thinks he is? A superhero. There is no evidence of him being that. Then how does he know? Has he done anything recently that suggests that he is a superhero. 
Maybe he remembers parts of his previous life. Maybe he remembers just enough. Maybe he isn’t quite sure whether what he remembers are his memories or his dreams. He has a calling. An instinct that tells him that he is supposed to do something. A voice inside his head that tells him that he is meant to save people. What if there was a voice. Why isn’t the voice telling him about himself? The voice only tells him about what he needs to do.
He travels from city to city to perform his duties. If he doesn’t do anything the voice becomes louder and louder. What does the voice tell him? The voice might tell him to do something. It has to be specific. Does it have to be instructive? Prescriptive? The voice tells him to go places. Maybe the voice has directed him for the past 25 years. Taken him from city to city. 
He was first asked to go to Indore. He was directed to a place where a robbery was happening. HE was supposed to stop it. The voice took him there and stopped. He didn’t know what to do. He tried to stop the robbers, but one of them stabbed him. He died. Only for a few minutes. He woke up with pain in his stomach and the robbers gone. The wound pained him, but it vanished in a few hours. He was all okay. This made him believe he was supposed to save people. Help them. Stop evil in whatever form he found it. He began his journey. 
The voice kept him in Indore for a while. It stopped telling him to do things. Then one day he was told to go to a place with a white shutter. He didn’t listen to it. The voice got louder and louder till he thought it would blow his brains to smithereens. He had to listen to it. He went to the white shutter. It was locked. HE didn’t know what to do. Thee voice also stopped. He looked around. There was nothing there to save. He decided to go back. But the voice became louder and unbearable till he turned back.
He stood at the shutter for a while. Thinking what to do. He considered breaking the lock. He applied his full strength, but he couldn’t summon it. It was like his powers were gone. He didn’t know what else to do. His attempts attracted the attention of a couple of people. They came to inquire. Our superhero made up a silly excuse. “Mai chori kar raha tha.” He said. Now knowing what else to say. It made him feel stupid. “Mai kuch bhi bol sakta tha. Koi aur bahana jaise…” He thought for a little while, but no other excuse came to his mind. He decided that the only idea is by default the best idea. He stood waiting for the consequences. He was prepared to fight them. 
The men were surprisingly calm. They scanned him from head to toe. A damdar brand hoodie. Dirty and torn at the shoulder. A plain Tshirt and trousers too short for him. They told him to leave and not come back and they won’t report him to the police. 
The superhero thinks about it. It’s a good deal. He thanked them profusely and turned to leave. But as soon as he took a step, his ear started ringing again. The voice was a shriek now. He turned and it stopped. THe men had seen enough. They warned him again. He pleaded that even if he wants to go, he can’t. The superhero suggests to call the police. That way they can arrest him and take him away from this place. The men suddenly tense up. THey don;t like the idea of calling the police. They tell him that instead of the police they will beat him and teach him a lesson. 
The superhero warns them not to get aggressive. He fears for their well being and tells them to back off. This seems to amuse and anger the men and they beat the superhero up. The superhero hits them with all his force but it barely make a difference. The superhero is lying on the ground at the end. Beaten up. The men pick him up and throw him a few blocks away.
The voice tells him to follow them. After arguing with it for a few seconds, he is forced to do as told. The men go around the warehouse complex and enter the building through the back door. The superhero follows. 
It’s dark inside. Stuff stored and stacked haphazardly everywhere. It looks like people’s discarded stuff. Racks, old television sets, tattered charpais. The men can’t be seen anywhere. He goes forward and sees an old rifle.
