Title: 1… 2… 1 2 3 4
   
   Part 1: Govind and Shweta are both 21 year olds. They have been friends for 15 years and have dated for 5. They live in a small town. As our story starts, they are sitting near a fountain, eating kachori from a road side stall. Govind proposes to Shweta very casually. Shweta thinks he might be joking. Govind assures her that he is serious. Shweta tells him why they can’t get married, no. 1: They are young. Govind says it doesn’t matter they both know they love each other. No. 2: THey both don’t have a job. Govind tells her that she can get a job, he wants to be a musician. They have an argument about this. Shweta tells him that to have a stable family and a marriage he needs to get a job. Govind thinks he has to follow his dreams. This is the right time. He is young and has enough time to struggle and make it. Shweta is frustrated, but relents to his arguments. No. 3. She doens’t know if her father would agree. Govind takes out his cell phone. THere is a group call to his mom and Shweta’s dad. They’ve been listening. They both approve. Finally no 4: He hasn’t proposed to her properly. A friend gets up from the opposite side of the fountain and hands Govind a guitar. Govind sings a song confessing his love and asking Shweta to marry him. As we fade away. 
   Part 2: 1 year later. 
   Govind declares that he is not going to get married to Shweta. His mother tries to put some sense into him. She is not doing a good job. She threatens, emotionally blackmails, even hits him. But Govind is not relenting. He is not telling his mother either. 
   Shweta enters. Hits him with something. THey fight while the daal is being made. The pressure cooker is whistling. Shweta is hitting him and shouting at him. While his mother tells Shweta to knock some sense into him. It’s all very chaotic with the cooker and the fighting and the arguments. (Anger) Shweta then thinks that Govind is kidding. They’ve been together since childhood now they’ve been engaged for a year. And now he wants to break up. He is surely joking. Playing a prank. SHe tells his mom that he is playing one his practical jokes on them. And they are so silly they’ve fallen for it. (Denial)
   Finally Govind takes control. He takes Shweta outside to talk to her. He explains that they want different things from life. And if they stay together they will end up hating each other. Shweta doens’t understand.
   Govind wants to be a musician. SHweta wants him to get a job. THey will get married soon, and she wants a stable home for both of them. Govind refuses. He tells her that music is her passion. 
   A sequence of scenes we go though Bargaining. Shweta gives him various options like. He can work and purue music on the side. Maybe he can get a job in some music shop or something. Finally she accedes that Govind can focus on music. SHe will get a job and make money for the both of them. Govind refuses. He knows that Shweta’s father will never accept this. Shweta knows this is true. But she says that she will fight her dad. If he disagrees. THey will leave home and run away. SHe will get a job and earn for the both of them. He tells her that she will get a job now, but in a couple of years his not making money will start to bother her. SHe will get angry, pick fights with him. Then eventually she will start resenting him and hate him. He doesn’t want that to happen. 
   Shweta begins to cry. (Depression) She is sad and inconsolable. Govind throws away his stern behavior and tells Shweta that this is for their own good. He calmly explains, while she cries remembering that she will miss him. SHe doesn’t know life without him etc. 
   Finally Shweta accepts the decision and leaves. 
   
   Part 3: 2 years later
   It’s a wedding house. Decorations are everywhere. Govind comes in his scooty towards the back gate. Some people are carrying tables and chairs inside. He sneaks in with them. He sneaks through the corridor hiding his face. He finally encounters Shweta. Shweta is shocked at seeing him and shouts his name. HEr father comes out from another room hearing the name and hits him with a shoe. Shweta stops him from beating up govind and takes him inside her room. 
   Govind has had a change of heart. He has contacted Shweta a few times before this. He agrees to take up a job. He tells Shweta that he is going to marry her take up a job. Wherever she wants. He is having a tough time, doing music. THere is just so much rejection. He doesn’t know what to do.
   Shweta tells him that this is the wrong time. She is getting married today. Govind tells her that it doesn’t matter. He knows that she loves him. Shweta tells him that she does love him. But she has realized that what Govind said was true. He is not going to be happy taking up a job. And she is not going to be happy if he is not happy. Govind tries really hard to convince her. He thinks that she is getting married to take revenge on him. Shweta tells him that this is not the case. She knows the guy she is marrying. He is a good man. SHe likes him also. Govind tells her that he can not live without her. 
   Finally Shweta tells Govind that they’ve made up their minds about something. She tells him that she knows that becomeing a musician was Govind’s dream. He wouldn’t be happy giving it up. There is struggle everywhere. He has to fight it and make it somehow. On his own terms. He is just having a momentary doubt. He should go and try make it as a musician. Govind is angry and sad and leaves. 
   
   Part 3: 3 years later: Mumbai.
   
   We’re in a cafe. Some music playing in the background. Shweta enters and takes a seat. She is pregnant. She smiles looking at a something. WE see her POV, it’s Govind. He is singing one of his tunes. People are not paying attention. THey are talking amongst themselves. Only an old lady is cheering him on at the front of the cafe. Govind sees Shweta and smiles. He finishes his set and goes to talk to her. 
   
   They talk about their lives. Govind tells her that he couldn’t do much in their home town. So after she got married, he came to Bombay. Got rejected a bunch of times. Now he is singing in bars and cafes. He makes decent money. He has a youtube channel. That has a few subscribers. 
   Shweta tells him that she has a baby girl now. And she loves Govind’s music. Shweta puts it on her phone everynight to calm her down and put her to sleep. 
   Govind and Shweta talk about their lives. Govind is happy with what he is doing. Although Bombay is expensive but he likes it here and he is making enough money with these cafe and bar gigs. 
   Shweta has a husband and a 3 year old son. She seems reluctant in talking about her husband. She wants to work. Her husband makes a lot of money from family businesses. So she doesn’t really have to work anywhere. But she gets bored at home. And was thinking of taking up a job in advertising as she was really interested in TV ads and all. But she can’t. With her baby on the way, and her husband. She can’t think of doing both. 
   Govind encourages her to do whatever she wants. He tells her that sometimes sacrifies have to be made. You have to lose the thing you love the most to follow your dreams. Shweta understands that he is talking about her. 
   They continue talking as we fade away. We see them recounting old days. Laughing. 
   
   Part 4: 4 years later: Mumbai
   
   Govind is in a conference room. He is sitting with his manager. THere are a bunch of executives sitting in front of him. They are trying to convince him to do a song for their new soap brand. Govind doesn’t want to do it. His manager keeps whispering to him to hear the agency out. It’s a lot of money. GOvind tells him that he doesn’t work for money. While they are arguing we see Shweta tiptoeing to enter the conference room. THe executive looks at her angrily and gestures her to sit down in the corner. Govind turns to the agency executives and tells them that what he does is art and he doesn’t believe advertising has good music or it can be called art. THe executive has no answer. WE see Shweta hesitating, then suddenly she bursts into a song, “Hema, Rekha, Jaya aur Sushma, sabki pasand nirma. WAshing powder nirma. Nirma.” The executive is flabbergasted. He apologizes to Govind and tells him that she is just a junior copywriter. Govind is staring at Shweta and sings, “Tandurusti ki raksha karta hai lifebuoy, lifebuoy hai jahan tandurusti hai wahan. Lifebuoy”. The executive is stunned. Shweta and Govind sing at the same time, “Doodh doodh doodh doodh, Wonderful doodh. Piyo glass full” THey both start laughing. Govind turns to the executive and tells him that he will do it. Cut to:
   Shweta and Govind are in a car. Shweta is driving. GOvind tells her that he si proud of her for following what she wanted to do. Shweta plays it down, saying that she is only a junior copywriter. It’s not like she is the creative director. Govind smiles and says, “Yet!” Shweta smiles and nods, understanding what he wants to say. Silence for a few seconds. Govind asks her how her husband is. Shweta tells him that they got divorced. 
   They stop at a school Kids are coming out. One small boy of 4 years is waiting near the gate. He sees Shweta and runs towards her. He sees Govind. Govind doesn’t know how to behave near the kid. Shweta introduces them. Govind looks in his pocket to give him something. He finds something. He gives it to the kid. THe kid starts eating it. But Govind stops him. He tells him that it’s a harmonica. You have to play it. Shweta smiles as she sees both of them interacting. GOvind shows the kid how to play harmonica as we fade away.
