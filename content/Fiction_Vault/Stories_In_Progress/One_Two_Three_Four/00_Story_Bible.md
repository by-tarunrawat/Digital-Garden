# 1… 2… 1 2 3 4: Master Story Bible

## Narrative Structure: Four Eras
1. **Age 21 (The Breakup & Five Stages of Grief):** Govind breaks off his engagement with Shweta so she won't grow to resent his artistic poverty.
2. **Age 23 (The Wedding Eve):** Govind returns to stop her arranged marriage, but Shweta stands firm, urging him not to compromise his music.
3. **Age 26 (Mumbai Cafe Encounter):** Shweta is pregnant and married into a wealthy family; Govind is surviving as an indie cafe singer.
4. **Age 30 (The Ad Agency Jingle Reconnection):** Govind refuses to sell out to a soap commercial until junior copywriter Shweta spontaneously bursts into classic Indian TV jingles (Nirma, Lifebuoy). They leave together as equals.
