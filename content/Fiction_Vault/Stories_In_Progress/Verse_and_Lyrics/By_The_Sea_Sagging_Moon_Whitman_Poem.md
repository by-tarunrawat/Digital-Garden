Date: 05-02-2019
Topic: "By the sea, beneath the yellow and sagging moon." (after Walt Whitman)

By the sea, beneath the yellow and sagging moon, there lies a world completely dark. The creatures float like angels and demons. Spewing and spouting colors. Chasing after each other in the dark. Like its a full clear day. 
By the sea, beneath the yellow and sagging moon, I see you staring at me. Your face brighter than the moon. Even in this dark I can see it clearly. I can see the mole on your nose. Your lips shine in the moonlight. Inviting. Intoxicating.
By the sea, beneath the yellow and sagging moon, I make this promise to myself. I will love you the same each day if not more. I will shower you with kisses. I’ll warm you with hugs. I’ll please you with nonsense. And I’ll give you lots of rubs.
