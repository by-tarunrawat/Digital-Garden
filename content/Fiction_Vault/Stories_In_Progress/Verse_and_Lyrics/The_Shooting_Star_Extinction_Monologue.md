Date: 03-03-2019
Topic: You see a shooting star…

And I wish I had shit figured out. I wish I knew what I was doing. I wish I can find a way.
I see a shooting star and I wish for money, I wish for health and my family to be okay.
There is so much to wish for. I think I know what really matters. But I feel some needs need to be met.
Like money and career and a little bit of fame on social media. It’s not too much to ask for I feel. 
But it’s just a shooting star. It probably doesn’t even exist anymore. A shadow of what has been.
Does it not exist if it has died years ago? or does it exist because we see it now?
Does something really exist if nobody notices it, knows about it, cares for it? 
That means, most of us barely exist and some of are existing too much. 
And all of us want to exist a little more. 
We want people to notice us. A few aren’t enough. Our friends aren’t enough and neither is our family.
We want more. People in our offices, people we meet on the street. 
People we barely know on social media and people we never meet.
Maybe we are all shooting stars. 
Burning out. Wishing upon ourselves to go down in flames.
But once. Just once. Burn so bright; that anyone who sees us, wishes for the same.
