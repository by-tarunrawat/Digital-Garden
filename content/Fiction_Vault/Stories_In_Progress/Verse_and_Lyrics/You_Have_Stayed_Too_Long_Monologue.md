Date: 12-03-2019
Topic: “You have stayed too long”

You’re not welcome here anymore. 
What made you think you could stay here forever.
World is out there. 
You haven’t seen amazing things. 
You haven’t done anything worth remembering.
Will you exist after you’re gone.
For a few years maybe. 
In the memory of a loved one.
Then what?
You’ll be forgotten. Like your ancestors.
Your grandfather will be wiped from memory once you’re gone. 
Your father soon after.
Who will remember you.
You have stayed too long like this.
Stand up. Take action. Move forward.
Fall. Then get up. Move forward. Fall again.
Maybe your failures will guide others.
You will be remembered by your failures.
The one who has shown the way.
Put yourself out there.
That is the only way.
No one will see your value from where you are.
Show your worth.
Live your dreams now.
You will not see tomorrow. Not today.
Today you have this moment to deal with.
What will you do?
Stay here, a little longer?
Or take a step. Just one.
They are your dreams to live. 
Live them and be remembered.
Or, hope your children would have courage.
To live your dreams for you.
But if they are smart.
They’ll live their own.
And show their ancestors how it’s done.
