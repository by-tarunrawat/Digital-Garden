Kitni baatein kehni hai mujhko tumse. 
Saari shikayaten hi hain. Shayad tumhe kadvi lagen.

Mujhe napasnd hai jaise tum daant deti ho mujhko. 
Mujhe ye shikayat nahi ki tum mujhe daantti ho, ya chillati ho mujhpe.
Agar meri khata hai to mujhe manzoor hai tumhari gaaliyan.
Jitna sharminda karna chaho, kar lo. Mai khamosh rahunga.
Bura to bahut lagega. Mai kuch nahi kahunga.
Lekin ab tum aisi baaton pe sharminda karti ho,
Jinhe nazarandaz kiya ja sakta hai.
Bhool ya nadani samajhke.
Par jab ye nadaaniyan tum karti ho…
To bhi mujh par hi bhadak padti ho. 
Jaise isme bhi meri hi galti ho. 
 
Tum kehti ho ye cheezein zaruri hain tumhare liye.
Zaruri hai tumhare liye, isiliye mai kar raha hun.
Agar koi galti hui bhi hai mujhse,
To wo galti to kisi se bhi ho sakti hain.
Tum se bhi. 
Tumse huin hain kayi baar.
Magar mai nazarandaz karta hun.
Farak nahi padta mujhe ki tum kya galtiyan karti ho.
Mai hun use sudharne ke liye.
Kyunki yahi ‘job description’ hai mera.
Roz mai ye koshish karta hun ki tumhe khush kar sakun, hasa sakun.
Tum muskurao meri baaton pe. 
Hairan ho sun kar kahaniya jo mai sunaun tumko.
Mai chahta hun ki jab tum ghar aao to choomo mujhe, gale lagao.
Mai jo karta hun inhi iraado ke saath karta hun.
Mera aur koi maqsad nahi. 
Isliye chubhta hai mujhko jab tum kuch aisa kehti ho.
Jisse jazbaato ko thes lagti hai.
Ho sakta hai ye bhi meri hi galti hai. 
Mere jazbaat kamzor hain itne.
Zara si thokar pe thes lag jaati hai.
Lekin mujhe farak nahi padta jab koi aur mujhe kuch kehta hai.
Tumhari baat na jaane kyun dard deti hain.

Jaise dil nikal ke rakha jis ke saamne,
Usi ne sara khoon nichod liya.
Aisa lagta hai jaise meri neeyat mayne hi nahi rakhti.

Mujhe lagta tha ki tum samajhti ho mujhko.
Jaanti ho ki mai kaun hun.
Tumhe pasand hai ut-patang harkatein meri.
Tumhe pasand hai meri ye ajeebo-gareeb baatein.
Mujhe lagta tha ki tumhe pasand hun mai,
Shayad khud ko hi jhooth bol raha tha.
Mujhe ye bhi lagta hai kabhi ki meri wahi harkatein, 
waise baatein, jo tum pasand kiya karti thi,
Ab tumhare liye purani ho gayin hain.
Aisa hai to koi baat nahi.
Mai samajhta hun. Aisa hota hai.
Lekin aisa hai to tum yahi bata do mujhko.
Ye mujhe kam bura lagega.
Kam se kam mujhe pata to chal jayega ki wajah kya hai tumhari berukhi ki. 




Agar tum kehti ho ki tumhe mujhse pyar hai. 
To kya itna bhi pyar nahi ki meri jazbaaton ko samajh sako.
Agar mai tumhe batata hun ki mujhe kaisa lagta hai,
To ye kyun ek aur wajah ban jaati hai meri hi kisi nasamjhi ki.
Kya mai akela hi rahun apni in khayalon ke saath.
Shayad yahi thik hoga.  


Galti ye hai ki bhul jaata hun kuch cheezein,
Par ye mai kar bhi to tumhare liye hi raha tha.
Kya ye baat mayene nahi rakhti.

Kya ye baat mayne rakhti hai tumhare liye? 
Mera kyun, kya aur kaise… isse koi farak nahi padta?
Meri neeyat kya thi? 
Kyun itni si galtiyan itni chubh jaati hain tumhe…
ki tumhe baaki kuch yaad nahi rehta.
Kya ye itni badi galti hai,
Ki mere in jazbaat ka koi matlab nahi?
