> [!abstract] Editorial Diagnosis & Triage
> **Verdict:** Story Spine (verse | 2018_Crucible)
> **Core Spark:** The contrast between knowing that action is the only cure and remaining physically pinned to the chair as noon turns to night
>
> **Structural & Prosodic Critique:**
> - **Why It Fails Right Now:** Rhyming *concluded / deluded*, *band / hand*, and *outta luck / make a buck* turns acute psychological paralysis into a nursery rhyme. The bouncy AABB meter fights the heavy subject matter.
> - **The Core Idea Worth Saving:** The opening refrain (*It was noon and nothing is concluded*) and the central conflict—*My heart paralyzed but my brain is working overtime* while daylight bleeds into evening darkness—is a sharp capture of executive dysfunction.
> - **How to Fix It:** Strip all end-rhymes completely. Rewrite as rhythmic, unrhymed free verse or a tight prose-poem (matching the cadence of [[Kitni_Baatein_Shikayaten_Monologue]]), keeping *It was noon and nothing is concluded* as the structural anchor line.
>
> **Actionable Next Steps:**
> - [ ] Amputate forced end-rhymes (deluded, band, vice, buck, suited) while preserving the raw thoughts
> - [ ] Use *It was noon and nothing is concluded* as an unrhymed structural refrain across 3 tight stanzas
> - [ ] Connect to [[Courage_To_Be_Disliked_Full_Reflections]] and [[Standing_In_The_Doorway_Freewrite]]

Date: 26-11-2018
Topic: It was noon and nothing is concluded

It was noon and nothing is concluded
There is too much to do, I’m tired, restless but not deluded.

I possibly can’t do this on my own, I’m waiting for a hand
It’s not coming I know, I’m a one man band

It will soon be bright. Too bright to know wrong from right.
I still can’t make a choice, I can’t move ahead, I still can’t fight.

My heart paralyzed but my brain is working overtime.
I’m stuck, Gotta move, I tell myself, but my courage is dyin’

Ive read all the lines, heard all the philosophers, they say sitting on your ass is a vice
I know action is the key, I know it, you don’t have to tell me twice.

It was noon and nothing is concluded.
There is nothing done yet, I’m sad and getting deluded.

Soon the darkness will be here. 
I won’t know ‘coz I’ve already closed my eyes.
I don’t want to sit silently into the night,
I want to get up and fight.
I can’t bear the stares I get,
It pierces my heart like a cigarette.
I shouldn’t give a fuck, I’m outta luck, I’ve gotta make a buck,
But that’s not me. I’ve don’t care about money. I shouldn’t start.

It is noon now and nothing is concluded.
I need to take something up, make my mind up and just get suited.
