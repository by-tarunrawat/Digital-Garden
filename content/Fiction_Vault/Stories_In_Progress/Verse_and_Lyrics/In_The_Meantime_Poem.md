> **Verdict:** Story Spine (song_lyrics | 2018_Crucible)
> **Core Spark:** Accepting that figuring out life may take forever, while choosing to love and be grounded in the interim
>
> **Structural & Prosodic Critique:**
> - **Why It Fails Right Now:** Read silently as page poetry, placeholder end-rhymes (*universe / verse*, *stars / mars*, *understand / stand*) feel juvenile and telegraphed.
> - **The Core Idea Worth Saving:** Structurally, this is a **3-verse acoustic folk/indie song** with a built-in chorus turnaround (*In the meantime, let me just love a bit... Maybe I'll lose my way, but you'll bring me home / In the meantime*). The emotional thesis—parking cosmic/career ambition to stay present with your partner while unresolved—is earned and honest.
> - **How to Fix It:** Treat this explicitly as a **lyric sheet for acoustic guitar**. Replace the cosmic filler rhymes with grounded domestic details, and lean into the musicality of the *In the meantime* turnaround.
>

Topic: In the meantime…

I want to touch the sky. I want to fly so high that I can walk on the clouds. 
I want to run. I want to leave the world behind. If it was up to me I’d go so far I can’t see around.
But I’m trying to figure shit out. 
Don’t know if I’d ever figure it out.
Don’t see a way, I’ll give it my all anyway… 
In the meantime, Let me just love a bit
Let me have fun with it.
Maybe I’ll wander off, 
But you’ll bring me back,
In the meantime…

I want to see the universe. I want this verse to reach the stars.
I want to live on the moon. I want to live like a king. I will make a getaway house on mars.
Let me see what I can do.
Maybe it’s to ambitious too.
Maybe I’ll never get there, I’ll give it my all anyway.
In the meantime, Let me just love a bit.
I’ll be yours if you permit,
Maybe I’ll lose my way,
But you’ll bring me home,
In the meantime…

Maybe my ambitions are too lofty to understand. Maybe I’m a fool to even take that stand.
But I know I have you to back me up all the way.
And even if I lose my shit you’ll never let me stray.
In the meantime, let me just love a bit,
I’ll belong to you, for time infinite,
In the meantime.
