Jayanti Vora: Jayanti was a 26 years old widow when she decided to be surrogate for Shejali. She has a son who is 3 years old. He has a heart tumor which needed to be operated on. She was poor and could not afford the treatment. She tried reaching out to her husband’s family but they refused to help her. 
Her son’s doctor knew about Shejali and Rohan looking for a surrogate and suggests her to explore that option. She doesn’t want to do it, but she is out of options and talks to Shejali and Rohan, who immediately like her. They agree to pay for her son’s treatment, and 10 lakh rupees after she gets pregnant. Shejali has no option but to take their deal.

Jayanti’s son dies despite the surgery. This completely breaks Jayanti. Her only family gone. She feels alone. 8 months pregnant she runs away. She feels that since she has lost a child it’s only fair that she keeps this one.

She moved around the country for the fear of getting caught. She worked as a teacher to support herself and Ketana. She didn’t earn much. But she managed in providing for Ketana. She had cut all her ties from the previous life. 

She got the shock of her life when she got the court notice. At first she did not tell Ketana. She had never talked about it with her. Eventually she tells Ketana that she was her surrogate. Ketana got really angry with her first. She blamed Jayanti for taking her away from her biological parents. But she loved Jayanti. So, she forgave her.
After the court case finished, Ketana got a choice between staying with her mother or going back to her biological parents. Jayanti got scared by this. She thought that there was a good chance that Ketana would chose Shejali. Jayanti tried her best to convince Ketana to stay with her. Jayanti sees Ketana talking to Shejali in court. So, she fears that Ketana might chose to go with Shejali.
Ketana tells Jayanti that she doesn’t want to leave her. Despite whatever happened in the past, she still loves her. But she wants to know her biological parents. So, she suggest that the move to a house close to them. So that she can know her parents as well. Jayanti doesn’t want to share Ketana with Shejali and Rohan. But she agrees.

Ketana Vora: Ketana is a 17 year old. She has lived a nomadic life. Moving from one city to the other with her Jayanti. She is a good student and works hard. She has mostly taken care of herself when her mother is at work. She takes care of her mother as much as her mother takes care of her.
She was always curious about her father. She has asked Jayanti about him. Jayanti told her that her late husband was her father. She also tells her about the child she lost. But she never tells her about her own origin story.
One day after school, Ketana was watching TV waiting for her mom, Jayanti comes home and tells her about the court case. She tells her that she is not her biological mother. But she did carry her in her womb for 9 months and gave birth to her. She tells her that she agreed to the deal because her son was sick. And she needed the money for his treatment. When he died during the pregnancy, she felt all alone. She had no family to go back to and Ketana was the only one left in her life. She knew that if she gave her away, she would never see her again. So she fled. 
This makes Ketana really angry. She is disappointed that her mother lied to her about her father and her family. She understands the pain Jayanti must’ve gone through at a child’s death. But she is angry that But she loves her and doesn’t want to leave her. So she supports her in the court case. 
She is curious about her biological parents. But she hates them because she thinks that they want to separate her from her mother. She is determined to stay with Jayanti and doesn’t want anything to do with Shejali and Rohan. Her heart softens after she sees Shejali break down in tears after the court verdict.
She goes to talk to Shejali after the verdict. Shejali doesn’t think Ketana will choose them. She tells Ketana that they have looked for her for 17 years and have nothing but love for her. She tells Ketana that she hopes to get to know Ketana more. And would like to talk to her and meet her if she wishes. Ketana tells her that she wants to think about it and goes home. 
Ketana after thinking about it, decides that she wants to get to know her biological parents. She tells Jayanti, who is reluctant at first. But Ketana tells her that it is her decision as the court said. She pacifies Jayanti by saying she doesn’t want to leave her. She wants to work out a middle ground.
She talks to Shejali again. She tells her that she would agree to move to live close to Shejali if Jayanti moves with her. Shejali agrees. She tells her that they have a smaller house right behind their house, where Jayanti and Ketana can stay.
Ketana is slowly getting excited about meeting her parents. But she is apprehensive too. She is scared what if this doesn’t work out.

Shejali Sinha: Shejali Sinha is a 45 year old strong confident woman. She is an equal partner in the family business. She always wanted a small family with two kids. She worked in a small software firm before she got married. After she got married she and her husband decided to quit their jobs and start an IT company.
Shejali always wanted to have kids. So, when her company started to do well, she had Pratik. After she had Pratik, she started to get less and less involved with the business. 
Shejali and Rohan wanted another child. But because of complications with her previous pregnancy, doctors told her that it might be dangerous for both her and the child. The doctors suggest that they can go for adoption or surrogacy. 
Rohan is on board with the idea straight away. He thinks that surrogacy would be the best way to go. Shejali is not sure about another woman having her baby. She wants to have a baby herself. SHe is adamant. Rohan tells her he doesn’t want to lose her. He would not let her risk her life like that. He tells her that it will be of no use to the child if she is not there to take care of her. She realizes that Rohan is right. And even if she doesn’t carry the child in her womb, it will still be her blood. She agrees.
Her doctor introduces her to Jayanti. She hears her story and feels sympathetic towards her. She doesn’t want to take advatage of her bad situation, so , she offers to pay her 10 Lakh Rs and pay for her son’s treatment.
When Jayanti’s son dies, she offers to bring her home to take care of her. She agrees, but right before moving, she flees. At first Shejali doesn’t believe Jayanti would do something like this. When she realizes the truth, she gets angry and reports it to the police. Police finds out that Jayanti has left the city, but they don’t know where she went. 
Shejali realizes that she might never get to see her child and goes into depression. Rohan seeing her like this hires a private detective. But after a year he comes up empty handed. Shejali accepts the situation and drowns herself in work. She stops paying attention to Pratik. 
Years later, the detective finds Jayanti. Shejali wants to go talk to her and sort things out. But ROhan tells her that they well only get Ketana back going through the legal system. He tells her that they will file a suit against her for fraud. ANd they will demand the money and Ketana back.
Shejali doesn’t care about the money. She just wants to get Ketana back. Rohan tells her that Jayanti might not give up Ketana so easily. That’s why they should create pressure on her by going through the court and demanding the money back.

During the court proceedings Jayanti tries to talk to Ketana. But her lawyer advises her against it. She feels that she needs to convince Ketana that she will be loved. When the court gives the verdict, she is scared that Ketana will decide to stay with Jayanti. 
Ketana comes to talk to her after the verdict. She is surprised and happy. Ketana tells her that Jayanti is her mom and she will never leave her. She also tells her that she does want to know Shejali and Rohan as well. She wants to reach a compromise. She offers to move close to them only if Jayanti moves too.
Rohan agrees to it. Shejali tells Ketana that they have a guest house behind their house where she can move. Shejali is excited about the prospect of living so close to her daughter. Although she doesn’t want to see Jayanti, she is willing to pay that price.

Rohan Sinha: Rohan Sinha is a 47 year old man. He has been a businessman ever since he graduated college. Right after college he started a mail order printed T-shirt business. It didn’t do well for a while. Rohan was tenacious. He kept on printing pamphlets and mailing them. He met people at trade fairs and built a network. 
Rohan met Shejali at a small business trade fair. Shejali was representing the company she worked for. She was meeting businesses and pitching them transitioning their business to websites. She met Rohan at his company’s stall. She talked to him about getting a website. Rohan really liked the idea. But he told her that his business could not afford to spend on a website. 
Shejali and Rohan met at another trade fair after that. His business had started doing well. She talked to him about getting a website again. Rohan was charmed by how smart Shejali was. He wanted to meet her again so he sets up a meeting. They start talking on the phone. Business at first. THey become friends. Start meeting more often. Rohan proposes soon after. Shejali accepts. 
After they got engaged. Rohan started spending more and more time with Shejali. Shejali would talk to him about work. Rohan could listen to her talk all day. He was awestruck by her intelligence. Shejali would often tell him how much are their clients willing to pay for websites. She would tell him how easier it is becoming to create websites. How there are thousands of young kids graduating everyday that could create professional websites in a day. 
Rohan had asked Shejali if she wants to run his business with him. He only hoped Shejali would join. Shejali had told him that she liked her job. His business was doing well. But there wasn’t much that he could do with it alone. He wasn’t satisfied with whatever success he had. He sold the business for a 85 lakh rupees. It had done well over the years. 
He hadn’t told Shejali about this so he was nervous telling her. Shejali was surprised. She asked him what he planned on doing. “I’m starting an IT company. I’ll be making websites” he said. “Really?” she asked. “Yes. With you.” Shejali laughed.
3 months later, Rohan started the business with Shejali as a 50% partner. 3 months later they get married. Shejali wanted to talk about kids. Rohan convinces her to wait till the business start doing well. A couple of years later they had Pratik.
Rohan starts dedicating more time at home as his business grows. Rohan loves being a father. He often suggests having another kid. He wants to have a daughter. He loves that Shejali wants to have a kid too. They are both happy with their life and hopes for the future. 
Their doctor tells them it is dangerous for Shejali to go through another pregnancy. Rohan is heartbroken, but realizes that Shejali will take this news worse. So, he pretends to keep everything together. They visit other doctors. But they all tell him the same thing. 
Their doctor suggests surrogacy or adoption if they really want to have a kid. Rohan is on board with surrogacy. Shejali’s reluctance confuses her. He doesn’t understand why she has to think about this so much. It is the most obvious way. He doesn’t want to put her in any kind of danger. They would be getting a baby, without losing her. He convince Shejali. 
He likes Jayanti. She is well mannered and honest. She is already a mother. He is excited that they will finally have another child. He is absolutely overjoyed when he finds out that it will be a girl. While Shejali is busy with Jayanti and taking care of her needs. He gets busy preparing things for the baby. He paints the room. Buys the crib, clothes and toys. 
He was at the office when Shejali called him. Jayanti was missing. And so was Ketana. He felt dizzy. He somehow ran towars the elevators. Tears running down his eyes. He drove back to the hospital. Police was already there. They told him they would find them.
A few months had gone by. Rohan called Inspector Rajiv almost every week. To get updates. With nothing happening from the police, Rohan decides to hire a private detective. An ex army officer. But he fails as well. Rohan has to accept the fact that he might never see his daughter again.
Rohan remembered Ketana everyday. HE often wondered what she looked like. How she talked. WHat kind of jokes she cracks. How she laughs. He had forgotten about the detective. He liked how Ketana wanted to handle the situation. He doesn’t want to see Jayanti. But he is proud of how smart and kind his little girl is. For Ketana he is ready to forget everything else.
