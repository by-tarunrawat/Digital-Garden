The world is progressing. There is world peace. Governments of countries work together. 

There are more women in government than ever before. Most world leaders are women. 

Families are compact. Most people have only one child. Quite a few families decide not to have children. Adoption is one of the primary ways people decide to have children. Surrogacy is a legitimate business now. There are companies who hire women to give birth. These women are paid well. For one surrogate, the process takes 9 months to two years. It is a contract with the company. The women are paid well and highly respected. Almost revered. After the pregnancy the surrogate is supposed to keep the baby for another one, one and a half year. Where she keeps the baby is based on the contract she has with the company and the parents. 
Some parents take and keep the baby right after she is born. Most parents take the services of the surrogate to take care of the baby for the initial few months. This has been recommended by regulatory agencies and health organizations. It provides babies with the natural milk from her mother’s breast. And the psychological acclimatisation of the parents to the new responsibility on their hands. The government subsidizes these costs by paying half the expenses of hiring a surrogate if you have the appropriate health insurance. People paying a higher premium would get access to the best surrogacy clinics and best professional surrogates. 
People who do not have money usually take the baby right after she is born. But subscribing to a professional babysitting agency is much cheaper than hiring the surrogate for the full contract. They also have to go to a B or C level surrogate agency. 
Breast milk is available in supermarkets and vending machines. There are two types of breast milks in the market. The most widely sold breast milk is processed. Brands have created factories to convert animal milk to woman’s breast milk. Factories take animal milk — cow, buffalo or goat, process it — adding of removing chemicals and hormones, boxes it and sells it in the market. 
Another niche in the breast milk market is the authentic breast milk brands. This the milk gifted (Pumped) by real women for your baby. Professional surrogates in the B and C level, sell their milk to organic breast milk companies to make extra money. It is a win-win situation for them. Parents at that level usually take their baby right after they are born. They are not earning anything after that. They usually don’t have other jobs. So selling their milk is usually their source of income for the next few months.
Kids generally grow up knowing their surrogate. They don’t have a relationship with them. But they know who they are. Government keeps the record of the surrogate’s medical and family history anyways. Some kids have been known to get attached to their surrogates. But parents usually send kids to therapy which helps in the child detaching themselves from their surrogates. 
There was a case in a school where a group of friends ran off to find their surrogates. The surrogates were true professional and alerted the authorities at once. The children were taken into custody and sent back to their parents. Upon returning one of them committed suicide. The others were sent to therapy immediately. This was a landmark incident. Since then it is a recommended practice to send the child to therapy if a similar situation occurred. 

Agencies also provide sperm. But this is a cheap expense as companies can mixing and formulating a new sperm. For example they can mix an athlete’s sperm with a scientist sperm to make a new genetic combination that has both the athletic gene and the smart gene. 

A, B and C level surrogate agency.

The A level surrogate agencies are considered the best in service and product. Their hiring process for professional surrogates is stringent. They interview candidates, do a thorough medical and psychological examination. The professional surrogates in an A level are intelligent, good social skills and have good physical attributes. 
A level agency also has the rights to the best sperm in the market. They have a rigorous process for sperm depositors as well. They test and examine their genes and classify them with different tags. Sperms qualified under different tags. Like athletic, intelligent, focus. With each type of sperm there is one primary tag, 4-10 secondary tags, and can have any number of tertiary tags. The tags classify what kind of genes are dominant. Parents can look at the tags and decide which sperms to mix. They also have access to records of the depositor to assess their accomplishments. 
These agencies buy the rights to the sperm they get. The depositor is not allowed to deposit their semen anywhere else. They do get royalties after each use of their sperm. Some of the depositors make a good salary by marketing them. The main competition in the Sperm market among the A level agencies is the premium sperm. Agencies buy celebrity sperm and their rights. They can then add their DNA to the final mix.
Professional surrogates at an A level agency can charge from 10,000$ to 50,000$ a month out of which 20% goes to the agency
Sperm depositors get 5000$-10,000$

A B level surrogate agency is a good choice for a middle class family. They have decent choice among professional surrogates. Healthy women, with good genes. They cater to a price conscious customers. They have attractive deals for the prospective parents. 15 month packages for the surrogate. They provide competitive service for middle class prospective parents on a budget. There is a good choice available for genetic selection. The gene pool available is varied. This is the largest category in surrogate reproduction market. 
This level has the highest number of professional surrogates and sperm depositors. This has led to a highly competitive market for agencies as well as professionals. 

A C level surrogate agency is a good option to become parents if you don’t have money. Although, there are very few profitable C level agencies. Most people at that financial level prefer having children the old fashioned way. Agencies at this level usually target a specific income group, but their main consumers are lower middle class families that can’t have children. 
Not much choice available in professional surrogates. The number of professional surrogates far exceeds the number of prospective parents at this level. Most C level agencies barely survive past the 2 year mark. 


Surrogacy as a profession was frowned upon a couple of decades back. In fact, till about 2025 it was done in secret. There was no regulation. Only rich people had access to it. Almost all women in the world gave birth at least once. Can your imagine? If you wanted to have a baby, your wife would have to go through the 9 months of pregnancy and then labor. The, exhaustion, the pain. Women had to juggle between work and home. It was almost always the women who had to make sacrifices in personal life and career, if they wanted to start a family. They were oppressed in many parts of the world, if they were infertile. Sounds incredible. Like a fantasy. But as a society we learn from our mistakes. 
In 2026, Sid Mayer, the first female president of USA, had her first surrogate child. At the age of 57. She was also the first openly homosexual president in the world. She helped changed perception of society and lawmakers. Initially most surrogacy was done through unreliable channels. You had to deal with shady agents, vague laws and bad service. She and her successor Arnold Lacey made changes to the law, setting up a secure infrastructure to adopt children via surrogacy. 
A government registry was setup where women can register as professional surrogates. And future parents can find the right surrogate for their child.
