God appeared on earth. Had a one on one conversation with everyone.
Since the incident, there has been a dramatic reduction in crime.
People are happier. People are honest.
Everybody in the world stopped doing everything. If there was an observer looking at all this. He would just see people standing with their eyes open. 
Everyone was having a conversation in their heads. This went on for an hour. 

I am a teacher at a school. I was in the class teaching algebra. I don’t remember how it started. I found myself in a garden. With beautiful colors. I was in a strange location. But I didn’t feel panic or shock. I didn’t know where I was. I didn’t know how I got there. But I was calm. I felt safe. I felt a familiarity with the surroundings even though I couldn’t tell why. 
I wandered there for a long time. It was bright, It was a large garden filled with flowers that I had never seen. A girl came forward. I recognized her instantly. She was my 8 year old daughter. I started crying. I hadn’t seen tat face in 5 years. She had died in a car accident. I was driving the car. 
I ran towards her and picked her up. I held her so tightly. I thought I’d never let her go. I kissed her all over her face. Tears were running down my face. I felt happy. A thought struck me. What is she doing here? How is she alive? Is this a trick? 
I put her down and asked. “Baby, how are you here? And where are we?”
“Where do you think papa?”
“I don’t know. I was in the class. I was…”
I hadn’t thought about all these things. I was just overwhelmed by the beauty of this place. And then her.
“I don’t know beta. Am I dead?”
“No.”
“Am I dreaming?”
“No.”
“I was in the class and now I’m here. And you are here in front of me. I don’t know what to think.”
“Look around you. What do you think this is not.”
I looked around. The flowers didn’t seem familiar. In fact I had never seen them in my life. The grass had a shine to it. I walked towards one bush. Looked closely at the flowers. I had never seen such precise design in my life. I hadn’t seen such geometry in nature before. 
“I’m not on earth am I?”
“Correct”
“But, how is this possible. I was in school a few minutes ago.”
“And your body still is.” There was a strange intelligence and maturity on her face. “But you are here. This is where I choose to meet you. I have made this place for you, for this moment, to meet you.”
“You have made this? What are you saying baby.”
She didn’t say anything. SHe just looked at me. THe way I look at students when I give them a problem and wait for them to figure it out. I know that face. What does she want me to figure out? She made this place? What does that even mean. She is saying she is some godlike creature? Is she saying she is god?
“Yes” she broke my train of thought.
“Yes what?” I said.
“I am saying I’m god”
She is god? I don’t understand. And how did she know I was thinking that?
“I know everything about you papa”
“I don’t believe in god”
“Doesn’t matter”
“Why doesn’t it matter?”
“Because truth doesn’t need the backing of opinions. Truth just is.”
“SO you are saying you are THE GOD? The one who created the universe in 6 days. The one who brought the floods to Egypt.”
“The universe took a little longer. It still is a work in progress. ANd no, I didn’t bring the floods to Egypt.”
“What kind of a god are you?”
“I’m not the god that your scriptures and books talk about. Its difficult to comprehend me but I’ll try. I am the universe. I am everything that exist in the universe. I am time and space. I am matter. I am you and everything outside you.”
I didn’t understand any of it. I felt like a child in front of this little girl.
“So why are you in my daughter’s body?”
“I wanted you to see her.”
“But this is not my daughter is it? Its god.”
“So are you. You are me as well.”
“What does that even mean? How can I be you?”
“It means that I am everything you see and don’t see in the universe.”
