Date: 11-03-2019
Topic: Give me a moon story.

There is a god. This god wasn’t ‘the god’. It was one of the several gods that reside in this universe. Universe has a trillion gods. Maybe more. Nobody has ever kept a count. We are calling them gods because compared to us - people who are telling and consuming the story — they have vast power. This story is of one such god.
These gods have many children. Some small, some big. All going around it. Trying to leave their parent’s influence. But tied by it for all their life. Their destiny, tied with their god.
