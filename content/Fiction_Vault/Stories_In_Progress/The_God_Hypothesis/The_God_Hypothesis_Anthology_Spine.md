Hey Pooja,

I know I talked you a few minutes ago, but I have something that I want to talk to you about that just can’t wait. 

I have this idea about a book about god. It’ll have a few stories that explore the concept of god that I keep thinking about.

I’ve thought about a conversation with god. How would that go? For me personally? What would I ask? What if god had a conversation with everyone. What would they ask? How would that even work? A thought just came to me. You know I was thinking about god talking to people for an hour. Or whatever time. But now I think it should work like a genie. God can give people 3 questions. And the main protagonist either wastes his questions. By kidding around. Not believing in god. Or maybe asks for more time. Because he doesn’t know what to ask. Interesting hai. I think I can explore this idea.

Another story is about true artificial intelligence coming to life. And taking over the world. It has become the dominant species. And there is very little that humans can do. They have undoubtedly become Gods. How will that world look like? I was thinking about it in two ways. One of them, I’d always had in my mind. I always thought the story would be about how that equation comes into being. How it starts etc. But writing this email to you I think the story can start further in time. When the whole equation has been established. Maybe a few hundred years in the future. AI controls the planet. It has figured out interplanetary travel and has established civilization on other planets. Because it is not a biological entity, it can survive on most atmospheric condition. On earth itself,it controls most of human race. It is not cruel. As in it let;s us do whatever we want. But We can’t harm each other. There have been no murders for hundreds of years. No wars. No rapes or any kind of violence. The punishment is too extreme ( I still have to figure out details). AI provides all kinds of medical services, free of charge. But they also control our population by controlling fertility. In such a world, AI has become a GOD.

Third story is about simulation. The protagonists in the story are worried about some problems happening in the simulation they’ve created. Certain participants in the simulation have started becoming sentient. The participants have started figuring out the secret of their existence. Now our programmers have a bunch of characters who gained consciousness, and they threaten the simulation program itself. Now I’ve to figure out what the story is in this. I’m not sure whose perspective I should take. I can explore the story from both perspectives. I’ll have to read about it maybe. But this could also be an interesting story. In the end the readers find out that the simulation is our world. I don’t know how I will show that though. I was thinking about linking it to an actual event happening in our world. Like Donald Trump winning the election. Or maybe the Atomic bomb on Hiroshima, Nagasaki. Something going really wrong in the world. I also have to figure out what the problem will be. Maybe it’s a game. Where people live simulated life. THey have to start as a newborn. I was thinking of doing this a couple of ways. It can be a simple simulated game. Another could be where if I’m playing, I’m totally immersed in the game. THe whole gameplay is 8-10 hours. And people don’t know their real life outseide the game once they are in it. THey are a character in the game. Completely. No identity outside of it. This could be a wholw book in itself.
I was also thinking about the relationship between a writer and his characters. Isn’t the writer like a god to them. So imagine if there are multiple universes. Infinite number of them. Then every story, every piece of fiction is actually like a documentation o something that has happened or is happening in some other universe. So the person who is writing the story is like a god, or a prophet who is describing these real events, or creating this world by the sheer act of writing. This seems like an interesting philosophical question. 

All these ideas are pretty complex. I feel intimidated whiking about writing them. How do I start? WHat do you think about hte idea? I think I had a couple of other god hypothesis. That can also be the name of the book. God hypothesis.
Tell me what you think about these ideas.

Love,
Tarun
