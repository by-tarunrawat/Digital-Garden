God meets people. Talks to them. 

People would question why a person is calling themselves god.

God takes you to a different reality?

There are 7Billion people in the world. God visits everyone. Every human being.

How is that even possible?
Just appeared out of nowhere. Goes to people in a non threatening body. Somebody Familiar. A face they can recognize. Something they would trust implicitly and won’t be afraid of.
Someone’s dead father.
Knocks on the door. An old man standing. It’s his father. He just stand there. The old man says something. Something to make this person trust him. And let him in. 
He does. They talk. The old guy tells him he is god. He believes it. Why would he believe it right away? Because he can see his dead Father in front of him. And he has an overwhelming love for him. He is happy at this moment.

Scientists asking about universe. Reason for existence.

Reason for existence. A lot of people will ask that in different ways.
What am I supposed to do in life?
Which religion is the right way to live a happy life?
What is my purpose?


Journal. With each interview as a chapter. Several chapters each covering different aspects. Story?

Its my story. The journalist’s story. What is his angle?
He didn’t meet god. He doesn’t know why? Everyone met God. Talked for hours. 
The world is a better place because of it. But God missed him.
It happened through the course of the day. I was working on something. I was trying to write. Watched a few youtube videos. Watched a couple of episodes of netflix. Ordered food in. Nothing happened. Social media was silent too that day. THere wasn’t going on much on twitter. I was watching old stand up clips. Watched a few cricket clips too If I remember correctly. Then some chimp videos, bbc earth wildlife videos. I wrote one line. I wrote more I think. I wrote several lines. Like, 6-7. I deleted them though. It wasn’t what I wanted. Words weren’t really coming out that day. I just sat on the computer. Waited to write something. I wrote, “She crouched behind the cubicle, mobile in hand, switched on selfie mode on the camera, and slowly raised her hand just enough for the camera to find her boss.” After 15 minutes, could’ve been 10, I opened another tab to see ‘what up with youtube’. Didn’t write anything that day.
Went to bed at 3 in the morning. Woke up next day, twitter was flooded with people tweeting sentimental stuff. Apologizing to people. I felt a bit weird at first. I didn’t know what was happening. But you know when people are passionate about something. THere is a vigour about what they say and write. Their is some kind of loyalty to the cause. You know you don’t want to argue too long with a person like that. You’re looking for an out as soon as you get in that conversation. This seemed something like that.
Everyone had that vigor. Everyone’s words were passionate and heartfelt. Some confessions that were shocking. I personally got taggged in 14 apologies. My mom, had called me on my phone. She told me she was always protective of me. Didn’t want me to get hurt. So she nestled me. She hopes that I understand that it was all because she loved me. There were other things said. I didn’t know why she was saying all this. I asked her once why this? Why today? She said, Because, “Right now is the most important time for the most important things.”
I held myself together till I was on the phone. It came out a single drop from each eye. Then I couldn’t stop the outpour. I felt relieved. Its hard to explain. But I’ll try. I moved through life blaming myself, my family, my circumstances for my failures. But I was alone in the those failures. I felt eyes judging me all the time. People contemplating giving their time to me. Even my own friends.
There were other people texting me and tagging me in their tweets. I honestly didn’t know what was happening. 
I called Brahma. He started with “Tarun, I love you. You are my best friend. You have been instrumental in what I have become and I hope you will always be there.”
I was hoping he wouldn’t be like the others. I didn’t know whether to ask him or not. 
“What’s going on Bra?” I asked.
“I’m purging man.”
“What do you mean?”
“Having honest conversations with everyone I know. Telling them how I feel. What they mean to me…”
“Wait..”
“What?”
“Why are you doing this? My mom called me in the morning as well and…”
“I know man. I talked to her as well.”
“My mom?”
“Yeah”
“Why? I mean. Why did you call her?”
“She called me man. She wanted to talk to me about something.”
“Like what?”
“I don’t know if I should tell you man.”
“It’s my mom dude. You have to tell me.”
“I can’t man.”
“Dude. Come on. You just said, I’m your best friend. You won’t tell your best friend”
“Is there something you want to say?”
“What?”
Why was he behaving this way? We’ve never had secrets before. There is something strange going on for sure.
“Did you call me to say something?”
I wanted to continue the discussion but I knew he wasn’t going to tell me. He refusal had determination.
“Yeah, I… What is happening in the world?”
“What is happening with the world?”
“People are being nice and sweet and everyone is apologizing for things. People have stopped saying hello. All calls are starting with a bloody sorry. What is happening? What are people doing it?”
There was silence for about 5 seconds before I said, “Is it some kind of viral thing? Some new sorry challenge or something? There is this girl. I went to school with her. I was with her in 12th class I think. I’d added her on facebook a while back. Anyway, she had written a few posts about self discovery and apologies and all that crap. And in all that crap she had tagged me in a post where she was sorry about everyone she felt superior to in her life. Granted there were about 300 people on that list. But, she apologized for keeping herself on another pedestal than everybody else. I don’t know this girl. Haven’t seen her in years. She is apologizing to me. WHAT THE FUCK IS HAPPENING?”
“She was probably purging as well.”
“What the fuck is purging”
“Calm down man. Why are you so angry?” I didn’t say a word.
“I don’t know what everyone else is calling it. I call it purging”
“Continue”
“I’m meeting and calling everyone I know and talking.”
“Talking?”
“Yeah. Just talking. And everybody else is doing the same it seems.”
“People are tweeting that shit. There are snapchat messages.”
“It’s their way of doing it man.”
“But why?” I couldn’t understand what was happening. We’d been talking for a while. I was getting calls from a lot of people. I think Brahma was too. He told me he was getting an important call. He told me he wanted to have a long conversation with me later. 

It was a stranger who told me what had happened. The whole thing was certainly weird. I had gone through all hypothesis.
This was some kind of chemical weapon. Some psychoactive drug. That gives some kind of dopamine hit. Or DMT or something.
Some kind of brainwashing. Through TV or mobile of something. Both would be difficult at this scale. 
I really thought this was some kind of an internet viral challenge. If you get nominated, you have to do all kinds of shit.
 It was too many people; people I know. The timing of it. The sincerity. Didn’t feel like the right answer. It was my best guess.
I felt trapped in the mystery. I had received 20 calls since then. My brother called, as did my sister. I asked them if this was an internet challenge or not. My brother chuckled. He thought it was funny. “It does seem like one of those icebucket challenges,” My sister ignored it. Kept talking.
I didn’t say anything to anyone else after that. I just took their calls and listened.
An old friend called me to tell me how much I hurt him in college. I used to ignore him. Make excuses to get out of hanging out with him. He was hurt by our behavior back then. He as okay now. 
An ex girlfriend called with some complaints as well. Rest of them were same as the others.

I had gone out for a smoke. I couldn’t find a single shop open. There were very few cars on the road. I saw a few people waiting for the bus. I asked them direction to the closest cigarette shop. An old 55-60 year old guy stood up. Took a packet out of his pocket and gave it to me. “I’ve quit today.” He said. And went back to his seat.
