# Hotel Bhoot: Master Concept Bible
- **Protagonists:** Seemit Sharma (ex-banker), Shohini (ex-air hostess), children Asmita (15) and Vibhor (10).
- **The Premise:** Buying the *Hotel Mountain Queen* in Mussoorie to save their marriage, only to find the staff has fled due to hauntings.
- **The Turn:** Seemit negotiates with head ghost Devendra Joshi (British-era soldier) to employ the ghosts in room service and kitchen operations, turning the property into an adventure hotel.
