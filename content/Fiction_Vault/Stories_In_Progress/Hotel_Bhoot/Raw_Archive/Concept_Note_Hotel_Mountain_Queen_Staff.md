Hotel Bhoot: Concept Note
   
   Logline: A dysfunctional family buys a hotel only to discover that it’s haunted, and decide to employ the ghosts as hotel staff. 
   Genre: Drama, Comedy
   
   Synopsis: Seemit Sharma (40) has bought the hotel Mountain Queen in mussoorie and has shifted with his family. His relationship with his wife Shohini Sharma (38) is not as it used to be. They fight a lot as Shohini thinks Seemit doesn’t care about the family and doesn’t give time to them. They have two kids. 15 year old Asmita and a 10 year old Vibhor. 
   As they shift to Mussoorie to manage and run the Mountain Queen, it’s in a bad state. There has been a constant decline in the guests that have come in and most of the staff has run away. Right now they only have two employees working at the mountain queen. But they are left with one old cleaner Ravindra (65) as the other employee also runs away. 
   Seemit finds out that the reason everyone is keeping away from the hotel is that it is haunted. THe ghosts chase away any guests that come. This creates a problem for Seemit as he has taken a huge loan from the bank and if he is unable to make the hotel profitable, the bank will take it and sell it to a local builder Mr Tyagi. Mr Tyagi has plans to raze the building to the ground and build a new shopping center. 
   At first Seemit is frustrated by this. He brings tantriks and pandits to get rid of the ghosts. But to no avail. Soon, his family makes him realizes that he can use the situation to his advantage. If the hotel is destroyed, so will the ghosts. He convinces the ghosts to help him run the hotel and in turn he will make sure that they always have a place to stay. The ghosts agree to help him out. 
   Seemit rebrands the hotel as a haunted hotel for adventurous people. It picks up. The ghosts act as the hotel staff. Working in the kitchen, room service and all. The hotel is doing well, much to the disappointment of Mr Tyagi. Mr Tyagi tries to ruin the hotel in different ways. But now Seemit has the help of the ghosts to thwart those plans. 
   
   Characters:
   Seemit Sharma: He used to work as an investment banker in Delhi. But the pressure of work took a toll on his family life and mental health. He spent less and less time with his wife and kids. Started fighting with his wife. Even couples counselling didn’t help. His relationship with his kids deteriorated as well. HIs daughter Asmita did not respect him. She uses to be close to him when she was little, but as she has grown up she has gotten further away from Seemit. His work was taking a toll on his mental health as well. He had an violent episode in the office. After which his boss gave him a few weeks off. Seemit decided that all of this needed to stop. He will not have a family if this goes on. So he decided to come back to his home town and run a hotel that he loved as a kid. He hopes that his family will be together, will spend time with each other and they will get back to a normal close knit family. 
   
   Shohini Sharma: Shohini used to be an Air hostess as in her youth. At 22 she met Seemit on one of the flights. They hit it off and started dating. Soon they fell in love and got married. Shohini was still working, but that stopped once she had Asmita. She became a full time mom. This made her sad and resentful. Seemit also became obsessed with his work and barely spent time with her or the baby. When Asmita was 4 Shohini decided to start working again. But she got pregnant again with Vibhor. This put a dent in her plans of having a career at all. Her relationship with Seemit suffered. SHe was raising these two kids alone with no help from Seemit. She wanted to divorce him. But Seemit had a mental breakdown at work. HE had no one but Shohini to help him out. She still loved him and gave him another chance. When Seemit decided to buy a hotel, she was excited about it. But when  she founf out that they will be shifting to a small town, she was annoyed and fought with Seemit. Seemit told her to give him one last chance. They will try to run the hotel for a year. If it didn’t work. They will shift back to dehli. Shohini reluctantly agreed. 
   
   Asmita Sharma: Asmita is a typical 15 year old going to school. She has her friends in Delhi. She likes to read and draw. But she has gotten into partying in peer pressure. Her friends are the cool kids of the school. She thinks herself to be lucky to be their friends and does whatever they do. Her father doesn’t like this and scolds her for it. But she doesn’t care. She thinks her father is barely around, so she lies to him about what she is doing or where she is going. She hears her mom and dad fight all the time. THat’s when she puts on her headphones and draws. 
   She is angry that her dad has decided to move cities. She is angry that he didn’t even ask her for an opinion. But she has no choice. She will have to start over again. Make new friends. She is extremely doubtful that there will be anyone as cool as her friends in Delhi. SHe heard her father tell her mom that they will give it a year’s time, if it doesn’t work, they will go back to delhi. So she decided to sabotage the hotel so taht they can move back to delhi. 
   
   Vibhor Sharma: Vibhor is a naughty child. Always getting in trouble at the school. He has gotten suspended a few times for bullying children from his class. He used to be a good student, but recently his grades have gone down as well. His parents have tried scolding him, punishing him, but it hasn’t worked. He has no friends to play with, so he plays videogames all day. The only friend he has is his sister. He admires his sister and wants to be close to her all the time. But she thinks of him as s nuisance and avoids him. When he finds out that his family is moving, he is happy at first. But seeing his sister unhappy he also decides that he doesn’t want to move. He decides that he will help his sister in sabotaging the hotel and moving back to delhi. But during his stay he will get closer to her. This will create a doubt in him about moving back to delhi, because that would mean that his sister will go back to her friends and will not have time for him anymore. 
   
   Ravindra Joshi: Ravinder Joshi has worked in the hotel for 35 years. His father Devendra Joshi(Who used to be in the british army and ran away) bought this land and the house that was built on it. Devendra made the hotel what it was, but he died young when he was 35. Ravindra’s family ran into financial troubles after that and had to sell the hotel. RAvindra started working at the hotel when he was 29, as he had loving feelings for the place and has worked there since then. For some reason, the ghosts haven’t bothered him that much and he stayed on till now. 
   
   Mr SP Tyagi: He is a real estate businessman. He has amassed a lot of wealth and wants more. He has his eyes set on the Hotel Mountain Queen. It is in prime location and he has a deal in place to construct a shopping center and sell it for 50 crores to a buyer from Bombay. He will try everything in his power to get the hotel.
   
   The Ghosts:
   
   Devendra Joshi: He is resentful of the fact that he died early and didn’t get to live the life that he wanted. He doesn’t come out that often, but he is the leader of the otherwise unruly pack of ghosts. 
   
   Sheetal Chaddha: Her husbnd killed her in the hotel and got 14 years of prison. She hates to see any happy couples in the hotel. She scares any couples that come to the hotel and scares them off. 
   
   Sonal Tickoo: Sonal died when she was 12 in a drowning accident on the hotel premises. She now only wants to people to play with and tries to make friends with any kids that come along. But this scares away the kids and the parents. 
   
   Varun Bisht: He was a local farmer who supplied veggies and fruits from his farm. He died at the hotel in an accident. He is now trapped in the hotel and can’t go out. The only time he is happy is when he sees his daughter and wife, who are still alive. 
   
   There will be other ghosts (minor characters) that will have similar story. The ghosts don’t really get along that much. THey only listen to Devendra’s ghost. But Devendra is lonely and keeps to himself most of the time. He only comes out when he sees guests not respecting his hotel. 
   
   Storylines: 
   
   1. The family dynamics of Seemit and his wife and kids. How they fight with each other and then come together to run this hotel. How the relationship between husband and wife, father and daughter, and sister and brother gets resolved.
   
   2. The ghosts and the Sharma family getting together to save the hotel. Working together with ghosts, while Seemit tries to mend his relationship with his family. 
   
   3. The builder Mr Tyagi and his antics to get the hotel. The conflict between Seemit and his family and Mr Tyagi.
