Hotel Beiman:

A 28-year-old man, Fugga, inherits a boutique hotel from his a distant relative, who had been estranged. He own life and career has been disarray so he thinks of it as a chance for a new beginning and decides to run it. He finds out there are chor-uchakke occupying some rooms in the hotel as well as a neighboring 5 star hotel owner who wants to seize the property. Fugga tries to drive the goons out, but instead they drive out all of the staff. On the other hand the hotelier is doing everything possible to hamper his business so that the hotel becomes a loss making affair and he can buy it for cheap. Now it’s up to Fugga to somehow reform these gundas and make them work for him in the hotel while also coming up with strategies to keep his hotel afloat. 

There is an added conflict because a 5 star hotel neighboring tries to destroy business of our hero’s hotel to buy the land and expand his own hotel. Our hero falls in love with the hotelier’s daughter. 

Stories: How at first he tries to drive them out, but they drive out the hotel staff. 
He decides to employ them so he has to reform them one by one. We get to know their stories.
Various tactics old hotelier will use to destroy this hotel. 
The love story between the hero and the hotelier’s daughter.
