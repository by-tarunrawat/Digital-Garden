Date: 11-11-2018
Topic: You’re standing in a doorway

I’m standing at the gate. I’m 5 years old — come back from school. There is lunch made at home. Just like everyday. I know there will be daal, and some vegetable. I hate to eat vegetables and I know my mom will force me to eat it.  Afraid of going in, I start crying on the gate. Mai sabzi nahi khaunga. I’m crying loudly now. I don’t know what else to write for this prompt. 
Doorway. I’m standing in a doorway. I don’t remember standing on the doorways too much. I don’t visit a lot of people. Even as a kid I didn’t go to my relatives’ houses. I like staying home. This prompt isn’t inspiring anything.
Doorway. What can a doorway signify. An opening to some kind of opportunity. I’m just standing there. Afraid to go in? Afraid to move ahead. Stuck in my place. I don’t know what’s inside. Why am I afraid to open the door and go in? What can happen? I’ll fail. People will think I’m a loser. My friends will think less of me. 
I’m failing anyway. By not opening the door I’m preventing myself to get to what’s on the other side. Maybe people think I’m a loser. Maybe they don’t. How does that affect anything? My friends adore me; love me. Nothing has changed that. Why am I afraid of trying even? I’m losing without playing. THe least I can do is play. Get on the field. Take a step towards the door and knock. Maybe it won’t open. I’ll bang on it. If nobody opens it. I’ll go to another. There is dearth of doors. I just need to knock.
