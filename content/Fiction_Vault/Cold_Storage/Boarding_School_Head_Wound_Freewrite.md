Date: 18-11-2018
Topic: Write about a wound.

I have this wound at the back of my head. That has a few stories associated with it. It’s not just about how it happened. It os also about what happened after it happened and what happened years after it happened.
SO let’s start with how it happened. I used to study in boarding school. We had prep time scheduled at the end of the day. During prep time we would study in our class and then go back to our dorm when it was over. This particular prep night, I was reading a comic book that I had borrowed from a friend. After the prep was over and we were all back in the dormitory, Vishal Rathi asked for the comic book. Since it wasn’t mine I refused and told him he can take it from whomever I was going to return it to. Apparently he wasn’t in the mood to hear no, so he grabbed and pulled it from my hands. This tore the comic book in half. Now this was not my comic book I had borrowed it, so I had some sense of responsibility for it. Also, in those days, I used to get angry really quick. So, naturally we got into a fight. We were hitting each other, grabbing necks and collars, pushing and pulling, slapping, punching and what not. 
Finally sense prevailed in some of our dorm mates and they tried to pull us apart. During that pulling apart scene, I tripped on someone’s leg, and fell backwards on the frame of the bed. Now, the frame of the bed was made of iron. So, it instantly tore my head open. I was taken to the dispensary, where the nurse was called. THe house-mistress shouting at us; thinking we were playing cricket in the dorm. Anyway, as soon as the nurse arrived in the dispensary, the electricity went. People started running to see what happened to the electricity. I was sitting like that, an open would on my head, for 30-40 minutes. When the electricity didn’t come back,the nurse lit a candle and started sewing up the wound. I got 2 stitches I think. But that whole injury did a long term damage.
She put a bandage around my head to cover up the wound. I remember going in front of the mirror the next day, to comb my hair. I thought I looked pretty good. I also felt pretty good that day because of all the attention I was getting from people. People who didn’t already know about the injury. Mainly teachers and girls. At least some good came out of the whole thing.
