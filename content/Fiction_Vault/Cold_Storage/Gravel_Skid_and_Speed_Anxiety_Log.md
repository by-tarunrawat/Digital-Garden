Date: 24-03-2019
Topic: "I remember how it was to drive in gravel." (after Theodore Roethke)

It feels like the earth beneath you is moving. It is difficult taking sharp turns. You will slide to the other side with the moving ground. I’ve had a couple of accidents like that. I’ve seen many happen. It’s quite dangerous. The only way to survive is to drive slow. Slower if the quantity of gravel is more.
Where does the gravel come from. Mainly from broken roads. Sometimes from construction material. I don’t know what else to say. I’m feeling strange uneasiness today. I see my time just passing me and me passing time. Why don’t I actually o something. I make big plans. But I’m not following through. I need to make big changes now. Time for small things is over. I need to actually somehow drive through this gravel in full speed. I’ll fall probably. And faster I’m moving the harder the fall will be. But I don’t see any other way. If I go slow. I fall far behind. What is slow anyway. I need to move move move. I can’t stop. I’m going too slow.
