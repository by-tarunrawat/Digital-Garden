It's great physical exercise and keeps you sharp. I loved it. I still love it. BUt we're on a little break up. I don't know how to get back together with it. 
I rememebr when i used to play. I could play in rain or sunshine, I didn't care if it was cold as the north pole or hot as the Sahara. I just wanted to play. 
Even movies I really love. Visual storytelling I should say. I mean I love books. BUt movies over books anyday for me. I still watch movies though. Although, I'm not as passionate about them as I use to. I owuld download movies and watch them all the time. Now it's youtube or some TV show that would binge.
I think if I think carefully I wanted to be a cricketer or a movie star. I remember watching Gobvinda or Amitabh Bacchan, I would want to be coolie, a cop, army officer. I think becoming an actor could have done that for me. 
Even cricket. I could run around all day on the field. I think I wold have become a great cricketer. I think a lot about the game. I try to learn a lot. I'm interested in learning. I'm commited to it and I'm good at it. I don't know what happened I should've given more of a fuck to it. What the fuck man. WHat is wrong with you.
