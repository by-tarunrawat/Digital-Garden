Date: 10-11-2018
Topic: Write about a time somebody said no

It is difficult to say no to people. I am generally pretty cautious in asking people for favors as well. I feel like I don’t want to put undue pressure on anyone to do stuff for me out of some obligation or pressure. I think in anticipation of hearing a no I don’t ask people for anything. That might be one of the reason why it difficult to find a job. To find a job you have to ask people for favors. Look for an opening in their organization. Or even get me an interview. I feel that by my asking them for something they may be going out of their way. They might say no. They may not like me after that. I’ll be the guy who asks for stuff. It is pretty irrational but that’s how I feel. I realize that I have to change this thing about myself.
I’ve gotten a lot of nos in my life — mostly from my mom. She would say no to everything that I would ask. “Can I get a mobile?”, “Can I go out to play?”, “Can I go to Mussoorie with my friends?”. Answer to each of these question is a big fat no. What is a no that has really stung me? 
I think the interviews I’ve given over the past year and a half have shaken my confidence pretty bad. All the organizations I’ve interviewed for have said no. Hence the continued unemployment. 
There was this one time where I interviewed for a company. They said they can’t take me because I don’t have experience in the domain. Fair enough. They called me again for another post. They felt I would be right for that role. I interviewed and they said no again. But the one that stung me the most was for this advertising agency that I interviewd for.
I interviewed for with this guy who was an ex MICAN. The interview went well. I think. He was pretty cordial He was the one talking during the interview. I had to butt in to tell him about me so that it doesn’t look like I don’t say much or I’m uninterested. At the end of the interview he told me that I’m in. I just have to talk to the owner of the company and discuss salary and stuff. I was pretty confident. I was making plans for the future. My ex worked a floor below me. It would have been great catching up with her. A couple of days later I met the owner. Even that interview went well. He told me that I’d have to talk to Amit about the salary. And that I’d be contacted soon enough. I waited for a few days. I didn’t want to seem pushy. But after a few days when I didn’t hear from them, I messaged this guy. He didn’t answer. Even my message didn’t go through to him. I didn’t understand why? I read online about why m message wasn’t going through, and found that the it was because this guy had probably blocked me. I should’ve gotten angry at this. I should’ve called him and asked for an explanation. I don’t know why I didn’t. I didn’t want a confrontation. I think I didn’t want to hear a solid no from this guy. I felt betrayed. It was stupid. THe whole process was stupid. Why did they talk about salary when they didn’t want to take me. Very unprofessional. I left any hope. But still didn’t understand what happened.
Days later my ex talked to the HR in the company. SHe asked her about what was happening with the hiring process. THe HR told her that they were looking at someone else for the role. And that I was to unstable to be hired.
I feel incompetent. I’m not. I’m intelligent and smart. I have quite a lot of experience. But these processes have really shaken my confidence. I feel like no one will hire me. I keep justifying various things to them. I keep justifying why I left my job; my boss screwed me over but I don’t tell them that. I justify my salary. This one guy thought I don’t know anything about marketing. Maybe I don’t. Maybe I need to do something else.
