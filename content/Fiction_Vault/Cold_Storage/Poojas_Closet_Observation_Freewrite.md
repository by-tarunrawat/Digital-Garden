Date: 04-12-2018
Topic: Describe the contents of someone’s closet

On the doors of the clothes closet there are two hangars. These hangars have circular holes to hang scarves and ties and thing like that. Things that are light and long. These hangars are on both the doors of the closet. I forgot to mention: It’s a double door closet. 
There is one big compartment on the left side of the closet. Here all the things that can be put on a hanger are hung. Things like saarees, pants that can’t have more than one fold, fancy shirts and all. On the bottom of this shelf, there are smaller things, like perfumes, creams, bottles and stuff. There are smallish bags and boxes crammed into the space towards the back. 
On the right side of the closed there are three small shelves. I don’t remember correctly, but it’s three shelves. On each of these shelves, clothes are kept folded in a neat and tidy fashion. It is in a line. Nothing coming out of the side. Nothing shoved in. Everything in place. One of the self is for t-shirts, one for jeans and shorts and I don’t know what is in the third one. But even this is pretty neatly kept.
Then there are drawers. Left drawer has perfumes, creams, and makeup stuff. THe right one also has similar stuff I think. Like small jewelery boxes, sun screen and stuff.
The bottom shelf just have old stuff. Big boxes and shit. The closet smells really nice.
I don’t have much to write in this as well. I should’ve described my old closet. But It’s good that I attempted Pooja’s. It demonstrated that I need to observe things more carefully. It will help me in my writing. It might help me somewhere or the other.
