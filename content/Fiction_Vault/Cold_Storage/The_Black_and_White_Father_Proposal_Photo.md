Date: 14-12-2018
Topic: Write about a black and white photograph.

Photographs are amazing in the way they capture time. Nowadays every instant of your life can be captured. I’m glad for this change in our reality. We can see things that we never thought we could see. Not without spending a fortune. Now, we click pictures of anything happening to us. Weddings, birthdays, random parties, our trip to the toilet. Anything. And the images are getting sharper and sharper. There are features to smoothen your skin, make your eyes big, beautify you and so many other things. Most of us don’t even remember a time when this was not possible. Even before a time when people start having cameras. You had to go to a special place to get your pictures clicked. Or call a photographer.
I still have a lot of physical images back home. Printed on a thick paper. Colored mostly. But a few black and whites as well. There is my dad’s picture that he sent to my mom’s house for the marriage proposal. My mom when she was still a young school going girl. I can’t get over my dad’s picture though. He looks like me. Or rather, I look like him. I hadn’t paid much attention to this picture before he passed away. After that I posted it on social media, I get memories of that image. Mostly when I think about dad. 
A lot of comments I got on that photograph was how similar both of us looked. That made me happy. He looked handsome in it. That’s not how I remember my dad though. He had long wavy hair in the image. In real life he had short curly hair combed back. In he photograph he looked thin and fit. Not that he was fat in real life, he just had a big tummy. And his mustache. He started keeping it later in life.
Black and white images are unique. It takes away the color, but there is a character about it. It makes the image serene. It gives the photograph an artistic look. I don’t now how that happens. You’re taking away all the colors. Isn’t art playing with colors. Any image clicked as a black and white, has an extra artistic value. You’ll look at it a little longer to see what is going on. What idea has the photographer tried to capture. It’s a cheat code. To getting a little more attention.
This photograph though. I always imagine this picture when I think about my dad. There are other images too. But this one is quite prominent. Maybe it reminds me of the time in my father’s life I know nothing about. I still know a little bit about him later in life. Not much though. But he never talked to us about his younger self. He would tell us about how he transitioned into an english medium school from a hindi medium. How he was really good at trigonometries. I also know the story of how he went to see my mom. But apart from that I know nothing. I just have that picture from that time. 
It tells me my dad was a flamboyant, and stylish young man. At least when the picture was taken. I have seen a few other pictures of him as well. SO I think he did have a sense of style. I don’t know aht happened to that. He stopped caring about clothes. This came down to me as well. I don;t care about clothes much also. My brother does. It seems to me he was split in two. My brother and me.
