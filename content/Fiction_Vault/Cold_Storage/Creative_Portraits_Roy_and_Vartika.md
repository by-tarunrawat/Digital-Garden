Write about the most creative person you’ve ever known. Explain why you feel he/she is so creative, offering examples of his/her creative accomplishments.

The most creative person? Huh! The first name that comes to mind is Roy. Although even Vartika seems like a good choice. Let’s talk about them one by one.
Let’s start with Roy. Abhimanyu Roy is a one of my best friend and a musician. He plays guitar generally, but he picks up most instruments quickly. Anyway, I think expertise-wise guitar is his weapon of choice. 
Why do I think he is creative? It’s because he creates beautiful music. He creates beautiful music like I make tea. With ease. He can pickup a guitar and just start playing. He plays songs you’ve heard, songs you have no idea existed and then he’ll make up his own shit. Even when he plays songs you’ve heard, he can put his own spin on it. Make it… different. 
His own music is also really good. For a long time he struggled with getting people who could fill up parts of the song that he was unable to do. Keyboard, drums, lead or rhythm guitar. But now he has figured out a way to record stuff and record all the different sounds himself. He doesn’t need anybody else. And the songs turn out amazing. It’s mind blowing truly. He does it through looping various sounds to the something that records and plays it back. 
The best thing about it is that he comes up with new songs and tunes almost everyday. I won’t say all the tunes are great. Some of them need work. But still that kind of prolificness is something to be admired. 
He is creative because he creates a lot of stuff. Most of it original. Without anyone helping him out - although that is not one of the parameters for being creative. 
Although, there are a few things that I would like to tell him that would make him better. Like, his songs sometimes have no highs and lows. They are long songs, and to be interesting, they need to tell a story. With music. And in any good stories, there are highs and there are lows. There is a struggle, and there is a resolutions. There are different emotions in a story, but not necessarily. His songs sometimes get a little monotonous. 
Another thing that I would tell him is to improve his songs. Now I’m not a musician. As a listener I like his music. Do I think they are perfect? Not at all. There are a lot of things he could do. I’m not the one to suggest what, but one example would be to bring some kind of variation in a single song. Or cut the song length.
But its fine. He has a good ear. And he is smart. If he keeps at it. Doesn’t get too attached with his songs. He will figure it out himself.

I was also thinking about Vartika as a creative person. She is a good dancer. Se was a good dancer anyway. A good actress as well. SHe had a lot of passion for both. But she left both unfortunately. She succumbed to mom’s pressure. Our mom was worried about her career. She didn’t know what kind of a career Vartika could have in acting. 
She could have fought for it. I could’ve set a better example for her. I could’ve fought for her too. But she didn’t want to fight for herself. I can only support her. I can’t push her. Then it becomes my decision instead of hers.
