Date: 22-11-2018
Topic: Write about the horizon

Where the world ends. Sun hides at night. And all the lines meet. People say two ends of a river never meet. But all parallel lines meet at the horizon. I see the sea. There must be a waterfall there. We know better. There is none. It’s just endless. It’s not. It just goes on and on. You try to reach it. It goes further. Its out of my reach forever. 
It scares me. Shows me my nothingness. Curved only slightly. Too slight for me to even detect. Unattainable yet beautiful.
Horizon is used as a metaphor for new beginnings. It is used like around the corner, new horizons. A sun set over a ocean makes everything red. It is gorgeous. Bloody. Like a war has just ended. Everything so quite. Only the sounds of birds. Going home. Trying to find their way. The way these birds fly in a pattern. So fast. Synchronized. It is as if they’ve practiced it a million times. They have. Not a million times not a billion times. Maybe more. Over hundreds of millions of years. Sewn into their DNA. By their mothers and fathers and their mothers and father. The horizon is the same. Practicing the same thing over and over again. Over billions of years. Without anyone to appreciate. Or even notice. The we arrive. And attach meaning to a simple happening. DOes this have any meaning. Except for what it is. I don’t think so. But it has meaning to us. 
Horizon. End or a new beginning? Endless or withing our grasp. 
Always out of our grasp. Yet making ends meet. What a paradox.
