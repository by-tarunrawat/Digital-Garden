Date: 24-01-2019
Topic: These are the pleasures I’ve known.

Watching movies used to be one of my favorite things to do. TV shows, stories, videos; anything will do. I used to watch everything. I’ve stopped doing that now. I haven’t stopped altogether. But I’ve definitely reduced. Everytime I think of watching something, I feel a guilt. I’m not making any money. I’m not doing anything worthwhile. Smoking all day. It feels like adding another thing to the list of time wasting things I do. 
If I had a job I would probably watch movies and shows all the time. I’d still like to read. THere would not be any guilt. I should do things I love doing. 
I love playing cricket. All that toiling in the sun to get a wicket. To run around and exert your body. It is the happiest I am. Aman told me I should play regularly. I should go out and find opportunities to do it. He is right. And technically this won’t be wasting time. But I don’t know why I don’t do it. I should Register in a club. Play matches. Play nets and all. FOr that I need to stay in one place. Bangalore seems like the best place.I have to make that happen.
I masturbate a lot. This is something I need to reduce if not stop altogether. I’ve tried a few times. Unsuccessfully. I do it everyday. Sometimes twice or thrice. STOP.
Weed has been a real godsend for me. I love weed. I go quite a lot when I smoke. I don’t talk much anyway. But on weed, I’m especially quite. Maybe I don’t want to make an effort of having a conversation. Is it making me lazy. I read someone say it is a motivation wrecker. I don’t know who said it. But it seems true. It seems to stop action. Maybe I need to stop it for a while. Till I get my life together.

***********
On a separate note. I think I need to make tough, big decisions. I was thinking about a couple of things I need to figure out. First of all, I need to decide if I’ve to do a job or do something else. If I look for a job, I need to figure out what I can do right now. What are my oppportunity, and how do I go about it. I need to figure out the whole scene. Maybe I can free write and get some idea. Maybe get some clarity. 
If I’m doing something else then I need to decide and tell myself. Then work on it properly.Figure out that stuff also. I can try and free write on this as well. I need o think about ideas on what all I can do. Just brainstorm.
