As a way to help you notice your surroundings and work space (and, perhaps, improve them), write a detailed description of your work space. Pan your eyes all around you. Look out the window and describe the view. Again: details , details. To this writing, add a few paragraphs describing your feeling about the space. Is it a haven? Does it offer enough privacy Do family and friends respect eh boundaries of this space If you write in more than one place, do this exercise for each one.

Right now I’m writing in my bed. When I’m in dehradun, this is where I mostly write. I’m inside my quilt. It is cold inside. The curtains are drawn so I can’t see whats outside. Generally when I write here, the dog tries to come sit on my lap. Continuously disturbs me. I don’t mind. My mom generally respects my privacy. Even if there is noise — from the TV or anywhere else — I put on my earphones or I put some music on. 
Sometimes I write on the dining table. It is also a decent place to write. My mom passes that place quite often. So, the privacy isn’t what I want. 
I don’t think I have any place here that is ideal to write. It is full of one distraction or other. There is noise. The view outside the window isn’t that great either. 

When I go to bangalore, I stay and write at Pooja’s place. When I write in her room I sit on the beanbag. I rest my back on it. I find it quite relaxing. Another place I try to write is her drawing room. There is a small stool where I keep my laptop and try to write.

Even this is not an ideal place to write. I can’t write when she is home. Or some other friend is over. I need a certain amount of privacy.

THe hoouse that I was living in previously was decent when I think about it. It had a good table on which I could keep my laptop and write. There was a decent chair as well. I had bean bags if I wanted to realx and write. There was a small table on which I could get enough leverage while sitting on the bean bag as well. Now that I think about it. Although not ideal, that house was the best place for me to write. And I just procrastinated. 

I think I need my own space to do some work on my own. I was talking about going away in some other prompt. I think I do need to go away. For a few months at least. I can’t work like this. I need to have my own place. I’m so dependent on other people. I can’t do things on my own term. The way Pooja also treats me. Making plans for me. It’s embarrassing. I need to do something about it. I NEED TO GET MY OWN PLACE.
