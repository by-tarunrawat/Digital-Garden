Topic: You are in a motel room

It feels empty. Even thought the roads looked pretty crowded. Looking at the gallery that I passed through to get to the reception, I thought it was closed. But At least I found someone at the reception. I didn’t have any charge on my phone. I was drunk and stoned. With nowhere to go. I was supposed to go to Shreshth’s place. Couldn’t remember the address. Couldn’t call anyone. The Uber I was in could’ve taken me anywhere. As a guy we take some things for granted. THe most he could’ve done was rob me. A girl would’ve had to think about so much worse.
I remember I called Aarohi with whatever juice was left in my phone. I couldn’t find the house. So I thought if I called her she would either give us the right way, or call me back to her house to spend the night. I don’t think I was expecting too much really. Chetan was there, Akansha too. It wasn’t like she would’ve had to spend the night with me alone. And I was in trouble. I had nowhere to go. Thankfully the Uber driver was a good guy. He booked a hotel for me and left me there. He really didn’t need to. That was the location he was supposed to drop me at. He could have just dropped me there and that would’ve been it. I would’ve had to find something to spend the night.
The motel looks fine. Just a little cold and spooky. THe halls leading up to the rooms are narrow and dingy. I have asked the guy at the reception to get me a charger. I could get a cab in the morning at least. But I like the fact that I have the whole room to myself. I’ve put on the TV to sleep. Put my phone on charge. THere was some banging on the door. How did I spend the night. Wow. I mean. This was crazy. I really could’ve been robbed or kidnapped. THe next morning I went to Aarohi’s place again. Is that where I went. I think so. My bags were there. 
I don’t really like Aarohi. She is not a kind person. She might be loyal to her friends. And she might have her qualities. But I don’t see what they are. I don’t generally dislike people. I’m not exaggerating about this. I don’t dislike people like that. And I might be wrong about this. But Aarohi is pretty cruel. SHe doesn’t care about other people. She certainly doesn’t look like she cares about their feelings. Especially if those feelings are in her way. And her way could be just her own entertainment.
Am I saying this because of this one incident? No. Am I saying this because she calls me out. What do I mean by that? I didn’t say ‘nice to meet you too’ to her boyfriend. SHe called me out on that. Does that really matter. DO you really need to repeat these things. It’s so fake. But we have to? I don’t think I did anything wrong by not saying it. But she felt bad about it. Whatever. 
At Tathu’s wedding — where I had to stay at the motel — I was talking to her. I was being candid. We were talking about meeting all these MICANs at weddings. And talking about the same things. I hate it. And maybe I hate it because I don’t have anything going for me. I don’t have a job. I don’t have any prospects. I’m doing absolutely nothing. Whatever the reason, it’s uncomfortable for me. Telling everyone the same thing over and over again. “I’m not doing anything right now”. It’s my standard reply to “What are you doing nowadays man?”. “Nothing. Nothing right now. I’m in Bangalore for now. Not working anywhere.” It’s embarrasing for me. I’vebeen saying that for almost 3 years now. Wow. Haven’t had a job for 3 years. That is ridiculous. I don’t think I’m getting one now. Nothing that I’ll be happy with anyway. So, yeah, I was talking to her. And I said I don’t like talking to all these micans again at again at weddings. And she said it’s because you don’t have a job, or something like, it’s because you are not doing anything. It hurt me.
Even Pooja seems worried now. SHe is thinking about moving to seattle. And if things don’t change, she will have to break up with me. SHe can’t marry me. She can’t take home a loser like me and tell her parents that this is the guy she wants to marry. SHe’ll be laughed off. And what will I tell them. What do I do? What are my plans? Nothing is not an answer.
Why does this breakup seems inevitable? I feel like there is an expiry date to this relationship as well. Fuck. I won’t be able to handle that man. It’s difficult to handle our situation right now as it is. We’re so far from each other. And want to be together. WHat do I do? I want to be with her. I want to be with pooja without these restriction. But what do I do. I can’t just stay with her indefinetly. What do I do man? 
I have to figure something out. ANd I have to figure it out quickly.
