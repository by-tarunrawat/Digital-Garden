Write about a time when your creativity flowed, perhaps when you were immersed in a  project or when you spent a few hours at a coffeehouse scribbling in your journal. Try to describe the feeling. Describe, too, the circumstances - the time of day, the location, your mood before beginning. In this exercise, try to get to know your creative self a bit better.

When I was in MICA, there was this one time I was hanging out with Roy. I was talking about writing and the kind of poems I’ve written. He asked me why I don’t write anymore. I wondered about it. For like a second and told him, It was because I don’ know what to write about. He said he’ll give me a topic. He thought about it but couldn’t come up with anything. 
We chilled a bit more, but we were getting bored. So he said “Boriyat ho rahi hai yaar, CHalo kisi ke saath sex karte hain.” Hearing him say this, I thought this was a good topic to write about. Getting bored, so have sex to get rid of it. Simple.
I picked up a pen and paper and started writing. It started with a line. Something we were doing at that moment. I don’t even remember. Something about getting bored. Then I came up with another line to rhyme with the first. Then I started thinking about, what other things we do to get rid of boredom and match them with sexual things. It started flowing. Some lines I was cracking myself up. I finished the whole thing in an hour if not less. It was amazing. Now that I think about it. Most things I’m proud of I finished very quickly. Didn’t take much time at all.
Like when I wrote the play in school, we finished it in a couple of hours. Just sitting, one idea after the other. Although, there were two other people helping me out on that one. 
I remember this one time I had seen ‘The Atonement’. I loved the movie. It made me sad. The unfinished love story. The main protagonist writes a letter, that we never get to see. It never reaches the heroine. He dies before he could meet her again. It moved me so much that I wrote that letter for him. I could channel his thoughts. It was as if I could feel his love. I could feel his pain. It was almost like I was that guy writing for my love. Far away, realizing I will never meet her again, and she might never read this letter. I am welling up now a little bit thinking about it. Anyway, I finished that one in an hour as well. I don’t think it took longer than that. There wasn’t much that I had to edit. I had poured my heart out.

Most of these instances were late at night. Although, I think there were a couple of poems that I wrote in the evening. Some of them were in the office. The sex poem was in my hostel at night. I was sitting in my bed. I was extremely bored. Which was the case when I was writing at Infosys as well. The atonement letter was different in that regard. I wasn’t bored. I was deeply moved. It was like there was something calling inside me to write it. I just couldn’t stop myself. I think I’ve gotten pretty good at stopping myself now. There are these times that I feel these urges to write things down. I get ideas and stuff. I usually tell myself that I’d write about it tomorrow. Or some other time. I procrastinate. And I rationalize my procrastination. Sometimes, I’m in bed with pooja, sometimes I’m just out with friends.
I have an idea. Its not a new idea. I can’t just whip out my laptop whenever I feel those urges. I don’t have my laptop most of the time. And sometimes it’s just weird. So, I have a mobile. And I have softwares that I can use. I should start using them.Instead of checking instagram every few seconds and checking what video can I watch on YouTube, I can write on my phone. THen maybe explore those topics later, if they are unfinished or they need to be expanded.
All I have to do now is build that habit. I don’t do that as of now. SO how can I remind myself to do it. It’ll be hard. But I’ll just have to remember and just talk myself into it till It becomes a habit. There was this website for making habits. WHere you need to do something for 30 days to make it a habit. I’ll do that. 
So, here is the plan of action:
If I get an idea. Then, I pick up my mobile and write about it. Repeat for 30 days. And then hopefully all my life.
