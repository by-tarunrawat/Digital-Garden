Date: 13-12-2018
Topic: These are the things women know about love

What do women know about love? What do they think love is? Movies will have us believe the whole prince charming scenario. And to be honest, if you really wanted to project every love story to that scenario, you will succeed. Anyone can play the role of prince charming. I mean two people in a long distance relationship might not be happy with the arrangement. But they can certainly project their love story as a prince charming saving the day kind of story.
But let’s go deeper than that. Let’s start with someone I’m really familiar with. My mom. She loved my dad. Still does. I can see the longing she has for him. She hasn’t gotten over it. I don’t think she will — like this at least. I’ll have to take some action. 
Anyway, my mother had an arranged marriage. She had me in the first year of her marriage. She was 20 when she got married and had me when she was 21. I’m guessing somewhere around that time she fell in love with my dad. Both were young, and guessing by her age, and the stories I’ve heard, my mom didn’t have a lot of interaction with kids of the opposite sex. My father was probably the first man she had in her life. I think initially for her it was a responsibility. There was no other option, but to fall in love. So, it was inevitable. To her it probably became about support, loyalty. If you give another person a fair chance, and take care of them, you often form some kind of bond anyway. I think she knows loyalty. Even after his death, she tries to be loyal to my father’s side of the family. It’s an obligation that has become a social circle.
Women look for security, support —which can be financial or emotional— attraction, loyalty.
Nobody wants to feel insecure in a relationship. Constantly wondering if it is going to last or not. It stops people from taking action. Big action. That big step. Commitment. But that I think is a make opinion. I think women get emotionally invested in a relationship faster than a man. Does that mean lesbian relationships will work the best? Maybe not. But why do women do that? It is dangerous. Coz in case of failure, there is a chance, you will get hurt if you’ve invested so much. Is it worth it though? I guess. The return is great. It is the best. Women know that there is a chance of failure, but that is not the way to start anything. There is a risk of failure in almost everything you do. Does that mean you do nothing. To get something of worth, you should be willing to sacrifice almost everything. Women in general know that in a relationship.
That was an interesting half baked theory. Let’s make a list. A random list. 

Women know that for them the world is a buffet. Heterosexual women have a larger pool of men willing to date them.
Women know that on tinder, their success rate of getting matched is insanely higher than any man they know. Combined. 
Women know how to argue. 
Women know that a key to a good relationship is open communication. Although most of the rules of this communication are made by women.

I don’t know if I’m fit to answer this question. I’m just free-balling here. I don’t know what I think about love. I just know when I see it. I think most people know. Are there rules. Are there things you are supposed to know? I didn’t get a notification for that class. Waise, there are shit tons of videos about love. What to do, what to expect, How to tackle different things. 
Fuck it.
