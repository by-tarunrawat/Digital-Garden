THe dilemma at the heart of my story is the battle inside that goes on in any human. THe battle of what the world expects one to be and what you want to become. And that scale is so unfair because most of the time you don't even know what you want. SO to get it is so unimaginable. Because you don't know. You spend most of your life living according to the expectations of others, maybe following someone else's path or maybe you are easily influenced by your peers. THe problem is nobody is taught to think for themselves, nobody is taught to think critically, and nobody is ecouraged to follow their passions. Are these passions clearer at a young age? more than when you're old. Maybe. At a young age, I think, you try everything. And if you're free to explore all these things, then you'd probably know by the time youre adult, what you want.
But kids are directed. THey should be free to explore things. BUt they are taken in a line put in uniforms and told to repeat after other.
So the dilemma at the heat of my story is the need to become what you want vs fitting in, and keeping people happy, and be safe and secure.
The dilemma is what do you do when you have to choose between living a normal life that is safe but boring and a life where you have to explore yourself.
I think the dilemma is still not clear. Maybe it'll come.
