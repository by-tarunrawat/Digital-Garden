Date: 27-11-2018
Topic: Remember a sound.

It sounded like the sky was falling down. I had closed my eyes. I had tried psylocibin for the first time. It was mild but it definitely had an impact. This sound was way later in my trip. There was some residual high of the mushrooms. I could feel just a little bit. WE had something to eat and had come up for a joint.
We smoked that hash joint and I was sitting inside a couple of blankets outside our room. It was really cold. Any part of the body outside the blanket would instantly feel the need to come back in. I got high instantly. I think it was a combination of the mushrooms and hash. 
I sit there in the cold when it started raining. Light at first. Then it came pouring down. It became colder in a matter of minutes. I closed my eyes. I wanted to recreate the visuals and the sound that I had experienced before. But this was something else. I could see some visuals. There weren’t as bright as before. But there was something still there. 
The rain was so loud. There was music playing as well. But I remember the sound of the rain overwhelming the music. It felt like a natural disaster. I’m sure I’ve seen days when it has rained harder - louder even. But that day it felt like somebsy was pouring buckets from the sky.
You know sometimes when you’re sitting under a tin shed and it starts raining. It was like that. Except there were no tin sheds. Only brick roofs, and trees and mountains. The night was so dark. I couldn’t see anything outside our veranda. There wasa  tree not 50 ft from our rooms, but even that wasn’t visible. I was trying to look every few minutes. But the sound of the rain along with the music felt so trippy. I didn’t want to open my eyes. The only reason I would open my eyes, was to check on shreshth. Or to see what’s goin on. Maybe to check the time. 
It was amazing. I would love to do mushrooms again. I just don’t want to go through the whole process of talking to a dealer and figuring out if the mushrooms are good or not. That too after paying a lot of money. I would rather hunt for them. Take them back and check them out on our own.
