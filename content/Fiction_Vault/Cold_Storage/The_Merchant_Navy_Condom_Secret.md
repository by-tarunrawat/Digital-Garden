Date: 25-11-2018
Topic: Write about a time you found out about something you weren't supposed to know

I’ve known a lot of secrets. I can’t think of anything I found out that I wasn’t supposed to know. Related to my parents? I remember I saw a dirty magazine that my father had. It was hidden somewhere in his room. I had this habit of looking for stuff in my parents room. Lie just going through stuff. I don’t remember what the magazine was. It had articles, and a lot of pictures. There were Pictures of naked women. Which, at the time, was a big deal. I hadn’t seen naked women. There was no Internet. Sexual things to me were actors kissing in movies. Making movie love. Which wasn’t much. It was mainly a girl and a boy rolling around on the bed. 
I had also seen a condom in — I don’t remember if it was his closet or his briefcase. If it was his briefcase then, I don’t want to say it. Maybe I’m not remembering it properly. It was that iron closet that we have. It was in that. It was some Chinese brand I don’t know about. He might have bought it on the ship. In Singapore or something. Why would he buy condoms in Singapore. There was just one. He might have bought a pack. I don’t want to think about my parents having sex. I mean of course they did. 
Its just that I’ve heard so many stories from Samarth about people in merchant navy having sex with prostitutes in ports. He has told me about these horny sailors, single, married, married with kids. Doesn’t matter. My dad was a good guy. BUt I don’t know much about him. I certainly don’t know about his work life. When he sailed. He wasin the merchant navy for more than 30 years. Out of those 30 years he was married for 20-25. I don’t know man. I don’t want to say he cheated on my mom with prostitutes. But I don’t know. Maybe he didn’t. He was a good guy.Everyone said so. And he was a good guy. He was simple. He did drink. He got drunk in like a couple of pegs. And from what I’ve heard of ship parties, you don’t drink just two or three pegs.
I didn’t think this prompt would take me in this direction. This was surprising. I had suppressed these things inside for a while now. I don’t think I had thought about this even. It just came out. Maybe I’m not meant to write to make money or be famous. Maybe I just need it as therapy.
