Date: 24-11-2018
Topic: Write about a bed

I like mattresses more than beds I think. Most of my life, after I left home, I’ve slept on the floor.Jst a couple of mattresses stacked on top of each other. It feels more comfortable to me. Even at MICA, when I had a single bed available, I bougt a couple of mattresses that I kept on the floor and slept there. My bed was generally empty. Or some friend would sleep on it.
This changed when I moved to Bangalore. At Roy’s house I slept on the floor. Then I got a job. Got a house, after a lot of searching. I finally bought my first bed. It was from Urbanclap. It was a king sized bed. Huge. I could roll play football, it was so big. I got a mattress for it from Aman. He sold it to me. It was the most comfortable mattress I’ve owned. And I’ve owned a few mattresses in my life, even if I haven’t owned any beds. 
I used less than half the bed when I slept. I didn’t need more than that. Only when I had girlfriends, was when it was fully utilized. But even then, pooja slept mostly snuggled with me. We were still using less than half the bed. 
I’ve generally had problems with not fitting in the confines of a normal bed. I had no such problems with this one though.
I still sleep on my childhood bed at home. It’s nice.But I’m heavy set and so is my brother. We’ve made kind of a curvein the mattress where our butts rest. It’s funny to see it. It’s not really uncomfortable. But would I prefer a bed with straight mattress? Yes. Definitely. Anyway. I felt like I’d grown up in life when I bought my bed. I don’t know why I felt like that. I’d been working for a while. Lived on my own. But a simple purchase of a bed made me feel like I had grown up.
I might have felt a little sad when I had to sell it. Maybe the sadness was about leaving the house. Maybe it wasn’t even sadness. I don’t know how to describe the feeling. I felt embarrassment that I had to leave the house. Inadequate that I couldn’t affor to live on my own and will now have to depend on other people. I felt sad because I had spent some really good times in that house. My friends lived nearby who I hung out with quite often. It was a good life. I felt angry, that this was happening to me, and I couldn’t do anything about it.
Now, some girl is using that bed. I hope she is having fun. I’m sure she is. IT is a great bed. With a brilliant mattress. I don’t think I’ll see it ever again. But I want to buy another bed. Not because I like sleeping on beds, or because it makes me feel grown up. It’s because it signifies something to me. It signifies that I’m standing on my own two feet. Or resting my own fat bum. However you want to phrase it. A bed isn’t just a bed anymore. It’s my freedom.
