Write a character description or a poem about a person based on you, one struggling to create some type of art but who is bound by family obligations. When you finish, ask yourself how you feel about this person. Are you sympathetic to his struggle? Then ask yourself of you extend such sympathy to yourself.

Sanjay Murthy is a closet poet. His dream is to be published one day. He works as a senior software engineer at a big multinational company. The job doesn’t challenge him mentally. Although it takes a lot of his time. 
He wants to quit his job and work as a poet and a writer full time. He hopes that someday people will pay to buy his books so that he wouldn’t have to work at his boring job anymore. He is afraid of taking the leap.
His parents want him to work at the same job for life, get married, have children. He wants to get married too. But he fears that if he gets married then he will be even more obliged to stay at his job. He believes that then he will never be able to quit and follow his dreams.
He does have a girlfriend who he loves. She encourages him to pursue his dream. He is afraid of failure. What if he quits his job and his writing doesn’t work out? He doesn’t have confidence that he will be able to support himself financially doing this. He has seen too many failed poets and writers.
