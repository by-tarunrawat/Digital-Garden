Date: 08-01-2019
Topic: Blue the color or the emotion


Here goes the blue color. It’s not right if it’s not blue. Sky is blue. SO is the sea. Not always. But you get what I mean. Teal, Aqua or indigo. There are many more shades that nature shows. It seems it’ll taste cold and bland. 
Color of death, infection, democracy. It’s the opposite of communism. It’s the same as gloom. Eye after a punch, my brain after a hunch. The color of ink I used when I was a child. Our masters painted red showing the difference between us and them. It’s the yin to red’s yang.
The music that oscillates your body real slow. Dance with feet stuck in one place. Head side to side. 
Something old and something blue. Blue october.
