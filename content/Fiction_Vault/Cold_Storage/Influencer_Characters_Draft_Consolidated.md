Characters:
Acchoo urf Aaroh Chauhan, Kasar, District Almora me rehne wala ek mamuli ladka hai. Lekin agar aap usse poochen to wo kahega ki wo ek celebrity hai. Uske pita ki Almora sheher me ready-made kapdo ki dukaan thi. Acchoo Almora ka influencer tha. District Almora ki jansankhya sade aath lakh se thoda upar hai. Iske gyarah pratishat log, Ek lakh teen sau chaunsath, Achoo ko uske social media par follow karte hain. Is lihaaj se Aachoo ka apne aapko Almora ka lifestyle, fashion aur travel influencer samajhna kuch galat nahi tha. 
Iske liye Acchoo ne kuch kam mehnat nahi ki hai. Ja ja ke Almora ke logon se jaan pehchaan banayai, unclon aur auntiyon ko social media seikhaya, like follow karna sikhaya. Ab Acchoo ke paas interns hain. Jo waise to Aachoo se Influencing seekhne aaye hain, lekin poora din, Aachoo ki ghulami karte hain. 
Almora me Aachoo ki ek celebrity ki tarah baat hoti hai. Wo poore almora me, apne pitaji ki dukaan se liye gaye, naye naye kapde pehen ke aise ghoomta jaise Golu Raja khud apni praja ki khoj khabar ke liye nikle hon. Aachoo jitna apne kapdon ke liye almora ke social media par chaaya hua tha, utna hi apne #TanManDhan life coaching ke liye. Wo Almora ke logon ko jeena sikha raha tha. Ye sab karne pe Acchoo ko social media pe anekon likes aur comments milte. Aachoo ke pitaji, Chauhan ji, bhi isse bahut khush the. Acchoo ki posts dekh ke Almora ke log unki dukaan par aate aur inka dhandha badhta. 
Acchoo ke pitaji to chahte hain, ki Acchoo “family business” take over kare, lekin Acchoo ko pata hai ki ye dukaan shukaan uske liye bahut choti baatein hain. Abhi wo Almora ka celebrity hai, ek din wo India ka celebrity banega. Is baat ki usne Golu devta ke mandir me kayi baar guzarish ki hai ki use Chic Blogger Awards me Regional lifestyle influencer ka award dila do. Usne kab se Golu devta ko likhit arzi de rakhi hai. Lekin abhi tak Golu devta ne nomination bhi nahi dilaya. 

Satyendra Chauhan urf Chauhan ji readymade kapdo ke maalik hain. Inki dukaan Almora me 26 saal se hai. Ye kayi naslon se Almora ke ek gaaon Kasar me reh rahe hain. Chauhan ji ke baap, dada, pardada, yahin kasar me kisaan hua karte the. Chauhan ji, kisaani se duur, Army me jaana chahte the. Wo apne parivaar se kasar chod ke jaane waale pehle insaan hote. Par, saari parikshayen pass karne par, Chauhan ji ko pata chala ki unke ghutne ek dusre se touch karte hain. Unhe samajh to nahi aaya ki ghutne choone aur sarhad par ladne me kya connection hai, par wo apna sa muh lekar kasar wapas aa gaye. 
Wapas aa kar Chauhan ji ki shaadi karwa di gayi. Ab jimmedaari aa gayi to kuch kaam karna tha. Chauhan ji ne Almora market me choti si readymade kapdon ki dukaan khol li. Shuru ke kuch saal dhandha acha nahi chala. Almora ke logon ko kapde silwaane ki aadat thi. Chauhan ji ke bhagya me akasmik badlav aaya jab unke ghar Acchoo ka janam hua. Un dino Smt Savita Chauhan, Chauhan ji ki Smt ji, Chauhan ji ke saath dukaan me kaam karti thin. Achoo bhi unke saath hota. Bazaar me ghoomti mahilayen, bade boodhe dukaan me baccha dekh rukte. Kuch log khelte, kuch pyar dete. Achoo ki mummy ek shatir saleswoman thi. Unhone Acchoo ke ‘cute roop’ ka bharpoor istemaal kar Saikdon readymade kapde bech daale. Aap keh sakte hain, ki paida hote hi Acchoo, Chauhan and Sons Fashion ka brand ambassador ban gaya. Chauhan ji ko yakeen ho gaya ki Acchoo unke liye bahut lucky hai. Sakshaat lakshmi ka avatar. 
Chauhan ji ko Acchoo ki har cheez par garv tha. Chaohan ji uski itni tasveeren kheechte the ki Achhoo ke muh se nikla pehla shabd hi “Photo” tha. Uske baad se Chauhan ji ki duniya Almora me unki dukaan aur Kasar me unka ghar parivar ho gaya. Achoo ki har harkat, har shaitani par unhe naaz tha. Wo abhi tak ye dikhava karte hain, ki wo Acchoo ke saath kitne strict hain, par wo Acchoo ki aankhon me ek aansu bhi nahi dekh sakte. 

GeeGro saab Kasar ke popular Badnam cafe ke maalik hain. Cafe ke neeche hi GeeGro sahab ne ek tourist guest house khol rakha tha. Ye cafe Geegro sahab ne kareeb 10 saal pehle khola. GeeGro sahab cafe me poora din Ganja phookte paaye jaate hain. Cafe ki popularity ke chalte yahan kaafi jawan ladke ladkiyan aur tourist aate hain. Geegro sahab ne cafe banane me jo akalmandi ki baat ki wo ye thi, ki unke cafe ke saamne panchachooli ke pahad dikhte the. Ab touriston ko aur kya chahiye. Garam chai, aur panchachuli. 
Geegro sahab Acchhoo se pehli baar kareeb 8 saal pehle mile. Us samay wo school ke baad, shaam ko cafe me aaya karta tha. Neembu paani, cocke masala, mushroom maggi order karta, fir unki photos kheechkar, na jaane kya karta rehta tha. Usko Geegro sahab bahut dilchasp aadmi lage. Acchoo ke hisaab se GEeGro Sahab badi bhaari bhaari baatein kiya karte the. Acchoo un baaton ko dhyan se sunta aur apne social media par daal deta. Geegro sahab ho Achoo se bada lagav ho gaya. Wo Achoo par apna haq sa samajhne lage. Apne hi cafe me usse kabhi chai banwate, kabhi maggi. 
Unko ye Achhoo ki social media ki latt achi nahi lagti thi. Wo dekhte ki Achoo video banane, followers badhane ki hod me laga rehta hai. Social media par likes na aayen to Acchoo ka chehra utar sa jaata tha. Wo Acchoo ko aksar bolte ki ye 5 minute ki shohrat me kuch nahi rakha. Par Acchoo ko ye baat samajh nahi aati. Use lagta Geegro ko is baare me kya pata. Wo ek chote se sheher ke, chote se gaon me apna chota sa cafe chala rahe hain. 
RAaz ki baat ye thi, ki Geegro asal me jaane maane abhineta/khalnayak Gulshan Grover the. Aj se 12-15 saal pehle Filmi duniya aur shohrat se pareshaan hokar wo industry chod kar chale gaye. Poore bharat me jagah jagah ghoome. 10 saal pehle wo kasar aaye aur yahin ke hokar reh gaye. Itna ghoomne firne me unhone na apni daadi katwaayi, na baal. Hipster kapdon me babaon jaise dikhne lage the. Isiliye logon unhe pehchaan nahi paaye. Wahan ke logon se milne julne ke baad, kuch logon ne unhe bola ki wo ekdum gulshan grover jaise dikhte hain. Geegro ne is baat ko mazaak me taal diya. Wo jis hujoom se bhaag ke shaanti ki talash me aaye the, wo chin jaati. 
Unhone Kasar me ek phadi ka tukda khareeda aur wahin apna ghar bana liya. Fir kuch saal me ek cafe aur ek guest house. Saal ke zyadatar mahine wo yahin guzarte hain. Beech beech me kisi film ka role aa jaye, logon ko, gaanv ja raha hun, bolkar nikal jaate. Na koi sawaal karta na koi jawab dena padta. 
Kasar ke ‘youngsters’ Geegro se kaafi prabhavit the. Unki rahasyamayi personality bacchon ko badi akarshak lagti. Bacchon me Geegro saab ke baare me kuch afwayen thi. Koi kehta tha ki Geegro saab ek yogi hain. Beech beech me tapasya karne chale jaate hain. Koi kehta tha ki GeeGro saab tantrik hain, koi kehta ki Geegro saab ek zamane me jasoos reh chuke hain. Geegro saab ko ye sab afwaon ki khabar thi, par ye unke liye achha hi tha ki kisi ko pata na chale ki wo kaun hain. 

Dhenpu urf Pradeep Joshi, Accho ka sabse purana dost tha. Waise to wo khud kaafi talented tha, par wo bachpan se Acchoo ke aage peeche ghooma karta tha. Behtareen gayak aur nratak hone ke alawa, Dhenpu ne long distance se Engineering ki degree bhi pa li thi.
