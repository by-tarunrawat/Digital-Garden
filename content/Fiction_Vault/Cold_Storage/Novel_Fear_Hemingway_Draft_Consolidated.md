I'm afraid to write this story because, I don't know if i can do it. I want to be a write, but that doesn't mean that I am one. And my mom would be fine with me being a wroiter, if i was good at it and making some money, having a career. It's not exactly a career if you're not doing anything. I procrastinate a lot, I think that I'll write something today, or tomorrow. There are ideas in my head, but I cant seem to get myself to writing. Failure is a reason. WHat if I put in 90 days, 6 months in writing a novel and I don't have anything to show for it, or I do have something to show for it, but It's stupid, it's not good. I woud have wasted 6 months into doing something that I got no use for anymore. What will I do then. No prospects, no job, no career, and now I Did something that didn't come out as I expected, or that would pay my bills. That would be a failure for me. But at least I'll have the saisfaction of writing a novel, however good or bad it is. THat would be an achievement. I don't know how that would feel if I don't have a good thing on my hands. I have nothing on my hands right now. I am also afraid to write, because I fear that I don't know what to say,. I think I'm not a good writer. For now anyway. But maybe I'm not good at all. What about my own story? Am I okay with bleeding on paper, as hemingway suggests. Will I be okay with my deepest darkest secrest coming out? Maybe, maybe I can hide them. I dono't know if that's such a good idea. It will make the stories I tell fake. Would that be fair to anyone?
