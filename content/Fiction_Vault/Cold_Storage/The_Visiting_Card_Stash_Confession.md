Date: 15-11-2018
Topic: Once, when no one was looking…

Once, when no one was looking I took some weed from a friend’s box. I was out of weed and broke. I hadn’t been working for a while, and spending money on weed seemed like a luxury. I had little money and I didn’t know if I’d have weed for a while. SO I took some.
Why didn’t I just ask you say. Because I’m a dumb-ass. I don’t like asking. I feel like they might say no. And even if they say yes, they’d make a face which says. Fuck you man. Can’t you get some of your own. I didn’t want to put myself in that position. Them too I guess. But it was more about me than them.
How much did I take and how I did it you ask? I took very little actually. It was mainly dust. I didn’t want to take anything that they’d miss anyway. They would’ve used it, maybe. Probably not. They may have used it in times of weed-draught. And looking back, they did. So anyway, I took a little bit of the wasted branches and fallen buds that were thrown away into the box, not to be used again. I might have taken enough for a couple of joints. I know how to spread that over three. I’m talented that way.
How I did it is also pretty sly. I was rolling a joint. I had a visiting card in my hand to make the roach. I don’t remember If I told them to get something, or they just went to their room on their own volition or not. But As soon as they were out of sight, I flicked  a little bit from the box; wrapped it in another visiting card; and put the card in my wallet. I didn’t feel nervous or anything. I did feel guilty. I felt so small I wished I could crawl under the bed. Why can’t you get a job you piece of shit. You have to resort to this to get things you want. What would anyone think of you if they found out. What do you think of yourself right now.
I was ashamed. But I still wanted it. I went home and smoked it. The guilt was gone. All that remained was smoke and ash and the numbness in my heart.
