Date: 10-01-2019
Topic: Write about a quilt or a blanket

I love winters. Because I get to sleep in a warm quilt. I love how snuggly it is. There are a lot of things to like about winter, but a quilt is one of the top 3 things for me. Even now when I go home, I lie inside a quilt all day. Either working on my laptop or watching TV.
Some days my mother puts it out in the sun. I don’t know what it does. I think it freshens the quilt up. But I’m guessing here. It could be anything. IT certainly becomes warmer. Its so comforting to sit inside a freshly warmed quilt. Especially if its cold outside it. 
My dog sleeps with me inside the quilt. He likes the warmth of the quilt as well. Its an old quilt. We’ve had it for a really long time. There are two of those quilts. Part of a set. The covers keep on changing. Me and the quilt are the same. It has patterns on the top. THe background is a late evening sky dark blue on which there are patterns of bright yellow. They look like menhdi designs to me.
THere is something about me and quilts. It seems like I’m always inside them. Right now I’m in a really skinny quilt. At my house in Indiranagar, I was always on my bed inside a quilt. I should not be in a quilt. Not all day. Its bad habit. But is it? WHy? I’m not being productive. I’m not doing anything to help myself inside a quilt. I forgo all responsibility and action inside a quilt. Just roll one up and keep passing the time. THis is no way to live. I have to get up early morning. Make the bed, brush and poop, and exercise. I’ve loved quilts all my life. But this is high time I get out of it and do something for myself.
