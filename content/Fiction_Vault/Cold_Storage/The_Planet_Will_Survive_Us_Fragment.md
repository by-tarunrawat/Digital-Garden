This is not about us. It is about the planet. It is about the rest of the life on earth. It is about the future of our children. These are the things that are said about climate change and the effect humans are having on the planet.
Planet will survive. It doesn’t matter if we’re on it or not. It doesn’t matter if it is habitable for us or not. It doesn’t even matter if there is any life on earth or not. The planet will survive. There might not be green trees. Small insects. Lions and giraffes. Humans. But like trillions of other planets in our universe. It will survive. 
Look at Venus. It is the hottest planet on earth. No life can sustain itself. It’s atmosphere is toxic. But the planet is still there. Unless it disintegrates. Or it gets swallowed bu the sun or something, it will remain the second planet of our solar system. 
We are not about to blow up the planet. So we don’t need to worry about it. All planets go through transitions already. Not over a hundred or a thousand years. Over millions and billions of years.
