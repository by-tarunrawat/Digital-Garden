Date: 14-11-2018
Topic: Write about bathing

Hated it when I was a kid. I mostly had no control over how I bathed. Living in the hostel it would be days before I would bathe properly. The water was cold, and so was the weather. The water on my body felt like needles. I love bathing with hot water. I could stay under the shower for hours if I could. But one shouldn’t waste water. There less of it in the world as it is. 
The warm water on my body. I feel it on my bald head. Dripping down my face and lips. I like to blow on it. It creates a splash. I love showering more than using the bucket. I think it’s because in a bucket I know when the bath will end. I know it will end in 5-10 minutes in the shower as well. But I can extend it to as long as I want depending on how relaxing it is; Or how much fun I’m having.
Using the bucket this is what I should ideally do: Fill it up half to three-quarters. Then get the temperature down with cold water. Take a bath. 
This is the ideal scenario. But I like bathing. And I like hot water. So what I do is, Fill the bucket up completely with hot water. I don’t leave any space for cold water. To bring the water to a temperature where it wouldn’t burn my skin. I do that with the mug. I fill the mug up to half to three-quarters. Open the cold water tap. Then fill the mug up completely with cold water. Letting the excess spill over onto the bucket, till the temperature is good enough. It’s a longer process, but I’ve done it for years now. I think it’s the best way for me.
I’ve never taken a bath in a bath-tub though. Pooja thinks I’m missing out. I feel so too. A little bit. Imagine sitting in warm water. Not having to worry about the temperature again and again. My work of having to constantly balance the water temperature. Or having to worry about wasting too much water by standing under a shower for too long. 
I will use a bathtub someday. I will buy or rent a house with one in it. I think that will be the ideal way for me to take a bath.
