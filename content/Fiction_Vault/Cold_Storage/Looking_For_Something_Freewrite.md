This cuts right to the heart of the "creative writing MFA / course industrial complex"—paying someone an exorbitant sum not to learn a secret technique, but simply to buy permission and external validation for what you already know you should be doing.
Looking for something

I have been looking for a long time. Unsuccessfully. I have to change something. My approach, or my goals. I don’t know if I have goals. Maybe I’m looking for them. Maybe I want to find what I want first. I need it right now. I have so many distraction man. Fuck. I am enough of a procrastinator to have things piled up on me. I procrastinate so much. DO all writers do this? Am I a writer? I don’t think I’m a writer yet. I can call myself that. It doesn’t matter. Fuck. Do all writers struggle with writing the way I struggle with writing. I’m constantly reading about it. How to do this and that. What the fuck. Why is it so hard. Why can’t I just put my thoughts down on paper. What am I waiting for? What is it that I’m looking for? When will I find it? Am I just kidding myself Can I even do this? 
Voice? Am I looking for my voice? I have to put something on paper to struggle with that. Am I being to pedantic about this. Thinking about stuff that I need to deal with later? Right now I just need to move my fingers and write. That’s what everybody says. 
IS this what I want to say to the world? How I suck. How I suck at the most basic thing any educated person knows how to do? No. I don’t think so. I don’t think any eduycated person can do this. THis is hard. I’m not dumb. I’m just struggling with something. I just need to work through it. I can. I will. But how? How will I do it?
Do I really want to tell stories? What do I want to write about? How do writers figure that stuff out? By writing I guess. It just has to come out through fingers. On the page. There is no substitute to this for now.
Or I join some school — or a course — where they teach creative writing. I’ll spend thousands of rupees and they’ll tell me the same thing I’m telling myself. Write everyday. Write for longer. Give it time. I don’t know. Maybe that will give me some kind of validation. I know this stuff. BUt some experienced writer telling me the same thing will validate what I know. I’ll know what I know is right.
It’s twisted. I just need to get on with it and keep on writing. Keep on looking. I’ll find it. I just have to keep faith.
Also, how will I know what kind of shit is worth sharing? Will I ever know? I think I’ll know when I’ve written something worthwhile that I would like to share. I just need to keep on writing garbage. What I’m doing everyday anyway. Garbage garbage garbage.
Shit shit shit.
Fuck it!
