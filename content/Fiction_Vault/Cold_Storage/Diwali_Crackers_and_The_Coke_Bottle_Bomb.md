Date: 09-02-2019
Topic: Write about fireworks

Fireworks were so much fun when I was a kid. I remember we’d buy some every diwali. There would be bombs, spinners, flower pots, rockets. Mom used to distribute it among us after the pooja. We would then light them up one by one. It would take an hour or so. We savored our crackers. 
They were mostly the same every year. But there was always some anticipation for the ceremonial fireworks. There were always other people who would start at sunset. We could only do it after the pooja was over. 
I don’t like firecrackers anymore. My fascination with them has gone. Now I see firecrackers I think about how much noise and smoke it is contributing. We’ve ruined our environment so much that we can’t enjoy things like these anymore. I’m not sure I want to anyway. 
There was a vendor, who we would only see during the diwali season. My mom had somehow made a connection between their families. So, he would give us the crackers at a discounted rate. It became a ritual to buy crackers from him from now on. Every year.
There were times I remember when a lot of crackers remained even after Diwali. We would light them during special occasions like India winning a cricket match. One time, I remember, My brother and I decided to make a firecracker on our own. We had a lot of crackers left. So, we took the inside stuff of the crackers — stuff that actually makes it burn nd explodes — and put it in a glass bottle of coke. Then we took the fuse from one of the other crackers and shoved it from the top. 
It was an amazing explosion. Almost like an actual bomb. There was so much light that we couldn’t see anything for a couple of minutes after that. Our windows were smashed. Our clothes torn at some places. There was ringing in my ear. Parts of the glass in my brothers thigh. My mom was absolutely furious. I was a little scared at the time. A piece of glass could’ve gone in either one of us’ eyes. We were lucky nothing bad happened. But the funny thing is, I remember that incident with fondness.
Fireworks during the first anniversary of Uttarakhand state formation were also amazing. I’ve heard about the fourth of july fireworks in the US. This was something like that. Fireworks went on for a long time. At the start it was amazing. There were designs and beautiful explosions in the sky. Fire making beautiful geometric patterns. IN so many colors. Rockets going really high and exploding in a huge circular pattern. It was amazing. WE stood outside the whole time. Just looking at it.
After a little while, the geometric patterns and the explosions became faint in the sky. Then they were hardly visible. The noise had the same ferocity. It was still loud. There was huge cloud of smoke that had covered the entire sky. It hid the moon behind it. What started with amazement at the fireworks ended with disbelief at the pollution. That might be the first time I realized how bad fireworks were. I think it was since that day that I stopped bursting them.
