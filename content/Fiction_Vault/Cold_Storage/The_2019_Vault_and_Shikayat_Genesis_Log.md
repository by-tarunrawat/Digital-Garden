Date: 28-02-2019
Topic: What if…

What if I kill a man. I can’t really kill a man. Hypothetically. It would have to be for really good reasons. I must be out of my mind. There are very few things that can do that to me. Danger to my family. Self preservation. Rage. There aren’t many other I can think of. I don’t think I’m someone who would go for revenge of any kind. 
If I kill a man, I would probably give myself up. Can I run from the police? I can. What will that achieve? I will be away from people I love. People I would want to talk to. Eventually I will be caught. There is no way to hide forever. Another option is to not get caught. Eliminate evidence. Remove fingerprints. That sort of thing. 
What if I finish my novel. Maybe not now. But just write a novel. Finish it. That was some good advice actually. Write stuff. I need to Create a blog also. I need to write with some purpose. I’ll have to start editing old work. Rework.
REWORK. 
There might be something I can use.
I will need to work for this. I will hae to give it a couple of hours everyday. At least. If I do want to be a writer. I need to work towards 5 hours. But let’s just aim for 2 now. 10-15 minutes a day will not work.

Tomorrow I will… I just realized I’ve turned this exercise into journaling. There aren’t any rules though. I don’t have to write poetry every time. Some kind of prose or whatever. I can write anything. So it will be a journal when I feel like it. 

So continuing, tomorrow I will do this prompt exercise first. As early as possible. Start Reading stuff through January. Work on them one at a time. See if something can be made out of it. Maybe I can make some document to archive any interesting thing I wrote on a particular day. I don’t know. We’ll figure as we go along. 
I have to finish my book review waala prompt. I will work on it by the side.
I was thinking about this poem last night. I was thinking about my dad. All I could think of was ‘shikayat hai’, and images of not being there. Me not knowing my father. There was gratitude. I’m willing to try and understand. I don’t know If I’ve dealt with his death yet. I want to work on that as well. 
I will make a file on my family. And write my thoughts in it. I want to know what I think about my family on a day to day basis. I care about them. I need to resolve issues with people in my life. Actually I can make a file about people in my life. Yeah. That would be better. I’ll do that as soon as I finish this. Which is now. Let’s jot down all the points first.

1. Prompt
2. Review and edit past entries and label.
3. Book review
4. People in my life
