Date: 26-12-2018
Topic: You hear church bells in the distance. 

I thought there was no one here. I hadn’t seen a cactus for the last two hours. It doesn’t seem too far. I can probably walk before I pass out. Maybe I’ll get some water there. There will be people there. I will talk to them. Tell them my stories. See their reactions. Gasps and giggles. 
I will tell them how I came upon this journey. Traveling all alone. All alone for weeks. I’ll tell them I’ve lived on berries when the hunted were few. I’ll tell them the story of my first catch. A boar. I had weathered the cold, wet and gloomy. It was with a hand crafted hammer. It was long; and brutal. I have scars to show for it. 
I’ve still got a few miles before I rest my feet. And sit in the shade of the chapel. I still can’t see it. God, how far is it? My feet are sticking to the ground. The sand feels like glue. It is taking all my energy to lift my feet and move it forward. Maybe If I use my hands as well. Yes! Maybe I can make it on all fours. 
I’M COMING. I’M COMING. I can’t waste my energy on screaming. I’ve used up all my spit. It feels like there is something stuck in my throat. I can’t gulp it in. I can’t spit it out. My last sip was 8 nights ago. I can’t help but think about that moment. How it felt on my tongue.
