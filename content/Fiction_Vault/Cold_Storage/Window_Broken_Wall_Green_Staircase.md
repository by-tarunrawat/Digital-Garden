Date: 28-11-2018
Topic: Look out your window. Write what you see

I see the staircase in my neighbors house. It is on the other side of the wall of my house. The wall is green. The staircase grey. We broke the wall a few years ago. Making a path from our house to theirs. So that we could come and go. Although, If I think about it, it is to their benefit more than ours. But it’s fine. At least my mom doesn’t have to jump the wall all the time. 
Their windows are dark right now. I can see some light through the balcony of one of their bedrooms. They have switched off the outside light. I can’t see their dog. She is a pretty dog. A good one too. Looks scary. But she is a sweetheart. They got her from… I don’t know where they got her from. She is a street dog. But well behaved. I feel they don’t treat her too well. I’m not sure. Because I don’t have evidence for it. But from what I’ve seen, I think I’m not far off.
The back area behind my window is pretty dark right now. There is a tank that is not visible right now. Not much is visible to tell you the truth. 

The sky looks dark too. I don;t see anything right now. I’m sucking at this prompt. Can’t write anything. Don’t see anything.
