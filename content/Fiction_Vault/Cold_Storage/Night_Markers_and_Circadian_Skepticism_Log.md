Date: 08-03-2019
Topic: Night.

When does night begin? Sun goes down? There is darkness all around.
Is it time? We’ll call anything after 7 PM, night.
If it’s the first, then what about places where sun stays in the sky for months?
If it’s the second, then will I not see night if I keep flying to times before it’s time.

Maybe it begins when I feel it’s time to make dinner plans.
When I decide it’s time to go out with friends.
Or just watch a quite movie and relax in bed.
It is definitely night when I sleep then rise up as the night ends.

It is not the darkness or the time. 
They are symbols. Signs. Markers.
It’s for convenience. A trick of the mind.
Morning’s bright, night when your mind gets darker.

Can I sleep in the morning and work at night? 
Will night still be night if I’m out and about. 
Doing morning stuff.
Not making dinner plans.
Not going out with friends.
And certainly not relaxing in bed.

And all day tomorrow, when my friends are at work.
I’ll be sleeping. Maybe catching up on an episode or two.
Will that still be morning?

Ummm… Sure. If you want it to be.
