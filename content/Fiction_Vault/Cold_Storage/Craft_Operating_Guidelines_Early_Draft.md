Ideas as products. Ideas don’t mean anything. I need to understand, explore and articulate ideas. If you write it down, it becomes something tangible. There is an authorship attached to it. You get credited for it. If the idea is good it reaches other people. The goal should be to record ideas. TO put them down on paper. Read more about them write down your understanding and put it in a form where it can be clearly explained to someone else.
Treat ideas like products. You need to write them down and put them out.

Prompt your thinking. Ask questions while writing. I’m in a phase where I think I’ll ask why a lot. But I need to ask questions to direct me to different aspects of the thought. Maybe give me a different perspective. ASking questions will also tell me how much I actually know about the topic and how much and what I need to learn.
Ask questions to get a deeper understanding.

Summarizing a few points.

1. Treat ideas like products. Put them down on paper to make them tangible priduct. I get to decide which product is good and which is not.
2. Do a 5-10 minute free writing exercise for every topic to find out our initial knowledge.
3. Go where an interesting idea takes you. Feel free to go on a tangent.
4. Ask questions at various points. Why, how, when, where, what.
