Make a list of people you see on a daily (or very frequent) basis, from the barista at the coffeehouse where you stop in the morning to regulars at your gym to  the neighbor down the street who waters his roses in the evening. Describe each of them in as much detail as possible. Write down any interactions you’ve had with them. Try to use at least one of these people to begin a story or a poem or whatever form seems suited to them.


The two brothers at universal general stores: We get most of our ration from a shop right opposite our house. It is always crowded. It’s owned by two brothers. The younger one is taller than the older. They usually have a stubble on their face. I would call the older brother a little chubby. The younger one is not fit, but he doesn’t seem fat because of his height. 
Both of them are quite social. They have to be running a business. They talk to their customers, recognize them, know what’s going on in their life in general. At least that is what I’ve seen in my experience.


Komal: Komal is Pooja’s roommate. She is from Delhi, but unlike other people from that place, she is quite soft spoken. Too formal sometimes. Like, she will sometimes ask, “Tarun would you go to the trouble of doing something something” or “Tarun can I trouble you for a small thing.” It seems like formal email language. I’m quite amused by it. She is a good girl, in the traditional sense of good. She is neat, well spoken, isn’t mean to people and is friendly. But I don’t know if that is really her. She sometimes behaves pretty absentmindedly. Although, we all do to a certain extent, so she may be forgiven for it. 
She has really big eyes and is pretty tall for an Indian girl. Her long hair are parted in the middle and run down her back. I’m not too big on noticing clothes. But I’ll give it a shot. She wears large Tshirts when she is home. Going out, she will wear a kurta with jeans. At least from what I remember.

Renuka: is Pooja’s maid. She doesn’t speak much Hindi, so it is pretty difficult to communicate with her. She comes in to wash utensils and clean up the house. She usually comes around 1-2 in the afternoon. She is whaetish complexion with short hair. Her hair come up to her shoulders, but she ties them up with pins and rubber bands. Her hair don’t really look short with a salwar kurta, which she usually wears. I haven’t talked to her much. I generally give her leftover food to eat. Which is not that tough to explain. I can just show her the boxes and make a gesture to eat, and she understands. 

Inam: I met Inam at Roy’s place. Young boy, about 21-22. He is from Kashmir and like people from Kashmir, he is quite fair. I’ve always wondered how his life was in Kashmir. What we read in the news and articles, it seems that the life there would be hard for anybody. Anyway, he is a jovial guy. We play fifa quite a lot. I haven’t talked to him much outside of that. Tall guy, good looking, I’m sure he wouldn’t have any problems getting girls to go out with him.

Akansha: I don’t understand why Akansha is so formal. She is nice to everyone. I don’t know how she does it. It would frustrate the hell out of me. I mean I’m nice to people. But she is on a different level. She reminds me of a lot of moms I’ve seen. She would do stuff for you without asking, she will do things you ask of her.
