Design the ideal writing space. Fill it with all the things you want and love. Take your time and really develop your descriptions. Ladle on the details. Now step back and look at your descriptions. Does it tell you anything about your writing goals and dreams? Does it say anything about your vision of what a writer is? Does it tell you anything about how you see yourself as a writer? Consider these questions. They will help you design a good place for you to write and will help you understand better your own writing impulse.

I imagine a room on the first floor of an independent house. The area where the house is, is not too crowded. There are other houses in the area. But they are at appropriate distances. I see wooden flooring. There are windows on all four sides of the room for plenty of sunlight to come in. But There are also thick curtains to cover those windows for when I want to be alone with my thoughts.
There are shelves on the wall. It has my old books. I don’t know why I’ve kept physical books in the room. I don’t read them anymore. I find digital books to be more practical. There is a big desk on one side of the room. There are two doors in the room. One leads inside the house. It is on the left side of the desk. Towards the farthest corner on that left wall. The other door is on the right. It leads to the huge balcony outside. This is on the nearest corner on this side. I have my own paintings everywhere in the room. 
The desk is solid wood. There are pens, and pencils, and art supplies on the desk. My laptop is on it as well. I have a chair behind the desk. It is a large moving chair with big armrests. The armrests are big enough for someone to sit. The chair can recline but also stay straight. It can swivel on the bottom and move around the room. There is a sofa facing me directly when I sit on the chair. It is touching the wall facing me. It is large enough to lie down and take a nap. 
On the farthest corner on the right wall, there is a fireplace. The light in the room comes from yellow bulbs. I don’t like white CFL or tube-light in the room. There are lamps on each corner of the room. A small chandelier on the roof. The room is big enough for me to walk around comfortably.
The door on the right leads to a balcony which is half the width of the room. It is big enough to walk around as well. I can easily host 5-6 friends here without cramping it up. It has a sofa and a coffee table. I write here sometimes. The view of clouds over a mountain. I can see greenery all around. It’s a good place to sit, smoke and think. Talk to people. Write.
I have a speaker system inside the room. I connect it with my phone or laptop to put on some classical music while I write. I experiment with ambient noises sometimes. There is not a lot of other stuff in the room. Only things that will help me write — notebooks, pen, books — and things that will help me paint — chart paper, canvas, oil pastels, pencils. And guitar. I always keep a guitar here. Sometimes I try to write songs. Sitting outside on the balcony.


***********

I don’t know what my writing goals are from the description. I can hazard a guess. I want to live a secluded life. I don’t only want to be a writer. I think I want to paint and make music as well. Maybe I feel all of these are connected for me somehow. Maybe each of these helps me with the other. If I think about it, I’m not the kind of person, who would be happy doing only one thing in life. In fact I was planning on putting a pool table in the room. But I thought that would be too distracting. I might just play pool all the time and never work. 
The place that I’m envisioning is pretty cold and secluded. It’s somewhere in the mountains. I’m not really a loner. I like company. I like to hang out with people and talk. But I think like most writers I like my space when I write.
I still can’t figure out any goals. What does the prompt mean by goals. What kind of stuff I want to write? Wat kind of a writer I want to be? I don’t know yet. I thunk I’ll discover that in time. I’m happy with practicing everyday for now. I mean ideally I would want something that I can put out. Maybe that doesn’t matter. Maybe I can put out whatever I write everyday. I don’t know. I’ve to figure a lot of shit out. I wish I could’ve started doing this earlier. I couldn’t have. I still procrastinate.
