Date: 10-12-2018
Topic: Write about a kiss

It was winter I think. I can’t really remember, but we were wearing blazers and sweaters. I was in engineering college. And I had been infatuated with a girl for three years. We had been friends for all this time. Her name was Shweta. Shweta Bora. I had told her I loved her. She hadn’t really reciprocated with words. Although, it seemed, she liked hanging out with me. She would hitch a ride from me every other day. Her girlfriends seemed to think she liked me a lot. The way she looked at me suggested they were not wrong. 
Anyway, Both of us, along with our friends, used to walk in the hills after college. Usually we would walk to the famous shiv mandir. This one time we decided to walk to this secluded temple that we could see from our college, but didn’t know the way to.
We followed some kind of trail. Reach there. Hang out for a bit. And then decided to walk back. There were three more people with us. On our way back there was this kind of a hill where we decided to sit and chill for a bit. Shweta and I sat together.
After a few minutes the others decided to explore the area. We decided to stay back. Now I don’t really remember how it happened. Maybe we talked about it. I don’t think we did though. We might have said something vague. Like I might have asked her to kiss her on the cheek or something. She agreed. I think that turned into a kiss. Yeah, I think that’s how it happened. I kissed her cheeks. I did it again. She turned her head. And before I knew it, we were kissing. She put her tongue in my mouth. It was weird. I had never kissed anyone before like that. It felt great though. I wanted to do it more. I didn’t want it to stop. But we were outside, and people were about to come back.
It felt great. A girl I had liked for so long kissing me. Her lips had some gloss, so I was already aroused. I could taste the lip balm. I don’t know what I was expecting though. The kiss was great and all, but it wasn’t really what I was expecting. Maybe I expected something extraordinary. I don’t know. Maybe I had been waiting for this moment for so long I had built it up too much in my head. It was my first kiss. But logically, it is just a kiss.
