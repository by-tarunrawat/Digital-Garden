Date: 17-01-2019
Topic: Write about a tattoo

I’ve wanted a tattoo for a long time. I think body art is great. The permanence of it scares me. I think that’s why I haven’t gotten it done yet. I don’t know what I will get anyway. I keep looking at designs. Like tattoos people are getting done on reddit and instagram, designs on google images, pinterest. I like a lot of them. But I don’t know which one I would like on my body.
I want the tattoo to mean something. I feel like I don’t have a set principles. I keep fluctuating between ideas. How will I know if there is something I will believe in all my life. There are so many tattoos that I like online. But I can’t get something just because it looks good. It has to have some significance to me. A person, philosophy, idea, design, could be anything. I don’t know.
My brother has a few tattoos. The first one he got was an ‘Aum’. It is a big tattoo on his back. The symbol itself is in bold and there is a portrait of Shiva inside the symbol. It is a nice tattoo. But god is an easy thing to stop believing in. There are too many things going on in the world to believe in a god. His second tattoo is a portrait of my dad with the words ‘we miss you’ written on top. He got this tattoo after our dad died. There is emotional significance to it. Even if the motional significance wears off, a father is a father. You can’t change your father mid life. His third tattoo is for our mother. He has gotten something written. No portrait yet. I feel like there will be one after she dies. It’s a horrible thing to say. But I’m extrapolating from a single data point. Both these tattoos are on his chest.
Pooja has a cute tattoo herself. It’s a small line drawing of baking tools. Which makes sense. She loves baking. She does that to de-stress. It makes sense for her to get something like this. Because it has meaning and permanence. And it is really well done.
I like this one tattoo of a DNA molecule with music symbols and piano keys incorporated in it. The message basically is that ‘Music is in my DNA’. Its a water color tattoo. Use of color is great. There are bright colors splattered around, which gives it a really nice look. 
There is this other artist, who draws really neat geometric tattoos. The patterns are so intricate. The lines so neat. I really like those as well. There are other random ones that I like. Nothing memorable. Earlier I used to consciously look for designs. I would think about getting these tattoos done. Now I don’t really think about it like that. Maybe I’ve resigned to a fact, that I will not get one done. Maybe I think this is something I can’t get done planned. Maybe It’ll come to me one day — a design, an idea, an image. I don’t know. I’ll keep waiting.
