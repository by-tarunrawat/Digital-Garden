Date: 14-01-2019
Topic: Close your eyes. Write about what you see.

I see Pooja. I also see the new Doctor. The latter because I’ve recently seen an episode of the show. Pooja is on my mind everyday. I see her smiling. Her image is not every clear and fading. She is walking towards me. Dancing a little. 
Her face is sad all of a sudden. Something has fallen down. It looks like some kind of pastry.  I kiss her and hug her. She smiles again. I pull her and we walk away from it. I swing her from my right hand to the left. She raised her left hand as if finishing a pose. 
It’s amazing to be certain of love. When you know the feeling you have for another person is love. It is easy to confuse infatuation and attraction for the real thing. I have done it myself a couple of times. In relationships I’ve wondered if I’m in love. Sometimes there was certainty, sometimes not. But even certainty had cracks. Right now my love feels invincible. Certain. No cracks.
