Date: 20-11-2018
Topic: Write about acceptable losses

Losing someone dear to you is never acceptable. Losing people I mean. I’ve lost a few people in life. My dad, both my grandmothers, a few other relatives. My dad I never really expected. Like most things in his life, I wasn’t there for it. Most things I knew about my father are from second hand accounts. Somebody telling me stories about him. My grandmothers both loved me. I was their favorite grandkid. They were both old, so their passing were expected at some level. But not really acceptable. My dadi, we knew a few days in advance. She was sick. She had been for a few days. It looked inevitable. It was a matter of time. Although all death is a matter of time if you think about it. My Nani, I had no idea about. I was in MICA at the time. I had no idea she was sick even. Mausi might have mentioned it I think. At the time, I was thinking about my dad mostly. I was told that he had suffered a heart attack. My mom and brother had gone to see him in Uruguay. His death I still can’t accept. 
I think death is acceptable, when the person dying is old, or suffering. You should at least get some time to make peace with death. 
Breakups become acceptable with time. If I had broken up with Rekha right after college, It would’ve been devastating. IT still was when we actually did break up. But I got over it in time. It was unacceptable while we were dating. During the last few months of our relationship, we weren’t talking much. I was still making some effort. She wasn’t. Every passing day I was contemplating breaking up with her. I didn’t know how I could. Or if I should. I did love her. I didn’t tell her that often. But I did. Maybe that made her insecure about us. Anyway, from not wanting to lose her, I went to breaking up with her. Me! Breaking up with her? I never thought I’d be someone who broke up with another person. And this was Rekha. First girl I loved. But it not only became acceptable towards the end, it was essential. For me and for her. I did try to get back together with her. SHe still didn’t reciprocate. 
Time. Time is what gets things from unacceptable to acceptable. From essential to expendable.
