Date: 01-12-2018
Topic: Write a love letter. To anyone

Hey Pooja,

I know we talk everyday. We talked a few hours ago. We even message each other on whatsapp all the time. I thought I’d write this letter to you to tell you how I feel about you.
First things first. I love you. I love you a lot. I couldn’t imagine I will fall in love this way. To miss someone like this. TO be the way I am in your presence. I want to touch you all the time. Not in a sexual way necessarily. Just touch you. My skin touching your skin. My cheek touching yours. My hand on your face. Anything. Its the only way I want to be around you. Just be in touch with you. Literally. 
I keep following you when I;m with you. When you leave the bedroom to go to the kitchen I follow you. When you go to watch TV I follow you. I want to see you all the time. Seeing you makes me feel happy. Plus you are so beautiful. I’m not trying to flatter you or anything. You are gorgeous. I want to look at you all day. That would be a good use of my time. I don’t think I’ll regret that at all.
You know when we cuddle, I love it. I love holding you close. Your face close to mine. It is easier to kiss. And kissing you is so much fun. Your cheeks are made for me to kiss. There is no other reason that they exist. They are mine to kiss. The way your skin feels on my body — it’s so… warm and nice. It feels like the most comfortable I’ve been. It gives me security. When you want me to hold you, it makes me feel wanted. It makes me feel loved. And I know you love me too. 

You’re so far away right now. I wish you were here. I wish I could touch you the way I do. I wish I could hold you, kiss you, follow you around. Soon. Can’t wait. I think I want to spend my days with you. Making you happy, ‘coz I know that will make me happy. Eating your cakes. Stroking your face, watching movies with you, talking to you. I want to do it all day. I want to do it everyday.

Pooja, I want to tell you that I love you again. I don’t think I say it enough. Even if I said it every hour everyday, it wouldn’t be enough. I want to see the world with you. See it from your eyes. Tell you how it looks from mine. See it with you in my arms. Share those moments with you. 

Let me end this letter by saying this. I didn’t think we’ll ever get together. It was not in my plans. I hadn’t thought about it. But now that it has happened, I don’t know what I will do without you. Isn’t that strange? You were never in my plans and now I can’t anything without you in it. 

Love,
Tarun
