Date: 16-11-2018
Topic: It’s what I do in the middle of the night.

I sleep. Prompt over. I twist and turn in my bed. I try to sleep. I don’t really have problem sleeping. I sleep like a baby. It is really hard to disturb me once I am asleep. I don’t even get up to pee. If I really need to pee I generally hold it in and go in the morning. But that is only if I’m asleep. I;m not lazy about it. I just don’t see the point in breaking my sleep. 
The only thing I really get up in the middle of the night to do is drink water. If I’m thirsty, really thirsty. I’ll get up and go to the refrigerator to drink water. People have told me to keep a bottle near my bed. It makes sense also. In fact Pooja keeps a bottle near the bed. So if I really wanted to I could drink without getting up. But I only drink cold, chilled water. Only cold water quenches my thirst. It gives me a weird satisfaction. I don’t know what it is.
I twist and turn in the bed quite a lot. I’ve seen other people sleep and they sleep normally. In one position. Roy, Pooja, Mannu, my mom. THey sleep in one position. Not for the entire night. But most of the night. I can’t do that. I don’t feel comfortable in one position. I have to change it every few minutes. When I’m just about to sleep, I do that more often. But once I sleep the frequency decreases. That’s what creates problems with cuddling also. I cuddle with my girlfriend at night. I love it. The touch of her skin. Her cheeks. A couple of inches away from my lips. I can kiss them anytime. I don’t have to move much. And I do kiss her cheeks all the time during the night. I can’t help myself. They’re so soft. And I love kissing them. I love cuddling. But to cuddle you have to be in one position. You have to either hold your partner, or let them hold you. Either way, you have to stay in one position. I can’t do that. Initially that created a problem. But now even Pooja understands. I have to turn. I fold my legs. Sometimes perpendicular to the bed. Sometimes along the bed. One leg stretched and one leg folded. In either direction. On my stomach. On my back. That is how I’ve learned how to sleep.
Nowadays, it is easier to sleep for me. But I miss cuddling with her. Although, I have a dog that sleeps with me. And I love how he loves to cozy up to me. But He can’t fill her place. Cuddling with him isn’t the same as cuddling with Pooja.
Anyway, I do a lot of things, in the middle of the night. GEt up to drink water. Cuddle. Twist and turn. 

I could’ve made some stuff up. But I don’t think I’m there yet. I’ll try that in another prompt. Be somebody else. Like a psychopath.
Maybe in this one if I was a psychopath I would get up and so something psychopathic. Like torture someone I’ve kidnapped. Or stalk random strangers. Stalking shouldn’t be psychopathic. Otherwise everybody would be a psychopath on facebook. No?
