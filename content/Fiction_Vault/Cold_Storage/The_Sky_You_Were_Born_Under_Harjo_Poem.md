Date: 06-12-2018
Topic: Write about the “sky you were born under.” (after Joy Harjo)

It changes color like a chameleon. Making everything like itself.
The blue of the ocean when the sun in high, 
The red when it’s about to die… for today.
The pillows of cotton that make frogs, horses, dragons are filled with tears — squeezed to give life.
Playground of birds. Gliding, soaring, galloping with the wind, I wish even I could fly.

This is the sky I was born under, 
Holds wars with clouds, 
Gives life, creates thunder.
This is the sky you were born under,
If you could fly you would wonder,
If is really above us?
You could never put asunder.

The breath we take, we take it for granted,
The sun on the skin, is less that what it is,
It’s perfect. Can go out and take it it. Take it in as much as you wanted.
The skin of the ground below, the shield from the weapons our there.
Shining bright, glowing, no makeup or cream,
Giving its all, for me and for you for everyone to share.

This is the sky I was born under,
A show of stars and worlds and galaxies all day,
What other marvels it’s hiding I wonder.
This is the sky you were born under,
A mother cradling her baby,
Or a father protecting his child,
A treasure of life that we can plunder.
