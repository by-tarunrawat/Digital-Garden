Date: 27-12-2018
Topic: This is how my heart was broken. 

It is going to be a thing isn’t it. Actually I remember. It was with Rekha. She had been my girlfriend for three years I think. When I finally decided to break up. Before that we had really few calls between us. Msgs mainly. Going back and forth. We didn’t really know how to be around each other anymore. We would maintain a distance. We hadn’t had sex in a long time. I really hadn’t had sex with her for so long. The last I remember was in Ahmedabad. I don’t remember anything after that.
Anyway, after a few unsuccessful break ups, where we would talk about breaking up, but wouldn’t, I decided to end it for real. I knew it was a tough decision. I knew it would hurt later. I didn’t know if I would ever get over her. I just knew that I deserved better from a relationship. She deserved better from a relationship. This wasn’t it. 
It was on a phone call. I was in Pune. Sitting on a bean bag at Dinkar’s house. When everyone else had gone to sleep. I called Rekha. We had been chatting for a bit. I don’t remember what. I was trying to gather the courage to do this. On the call, it just came out. I didn’t hesitate. She knew it was coming. She wanted it too. It wasn’t even a relationship anymore. It hadn’t look like one for a long time. She understood. We hung up. For the last time as people who loved each other. 
My chest was behaving weirdly. It was like there was someone pulling m heart out of my body. Tears rolled down my eyes. I tried not to cry. I couldn’t control myself. I put on a sad song on my mobile. Tears grew heavier. I put the song on repeat. I wanted to let out everything. 
I thought about the times we were together. How she was with me. We never talked though. That is probably my fault. We should have talked about stuff. We were not having sex. We should’ve talked about that. How will we even be together. With her in a different city than me. There were a lot of issues in our relationship that we needed to talk about. We didn’t. Those issues were never resolved. I don’t even completely know where we went wrong. Where was that moment where the love left.
We didn’t make enough effort for the other person. I tried for a bit. I was lazy. I could’ve done more. Maybe. I did try hard though. She had also closed herself at some point. She probably thought there was no future with me. Which is a fair concern on her part. I didn’t have a job. I was wandering. Like I am now. 
Maybe we’ll meet in the future. Find out what went wrong. Finally talk. When both of us are happy. Wounds turned to cool scars. Old stories with new perspectives. Unafraid of the truth. Maybe that time will never come. Either way I’m happy to go along.
