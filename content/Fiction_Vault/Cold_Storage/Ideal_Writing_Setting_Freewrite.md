Describe your ideal writing setting. Fill it in to the last detail. Perch yourself on a balcony overlooking the pacific. Snuggle yourself next to a fire in a richly paneled study. When you finish the description, read it with an eye toward patters and details. Do you prefer an open or a closed place? Light or dark colors? A sense of freedom or safety? No right or wrong answers here.

I like writing with my back straight. I also like writing leaning on something. I’m a fidgety writer that way. So I would love a chair that would allow me to do both those things. Like a nice comfy barcenlounger. With a table in front as well. The table is for when I’ sitting up straight. 
I love listening to music when I’m writing. But music with lyrics is quite distracting. I’ve experimented with instrumentals by Ludovico Eiunadi, and I’ve also tried some classical music. I don’t really know if it helps, but it’s not distraction. I can work without the music bothering me. Music relaxes a little bit. I think I need to experiment a little more with the music.If it’s helping or not. If it is, then how. 
I think I would love to write with a scenery in front of me. Probably hills. Cold weather. Snuggled up in a warm blanket. With a coffee or something. TV is a big distraction for me. I can’t help but watch it if it is available. So any place without a TV would be great. Even in a room would be fine. I would need a desk and a chair inside. 
I’m pretty paranoid about people looking over my shoulder. I don’t want anyone to see what I’m writing. That’s why I don’t write in front of people. And I don’t tell people when I’m writing also. I just don’t want anyone to see. So I don’t think I’ll enjoy public places. Like, I’ve read about people writing in cafes. And it sounds really romantic. But I can’t do it for now. Maybe when I’m a little more confident with my writing — which,  after reading about the writing process of other writers, may never happen.
Apart from that I’m not fussy about it. I’m just a procrastinator. Not too particular about the surroundings. 

So if I’ve to sum it up. I need a comfortable chair. With a paper and pen nearby (I hadn’t mentioned this). A table in front. Out in the open or in a room, it doesn’t matter. But I need certain privacy. Even the assurance that no one will peek over my shoulder. That is my simple requirement. It’s not too much to ask for. Is it?
