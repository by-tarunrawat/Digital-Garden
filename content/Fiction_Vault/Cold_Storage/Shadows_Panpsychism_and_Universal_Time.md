Date: 03-12-2018
Topic: Shadows

One thing that never leaves you. Unless you are in the dark. Then your shadow mixes with everything around you. Dark and attached to you all the time. My dog is like my shadow. Follows me around everywhere. If people did tat we would get sick of them.Even people we love. We can’t be followed everywhere.
Shadows tell us time. We don’t use to do so anymore. But It tells us where the sun is. Which tells us time. Our time depends on where the sun is. But that’s not what time is. Time is something else. It is continuous. It doesn’t depend on the sun. It will still be there when the sun isn’t there anymore. It will be there when we are gone. But will have any meaning then? If we are not there to observe it. If we are not there to see the changes time brings to the universe. Billions of stars dying and exploding. Planets revolving and rotating. Climates changing. Huge rocks in space changing the dimensions of other huge rocks in space. All this will be Not have any meaning unless there is some consciousness to see it. We are that consciousness. Before us time did not have meaning. After us time will not have meaning. 
Is that true. Am I giving us too much importance. Does it matter if observation is necessary for time to have meaning. A planet rotating to provide sunlight to one side gives time meaning. A rock changing dimensions of another rock gives time meaning. It is meaningful to that rock. It is meaningful to the planet. I am giving too much importance to humans. I don’t have understanding of consciousness yet. Maybe everything in the universe has a consciousness. In their own way they observe time. By the cracks in surfaces. By heating and cooling. The shadows they cast. The way moon casts its shadow on us and the way we cast a shadow on the moon. 
So if we tell time by looking at shadows. Maybe the universe does too? It is possible.
