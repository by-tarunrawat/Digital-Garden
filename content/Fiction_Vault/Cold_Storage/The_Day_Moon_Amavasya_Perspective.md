Date: 13-11-2018
Topic: Write about a day moon

Half moon. Barely visible. Bright sky. The sun trying to hide it with its brightness. Its an irony that the sun gives the moon its light and hides it as well. The moon is closer to us. Yet, it’s the sun that is its master.
Don’t remember seeing a full moon during the full light of day. It’s just there sometimes. The nights brings it to it’s full glory. It’s bright, lights the world. Not completely but just enough. Enough that the darkness doesn’t consume us. It is so powerful that it directs the movement of the seas. Yet, in front of the sun, it seems like it is taking orders from it. Up there in the sky waiting for the sun to go away. 
If I’m seeing the moon with the sun, that means there is total darkness on the other side of the earth. They call it the moonless night. Yet, the moon is still here. I can see it. It doesn’t have an impact on anything. But, it’s here. How is it moonless. Maybe it is for them. THe mooless night. Amavasya. Its about perspective. What’s not there for you is right here for me. Sometimes you just have to wait for things you can’t see. Patience. It’ll come. It’s there. Seen by billions. You just have to wait your turn. In life as well. It’s there. You just have to wait — and keep looking. Always keep looking.
