Date: 06-02-2019
Topic: The last time

The last time I wrote something I was proud of was years ago. I was in college. I had no pressures of job and money. I was sure I would get a job. I wouldn’t be in this position. I could’ve done much more than I did. Maybe having a plan is not a bad thing. 
What would I have done if I had the knowledge I have now? Not in terms of what I’ve read about writing, but in terms of experience. I would tell myself to write everyday. Like I plan to do now. Pick some books up. I would tell myself to nurture this habit slowly. There were so many events in college. I could’ve arranged a few myself. Written a play, and invited people to watch it anywhere. Thinking about it now is making me mad. I should’ve done that. That was great opportunity. I needed 5 maybe 10 people to watch. I could’ve gotten so many people to work with me. I could’ve made movies. Anything. There was so much opportunity. If only I could do that bit again.
The last time I was truly calm was a little under three years ago. I mean I’m generally calm. Especially on the outside. On the inside there is a lot of stuff going on. I’m worried about my future. I don’t know what I should do. There are no right answers. The thing is, there are no wrong answers either. But I still don’t know how to proceed. 
Stop depending on weed. That would be number 1. Fill your time with productive stuff. Create a website. Don’t worry if it is not good. Or makes you any money. For now. Just make a website. Keep writing everyday. Actually. The first thing to do is to finish the Star writer’s program assignment. Start working on it today. Do the dialogue bit first. Read the documents and start writing. Write anything. Just finish the damn thing today. We’ll work on it later. Then tomorrow, do the story challenge. Finish it and work on it later. 
I don’t know if I’ll get into the program. But I’ve got to give it my best shot. I am looking for opportunities aren’t I. This is one. It is right there. I do assignments for companies all the time. THis is something I actually want to do. SO I have to make this my first priority.
The last time I was actually enjoying my days was in college. There was stuff to do all the time. You were working on some assignment. Playing, working on commitees and stuff. There were so many things to do. And I used to enjoy my days. I couldn’ wait for the next day to arrive. I didn’t want to sleep sometimes because I didn’t want the days to end. It was an amazing time. It will never come back. But I want to make my life like that again. Fill up my days with things that I like doing. Things that I want to do.
