Date: 19-12-2018
Topic: Write about a stranger

The first image I got thinking about a stranger was this guy who had complimented me on my looks at a metro station. I was just walking along. Trying to get to the exit. I heard a voice from behind saying hello. I thought that this person was probably talking on the phone. After a couple more times I looked at him and saw he was looking at me. Then he pointed to my beard and said, “You have a great look.” I’m not great with compliments. I don’t know how to react. I just said thank you and moved towards the nearest exit I found. 
I don’t know why I get so awkward when I get a compliment. It’s not like I don’t like to be complimented. I’m a human and I like being praised for something. I feel like I’ve to say something nice in return. Maybe that is not genuine. Maybe I’m saying stuff because its protocol to do so. 
Like if someone says that is a nice shirt. I would have to say thank you. Your jeans or shoes or whatever look really good. Now I don’t know if they really look good or not. But I have to say something. Right? So I quickly have to observe what the other person is doing and comment on it. There is a lot of work involved. So it’s not just that I feel disingenuous, I’m also lazy. I don’t want to do the work involved in observing people, noticing good things about them and telling them.
A lot of the times I already have noticed something nice about someone, but I still won’t say anything. What is that all about. Why won’t I say anything? It’ll make the other person feel great. Where does this awkwardness comes from. 
I remember these people we were in the car with while going to kodaikanal. There were three girls. One of those girls told me my kindle cover was great. Now, I’m not my kindle cover. So, it’s nothing for me to feel great about. But I felt good. It was like she was complimenting my choice. Why can’t I do that to people. I’ll see something and keep it to myself. Isn’t that how you connect and engage with people you don’t know. Through something both of you appreciate. At the very least it’ll make the other person feel good about themselves.
