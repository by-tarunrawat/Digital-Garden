Date: 29-11-2018
Topic: Write about something you bought mail order

I’ll write about something I bought online. I think in this day and age, that is mail order. Isn’t it?

I bought these set of phone camera lenses once, from Aliexpress. Now mobile camera nowadays have become really powerful. In fact I have a joke that we now buy cameras that can call people. 8 mega-pixel, 12 mega-pixel. And now 24-30 mega-pixel. Mobiles are all about cameras today. 
It seems impossible to do anything to these cameras that can improve them in any way. But these lenses certainly did that. 
One of the lens in that set was 18x optical zoom. Now there is zoom capability in all these cameras. But these are digital zooms. Which means when you zoom, you get a pixelated image. You might as well just click a picture and then zoom it later on your computer. Optical zoom on the other hand zooms in on the image like a binocular. With the image as clear as you were clicking right beside it.
There were other lenses as well. Like a fish eye lens. I’m not really clear on what exactly it does. But it gives a really cool effect to the image. Then there was kaleidoscope, Wide angle, and a few more. Really cool shit. 
I got it for Pooja on her last birthday. Unfortunately, she never used it. Never. I used it a couple of times though. Once when I was testing it. And another when I tried to convince her to use it for one of her instagram pictures. It would have been cool.
I would have loved if the gift came to some use. But its okay. She liked it when I gave it to her. I guess that is fine for now.
