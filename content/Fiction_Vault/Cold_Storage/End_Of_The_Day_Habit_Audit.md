Date: 07-12-2018
Topic: The end of the day

I’m usually sitting on my bed. Trying to finish a prompt. Watching YouTube videos. Maybe watching some porn. Don’t want to do the last one. This prompt I generally try to finish before I get into the bed. In the afternoon maybe early evening. Leo tries to find the right position to sleep in. He wants to either get inside the quilt, or he wants to get on my lap. I like dogs like these. He is so cuddly. 
At Pooja’s the end of the day is quite different. We smoke a joint. Make out for a bit. Maybe have sex. Cuddle. Most days she sleeps early. I hold her with one half of my body. One of my arm is under her neck. I watch YouTube videos, or read something till I get sleepy. This reading habit that I have is good. I really like it. I don;t know if there are any positive effects of it on anything. But at least it’s better than watching videos all the time.
I have this dissatisfaction most nights. I have this lingering feeling that I haven’t done anything worthwhile today as well. I don’t know if I had this feeling when I was working or not. Maybe not. I wasn’t satisfied with working at a job. I wanted to do something more than that. But I didn’t have this feeling of worthlessness at the end of the day. I absolutely loathed having to go to the office next day. I was always trying to sleep at a reasonable hour. But I never felt like this. 
How should the end of the day look like for me. Okay, let’s start with what makes sense that I should be doing. I should be think about what I did in the day. How long and why. Then I should make plans for the next day. Detailed plans. What I think I should do tomorrow. At what time. With detailed instructions. This is similar to what I read in the ‘Power of Habit’.
This makes sense. There are two things I should do every day:
I should force myself to make a list of things I did that day, for how long, and why?
I should make a list of things I want to accomplish the next day. With detailed instructions on what to do.

I’ll start today. This is what I’ll write in my journal.
