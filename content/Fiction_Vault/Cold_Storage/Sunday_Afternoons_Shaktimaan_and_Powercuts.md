Date: 9-11-2018
Topic: Write about a Sunday Afternoon

Sunday afternoons were about watching television shows. I remember getting up early in the morning to watch Rangoli. There would be cartoons on TV. I remember Popeye the sailor, Tom and Jerry. I also remember watching Chandrakanta and Shaktiman. We didn’t have cable TV for a long time. We just had one channel. Doordarshan. Chandrakanta was a great show to watch. It was a fantasy period piece.Very few in Indian entertainment. It was great. The production wasn’t that impressive. It was okay. But the story was amazing. It was supposed to be a love story in the times of kings and magic and strange creatures. I don’t think I’ve read or seen any good fantasy things from India.
Another program I fondly remember was Shaktiman. It was a superhero show based on Superman. THe hero had similar powers. He could fly - although he would spin and fly instead of doing it normally like other superheroes. I don’t know what spinning achieved. I think he had super breath as well. Super strength of course. He also worked at a news paper as a reporter. His girlfriend was a reporter. He even saved his girlfriend by flying opposite to the spin of the earth at the speed of light to reverse time. They had lifted everything from superman stories. We didn’t know any better though. SO it was enjoyable. We would wait the whole week for it to be sunday so we could watch it. 
One of the things I remember from those days is the power cut. We used to get power cuts in the mornings. Sometimes right at the time when these programs were coming. It was so infuriating to me and my brother. There is one show to watch on TV and we can’t even watch it. There were no reruns. No streaming. And no youtube. Fuck that shit. 
I think I need to write about a particular sunday afternoon. I doubt I remember something specific. Forget it. Nowadays there are no sundays for me. Everyday is a sunday. I like it.
I associate sundays with staying at home lazing around. Sleeping a little late. Which is everyday for me now. BUt I want to do something with the rest of my day.
I don’t remember what we would do the rest of the day. I can’t remember. Watching TV is the ony thing I remember. Most sundays my father wasn’t home. Even if he was, I doubt we went out. I think we visited my Nani on sundays maybe. She loved me and she loved to pamper me. Is it weird that I don’t remember my sundays as a kid. Maybe most people don’t remember them.
Let’s see. There was no school. I don’t think I would meet friends - Maybe later when we were a little older. I don’t think I played cricket. I think I would go to my Nani’s place on SAturday nights. I’m not sure about that. HOw can I remember. What a shitty memory humans have.
In college too, I would just stay at home on sundays. Maybe go out to play snooker. Which we did everyday anyway.
Sundays really started to mean something when I started working I think. Weekends actually. Saturday would be a fun day. I went out somedays. Chill with friends maybe. In Pune I went to Dinkar’s place on friday evenings to spend the entire weekends at his place with him and Anjali. Sundays would be Lazying around again. Ruing the next day when we all had work and had to go to our offices.
I think I digressed a lot in this topic. Let me end with something that would be on point.
Nowadays Sunday afternoons are spent with Pooja. Lying in bed. Watching something. SHe bakes sometimes. Or cooks something amazing. It’s brilliant. We smoke a joint, make out and cuddle. It’s amazing. We have each other all to ourselves. Then the evening comes and she rues the fact that she has to go to the office tomorrow.
