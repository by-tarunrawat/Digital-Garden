Date: 28-12-2018
Topic: Write your morning.

I wake up at about 10. It’s not a good habit. I’ve tried to wake up a little early a few times. I’ve tried getting up at 8. It usually becomes 8.30 but it’s still good. I planned a run at 8.30. It was also a good opportunity to smoke a cigarette. Away from the house. Here, in Bangalore, there is no specific need to get up at 8-8.30. I don’t have to do anything. I could go for a run. I should. I need to attach purpose to it. I’ve to get up at 8 because I have to do my workout. Whatever it is. A run. Skipping rope. Calisthenics. Whatever the tool. The purpose is to get fit.
Anyway, I get up at 10. Hang out with Pooja for a while. Drink Tea. Expel things that need to be expelled from my body. Watch a few Youtube videos. Smoke a cigarette probably. Go for a bath and brush. I can see a few problems with the routine. If I need to get fit. I’ll have to wake up early. Brush, Workout, ‘expelling’, and bath. This should happen till 10 roughly. I would believe. A 30 to 0 minute workout should be good enough. 
So, wake up at 8.
Get ready to workout at 8.15. (including brushing)
Finish workout by 9-9.15.
Make Tea. Bath and bathroom activities.

This seems like a good morning routine to try. I should give that a shot, instead of doing what I do now in the name of freedom but is like as routine as any scheduled activity I could do.
I wake up at a certain time. I do same things every morning. I follow the exact same routine every day. Why not try another routine, that is better for the goal I have. Right now, I want to get in better shape. That is my goal. I want to shed about 8 Kgs. It’s not about abs. It’s not about biceps. I want to get fitter and stronger. So, I want to lose 8 kgs in a month. And I want to run 10 kms at the end of the month.
I’ve already made a morning routine. I wake up at 8. And work out immediately after. I can go for runs on a few days. Maybe twice a week at first. Then we’ll see. 
This also frees up some time for writing. I’ll see how it goes.
