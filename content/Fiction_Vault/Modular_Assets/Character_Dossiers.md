## Dramatic & Family Archetypes

### Pratik Sinha (The Replacement Son in the Shadow of a Ghost)
- **Role/Type:** 20-year-old creative trapped in a pragmatic family structure.
- **Core Conflict:** Sacrifices his dream of studying acting at NSD to take a safe business degree, performing academic excellence as the only currency that pierces his mother's 17-year depression.
- **Dramatic Tension:** Sympathizes with the runaway surrogate while watching his family's domestic orbit warp around the returned sister.
- **Source:** [[The_Outhouse_Surrogate_Bible]]

## Speculative World Mechanics & Systems

### The Professional Surrogate Market (Tiered Reproduction & Breast Milk Capitalism)
- **World Mechanic:** Reproduction fully industrialized into A, B, and C-tier corporate agencies. A-tier blends customized DNA tags (athletic/genius) with celebrity royalties; C-tier surrogates pump milk for organic vending machine brands to pay rent after birth.
- **State Control:** Emotional bonds between children and surrogates are treated as psychological contamination corrected through state-mandated detachment therapy.
- **Source:** [[The_Surrogacy_Tiers_Worldbuilding_Bible]]
