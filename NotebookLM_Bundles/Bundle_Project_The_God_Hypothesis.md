Date: 29-03-2019
Topic: Write about something astonishing.

I find our existence astonishing. Not the fact that we exist, but how and where we exist. We are living our lives everyday on this planet somewhere in the universe. The planet we live on is traveling around the sun at thousands of miles per hour. The sun that travels around the center of our galaxy along with its brothers and sisters. The galaxy is going through space at unimaginable speed. And we don’t sense any of it. We know about it. Through observation and science. But if someone smart hadn’t told us, we wouldn’t know. 
I can’t fathom how it looks to an observer far enough to see all these things. I wish I could be that observer. Zooming in and out of any location in space. I wish I could see the planets revolving and rotating, and the movement of the stars. The many galaxies that are spread across the universe. We don’t know how many. And all of this is moving — traveling if you will — through some invisible fabric of space time. 
I don’t understand where this universe is? Does it end? Does it have an outside? It should, shouldn’t it? Everything has an outside. Great minds say that the universe is infinite. I don’t know about that. I can’t really comprehend infinity, but keeping it aside, I’m not sure that the universe is infinite. Maybe there is something outside. We are in our solar system. Outside which are other star systems. Outside which are millions of stars going around a common center. This whole arrangement is our galaxy. Outside which are other galaxies. Big and small. We didn’t know about them for a long time. We thought they were other stars.
Right now we can see that our universe is big. Really big. Infinitely big. But maybe there is something outside. Maybe there are other universes floating around in a vast multiverse. Coexisting alongside each other. And maybe, there are other multiverses that are inside a ‘metaverse’ of some kind. And this goes on and on. Maybe that is the infinity that we talk about. Everything is enclosed in something and that goes on forever.
In all this is our tiny planet. Where we exist. A minuscule part of a humongous system. Thinking we are important.
