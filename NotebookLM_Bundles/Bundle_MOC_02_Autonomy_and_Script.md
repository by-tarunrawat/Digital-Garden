## Night 1: Equipment & Procrastination
1. Like the youth, do you feel determined from the outset to reject the philosopher’s theories? WHy might that be?
I don’t. I’m quite open to ideas. Especially about self improvement. I don’t think everything works for everyone. Not the same way at least. So, although the philosopher is right. Past has no effect on the present. But it does in a way. Doesn’t it?

2. “Everyone wishes they could change,” the youth says. Do you agree? If you could change one thing about yourself, what would it be and why?
I don’t know about everyone. Maybe. Most people do wish they could change. NOt everyone is completely happy with everything they have in their life. Somebody wants to change something physical, someone wants to change mental things. For someone it might be a very small thing. But most people do want to change.
If I could change one thing about myself, it would be my procrastination. Or the inability to take action. Maybe its the same thing. I procrastinate taking action. I think that is what I would like to change. Because it has stalled me in life. I think I’m stuck because of it. I probably procrastinate because of some fear. I don’t think the fear is of failing. Maybe it is a little bit. I feel sometimes I don’t know what I’m doing. WHat would people think about it. Would they make fun of me. It doesn’t matter. I know. But the feeling is still there. I should just stop giving a fuck. Should just go for something. Just sell something. Just do something. 

3. What “equipment” do you possess? Assess how successfully, on a scale from 1-10, you are using your equipment to bring happiness to your life in this moment. 
I think I have decent intelligence. I’m not saying I’m a genius or anything. But I pride myself on learning anything. Doing anything. I can pick up any new thing and learn it very quickly. I don’t think I’m using it. I can learn anything really fast. SO why not put it into use. I’m not using it as of now.

## Night 2: Inferiority & Career Regression

Its neither comforting nor distressing. I know that when you are in any kind of meaningful relationship with someone, you are bound to get hurt. I think that is the nature of interpersonal relationships. It can be a boss-employee, wife-husband, mother-son. Whatever the relationship, there is always a possibility of getting hurt. I think our job is to be empathetic and preempt these situations.

I feel pretty bad right now. I don’t feel I’m living up to my full potential. I do feel inferior right now. I haven’t achieved anything in life right now. ## Night 3: The Validation Trap & Cooley's Mirror
7. Answer the philosopher’s question: why does one want to be praised by others?
We want to be praised by others because it gives us validation. If our work, our ideas are not recognized by others,they feel useless. Not just recognized but praised and appreciated. It gives us the validation that what we are doing is worth something. Not just to me but someone else as well. We want to be praised, because we are insecure. We don’t have confidence in ourselves. The worth we give to ourselves should be enough. Isn’t it. But then what is the difference between being delusional about it and being aware. I mean, I could think that I’m a good writer. Or I could think I’m the greatest writer the world has ever seen. One is clearly delusional. But that is the worth I can attach to myself. It is from someone else’s feedback that I can gauge my value. Isn’t it?

8. The philosopher offers the following definition of freedom: “Freedom is being disliked by other people.” How would you define freedom.
I would say something similar to this. I would say freedom is the power to say or do anything without thinking about what other people think. It is the ability to be free of other’s opinions and criticism. You can not stop people from giving you advice. Telling you how things should be. How you should behave. What is good and what is bad. If you listen to it, it gives them power you. You feel happy because someone liked what you did. Or you feel depressed because someone criticized you. You become a prisoner to other peoples opinion. Your happiness depends on other people. 
To be truly free you have to rise above it. You have to free yourselves from what others think about you. Your only job is to do things for yourself. Things that make you happy. Things that you want to do in life. Leave what others think to them. It’s not your job to influence their thought. Your job is to just be.
It think even “The subtle art of not giving a fuck” proposes this idea, in a different way.

9. Do you have the courage to be disliked? OR do you know anyone in your life who seems to? Of so, do their relationships or ours seem “things of lightness” as the philosopher suggests.
I don’t think I have anyone in my life like that. Everyone is a prisoner of what other people think. There was this quote by some philosopher Cooley that Jay Shetty talked about in one of his interviews. “I am not what I think I am. I am not what you think I am. I am what I think you think I am.” We mold ourselves to be people we think others perceive us to be. If I think that others think I’m cool, I’ll work hard to maintain that facade. I’ll do anything to keep that up. Even though, that is not who I am. I am, and most other people, are afraid to show their true selves to others. Everybody wants to behave the way it is expected of them. They live lives the way society wants them to live. Graduation, job, get married have children, then retire. Everything in there is constructed by someone else for you. Your life is mapped out for you by other people. People you might not even know. People who are doing the same thing. They haven’t got a clue who they are as well. Yet we follow that path. We listen to these people. I have one life — that I know of. And I’ve lived more than one third of it living by someone else’s rules. I have to start living by my own. Before it is too late.

Apart from these, My friends from college. Which include my girlfriend. Even they give me a sense of a community. We have similar opinions and thoughts. Similar sense of humor. I can depend on these people as well.

My life is worth living regardless of whether I’m of use to these people or not. But a lot of what I do depends on them. I want to live in the cities where these people are. I go traveling with them. I play games, sports and do all kinds of things with them. Even with regards to taking a job, I would prefer taking a job where I can be with them.

12. The philosopher offers the youth the same advice Adler offered once: “someone has to start.” That is, to create a meaningful life, a sense of community, it must begin with you regardless of what others around you are doing. How practical do you find this advice? What are concrete ways you might begin to “start”?
That is true. I agree with Adler on this. I believe if you want to change something, you are the one that has to take the first step. If you don’t do the things you believe in, then nobody else will. 
It is difficult to start though. When no one agrees with you. You can feel alone. There might be times when you might think you are wrong. After all everybody else seems to think so. It can be difficult. But your other options aren’t so good either. You can’t live your life pretending you don’t believe in something. It will ultimately bring you unhappiness. You have to take responsibility for your own beliefs, your own ideas.

## Night 5: Presence, Action Plans & Meaningful Labor
13. Were you surprised, comforted, and/or fascinated to read that “there is no such thing as a 100 percent person”? How can you actively acknowledge this fact to yourself, as the philosopher suggests?
I’m not sure what this means. But yeah, I agree. There is no one who can say they are a 100 percent person. I’m not great at everything I do. I’m not completely good. Nor am I complete. There is always something that can be improved in everyone. That is just the imperfections of life. We have to accept that and try to get as close to 100 percent as we can.

14. Labor is one way we come to feel useful and worthwhile, and therefore happy. What aspects of your work give you a sense of fulfillment? Do some aspects of your labor detract from your happiness?
Right now nothing. There is nothing work-wise that gives me any kind of fulfillment. When I did work, there was this fulfillment of doing something with my life. Even though it wasn’t fulfilling in itself. But being useful to something, making money, and being able to say I work somewhere was fulfilling enough.

15. Share how you plan to cast a spotlight on the here and now. What sort of action plan can you make to focus on living in the present moment?
I don’t know really. I’ve, in fact, started doing the opposite. I mean I don’t know if its the opposite, but I’ve started planning out my days a little bit. I don’t follow the plan completely, but I try to. And that is enough for now.  I guess. I’m writing everyday. So that’s good. Hope it leads somewhere. For now I’m treating it like therapy. The question is weird. “An action plan to live in the present moment.” I mean how the fuck do you do that. Give in to all your impulses? Maybe. Do whatever you feel like all the time? Then we all would just eat ice cream, watch porn and masturbate. SO how does the action plan really look like?
So I think what this means is just giving everything to whatever you’re doing in the present moment. Give it your all. So If I’m writing, then I’m focused on this completely. If I’m cooking, I’m there in that moment with everything I have. Do not think about the end result. Do not think about how the story will turn out. Just write. And the story will take care of itself. Maybe not. But that is not the point of writing. Same with cooking. Stay focused on each moment and the final dish will be good.

The Hindi film industry can be very unforgiving, demanding, ruthless, unfair, inaccessible, almost dreamlike for the everyone else outside of the industry. In a normal scenario, if you want to work as an engineer, you just need the educational qualifications for it. You want to be a doctor, you study, if you get through the entrance, you study for a few years and become a doctor. This is the same for CAs, Lawyers, corporate management. I’m saying that most of them will never realize their dreams.

Many successful people, in their biographies say, how persistent they were towards their goals and that’s why they were successful. You’ve got to be dedicated, work hard, single focus on your goals, challenge yourself, suffer for your cause, learn from your mistakes and on and on and on. We, simple minded people, take it as a roadmap to success. We tell ourselves, if I work hard enough I will make it. I’m sure I will make it because so many of my idols have. It’s all bullshit of course. 
Working hard is no guarantee to success and being persistent often pulls you in at 300 kms an hours towards the huge tornado of poverty, inside you find uprooted dreams and discarded talent. Just swirling around. Ready to be thrown somewhere far away. So far from the 
Poverty is a cruel thing. If you’ve never had to think where your next meal is coming from, it becomes harder to be persistent. How can you focus on the next audition if your stomach is screaming - my god you pathetic piece of existence, put something in me or so help me god…

I remember, I used to dream I am on the pitch with Sachin Tendulkar, some nights I would win games, make centuries. Don’t hold yourself from doing things you want to do. It’s a recipe for an unsatisfactory future. 
I was taught the opposite lesson. Don’t be yourself. Don’t say anything. DO things this way. Think about what other people will think. Think about what society will say. I’m still told these things at home. My uncle told me to change myself during interviews. Which might be a good advice to get through a job interview, but a terrible advice to get through life. Pretend. Pretend you don’t when you do or do when you don’t. Pretend to be calm when you are simmering inside. Pretend wrong is right. Pretend they know better. Pretend you know better. Just pretend, pretend, pretend.

She’ll probably ask me why I want to do it. Then she’ll try to solve that. SHe is a good person. She doesn’t want to see me like this. She’ll probably think, I’m stressed — which is true — and that’s why I want to leave — this is not.
Maybe this is why I want my protagonist to run. The girl in my story. I want her to go away. To some place. It’s because I want to do it. Wow! I never thought about it this way. Maybe I should write this story. Maybe I will have to run away for it. 
How do you do it though? How do you just go away? Leaving all your obligations behind. People you love. People who depend on you. People who care about you. I feel if I did that they will be worried sick. How am I doing? How will they ever live without being in touch with me. Leaving would mean leaving all those relationships behind. How can I do that? Isn’t that drastic. When people say do what you want, it’s not entirely true. You can’t do everything you want without breaking a few hearts. Without hurting people. Do I want to be the person who does that? Or is it another excuse to not go for things I want to do?

I don’t know much about cars. I don’t drive. I tried learning once, but due to some reasons didn’t drive after that. And now I don’t have enough confidence to do it. 
Also we’ve never had a used car. We’ve had 3 cars in our house. The first one was a Maruti 800. It was good. Navy blue. Compact. Then we got a Maruti zen. It was silver gray. I remember it’s number as well. 8820. We sold both of them. I don’t know where they are now. 
Our current car is an i20. My mom drives it around. I still can’t drive. My brother bought a used car once. It was Mitsubishi I think. I don’t really remember. It was a decent car. I really don’t know much about cars. I’m just writing shit here. I’ve never noticed cars either. 
People do buy used cars all the time. Amit has a used car in Pune. Doesn’t look used. He maintains it well. He is a good guy. He takes care of things. He takes care of people. We keep in touch every now and then. But I can count on him. He is my best friend. Has been with me through a lot. I’m really lucky to have him as a friend. I don’t really believe in luck. But I thank whatever coincidence has brought us together.
A used car is like a friendship? No not a good premise for anything. I don’t know how to conclude that sentence. 
A used car is like a friendship… Still can’t come up with anything.

Nobody really wants a used car. If people had enough money, they would always buy a new car. No one will buy a used car. I don’t think I’m wrong. People buy used cars because they don’t want to spend an extra 2-3 lacs on a new one. It doesn’t seem like a good idea. People buy these things on loan anyway. If they had 7 lacs to buy a car. They would. But you have to analyze what you really want. Do you want a new car and take the financial burden or buy a used car. You’ll get what you want — probably. But it’ll have some mileage on it.
If you think about it, the mileage doesn’t matter. It’s just the thought. A new car makes you feel better than a used car I guess. It’s a matter of esteem I guess. A used car doesn’t have the same prestige. But what does it matter. If the car works fine, and looks good. Doesn’t have scratches or dents. Then an old car or a new car is one and the same thing. Even if there are niggles, the cost of repairs and services is far less than buying a new car. 
Why is prestige important though? Why do we want new things? Why can’t we be satisfied with old stuff. Do we want functionality? Or do we want the feeling of newness. Because as I see it, the newness is temporary. It will get old in a couple of weeks. Maybe a couple of months. Then what?
If you want functionality, and you buy whatever accordingly, then it will be there 2 weeks from now, 2 months or 2 years. WE’re just stuck in this strange perception. Strange ideas. New is better. Maybe. But do you need it?
