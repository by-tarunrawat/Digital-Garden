# Aachoo Online: Master Story Bible

## 1. Premise & Thematic Engine
- **Genre:** Dark Comedy / Media Satire (Series / Feature).
- **Logline:** When a penniless small-town boy's desperate bid for social media fame leads to a staged street altercation that goes viral as a sectarian hate crime, he is co-opted by an amoral digital PR strategist, a ratings-starved TV anchor, and a local politician hungry for power.
- **The Core Paradox:** To become someone in the modern world, you must surrender all authentic privacy and dignity; yet the only true defense against modern tyranny is total anonymity.
- **Thematic Axes:**
  - *Performative Living vs. Physical Reality:* Waking up early to do makeup only to climb back into bed for a "#wokeuplikethis" selfie; ordering a single bun samosa while projecting luxury dining.
  - *The Outrage Assembly Line:* Minor grievances repackaged by shadowy PR firms ("Sosal Baba") and broadcast on screaming debate television ("Nation Already Knows") to polarize electorates and juice corporate sponsorship.
  - *The Anonymity Counter-Philosophy:* "Gumnaami me hi asli shohrat chupi hai" (True honor resides in obscurity). Without anonymity in discourse, truth becomes impossible because public speech is held hostage by the mob.

## 2. World Mechanics & Satirical Architecture
- **The Grift Stages:**
  1. *Small-Town Micro-Celebrity (Kasar, Almora):* Aaroh Chauhan ("Aachoo") achieves 3,000 hyper-local followers because people summon him by sneezing. He attempts to monetize local grocery stores (Semwal Atta Chakki, Sharmila Aunty's store) and charges ₹100 for "How to be an Influencer" courses.
  2. *The Delhi Metamorphosis:* Starving in a Delhi flat with ₹3,199 in his account, dodging rent, and getting rejected by underwear brands demanding 100k minimum followings.
  3. *The Sosal Baba Machinery:* PR agencies acting as cyber-mercenaries who weaponize victimhood, stage mob incidents, coordinate WhatsApp forward networks, and supply controversy-ready guests to television news channels.

## 3. The Looking-Glass Thesis & Character Psychology
- **The Core Axiom:** *"Mai wo nahi jo mai khud ko sochta hun. Mai wo bhi nahi jo tum mujhko sochte ho. Mai wo hun jo mujhe lagta hai tum mujhko sochte ho."* (Charles Horton Cooley's Looking-Glass Self applied to algorithmic feed validation).
- **The Core Antithesis:** *"Sooraj ki tarah chamakne ke liye pehle sooraj ki tarah jalna padta hai."* Aachoo seeks public luminescence without personal craft, moral friction, or discipline.
- **The Climax Philosophy:** True integrity is not audience size; it is trust. One authentic person trusting your word outweighs ten million botnet impressions.

## 4. Macro-Influence & The JBDY Architecture
- **The JBDY Structural Model:** Follows the classic *Jaane Bhi Do Yaaro* comedic engine—two naive small-town creators stumble onto high-level corporate/political murder, transport the corpse across public venues and livestreams to clear their names, and end up framed while the oligarchs walk free.
- **The Ultimate Influencer (Bilas Gautam):** Contrasting small-time social media creators (whose fragile fame can be destroyed by a single offensive tweet) with global billionaires. Bilas Gautam runs the $50B Gautam Foundation, funding global health organizations and pharmaceutical manufacturers while shaping worldwide mandates. Real influence is not follower metrics; it is the structural power to rewrite the rules of society.

# Character Roster

### 1. Aaroh Chauhan ("Aachoo")
- **Age:** 24–26 (moves to Delhi from Kasar, Almora).
- **Psychology:** Insecure, attention-starved, fundamentally naive. Confuses follower counts with human love and dignity. Willing to fake gym visits, borrow friends' food for photos, stage romantic proposals with his mother's borrowed ring, and fake hate crimes.
- **Catchphrase / Philosophy:** "Mai hamesha se famous hun, bas abhi logon ko pata nahi hai."

### 2. Sosal Baba
- **Role:** Digital PR Fixer / Troll Kingmaker.
- **Modus Operandi:** Operates from the shadows; commands an army of botnets, content creators, and IT cells. Fixes election candidacies and brand crises using calibrated street chaos and communal tension.
- **Maxim:** "Fasadon, haadson, jungon me hi hum ek hote hain. Koi aafat na aaye to, koi apna nahi hota."

### 3. Abhijeet Rane / Samachar
- **Role:** Prime-Time News Anchor ("Nation Already Knows").
- **Conflict:** Plummeting TRPs, terrified of being ousted for younger, louder anchors. Needs constant scandal, screaming panels, and manufactured communal outrage to keep corporate advertisers from pulling out.

### 4. Pataka Guddi (Priyadarshini Singh)
- **Role:** Veteran Influencer / Aspiring Actress (1.5M followers).
- **Conflict:** Approaching late twenties; caught between wanting serious acting roles and being forced into provocative content and sensationalized reality shows (*Love Beach*). Sees through Aachoo's innocence and calls out his descent into hollow performance.

### 5. Puran Singh Rawat
- **Role:** Generational Politician from Dehradun.
- **Conflict:** Ignored by party leadership for decades because "jo dikhta hai, wahi bikta hai." Hires Sosal Baba to manufacture hate speech and viral TV controversies to secure a legislative election ticket.

### 6. Inspector Dev Kanungo ("Mr. Kanun")
- **Role:** Celebrity Police Officer (2M followers).
- **Reality:** Livestreams police encounters while possessing a bodybuilder physique hiding deep cowardice; gained his entire reputation from an accidental firearm discharge that killed a wanted criminal while he was recording a goodbye video.

### 7. Danger Singh & Nuclear Khan
- **Role:** Syndicate Loan Sharks.
- **Modus Operandi:** Danger Singh enforces street-level debt with goons (Dayan Dola, Bayan Dola, Chaudi Chaati). His superior, Nuclear Khan, runs a high-tech extortion racket forcing defaulters into an online livestream begging ring.

### 8. Ghoomti Chappal (Ghulam Chawla)
- **Role:** "Nomadic" Travel Influencer (500k followers).
- **The Reality:** A reclusive, overweight operator in a claustrophobic Delhi apartment using high-end green-screen setups and stock backdrops to fake world travel and yoga retreats. Sosal Baba's prime promotional puppet.

### 9. Dabboo
- **Role:** Aachoo’s roommate and loyal sidekick.
- **Conflict:** Bored corporate IT worker who moonlights as Aachoo’s amateur beatboxer and cameraman, craving the illusion of bohemian influencer excitement.

### 10. GeeGro (Gulshan Grover)
- **Role:** Owner of Badnam Cafe facing the Panchachuli range in Kasar; Aachoo's reluctant mentor.
- **The Secret:** He is veteran Bollywood villain Gulshan Grover. Disillusioned with toxic celebrity culture and the loss of privacy 15 years ago, he grew a flowing beard, adopted hippie robes, and retired to the hills. When tourists claim he resembles the movie star, he laughs it off.
- **Dramatic Function:** The voice of hard-won wisdom warning Aachoo against the narcotic trap of temporary fame.

### 11. Satyendra Chauhan ("Chauhan Ji")
- **Role:** Aachoo's father; proprietor of *Chauhan & Sons Readymade Garments* in Almora for 26 years.
- **History:** Rejected by the army due to knock-knees; turned his baby son into an in-store child mannequin whose very first spoken word was *"Photo"*.
- **Dynamic:** Despite outward gruffness, he drinks rum with Aachoo in the evenings, offering him total autonomy to pursue creative careers provided he has an honest plan.

### 12. Roma Jogan (Romila Joshi)
- **Role:** Podcasting Titan (40 million monthly listeners).
- **Background:** Escaped an arranged marriage in Dehradun with under ₹7,000 saved in a piggy bank (living an ascetic "jogan" lifestyle).
- **Style:** Unvarnished, drinks and smokes joints on-camera, hosts high-profile experts while plotting to retire to a 150-acre farm in Kasar with 4 dogs and 50 adopted children.
- **Negotiation:** Commands a ₹250-crore licensing fee from SwarApp, complete with a contract clause for a private helicopter to fly guests directly into Almora.

### 13. Bilas Gautam
- **Role:** Tech/Pharma Multibillionaire (Top 5 global wealth).
- **Modus Operandi:** Retired from chip/software manufacturing at 50 to run global philanthropy. Influences WHO policies, health mandates, and digital narrative management through cyber-firms and co-opted influencers.

### 14. Vidya Sengupta (@JamesBong / @SharonStoner)
- **Role:** Masked Satirical Meme Creator & Whistleblower.
- **Function:** Wears a Sharon Stone mask to systematically expose the fraudulent staging behind popular influencers, partnering with hacktivist collective @Incognito.

# Episode Beat Sheet

## Act I: The Local Legend of Kasar (Episodes 1–2)
- **The Sneezing Network:** Aaroh grows up in Kasar where any sneeze summons him to run household errands in exchange for jalebis and parathas. When smartphones arrive, the summons shift to DMs. He accumulates 3,000 followers and is declared "Kasar's Influencer."
- **The Monetization Disaster:** Urged by his friends to earn money, Aachoo demands sponsorship money from Sharmila Aunty's grocery store and Jagdish's flour mill. Both point out they already serve 100% of the town.
- **The ₹100 Masterclass:** He launches a handwritten course on "How to Become an Influencer." When villagers discover he posted the videos publicly on his own feed for free, they refuse to pay. When he attempts to extort ₹100 from everyone who liked the video, the town mass-unlikes his entire catalog, triggering an existential crisis.
- **The Delhi Enclave:** A touring group of elite urban creators (AlwaysRightKhurana, SoDeBabe, TanManNoDhan) arrives at the Kumaoni resort. Aachoo realizes his small-town metrics are a joke and defies his father to chase national stardom.

## Act II: The Capital Crucible & The Hoax (Episodes 3–5)
- **Starvation in Delhi:** Aachoo shares a flat with roommates Mohit and Dilbagh. With ₹3,199 left in his account, he calculates cart costs down to a single ₹52 bun samosa. Underwear brands reject him for lacking 100k followers.
- **Enter Sosal Baba:** Desperate, Aachoo approaches Sosal Baba, who assigns lieutenant Achraj Bhai to engineer controversial content.
- **The Staged Mugging Gone Wrong:** Aachoo hires his roommates to play street thugs so he can film himself heroically fighting them. The roommates arrive in bizarre disguises (one wearing a burkha, one with a fake maulvi beard). When Aachoo refuses to hit "a woman," a genuine street brawl breaks out and beats Aachoo bloody.
- **The Communal Spin:** Achraj Bhai uploads the raw footage. News anchor Abhijeet Rane frames the fight as a communal hate crime. Aachoo awakens as a national martyr, skyrocketing past 500,000 followers.

## Act III: The Studio Crucible & The Collapse (Episodes 6–8)
- **The Fame High:** Aachoo is invited onto prime-time debate shows, brand campaigns, and guest spots on daily soaps and reality show *Love Beach*.
- **The Mirror Encounter:** On *Love Beach*, he meets Priyadarshini (Pataka Guddi), who is trapped in her own degrading reality-show performance to survive algorithm drops. They trade brutal truths about their synthetic identities.
- **The Political Co-optation:** Puran Singh Rawat uses Aachoo's viral incident to stoke legislative election rallies. Sosal Baba orchestrates street protests that threaten real-world violence.
- **The Confession & The Fall:** When Aachoo discovers that innocent people are being targeted in retaliatory riots, his conscience breaks. He goes live to reveal the truth: the entire assault was a botched staged stunt for brand engagement. 
- **Resolution:** Aachoo is de-platformed, stripped of his verified badge, and abandoned by brands, TV networks, and fans. Penniless but finally calm, he walks away into the quiet streets, embracing true anonymity.

## Alternative Feature Arc: The Loan Shark Road-Trip & Green-Screen Hoax
- **Act I (The Debt):** After his viral 15-second dance decays from 200k to 12k followers, Aachoo borrows ₹50,000 from loan shark Danger Singh to purchase Sosal Baba's viral package. Sosal Baba vanishes. Danger Singh threatens to hand Aachoo over to Nuclear Khan's cyber-begging ring unless repaid ₹2,00,000 in 30 days.
- **Act II (The Parvati Valley Chase):** Aachoo and Dabboo scrape together bus fare to Parvati Valley to track down Sosal's client, travel influencer Ghoomti Chappal. They work at GeeGro's Badnam Cafe and cannabis farm. When they rush to Khudkhushi Point where Chappal is livestreaming, the scenic stall is completely abandoned.
- **Act III (The Green-Screen Unmasking):** In Delhi, they corner Ghoomti Chappal at an elite influencer gala and trail him home, discovering his entire travel persona is shot on a budget green screen in his bedroom. They catch Sosal Baba attempting to flee.
- **Act IV (The Murder Interrogation & The Livestream Jailbreak):** Caught near a political assassination, Aachoo is interrogated by celebrity cop Inspector Dev Kanungo. Aachoo demands copies of the interrogation video for content. Locked in a cell with a gangster who turns out to be a loyal follower, they stage an escape and launch a livestream crime-fighter series that explodes nationwide.

## The Unified 3-Act Feature / Miniseries Arc (PR Agency & Whistleblower)
- **Act I: The Almora Micro-Celebrity & The Rally Scandal**
  - Aachoo rules Almora with 1 lakh local followers, doing photo shoots with dumbbells and begging Golu Devta for blogging awards.
  - At a local college event, a corrupt MP is trapped by aggressive student protesters. Aachoo intervenes and films himself helping the MP escape.
  - The video goes viral, but public reaction turns vicious, labeling Aachoo a corrupt political shill. Aachoo posts an unhinged apology video that causes a mass follower exodus.
  - In desperation, he calls Sosal Baba, who invites him to Delhi.

- **Act II: The Outrage Machine & The Corporate High**
  - Aachoo joins Sosal Baba's high-tech PR and communications agency. He discovers a secret production floor featuring botnet farms, automated trolling squads, and creator puppets like Bollybuff.
  - Aachoo produces weaponized videos defending corporate and political clients. His follower count surges past millions; he gets brand deals, TV debate invitations, and a luxury apartment.
  - Midpoint Turning Point: Aachoo discovers that a targeted online smear campaign he produced triggered real-world mob violence where innocent protesters were severely beaten. He confronts Sosal Baba, who coldly dismisses the collateral damage.

- **Act III: The Whistleblower Broadcast & The Jailhouse Smile**
  - Aachoo decides to expose the operation by recording a secret documentary inside the agency.
  - Agency Chairman Mr. Jagani intercepts him, threatening to wipe his entire digital identity and delete every account he owns unless he reads a scripted lie.
  - Remembering his mother's lesson on trust, Aachoo initiates an unannounced livestream from his mobile phone, fleeing through the studio corridors with security in pursuit while broadcasting the botnet farm and extortion ledgers directly to millions.
  - The police raid the building. Sosal Baba and Jagani are arrested, but Aachoo is also detained for past fraud.
  - Final Image: Aachoo is escorted to prison, more famous than he ever dreamed, mobbed by fans taking selfies through the police van bars. He looks directly at the camera with an ironic, knowing smile.
