# Chakra: Master Story Bible

## 1. Premise & Characters
- **Protagonist:** Parikshit Roy (32), broke, indebted gambler fleeing Delhi.
- **Antagonists / Companions:** Rocky (in a saree) and Rambo (t-shirt and jeans), enforcers for trans mob boss Bhaiya Ji.
- **The Mystery:** Dhananjay Roy (grandfather) wills his 70-crore estate on condition of retrieving an heirloom from the unmapped Himalayan village of Chakra / Padmaview.
- **The Anomaly:** When Parikshit is shot by Rambo for trying to leave, he wakes up intact the next morning. Time resets every night at midnight; no one ages, and no one can leave.
