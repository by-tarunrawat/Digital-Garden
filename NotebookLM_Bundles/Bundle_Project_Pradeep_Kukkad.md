# Pradeep Kukkad: Master Project Bible

## 1. Character & Transmedia Premise
- **The Concept:** Pradeep Kukkad is a half-human, half-rooster singer-songwriter attempting to make it as a legitimate indie pop star and viral digital creator.
- **The Tone:** Utterly deadpan sincerity meeting existential livestock horror. He treats routine poultry processing (marination, tandoors, egg collecting) as profound human tragedy and civil rights violations.
- **Medium Pipeline:** Acoustic guitar tracks, satirical YouTube mockumentaries, Instagram reels, and short music videos.

## 2. Song Catalog & Thematic Themes
1. **Ek Khwab (Tandoori Martyrdom):** Capo 6 acoustic ballad lamenting a lover who was marinated and cooked in a clay oven.
2. **I Fell in Love with a Goat:** Capo 4 country-folk ballad celebrating inter-species love facing the shared threat of the butcher's knife.
3. **Mere Ande:** Raw blues/folk grievance mourning children transformed into breakfast omelettes.
4. **Trust Me Bro:** Club/party satire critiquing toxic party peer pressure and pharmaceutical escapism.
